Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Minimum share capital is EUR 1.00; the subscribed capital must be fully paid in before registration, either in cash or in-kind, and the managing director must confirm that the funds are at the company's free disposal. No statutory escrow (Treuhandkonto) is mandated for cash contributions; a German bank account in the company's name is sufficient. The UG must add the suffix 'haftungsbeschränkt' to its name, and an annual reserve equal to 25% of net profit must be accumulated until the share capital reaches EUR 25,000.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible in both the current and capital accounts; no exchange-control licence, surrender obligation, or prior-authorization mechanism applies to commercial payments or capital flows. Restrictions arise only from EU/UN sanctions, national investment screening (Außenwirtschaftsgesetz §§ 5, 55-60), and prudential bank-side compliance. Profit remittance from a German UG is unrestricted in amount and currency, but non-resident dividend recipients are subject to German dividend withholding tax at the statutory rate of 25% plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge (plus church tax if applicable), potentially reduced under an applicable double-tax treaty or the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive; any payment into or out of Germany exceeding EUR 12,500 must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None; German law imposes no mandatory conversion of export receipts, and the UG may retain 100% of foreign-currency proceeds in its accounts. The only binding obligation is statistical: each receipt or payment for goods, services, or financial claims above EUR 12,500 (or the equivalent value on the value date) must be reported by the bank/exporter to the Deutsche Bundesbank within seven calendar days of the value date or, for receivables, within seven days of invoicing. No minimum conversion ratio applies.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (German Federal Bank), Frankfurt am Main; monetary policy is exercised through the Eurosystem/ECB, while the Bundesbank administers Germany's external-sector payment statistics and the Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung, AWV §§ 60-70) reporting regime. Banking conduct supervision is shared with the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Acceptable only as an auxiliary collection and payment rail, not as the primary banking relationship. Passported EEA electronic-money institutions (e.g., Airwallex, Revolut Business, Qonto) may lawfully provide EUR IBANs to a German UG under PSD2 as implemented by the German Payment Services Supervision Act (ZAG); funds are safeguarded but are not covered by the statutory deposit-guarantee scheme, and significant concentration/third-party risk exists. An offshore EMI without an EEA licence or German agency cannot provide regulated payment accounts. The UG should maintain a German-licensed credit institution as the legal-entity bank for notarial free-disposal confirmation, tax authority communication, and commercial-register acceptance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital injection is executed via SWIFT MT103 (or SEPA Credit Transfer within the EU/EEA) debited from an account beneficially owned exclusively by the shareholder. The remittance information must state 'Gesellschaftereinlage / Kapitalerhöhung' together with the notary file reference; SWIFT purpose code 'INTC' (intra-company) or an explicit 'EQUITY CONTRIBUTION' narrative is accepted by German booking systems. For any amount above EUR 12,500, the Bundesbank payment-statistics report (Zahlungsbilanzstatistik, form ZP, capital-investment category) must be filed within seven days.
