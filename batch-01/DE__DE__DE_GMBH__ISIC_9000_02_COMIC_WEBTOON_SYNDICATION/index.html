Competent corporate registry

Commercial Register (Handelsregister) of the Local Court (Amtsgericht - Registergericht) at the company's statutory seat; the German civil-law notary files the application electronically.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The standard GmbH requires minimum nominal share capital of EUR 25,000 (GmbHG s. 5(1)). Before registration, cash contributions must be paid to at least one quarter of each nominal amount and, in aggregate, at least EUR 12,500 must be placed at the free disposal of the managing directors (GmbHG s. 7(2)). Funds must be deposited with a German credit institution in the company's escrow/business account, and the bank must issue a certificate for the registration filing; in-kind contributions must be fully furnished and verified in a formation report. The Unternehmergesellschaft (haftungsbeschränkt) with EUR 1 capital is legally available but is not adopted in this formation.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt — Federal Central Tax Office, www.bzst.de) is the competent authority for the issuance of the Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer (VAT ID) and for cross-border VAT procedures; the local Finanzamt (municipal tax office) assigns the Steuernummer (tax number) and administers VAT, corporate income tax, and trade tax; statutory tax filings are submitted via the ELSTER portal (www.elster.de); the Bundesministerium der Finanzen (Federal Ministry of Finance) is the supreme tax policy and legal-oversight authority.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the GmbH through notarial deed and commercial-register entry (Handelsregister) at the competent Amtsgericht; non-resident shareholders may own 100% of the GmbH. Step 2: Within four weeks of business commencement, file the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung (tax registration questionnaire) with the responsible local Finanzamt, describing the digital webtoon publishing, multilingual licensing, and Apple/Google IAP revenue streams; the Finanzamt then assigns the local Steuernummer for corporate income tax, trade tax, and VAT. Step 3: File a separate application with the BZSt (Form USt 1 TG or the BZSt online portal) to obtain the Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer, which is mandatory for intra-EU supplies, recapitulative statements, and OSS registration. Step 4: Activate an ELSTER account using the Steuernummer to submit monthly VAT preliminary returns, annual VAT returns, corporate income tax returns, trade tax returns, and the Zusammenfassende Meldung (EC Sales List).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Germany does not apply a US-style check-the-box election; a GmbH is mandatorily subject to entity-level taxation. Available elective mechanisms include: (i) Organschaft (fiscal unity) under §14 KStG and §2 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UStG, electable by concluding a profit-and-loss transfer agreement (Ergebnisabführungsvertrag) with a 100% German parent and registering the agreement in the commercial register; (ii) voluntary VAT registration under §19 Abs. 2 UStG for small businesses; (iii) the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) election under §18j UStG for EU B2C digital sales; (iv) the reverse-charge election under §13b UStG for designated domestic supplies; and (v) documentation and election duties concerning hybrid mismatch arrangements under §4k EStG and the ATAD implementation framework to prevent the denial of deduction or inclusion mismatches.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All entrepreneurs must register for VAT upon commencing business activity; there is no minimum turnover threshold for mandatory registration. The optional small-business exemption under §19 UStG is available where prior-year turnover did not exceed EUR 25,000 (EUR 22,000 until 31 December 2024) and expected current-year turnover is not projected to exceed EUR 100,000 (raised from EUR 50,000 by the 2024 Wachstumschancengesetz). Digital B2C webtoon sales through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing are subject to German VAT from the first euro without any de minimis threshold, as the place of supply for B2C digital services is the consumer's country of residence.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A German webtoon publisher may claim 0% German VAT or non-taxability for: (i) intra-Community B2B supplies to EU taxable persons holding a valid VAT ID — §§6a and 6e UStG, evidenced by the customer's VAT ID and the periodic recapitulative statement; (ii) exports of physical goods to third countries — §§4 Nr. 1a and 6 UStG, evidenced by customs exit confirmation; (iii) B2B digital licensing and syndication services to non-EU publishers where the place of supply is outside Germany under §3a Abs. 2 UStG; and (iv) B2C webtoon and e-comic sales to non-EU consumers, which are outside German VAT jurisdiction under §3a Abs. 3 UStG. Input VAT on related acquisition, production, translation, and platform-commission costs remains fully deductible as export turnover; documentary retention of customer tax status, IP licensing contracts, and payment evidence is mandatory at all times.

Germany does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping system. Mandatory structured e-invoicing (E-Rechnung) applies to domestic B2B transactions: from 1 January 2025, every VAT-registered business must be technically capable of receiving EN 16931-compliant structured invoices; from 1 January 2026, suppliers with prior-year turnover exceeding EUR 800,000 must issue e-invoices; from 1 January 2027, all suppliers must issue e-invoices in XRechnung or ZUGFeRD 2.x formats; EDI-based bilateral formats remain transitional until 31 December 2027. There is no government API clearance or pre-stamping requirement; however, invoices must be transmitted electronically to the recipient by a compliant exchange channel, and Apple/Google IAP settlement reports, merchant statements, and subscriber purchase records must be retained in machine-readable form for tax audit purposes.

Unsupported or fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger: (i) retrospective VAT reassessment with interest on arrears at 1.8% per annum under §233a AO and late-payment surcharges of 1% per month under §240 AO; (ii) liability under §14c UStG for an incorrect VAT display and administrative fines under §26a UStG and §§378–384 AO; (iii) prosecution for tax evasion under §370 AO with imprisonment up to five years or fines for deliberate misrepresentation and up to ten years in aggravated cases, with the assessment period extended to ten years under §169 Abs. 2 AO; (iv) retrospective denial or clawback of input VAT deductions under §15 UStG where export or zero-rating evidence is not produced during a VAT special audit; and (v) cross-checks by the BZSt and customs authorities against export declarations, Apple/Google IAP settlement flows, and bank records — unrepatriated export proceeds beyond 180 days without commercial substantiation create a presumption of non-arm's-length consideration, allowing income reattribution and withholding proceedings.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Euro is fully convertible under Articles 63-66 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union; Germany imposes no exchange controls and does not administratively distinguish between capital and current account convertibility. Profit remittance from a German GmbH to non-resident shareholders is freely permitted, subject only to German corporate law formalities (valid shareholder resolution, distributable balance sheet profit under the German Commercial Code) and, where applicable, dividend withholding tax of 25 percent plus 5.5 percent solidarity surcharge on the tax, with church tax applicable to German-resident individual shareholders. Treaty relief or the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive may reduce or eliminate withholding for qualifying corporate shareholders. No Bundesbank or BaFin pre-approval is required for inbound or outbound remittance transactions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany imposes no statutory surrender, repatriation timeline, or mandatory conversion ratio on export proceeds; a German GmbH may freely retain, convert, or disburse foreign currency receipts from webtoon syndication and digital publishing. The sole compliance duty is statistical: cross-border payments from or to non-residents that exceed EUR 12,500 per transaction or per month must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank under Section 70 AWV (Zahlungsmeldung), using the Bundesbank's electronic reporting channel within the prescribed timeline. The mandatory conversion ratio is zero percent.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (Germany's central bank) is the designated statistical and foreign trade reporting authority under the Außenwirtschaftsverordnung (AWV); regulatory supervision of banks, fintech institutions, and payment services is exercised by BaFin (Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. An offshore digital EMI (for example, a United Kingdom or Singapore e-money institution) without a German BaFin license or an EU/EEA PSD2 passport cannot lawfully provide payment accounts or treasury-clearing settlement services to a German GmbH. Viable structures are limited to BaFin-licensed German EMIs or EEA-licensed EMIs validly passporting into Germany under Article 28 of the EEA PSD2 framework. Tier-1 German banks generally decline to accept EMI balances as the sole treasury clearing rail due to segregation, counterparty, and settlement-finality concerns; therefore, EMIs are treated as supplementary collection channels for Apple IAP and Google Play Billing proceeds, not as the primary liquidity clearing backbone.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing for non-resident shareholder capital contributions: SWIFT MT103 for non-EU shareholders or SEPA Direct Credit for EU/EEA shareholders, credited to the GmbH's German capital escrow account (Gründungskonto), with the remittance information field clearly stating the purpose as 'Einlage in inländische Kapitalgesellschaft' (capital contribution into a domestic German limited-liability company) and referencing the notarial deed and intended Commercial Register filing.
