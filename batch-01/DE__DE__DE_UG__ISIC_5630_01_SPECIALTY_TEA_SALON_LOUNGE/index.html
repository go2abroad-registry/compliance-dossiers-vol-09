Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Locally competent Finanzamt of the Bundesland in which the UG has its registered seat (state-administered under the respective Oberfinanzdirektion); central federal authorities: Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt) for allocation of the VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.) under §27a UStG; central e-filing portal: ELSTER (www.elster.de).

E-government filing portal

Within one month after commencement of business, file the standardized 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire, Form 15/15a) with the local Finanzamt via ELSTER, attaching the notarized articles of association of the UG, Commercial Register excerpt, identity documents of all managing directors, and beneficial-ownership declaration (UL-Declaration). The Finanzamt issues the domestic Steuernummer (tax number); separately apply to the BZSt for the USt-IdNr., which is obligatory for intra-EU supplies and for the invoice content requirements under §14 UStG.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the UG is a capital company (corporate body) fully subject to corporation tax (Körperschaftsteuer at 15%), solidarity surcharge (5.5% of the corporate tax), and municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer, base rate 3.5% multiplied by a municipal Hebesatz typically between 200% and 490%); it is not partnership-transparent and no pass-through attribution of German-source income to non-resident shareholders arises outside the CFC/anti-hybrid rules; worldwide income is taxable in Germany unless a tax treaty assigns the income elsewhere.

Available election forms

German tax law does not provide a check-the-box (fiscally transparent entity) election. Applicable statutory elections and groupings: (1) Organschaft (fiscal unity) for corporation tax, trade tax and VAT, requiring a majority financial participation and a profit-and-loss pooling agreement; (2) Kleinunternehmer relief under §19 UStG where prior-year turnover ≤ EUR 25,000 and current-year turnover ≤ EUR 100,000, which exempts output VAT but forfeits input VAT recovery and is generally unsuitable for an entity-level UG with B2B customers; (3) §9 UStG option to waive VAT exemption for real-estate letting, irrelevant to tea-salon operations; (4) eligibility to convert the UG into a full GmbH after accumulating EUR 25,000 capital without a tax event under the Umwandlungsgesetz (mere corporate-form continuation).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold: VAT registration is mandatory upon the first entrepreneurial activity (§2 UStG), and the UG must report preliminary VAT (Vorsteueranmeldungen) monthly or quarterly. The only statutory relief is the Kleinunternehmer exemption (§19 UStG) for prior-year turnover ≤ EUR 25,000 and current-year forecast turnover ≤ EUR 100,000, which is normally inadvisable because it blocks input-VAT recovery and may distort B2B pricing.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate applies to: (a) goods physically exported from the EU to third countries under §4 No. 1(a) and §6 UStG (e.g., packaged loose-leaf tea, tea tins, and ceramic teaware); (b) intra-Community B2B supplies under §6a UStG where the customer provides a valid USt-IdNr.; and (c) B2B services supplied to non-EU entrepreneurs under §3a UStG by reason of place-of-supply rules (reverse charge). On-premises beverage service in the salon and take-away beverages remain taxable at 19%, whereas the sale of packaged tea for off-premises consumption qualifies for the 7% reduced rate under §12 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 UStG.

  • Export accompanying document (Ausfuhrbegleitdokument / ABD) or transit documentation as applicable

Germany operates no real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping system. Mandatory structured e-invoicing (XRechnung or ZUGFeRD 2.x) applies to domestic B2B supplies from 1 January 2025 on the issuer side once the recipient is e-invoice-capable; obligatory issuance is phased: businesses with turnover above EUR 800,000 from 1 January 2027 and all businesses from 1 January 2028. Retail B2C transactions may use simplified receipts, but every electronic cash register/POS system used by the tea salon must incorporate a certified Technische Sicherheitseinrichtung (TSE) under §146a AO and the KassenSichV, with per-transaction digital signatures and mandatory receipt issuance (Belegausgabepflicht).

Fraudulent or unsupported export zero-rating constitutes tax evasion under §370 AO punishable by imprisonment up to 5 years or a fine, and a VAT misdemeanor under §26a UStG with fines up to EUR 500,000. The Finanzamt may retrospectively deny the zero rating, assess 19% VAT with interest at 1.8% per annum (statutory rate from 1 January 2025), and extend the assessment period from the standard 4 years to 10 years in concealment cases; under §162 AO the taxable basis may be estimated, and repeated false declarations can lead to suspension of export privileges, seizure of goods and criminal prosecution under the Foreign Trade and Payments Regulation (AWV).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany provides full current-account and capital-account convertibility for the euro. There is no general exchange-control statute requiring licences for trade or capital flows; convertibility is guaranteed under Article 63 TFEU and the OECD Code of Liberalisation. Tea imports, ceramics imports, royalty payments and service fees are settled freely in EUR or any convertible currency. Equity injections, shareholder loans and profit repatriations are equally unrestricted for the UG. The only material friction is fiscal: dividends and profit distributions of a German UG attract 26.375% statutory withholding tax (26.375% = 25% Abgeltungsteuer-type Kapitalertragsteuer plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge on the tax) unless reduced or exempted under a double-tax treaty or the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive; foreign shareholders must file the appropriate clearance forms (Freistellungsbescheinigung or session certificate) before remittance. No sovereign prior-approval hurdle exists for remittances, provided the UBO is tax-compliant and AML-clear.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Germany or the euro area; export proceeds may be retained, netted, hedged and invested freely in any currency. The sole hard obligation is statistical: under AWV Section 67, a resident corporate exporter must electronically report each cross-border payment (inbound or outbound) exceeding EUR 12,500 to Deutsche Bundesbank within 7 days after the end of the month in which the payment occurred. The report uses the Bundesbank 'Individual International Payments Statistics' portal with transaction codes distinguishing goods (Warenverkehr) from services and capital transfers. Failure to report or incorrect reporting is an administrative offence carrying fines; it is not a surrender requirement and imposes no conversion timeline or ratio.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (Federal Republic of Germany); cross-border payment statistics and foreign-exchange reporting administered under the Außenwirtschaftsverordnung (AWV); monetary policy framework integrated with the Eurosystem of the European Central Bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a complementary disbursement and merchant-FX rail; Low as the sole primary treasury partner. Digital EMIs operating under EU PSD2 passports (Revolut Payments UAB, Wise Europe SA, Airwallex Ltd, Penta/FINN GmbH) can open onboarding accounts for a German UG via video identification, but they cannot accept protected deposits under the KWG regime, offer equivalent overdraft and interest-sweep products, or substitute for the Bundesbank statistical reporting lane assigned to resident credit institutions. Legal constraints: client-fund safeguarding obligations under Section 10 Zahlungsdiensteaufsichtsgesetz (ZAG); lack of deposit-insurance protection; restricted inward correspondent capability for CNY, JPY and INR; and mandatory linkage to a German domestic bank for corporate tax payments, statutory salary runs and commercial-register account attestations. The treasury blueprint therefore mandates one fully KYC-complete tier-1 German bank as principal banking counterparty and permits EMIs only for supplier FX conversion, repeated small-ticket disbursements and merchant acquiring aggregation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant sequence for the UG: (1) open the domestic German account after notarial incorporation and before Handelsregister registration, using a formation/escrow account restricted to the paid-in capital; (2) for operating injections, classify the funding as either equity top-up (notarized capital increase) or shareholder loan (back-to-back written Darlehensvertrag with arm's-length interest); (3) route the transfer from the beneficial owner's own overseas account to the German IBAN via SEPA Credit Transfer in EUR or, for non-EUR currencies, via SWIFT MT103 with a clear purpose in field 70 (e.g., EQUITY TOP-UP - DARLEHEN); (4) state the Bundesbank AWV code for capital transactions ('Direktinvestition/Kapitalverkehr') and file the required cross-border monthly transaction report by the 7th day of the following month for any single payment above EUR 12,500; (5) the receiving bank verifies the underlying agreement, the commercial-register extract and UBO clarity; undocumented inbound transfers are subject to hold-back pending clarification. Funds then enter the operational liquidity pool and are reconciled against the UG's compulsory reserve rule (25% of annual net profit reserved until EUR 25,000 is reached).For import settlements covering convoys of raw tea from East Asia and premium ceramics from Germany abroad the UG faces four rails. Default rail: SWIFT MT103 international credit transfer executed through the tier-1 bank with STP accuracy; reference fields must mirror the commercial invoice number, HS code and purchase-order reference to pass AML document-completeness checks. For new non-EU suppliers or transactions above EUR 50,000, use an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit issued as SWIFT MT700 subject to UCP600, with presentation documents comprising invoices, transport draft, packing list and phytosanitary/environmental certificates under agreed Incoterms such as CIF or FOB. For CNY-denominated purchases, settlement is channelled through SWIFT-correspondent banks linked to CIPS (Cross-Border Interbank Payment System), with CNH offshore clearing where available; JPY and USD settlements flow through CHIPS/Fedwire correspondent clearing via the German bank's New York/Tokyo correspondent network. Intra-EU suppliers in the tea and porcelain supply chain are paid by SEPA Credit Transfer, which benefits from the same-day clearing cycle and low float cost. All disbursements must match the customs value declaration to avoid red flags in post-trade monitoring.EUR/USD and EUR/JPY outright FX forwards (devisentermingeschäfte) with a licensed domestic bank under an ISDA Master or bank-specific FX termsEUR/CNY and EUR/INR non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for restricted-deliverability currencies to hedge tea and ceramic import payablesFX swaps (Devisenswap) for tenor smoothing and temporary liquidity conversionCross-currency interest-rate swaps (CIRS) where multi-currency working-capital facilities are utilisedEuropean-style FX barrier options providing protective EUR/CALL-PUT floors on large seasonal tea auction settlements

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium as a complementary disbursement and merchant-FX rail; Low as the sole primary treasury partner. Digital EMIs operating under EU PSD2 passports (Revolut Payments UAB, Wise Europe SA, Airwallex Ltd, Penta/FINN GmbH) can open onboarding accounts for a German UG via video identification, but they cannot accept protected deposits under the KWG regime, offer equivalent overdraft and interest-sweep products, or substitute for the Bundesbank statistical reporting lane assigned to resident credit institutions. Legal constraints: client-fund safeguarding obligations under Section 10 Zahlungsdiensteaufsichtsgesetz (ZAG); lack of deposit-insurance protection; restricted inward correspondent capability for CNY, JPY and INR; and mandatory linkage to a German domestic bank for corporate tax payments, statutory salary runs and commercial-register account attestations. The treasury blueprint therefore mandates one fully KYC-complete tier-1 German bank as principal banking counterparty and permits EMIs only for supplier FX conversion, repeated small-ticket disbursements and merchant acquiring aggregation.
