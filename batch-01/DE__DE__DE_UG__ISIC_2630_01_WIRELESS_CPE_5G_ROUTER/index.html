Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Commercial Register (Handelsregister) of the Local Court (Amtsgericht) at the company's registered seat; electronic filings transmitted via the federal e-justice portal (XJustiz/Justizportal des Bundes und der Länder) through a German notary.

Governing statute

German Limited Liability Companies Act (GmbH-Gesetz, GmbHG), primarily §§ 1, 2, 3, 5, 5a, 6–10, 35; Commercial Code (HGB) §§ 8–16 (Commercial Register); Federal Notaries Act (BNotO) and Notarization Act (BeurkG) for deed certification.

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarially certified Articles of Association (Gesellschaftsvertrag) using the statutory model protocol (Musterprotokoll) and the electronic Application for Registration (Anmeldung zur Eintragung in das Handelsregister) signed by all managing directors.

UG (haftungsbeschränkt) requires minimum subscribed capital of EUR 1 (GmbHG § 5a); mandatory statutory reserve equal to 25% of annual net profit must be accumulated until capital reaches EUR 25,000. Share capital must be fully paid in cash into a German bank account in the company's name before registration and remain at the free disposal of the managing director; no statutory escrow account is required. Recommended initial capital for trade creditability: EUR 1,000–3,000.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, BZSt) for VAT ID issuance and federal tax affairs; local tax office (Finanzamt) responsible for corporate income tax, trade tax, and wage tax; ELSTER portal for electronic filing; ATLAS customs system for export declarations.

E-government filing portal

A German Unternehmergesellschaft (haftungsbeschränkt) obtains tax registration after commercial register entry. The managing director must file the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung with the local Finanzamt within one month of business commencement; the Finanzamt assigns a local tax number (Steuernummer) for corporate income tax, trade tax, and VAT. Separately, the BZSt issues a VAT identification number (Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer) upon submission of Form UST 1, required for intra-Community transactions. For non-resident sellers or companies without a domestic place of registration, direct VAT registration in Germany may be required for taxable supply, import, or distance sales activity; certain foreign entities may need to appoint a fiscal representative under §§22a-22e UStG. Registration is imperative prior to issuing invoices with German VAT ID.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

German tax law does not provide a US-style check-the-box election. An UG is a capital company and is always classified as a corporation for German tax purposes, subject to corporate income tax and trade tax at entity level. No pass-through election is available for an UG. However, hybrid mismatch rules under §4k/4l EStG and §14 KStG may recharacterise payments or participations, and the option under §1a KStG allows a resident partnership (not a corporation) to elect corporate taxation; this is irrelevant for a UG. No separate entity classification election under the German tax code exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Small business threshold (Kleinunternehmer) under §19 UStG: EUR 22,000 gross turnover in the prior year and non-projection above EUR 50,000 in the current year (2025 onward: EUR 25,000 and EUR 100,000). Foreign entrepreneurs making taxable supplies, importing goods, performing intra-Community acquisitions, or undertaking distance sales in Germany have no general registration threshold; registration is required from the first taxable activity unless reverse charge applies. For EU distance sales, the EU-wide EUR 10,000 annual threshold for micro-businesses applies, but exceeded volume creates a German registration duty. Import VAT registration arises automatically upon customs clearance in Germany.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate applies to export supplies of goods under §6 UStG when the goods are actually transported to a non-EU territory by the seller or on the seller's behalf, and the recipient is an entrepreneur established outside the EU. The seller must hold documentary proof of export in accordance with §§22, 23a UStDV, including the customs export declaration and a transport document showing departure from the EU within the prescribed period. For services supplied to a non-EU entrepreneur (e.g., design, licensing, logistics coordination for 5G routers), zero-rating under §4 Nr. 1a UStG and §§8-12 UStDV applies if the place of supply is outside Germany under §3a UStG and the recipient is a non-EU entrepreneur; domestic input VAT is refundable. Intra-Community supplies to an EU VAT-registered business are exempt with input VAT deduction if the customer's VAT ID, Intrastat summary declaration, and movement evidence are maintained.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Ausfuhrzollanmeldung in ATLAS)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or CMR transport document
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement proving receipt of foreign currency export proceeds

Germany does not operate real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or pre-clearance by the tax authority. From 1 January 2025, domestic B2B invoices must be issued in a structured electronic format (XRechnung or EN 16931-compliant XML, PEPPOL network-based). By 2027, Germany will implement a near-real-time e-invoice reporting system to the BZSt through the national Peppol infrastructure. B2G invoices already require PEPPOL routing. VAT returns are filed electronically via ELSTER; e-invoice archiving and validation are required under §14 UStG. No fiscal device seal is demanded for standard IT manufacturers, but the e-invoice must include all mandatory VAT details when the supplier is established in Germany.

Export zero-rating is subject to strict documentary substantiation. If export proof is incomplete or unconvincing, the tax office retrospectively assesses VAT at 19% plus late interest under §233a AO (6% per annum). Deliberate fraud or intentional misleading of customs leads to criminal tax evasion under §370 AO with imprisonment up to 5 years or fines, unlimited clawback period, and additional penalties for incorrect export declarations. Negligent understatement may trigger a fine under §378 AO. Failure to keep records under §26a UStG carries penalties. The German Foreign Trade and Payments Regulation (AWV) additionally requires export proceeds to be repatriated within 180 days, failing which customs/administrative sanctions and potential export privilege revocation may follow. The tax authorities may cross-check ATLAS export declarations against logistics records, transport insurance, and banking transactions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The EUR is fully convertible on both current and capital accounts; Germany maintains no exchange controls, no prior-authorization regime for capital inflows or outflows, and no restriction on profit or dividend repatriation by a German Unternehmergesellschaft (UG). The free movement of capital applies under Article 63 TFEU and Section 1 of the German Foreign Trade and Payments Act (Außenwirtschaftsgesetz – AWG). Distributable profits of the UG may be remitted in EUR or any freely convertible currency by ordinary MT103 transfer without Bundesbank approval. The only compliance hurdle is statutory dividend withholding of 25% capital gains tax (plus any applicable solidarity surcharge or treaty-reduced rate under a double taxation agreement), which does not block remittance but requires evidence of beneficial ownership in a refund or reduced-withholding procedure. In addition, each cross-border payment or receipt exceeding EUR 12,500 must be identified to the bank with the correct AWV purpose code for the Bundesbank balance-of-payments statistics.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany imposes no export proceeds surrender rules, mandatory conversion ratios, or repatriation deadlines on export receipts. The UG may retain export proceeds in foreign currency indefinitely, convert them at market rates, or credit them to onshore or offshore accounts without license. The sole obligation is statistical: each export receipt or expenditure vis-à-vis non-residents exceeding EUR 12,500 must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank by the payment service provider using the mandated AWV purpose code, and the UG must supply precise reconciliation documents (invoice, contract, payment reference) to the bank to avoid misclassification and sanctions-screening delays.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (central bank of the Federal Republic of Germany) operating within the Eurosystem jointly with the European Central Bank (ECB); prudential banking supervision is carried out by the German Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin); cross-border statistical and foreign-trade reporting obligations are administered exclusively by the Deutsche Bundesbank under the German Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung – AWV).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for operational settlement, Low for capital escrow and trade finance. German or EU-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) and Payment Institutions (PIs) authorized by BaFin under the Payment Services Supervision Act (ZAG) can provide IBAN accounts for the UG's floating receivables and supplier payments, but cannot issue documentary Letters of Credit (MT700), provide committed revolving facilities, or in most cases route direct SWIFT MT103 traffic as a full SWIFT member. A regulated EMI such as Pliant, Qonto, or Airwallex may therefore be deployed as an auxiliary operational rail for EUR/GBP/USD collections, but the UG's statutory capital account, LC issuing desk, and export disbursement backbone must reside at a Tier-1 commercial bank that maintains a Frankfurt/Hamburg/Munich corporate banking presence.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Root cause: Non-resident beneficial owner without transparent chain of ownership in the UG (German registry search showing the Geschäftsführer but not the natural-person shareholder).

Defense evidence: Notarized shareholder register listing all natural persons owning 25% or more; current (less than 30 days old) Handelsregister extract; apostilled certificate of incorporation of the parent company; official passport and proof of residential address for each UBO; organizational chart showing the full ownership chain; declaration under Section 3 GwG confirming no bearer shares or undisclosed nominees.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder loan injections from non-resident shareholders are routed via SWIFT MT103 to the UG's German IBAN using the registered name 'Unternehmergesellschaft (haftungsbeschränkt)' and a precise remittance purpose line such as 'Stammeinlage UG ISIC-2630' or 'Gesellschafterdarlehen'. The instructing bank must include the AWV balance-of-payments code, and the German receiving bank will require a notarized capital-increase resolution or signed loan agreement, plus source-of-funds documentation if the credit exceeds normal entity capitalization thresholds. No Deutsche Bundesbank authorization is required, but a distinct Capital Account or shareholder loan sub-account must be maintained to prevent commingling with operating funds and to ensure tracing for later dividend distributions or loan repayments.Outbound trade payments are executed through two parallel rails: (i) TT_Wire via SWIFT MT103 for component supplier settlements, freight, customs duties, and distributor rebates, with SEPA CT used for EUR-denominated EU payments and SWIFT MT103 for USD, CNY, and other cross-currency wires; and (ii) Swift_LC via the Tier-1 bank's trade finance desk issuing Commercial Letters of Credit (MT700) in favor of overseas OEM component suppliers, complemented by MT707 amendments, MT710 transferable credits if required, and documentary collections (MT400). The UG will typically access an LC issuing limit of EUR 250,000 to EUR 1,000,000 under a German master trade finance agreement, with 90–180 day tenors, Incoterms 2020-based documents, and pre-export financing against the wireless CPE purchase orders. All outbound and inbound disbursements must be coded to the correct AWV purpose code for the Bundesbank statistics and the bank's transaction-monitoring systems.Vanilla OTC FX Forwards (EUR/USD, EUR/CNH) with tenors up to 12 months under a German Master Agreement for Financial Derivatives (Rahmenvereinbarung für Finanztermingeschäfte) or ISDA-CSA where cross-border netting is required.FX Swaps for managing the convertible currency mismatch between USD and CNY-denominated export receipts and EUR operating costs.FX Options (purchased EUR USD or EUR CNH call/put structures) with upfront premium payment, capping downside and preserving upside participation without contingent liability.Cross-Currency Swaps (EUR/USD or EUR/CNH) for long-dated receivable or supplier liability conversion, supported by the UG's bankable cash-flow assignment to the Tier-1 bank.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) are permissible only for non-deliverable emerging-market currency pairs such as EUR/NGN or EUR/NGN-like exposures; NDFs are not authorized for EUR/USD or EUR/CNH offshore hedging where a deliverable forward is available. All derivative contracts must comply with EMIR reporting, clearing and margining thresholds through the German Tier-1 banking counterparty and be confined strictly to hedge accounting documented in the UG treasury policy.
