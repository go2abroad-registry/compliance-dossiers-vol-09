Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

UG (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschränkt) may be formed with a minimum nominal share capital of EUR 1. No escrow account is required: the contribution must be freely available to the management and finally paid. Statutory reserve must retain 25% of annual net profit until the subscribed share capital reaches EUR 25,000. A non-cash contribution requires a documented valuation report and separate proof of value. Funds can be deposited in any licensed bank in Germany or in a notary escrow account if capital verification requires it.

Funding & unlock path: Occupational safety clearance is required before operations commence, based on a documented risk assessment (Gefährdungsbeurteilung) covering electrical works, work at height, and handling of photovoltaic modules, in accordance with the Occupational Safety Act (ArbSchG), DGUV regulations, and the Construction Sites Ordinance (BaustellV). Fire safety certification (Feuerbeschau / Brandschutzabnahme) is obtained from the local fire protection authority for offices, storage warehouses, and auxiliary buildings. Structural load inspections (Standsicherheitsnachweis) for storage racks and temporary office containers must be certified by a licensed structural engineer. The industrial supervisory authority issues a zero-disruption operating notification and regularly inspects compliance with emission and occupational threshold limits.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern – BZSt) for VAT ID issuance; local Tax Office (Finanzamt) for income and trade tax registration; electronic filing portal ELSTER (Elektronische Steuererklärung).

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident-owned UG, the managing director must file the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) with the competent local Finanzamt using ELSTER or in paper form. The Finanzamt assigns a unique Steuernummer (tax number) for corporation tax, withholding tax and trade tax. Separately, the UG must apply to BZSt for a USt-IdNr. (VAT identification number) using the official application form (e.g., Formular USt 1 TUNV). Registration must be completed before the commencement of taxable activities; foreign entities without a German establishment may be required to appoint a fiscal representative.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (UG is a German corporation with legal personality; subject to Körperschaftsteuer at 15%, solidarity surcharge of 5.5% on corporation tax, and trade tax at municipal rates; worldwide income with treaty relief and CFC rules; no pass-through treatment). — For a non-resident-owned UG, the managing director must file the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) with the competent local Finanzamt using ELSTER or in paper form. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (UG is a German corporation with legal personality; subject to Körperschaftsteuer at 15%, solidarity surcharge of 5.5% on corporation tax, and trade tax at municipal rates; worldwide income with treaty relief and CFC rules; no pass-through treatment).

Available election forms

German tax law does not provide a 'check-the-box' pass-through election for an incorporated UG. Relevant statutory elections include: (i) formation of an Organschaft (fiscal unity) for corporation tax and VAT grouping; (ii) VAT grouping under §2 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UStG; (iii) depreciation and valuation elections; (iv) CFC escape exceptions; and (v) hybrid mismatch alignment elections under ATAD implementation. The UG cannot elect tax-transparent classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For small entrepreneurs (Kleinunternehmer), exemption applies if annual turnover ≤ EUR 22,000 and ≤ EUR 50,000 in the current year, but this exemption is generally unavailable to foreign EPC contractors. Foreign entities making taxable supplies in Germany must register for VAT without a material threshold; intra-EU distance sales retain a EUR 10,000 annual threshold for simplified OSS declaration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports to third countries and intra-Community supplies qualify for 0% VAT if the goods are physically delivered or transported out of Germany and, for exports, proper evidence of export is retained (Ausfuhrnachweis). Services qualify as zero-rated or VAT-exempt when the place of supply is outside Germany or in another EU state and the recipient is a taxable person (reverse charge). For solar EPC contracts, 0% applies only to goods genuinely exported; services relating to immovable property are taxed in the jurisdiction where the land is located.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Elektronische Ausfuhranmeldung / ATLAS) with movement reference number
  • Commercial Invoice, Packing List and Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR consignment note
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Certified export evidence under §§8–9 UStDV (e.g., customs clearance certificate or delivery certificate)
  • Intra-Community sales list (Zusammenfassende Meldung) and EC Sales List entry for EU despatches

Germany mandates e-invoicing for domestic B2B supplies from 1 January 2025, with phased enforcement and full compliance by 2028. E-invoices must be issued in a structured format (CEN EN 16931, specifically XRechnung or ZUGFeRD 2.x) and transmitted via EDI or Peppol-type channels. There is no real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping by the tax authority in Germany; e-invoices are auditable through digital tax examination software.

Export zero-rating is strictly audited on documentary proof. If proof is missing or false, the Finanzamt retroactively assesses VAT at 19% or 7%, plus default interest (0.5% per month) and late payment surcharges. Under §370 AO, tax evasion can trigger imprisonment and fines; §378 AO penalizes negligent tax shortfall. Fraudulent export VAT claims may additionally lead to denial of refunds, seizure of goods, trade licence revocation, and disqualification of directors.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany maintains full convertibility on both current and capital account pursuant to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (Articles 63-66) and the German Foreign Trade and Payments Act (Außenwirtschaftsgesetz). There are no exchange controls, no mandatory filings prior to conversion, no approval requirements for repatriation of dividends, profits, capital gains, or loan repayments. Profit remittance from a German UG is legally unrestricted; however, distributions to shareholders are generally subject to German withholding tax of 26.375% (including solidarity surcharge) unless reduced by an applicable double-tax treaty or the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive. The euro is the lawful currency; conversion from foreign currency into EUR and back is freely permitted. Only statistical reporting obligations apply to cross-border payments and balances with non-residents; such reports are not authorization requirements and do not restrict convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export proceeds surrender rules in Germany and no mandatory conversion ratios. Export receipts in foreign currency may be retained in foreign currency, held on domestic or foreign accounts, or converted freely. No deadline exists for repatriation or conversion. However, under the Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung, §§ 67-68), every resident enterprise must report cross-border payments and receipts from non-residents to the Deutsche Bundesbank for balance-of-payments statistical purposes within five business days after the transaction value equals or exceeds EUR 12,500. This is a reporting requirement, not a surrender requirement.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (German Central Bank), exercising monetary authority and balance-of-payments statistics administration under the Außenwirtschaftsverordnung (Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance); Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht (BaFin) supervises banks and payment institutions for AML/KYC and prudential matters.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for primary treasury and capital escrow rails; MEDIUM only for incidental operational payments. Under the German Payment Services Supervision Act (Zahlungsdiensteaufsichtsgesetz) and the EU Payment Services Directive 2, an offshore digital EMI or neobank must hold a valid European Economic Area licence and comply with the German AML Act (Geldwäschegesetz) to provide payment services to a German UG. In practice, tier-1 German banks do not accept EMIs as settlement conduits for high-value cross-border EPC milestone wires or documentary credit operations. Offshore digital EMIs often lack the commercial banking infrastructure to issue or confirm letters of credit, hold project escrows, or provide physical KYC authentication for directors. Recommendation: use only for small operational disbursements and employee expense accounts, not for project clearing rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity capital injection into the UG must be routed via a standard SWIFT MT103 wire from the shareholder's bank to the UG's German account referencing (i) the German account IBAN/BIC, (ii) the precise shareholder name and address, and (iii) the payment purpose field stating 'Einbringung von Eigenkapital / Capital Injection'. Under EU Wire Transfer Regulation 2015/847, full originator and beneficiary data are mandatory; missing data will result in rejection. The inward transfer is not licensed, but if the amount equals or exceeds EUR 12,500, the UG must report the transaction to the Deutsche Bundesbank for balance-of-payments statistics using the AWV capital-flow code on the Bundesbank website. For German company law, a contribution in excess of the nominal share capital must be booked into the capital reserve (Kapitalrücklage) and evidenced by a notarized shareholder resolution. The bank shall verify the provenance of funds via prior source-of-wealth evidence, and the UG must present a certified copy of the potential share-capital registration and a negative certificate of debts from the tax office when increasing capital or being assessed for bank facilities.For outbound trade payments related to solar EPC subcontracts and equipment procurement, the UG will generally use (i) SWIFT MT103 wire transfers for direct disbursement of noted invoices and (ii) commercial letters of credit (L/C) for larger, milestone-linked settlements. The letter of credit may be issued via SWIFT MT700 to a confirming bank abroad subject to UCP 600; sight payment or deferred payment L/C structures are available. Documentary collections (DP/DA) under URC 522 may be accepted only where the counterparty has strong credit standing. All outbound transfers require: official invoice, proof of delivery or completion certificate, customs declaration for physical equipment, and sanctions/financial crime screening. Payments to beneficiaries outside the European Union must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank if the amount exceeds EUR 12,500.
