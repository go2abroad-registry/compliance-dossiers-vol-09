Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern – BZSt) for VAT identification numbers; local Tax Office (Finanzamt) for corporate income tax, trade tax, and payroll tax; portal: ELSTER (Elektronische Steuererklärung) and BZSt Online-Portal for USt-IdNr. applications.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a UG (Unternehmergesellschaft (haftungsbeschränkt)) must first register with the local commercial register (Handelsregister). The company then submits the mandatory tax registration questionnaire (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung) to the competent local Finanzamt, typically via ELSTER. The Finanzamt assigns a corporate income tax number (Steuernummer) and trade tax number. Simultaneously, the company applies to BZSt for a VAT identification number (Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer). For large or cross-border operations, an economic activity registration with the Federal Statistical Office (Destatis) may also apply.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope (unlimited resident tax liability with worldwide income for corporate income tax; territorial trade tax on domestic permanent establishment; UG is per-see a capital company, not transparent).

Available election forms

Germany has no check-the-box regime. A UG is per se corporate taxpayer (KStG, GewStG). Available elections include: fiscal unity (Organschaft) under Secs. 14–17 KStG for corporate taxation; VAT fiscal unity (Organschaft) under Sec. 2(2) No. 2 UStG; small-entrepreneur treatment under Sec. 19 UStG; and inventory and other timing elections for income tax. No hybrid mismatch entity election is available; hybrid mismatch adjustments follow ATAD/German §4k EStG.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No registration threshold for a UG because it is a fully taxable incorporated entity. A small-entrepreneur exemption applies only to individuals and non-corporate entities with turnover ≤€25,000 in the preceding year and ≤€100,000 in the current year. For intra-EU distance sales, the electronic-commerce turnover threshold is €10,000 on a pan-EU basis; exceeding it mandates German VAT registration and OSS procedures. Intra-EU acquisitions and imports require VAT registration regardless of threshold, and non-resident suppliers have no de minimis threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export zero-rating is available under Sec. 4 No. 1(a) UStG and Sec. 6 UStG for deliveries of goods transported by the supplier or purchaser to a destination outside the EU, provided the goods physically leave Germany and the EU and the supplier retains evidenced export proof (Ausfuhrnachweis). For intraocular acrylic lenses delivered outside Germany, the supplier must demonstrate (i) the sale to a non-EU counterparty, (ii) actual dispatch/transport to a non-EU territory, and (iii) receipt of the goods abroad. Services physically performed outside the EU, such as contract manufacturing trials or engineering services relating to lenses, may be zero-rated as non-EU destinations under Sec. 4 No. 1(b) UStG.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Ausfuhrbegleitdokument/ATLAS customs declaration) with MRN
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent transport document (CMR, Air Waybill, rail/sea waybill, or logistics provider confirmation)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit document evidencing payment by the foreign buyer
  • Commercial invoice with explicit statement '0% USt. Ausfuhr' (or 'Export' indication)
  • Proof of commencement and completion of transport (e.g. container tracking, insurance certificates)
  • If applicable, customs broker issuance of the confirmed export summary and export certificate

German customs/tax authorities rigorously audit export zero-rating under Section 6(3) UStG. Missing or deficient export proof triggers VAT re-claim of the 19% rate with statutory interest (currently 6% per annum) and possible late-payment surcharge under §233a AO. Fraudulent or negligent false evidence of export can lead to criminal prosecution for tax evasion under §370 AO with penalties of up to imprisonment of 5 years or a monetary fine, and supplemental tax surcharges up to 2.5% of the evaded amount. For improperly claimed zero-rating, the tax office issues an assessment adjusting the invoice to 19% and may also deny input VAT deductions, leading to a total clawback of both output and input VAT. The standard limitation period for tax assessments is four years, extending to seven years for omissions caused by gross negligence and ten years for willful tax evasion. In practice, the German customs authorities may request physical matching of the goods with shipping documentation through AEO and post-clearance audit; failure to satisfy the evidence obligations within 180 days of shipment triggers increased audit intensity and direct reverse-charge risk.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany forms part of the Eurozone; the EUR is fully convertible for current and capital account transactions without any foreign-exchange control permit for legitimate medical-device trade. Non-resident shareholders may freely inject equity into the UG, receive dividends, repay shareholder loans and remit royalties under EU free-movement-of-capital rules (TFEU Art. 63) and the German AWG/AWV. Practical hurdles: (a) cross-border payment statistical reporting to Deutsche Bundesbank for any transaction exceeding EUR 12,500; (b) German withholding tax on dividend distributions at 25% plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge (26.375% effective, church tax may apply), reducible to 0-15% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive or applicable double tax treaties provided beneficial-ownership and holding-period conditions are met; there is no minimum capital-exposure period for inward equity investment.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany imposes no export-proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion into EUR, no percentage-based conversion ratio and no statutory deadline for repatriation of export receivables from intraocular lens sales. Export proceeds in USD, CNY, CHF or other currencies may be retained offshore, swept to a foreign account or hedged at the UG's full discretion. The only binding obligation is statistical reporting to Deutsche Bundesbank: any payment received from a non-resident exceeding EUR 12,500 must be reported by the resident beneficiary within five business days after the end of the calendar month in which payment was received, using the Bundesbank External Payments Reporting System (Zahlungsverkehrsstatistik) with the appropriate trade-payment code (e.g., goods-payment code for ophthalmological medical devices). Failure to file is an administrative offence but does not restrict convertibility or the validity of payment.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (Frankfurt am Main), acting as the central monetary, balance-of-payments statistics and AWV-reporting authority, in conjunction with the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action (BMWK) as the competent authority under the Foreign Trade and Payments Act (AWG), and the Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht (BaFin) as the banking and AML supervisory authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-to-Low for this structure. EU-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) operating in Germany under the Payment Services Supervision Act (ZAG) and PSD2, such as digital B2B banks, can provide EUR IBANs, FX conversion APIs, payer identification and segregated e-money accounts. They are, however, structurally unsuitable as the single treasury rail for ISIC 3250 medical-device manufacturing because: (i) an EMI licence does not permit commercial lending, (ii) EMIs generally cannot issue, advise or confirm documentary Letters of Credit (MT700), (iii) high-risk regulated sectors with physical production and high value-added supply chains are commonly declined by the EMI's own sponsor-bank compliance, and (iv) AWV statistical reporting duties shift onto the corporate client and are not consistently automated by fintechs. Feasibility rating: viable only for pre-funding, FX float and segregated sub-accounts; the principal liquidity and LC rail must remain with a tier-1 commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injections by non-resident shareholders must be executed via SWIFT MT103 (full-amount, no fees deducted) to the UG's capital escrow account at the selected tier-1 bank. The payment must reference the notarial deed and the UG's commercial register file to ensure unambiguous capital-allocation treatment. The ordering field should state clearly: 'Kapitaleinlage - Stammeinlage der UG (haftungsbeschränkung)' so the bank can credit the capital escrow rather than an operational position. German AWV reporting: the UG's bank and the receiving company must report the inbound payment if equal to or greater than EUR 12,500 under the Deutsche Bundesbank payment-statistics regime, using the capital-transaction category (share-capital participation) and reporting the debtor's name and country. Verification package for the notary's certificate under section 8(2) GmbHG includes: SWIFT receipt confirmation, MT103 message from originator bank, and no-third-party-payment evidence: the transfer must originate from the shareholder's own account, not a client or agent account. If shareholders wish to inject lending, a separate cross-border shareholder-loan must be reported under AWV loan-debt categories, documented by a written loan agreement, and distinguished from paid-in capital to avoid re-characterisation by the notary or German tax authorities.Primary clearing rails: (a) for EU and SEPA-area suppliers: SEPA Credit Transfer (ISO 20022 pain.001, CAMT.053 reconciliation) and SEPA Instant where required for fast raw-material procurement; (b) for non-EU suppliers of acrylic co-polymer, lathe consumables and cleanroom consumables: SWIFT MT103 direct wire with mandated purpose-coded text covering the invoiced goods; (c) for capital equipment and high-value batches: documentary Commercial Letter of Credit (SWIFT MT700 issued by Commerzbank or UniCredit, advised through the supplier's confirming bank), with sight or 60-90 day usance payment against documents: commercial invoice, full-set bill of lading, insurance certificate, CE/ISO quality certificates and any pre-shipment inspection report; (d) for smaller emerging-market prepayments: documentary collections (D/P at sight) may be used to mitigate risk. All outbound wires exceeding EUR 12,500 require AWV reporting to Deutsche Bundesbank. Trade finance desks should incorporate an LC confirmation and transit-title clause (CIF or CIP Incoterms 2020) to preserve bank control over documents.Deliverable Outright FX Forward contracts (EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, EUR/CHF, EUR/CNH) executed under the German Master Agreement for Financial Collateral Transactions (Deutscher Rahmenvertrag für Finanztermingeschäfte) or ISDA where the bank uses ISDA architecture.Non-Deliverable Forward (NDF) contracts for receivables arising in non-convertible or restricted currencies (e.g., INR, EGP, NGN, PKR and certain Asian currencies) where onshore deliverable forwards cannot be legally accessed by a German UG, settled in EUR or USD on the fixing date.FX Swaps (spot vs. forward) for temporary rebalancing of multi-currency operational liquidity without removing funds from the EUR cash-pool.Cross-Currency Swap (XCS) matching the UG's long-term USD-denominated LC repayments or US hospital receivables against EUR liquidity, provided the exposure exceeds EUR 1 million and is collateralised under standard credit-support annexes.Vanilla FX Options and Zero-Cost Collar structures on EUR/USD to protect the strategic export margin when USD revenue from intraocular lens sales is contracted but undelivered; use of these instruments remains subject to EMIR reporting, margin threshold analysis for the UG as a Non-Financial Counterparty, and the bank's derivative product approval framework.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium-to-Low for this structure. EU-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) operating in Germany under the Payment Services Supervision Act (ZAG) and PSD2, such as digital B2B banks, can provide EUR IBANs, FX conversion APIs, payer identification and segregated e-money accounts. They are, however, structurally unsuitable as the single treasury rail for ISIC 3250 medical-device manufacturing because: (i) an EMI licence does not permit commercial lending, (ii) EMIs generally cannot issue, advise or confirm documentary Letters of Credit (MT700), (iii) high-risk regulated sectors with physical production and high value-added supply chains are commonly declined by the EMI's own sponsor-bank compliance, and (iv) AWV statistical reporting duties shift onto the corporate client and are not consistently automated by fintechs. Feasibility rating: viable only for pre-funding, FX float and segregated sub-accounts; the principal liquidity and LC rail must remain with a tier-1 commercial bank.
