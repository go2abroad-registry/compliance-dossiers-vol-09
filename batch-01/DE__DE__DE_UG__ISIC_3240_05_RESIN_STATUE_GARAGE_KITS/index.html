Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible. Germany maintains unrestricted cross-border current and capital account movements under TFEU Article 63, the German Foreign Trade and Payments Act (AWG) and the AWV; no exchange control, approval, quota, or quantitative limit applies to inbound equity capital, pre-order deposits, supplier payments, or profit remittances. Practical hurdles are limited to EU sanctions/Russia export controls, dual-use/cultural goods classification for decorative statues, and the mandatory statistical reporting on cross-border payments above the statutory threshold. Profit/dividend remittance to non-resident shareholders is unrestricted, but if paid as dividend, German withholding tax applies unless a treaty or EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive exemption reduces it.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion requirement applies to export proceeds in Germany. A UG is free to invoice in EUR, USD, GBP or any other currency and may hold resulting balances abroad or at a German bank. For payments received from non-residents above €12,500, the collecting bank must code and report the transaction to the Deutsche Bundesbank for balance-of-payments statistics, identifying the payment as goods-related commercial trade. Only that statistical duty exists; there is no deadline for repatriation, no 30-day surrender rule, and no obligation to convert to EUR.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (German central bank; official foreign-exchange statistics and payment-reporting authority under the Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (AWV), acting with the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action as licensing authority under the AWG).

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For a non-resident shareholder's EUR capital injection, route by SEPA CT (EEA) or SWIFT MT103 (outside EEA/Brexit) strictly to the UG's own EUR IBAN at Commerzbank, with the remittance description 'Stammeinlage UG (haftungsbeschränkt)' and the bank's cross-border purpose category 'Capital contribution / direct investment', which the bank maps to the Deutsche Bundesbank balance-of-payments statistical classification for equity acquisition. The shareholder must not combine the capital contribution with trade payment; no cash settlement. On receipt, the UG must obtain a statement from the bank confirming the value date, keep the general meeting shareholder-resolution or subscription certificate, and have the managing director record the share capital contribution in the capital contribution list; while the UG capital reserve remains below €25,000, the statutory prohibition on distributions to shareholders applies. If the contribution originates in USD/CNY, convert it only via a direct FX forward at the same bank so the EUR capital value is fixed and verifiable.For suppliers in the EEA: SEPA Credit Transfer, preferably SEPA Instant for urgent resin/paint supplies. Turnover: Pursuant to § 267 HGB, a UG (haftungsbeschränkt) is classified as a small corporation (kleine Kapitalgesellschaft) if it does not exceed two of the following three thresholds on two consecutive balance sheet dates: total assets EUR 6,000,000; annual turnover EUR 12,000,000; average annual employees 50. Small corporations are exempt from statutory audit. If thresholds are exceeded, audit obligation arises; classification as medium-sized corporation (mittelgroße Kapitalgesellschaft) triggers audit requirement pursuant to § 316 HGB.
