Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

The competent local district court (Amtsgericht) acting as Handelsregister court for the company's registered office, through the electronic Handelsregister filing system.

Governing statute

German Limited Liability Companies Act (Gesetz betreffend die Gesellschaften mit beschränkter Haftung, GmbHG), particularly Sections 1-5a (formation, capital and Unternehmergesellschaft), Sections 6-8 (managing directors and registration application), Sections 9-11 (registration effects and liability), Section 35 (management representation), and Sections 39-40 (changes and shareholder list). Commercial registration is governed additionally by the German Commercial Code (Handelsgesetzbuch, HGB), Sections 8-12.

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarized application for registration in the Handelsregister (Anmeldung zum Handelsregister), accompanied by the notarized articles of association (Gesellschaftsvertrag) or the statutory model protocol (Musterprotokoll), managing-director declarations, shareholder list, and evidence of registered domestic business address.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 143 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

A UG may be incorporated with stated share capital of at least EUR 1. The subscribed capital must be paid in full in cash before registration; contributions in kind are not permitted at formation. German law does not require a statutory escrow account, but the capital should be transferred to a company bank account after notarization and before the managing director confirms payment to the notary. The company must retain one-quarter of annual net profit, subject to statutory adjustments, until statutory capital reaches EUR 25,000. The stated fee is an indicative USD equivalent of the commercial-register court fee; notarial, publication, translation, banking, and local administrative charges are additional and the exact court fee is determined under the German Court and Notarial Costs Act (GNotKG).

Funding & unlock path: Obtain project-specific building and structural approvals, including structural calculations, geotechnical verification, temporary-works and load-control documentation, and inspections required by the competent building authority. Prepare an approved fire-protection and emergency-response concept where required, coordinate with the local fire authority, and obtain fire-safety acceptance for relevant premises. Comply with the German Occupational Safety and Health Act, Construction Site Ordinance, workplace rules, lifting and scaffolding requirements, traffic-safety controls, personal-protective-equipment obligations, and statutory accident-insurance requirements. A separate national civil-defense operating license is not generally required, but emergency, evacuation, rescue, and disaster-coordination measures may be imposed by the competent authority or contracting body.

Paid-in capital rule: A UG may be incorporated with stated share capital of at least EUR 1. Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
The managing director resolves that: (1) the Company shall accept the subscription by each founding shareholder for the shares stated in the notarized articles and confirm that the subscribed capital shall be paid in full in cash before registration; (2) the registered office and domestic business address stated in the articles shall be adopted; (3) [Name] is appointed managing director with authority to represent the Company in accordance with the articles and applicable law; (4) the managing director is authorized to open the Company bank account, receive the capital contribution, instruct the notary to file the registration, and complete all tax, trade, banking, and licensing registrations necessary for commencement of the Company's bridge and highway infrastructure business.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Mandatory Environmental Clearance

There is no single federal industrial licensing board for bridge construction. The principal authorities are the local Gewerbeamt for trade notification, the competent Landesbauaufsichtsbehörde or local Bauordnungsbehörde for building control, the competent road authority for federal or state highways, and the relevant state or local environmental authority for project approvals.

Category: Gewerbeanmeldung for the construction business, supplemented by project-specific plan approval or planning permission under applicable federal or state road law and building law; additional permits may include water-law, nature-conservation, waste, traffic, and emissions approvals.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, BZSt) – https://www.bzst.de; local tax office (Finanzamt) for tax registration and compliance; ELSTER e-filing portal – www.elster.de

E-government filing portal

After commercial registration in the German Commercial Register (Handelsregister) and local trade office (Gewerbeamt) registration, the UG or its fiscal representative submits the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) to the competent local Finanzamt using ELSTER e-filing. The Finanzamt issues the statutory Steuernummer. Separately, an application for the EU VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.) is filed with BZSt. Foreign entities without a German permanent establishment may be required to appoint a domestic fiscal representative. Typical processing period is 4–8 weeks.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — After commercial registration in the German Commercial Register (Handelsregister) and local trade office (Gewerbeamt) registration, the UG or its fiscal representative submits the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) to the competent local Finanzamt using ELSTER e-filing. The Finanzamt issues the statutory Steuernummer. Separately, an application for the EU VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.) is filed with BZSt. Foreign entities without a German permanent establishment may be required to appoint a domestic fiscal representative. Typical processing period is 4–8 weeks.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Germany. The UG is a per-se taxable corporation subject to corporation tax (Körperschaftsteuer), solidarity surcharge (Solidaritätszuschlag), and trade tax (Gewerbesteuer). A fiscal unity (Organschaft) election with a controlling German or EU parent may be available for corporate income tax and VAT purposes, subject to profit-and-control pooling agreements. Hybrid mismatch rules under the ATAD have been implemented; no elective transparent classification for a UG.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Regular VAT registration is mandatory from the first euro of taxable turnover for entrepreneurs; the small-business exemption (Kleinunternehmer) applies when quarterly turnover did not exceed €22,000 in the preceding year and is not expected to exceed €50,000 in the current year. For EU cross-border distance sales, a €10,000 annual threshold applies before German VAT registration is required.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% export VAT applies to physical goods supplied to non-EU destinations if the goods are actually exported and the export is evidenced by official customs documentation (Ausfuhrnachweis). Direct, indirect, and deemed exports qualify. Bestimmte cross-border services, including construction work physically performed outside Germany, are not subject to German VAT only if the place of supply is outside Germany; proper documentation and international contract provisions must be maintained. Export-related freight, insurance, and ancillary services may also be zero-rated or exempt when in direct connection to export goods.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Ausfuhranmeldung)
  • Bill of Lading (Konnossement)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Zahlungsnachweis)

From 1 January 2025, all German businesses must be able to receive electronic invoices (XRechnung/ZUGFeRD). Mandatory e-invoicing for issuers is phased: from 1 January 2027 for businesses with a prior-year turnover above €800,000, and from 1 January 2028 for all. No real-time fiscal clearance, API-linked continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping regime exists. VAT returns and advance returns are submitted via ELSTER; B2G invoices must use the mandatory national e-invoice format.

German tax authorities may retrospectively audit export proof (Belegnachweis), transport documents (Gelangensbestätigung), and payment flow within the statutory assessment period (4 years; extended to 10 years for deliberate tax evasion). False zero-rating claims trigger additional VAT assessments, late-payment interest at 0.15% per month, fines, and criminal penalties for tax evasion (Geldstrafe or imprisonment of up to 5 years; up to 10 years in especially serious cases). Fraudulent export schemes involving fictitious deliveries or forged customs stamps are prosecuted rigorously.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany is a fully convertible jurisdiction for both current and capital account transactions. No exchange control regime or central bank prior approval applies. Profit remittances by a German UG to non-resident shareholders are unrestricted and may be executed via standard dividend distribution, subject to domestic corporate income tax (15% plus solidarity surcharge and trade tax) and, where applicable, 25% withholding tax (26.375% incl. surcharge) reduced by any applicable treaty or EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive (85% exemption or 0% pillar withholding on qualifying participations). The euro (EUR) is freely tradable against all principal currencies. Only balance-of-payments reporting obligations exist under the Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (AWV).

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany imposes no mandatory export-proceeds surrender and no statutory repatriation period or forced conversion into EUR. Export receipts are freely retained in any currency and may be held on onshore or EEA/offshore accounts, subject to the German customs and export-control documentation requirements and the general obligation to report cross-border payments exceeding the statutory threshold (currently EUR 12,500) to the Deutsche Bundesbank within seven days of the transaction date for statistical purposes.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (Germany) / Eurosystem (monetary policy by ECB)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for German BaFin-licensed or EEA-passported Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) holding a branches/passport notification under PSD2. A German UG may lawfully hold operational payment accounts with such an EMI, but such accounts are not substitute for a core bank account, do not provide credit or cash-management facilities, and are often rejected by counterparties for infrastructure EPC contract settlement requiring LC issuance. Offshore (third-country, non-EEA) EMIs cannot lawfully provide payment services to German residents without a BaFin licence or an EU passport; use by a UG is a regulatory breach and may trigger BaFin enforcement. Onboarding viability of reputable EEA EMIs is 'Medium' for ancillary payables/receivables, but 'Low' for escrow or LC-backed disbursements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound equity injection is executed by wire transfer from the foreign direct-investor via SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 interbank cover) to the UG's operational account at a German Bank. The payment order must include a clear reference to the underlying purpose (e.g., 'Kapitalerhöhung / Unternehmenseinlage') and, when >EUR 12,500, the UG must retain evidence and submit the cross-border balance-of-payments (Zahlungsbilanz) declaration to Deutsche Bundesbank within seven calendar days. For debt funding (intercompany loan), the loan agreement must be dated and verified; Bundesbank reporting threshold and monthly loan-liability monitoring under AWV apply. If the inbound capital injection is earmarked for infrastructure EPC contract, the remittance must quote the construction tender or contract number; bank-side AML verification will require the director's passport, the constitutional instrument (Gesellschaftsvertrag/Umlaufbeschluss), and proof of approved construction licence.Clearing rails for trade settlements include: (a) direct SEPA/E-Zig (TARGET2) in EUR for low-value lay-down payments and supplier advances; (b) SWIFT MT103/MT202 for cross-border EUR, USD, or third-currency disbursements to subcontractors in non-SEPA countries; and (c) irrevocable Commercial Letters of Credit (LC) issued by a German confirming bank, particularly for a sovereign highway bridge contract with a foreign public authority requiring LC payment. For LC settlement, the UG must open a compliant escrow account at the German issuing bank, accept red-clause advance drawings, and align letter-of-credit risks with the underlying FIDIC-style contract.
