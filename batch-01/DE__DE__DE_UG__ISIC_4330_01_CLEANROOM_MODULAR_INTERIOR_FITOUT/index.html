Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

For a UG, minimum share capital is EUR 1; the declared capital must be fully paid in cash before registration. No statutory escrow account is mandated, but the capital must be at the managing director's free disposal. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Germany's tax administration is decentralized: the local tax office (Finanzamt) competent for the seat of the UG is responsible for income tax, trade tax, and VAT assessment; the Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, BZSt) issues the VAT identification number (Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer, USt-IdNr.). Official electronic filing is performed through the ELSTER portal.

E-government filing portal

The UG, including a UG established by non-resident owners, must file the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) with the locally competent Finanzamt through ELSTER or in paper form; the Finanzamt then assigns a unified tax number (Steuernummer). The BZSt issues the VAT ID (USt-IdNr.) upon separate application (form USt 1 TG for non-resident entities). Registration must be filed without culpable delay and generally no later than one month after the first economically relevant domestic activity; the VAT ID request is processed once the Finanzamt confirms the operative business status.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — the UG (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschränkt) is a domestic capital company (Kapitalgesellschaft) and a taxable person in its own right; it is subject to corporate income tax (15%), solidarity surcharge (5.5% of corporate income tax), and municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) at entity level, and it is a regular VAT entrepreneur for Umsatzsteuer purposes. There is no statutory pass-through or territorial-conduit classification for a UG; income is taxed at the entity level before distribution. — The UG, including a UG established by non-resident owners, must file the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) with the locally competent Finanzamt through ELSTER or in paper form; the Finanzamt then assigns a unified tax number (Steuernummer). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the UG (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschränkt) is a domestic capital company (Kapitalgesellschaft) and a taxable person in its own right; it is subject to corporate income tax (15%), solidarity surcharge (5.5% of corporate income tax), and municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) at entity level, and it is a regular VAT entrepreneur for Umsatzsteuer purposes. There is no statutory pass-through or territorial-conduit classification for a UG; income is taxed at the entity level before distribution.

Available election forms

German law grants no check-the-box pass-through election and no hybrid-mismatch flow-through election for a UG. Available elections and mechanisms: 1) VAT and corporate/trade tax fiscal unity (Organschaft) upon financial, economic, and organizational integration plus a profit-pooling agreement; 2) small-business exemption under §19 UStG for resident entrepreneurs with prior-year turnover not exceeding EUR 25,000 and current-year turnover not exceeding EUR 100,000; 3) option to waive VAT exemption on real property transactions under §9 UStG; 4) reverse-charge allocation of construction services under §13b UStG for non-resident contractors; 5) the UG may elect business commencement timing for registration-month alignment with customer contract milestones.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Domestic small-business relief applies only to resident entrepreneurs: VAT exemption where prior-year total turnover did not exceed EUR 25,000 and current-year expected turnover does not exceed EUR 100,000. Non-resident entities are mandatorily VAT-registered from the first domestic taxable transaction without any threshold. A resident UG engaged in cleanroom fit-out contracting is requisd to register once the EUR 25,000/EUR 100,000 turnover thresholds are exceeded; if it provides construction services (Bauleistungen), registration is also triggered by reverse-charge liabilities of German recipients under §13b UStG.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating for exports is available under §6 UStG for goods transported by the supplier or a third party from German customs territory into a third-country customs territory, and under §4 No. 1(b) UStG for intra-Community supplies where the customer has provided a valid EU VAT ID and the goods are transported to another Member State. Modular cleanroom wall and HVAC units permanently installed abroad as real property improvements fall under the place-of-supply rule for immovable property work, which is where the property is located; cross-border B2B services are taxed at the recipient's seat and must be invoiced without German USt, with the reverse-charge mechanism applied in the customer's Member State. The German UG can claim refund of input VAT on export-related purchases only if export proof and bookkeeping evidence are completed and archived within the statutory documentation period.

  • Customs export declaration with MRN in the German ATLAS/AES system (Ausfuhrlieferung)
  • Bill of Lading, CMR waybill, or air waybill evidencing physical outward transit
  • Gelangensbestätigung (receipt confirmation) for intra-Community supply proof
  • Commercial invoice and purchase order referencing §4 No. 1a/1b and §6 UStG
  • VIES or official confirmation of the EU customer's valid VAT ID
  • Bank transfer/inward remittance certificate and letter of credit documentation

Since 1 January 2025, every German-resident business must be able to receive structured electronic invoices (E-Rechnung) in XRechnung or ZUGFeRD 2.x format for domestic B2B transactions; the obligation to issue e-invoices becomes mandatory on 1 January 2027, and on 1 January 2028 for businesses with 2026 turnover not exceeding EUR 800,000. Germany does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping system; VAT liabilities are discharged through periodic advance VAT returns (Umsatzsteuervoranmeldung) filed via ELSTER (monthly for tax > EUR 7,500, quarterly otherwise) and through the annual VAT return. Cleanroom fit-out supplies performed in Germany by a foreign contractor are subject to reverse-charge taxation of the German recipient under §13b UStG, with the non-resident contractor's invoice indicating 'Steuerschuldnerschaft des Leistungsempfängers'.

German export zero-rating claims are ex post audited by the Finanzamt; missing or inconsistent export proof triggers retroactive assessment at the standard 19% rate on the invoice net amount, plus a late-filing surcharge of up to 10% of the assessed tax (§152 AO) and monthly arrears interest under §233a AO (legacy 0.5% per month, subject to constitutional adjustment). Fraudulent zero-rating or intentional false export evidence constitutes tax evasion under §370 AO punishable by imprisonment of up to ten years and monetary fines; the unjustified VAT refund is clawed back with penal interest of 6% per annum (§233a AO Hinterziehungszinsen). Violations of the §13b UStG reverse-charge allocation are separately sanctioned as VAT misdeclaration where the recipient fails to self-assess the tax.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies to both current and capital account transactions under Articles 63 and 64 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (TFEU) and the German Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung – AWV). There are no forced surrender rules or mandatory conversion ratios. Profit remittances from a UG to non-resident shareholders are freely permitted, subject to the statutory limits applicable to UGs, corporate substance checks, and the withholding tax regime (25% or reduced under a double tax treaty). The only operational hurdle is the balance-of-payments statistical reporting obligation for any cross-border payment of EUR 12,500 or more, which must be submitted to the Bundesbank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory export-proceeds surrender or conversion rules exist in Germany. All export receipts may be retained in Euro or any freely convertible currency, and can be used for imports and financial obligations without restriction. For cross-border payment statistical compliance, any individual export receipt of EUR 12,500 or more must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank within five business days after the end of the respective reporting month, including the amount, counterparty, and the targeted purpose code.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (German Federal Bank) as national competent authority, in coordination with the European Central Bank (ECB) for monetary and foreign exchange policies within the Eurosystem.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-High feasibility for German-licensed or EEA-passported electronic money institutions (EMIs). A German UG may validly hold a business account with an EMI such as a PSD2-licensed platform (e.g., SolarisBank, Penta, Qonto) for SEPA and SWIFT payments. However, offshore digital EMIs without a German/EEA passport face severe legal constraints under the Payment Services Supervision Act (ZAG) and the German AMLA/KYC standards; they cannot be used as the sole treasury rail. A long-term treasury structure should pair a commercial bank for credit and LC capacity with a regulated fintech account for day-to-day liquidity, provided the EMI has a physical-authorised agent in Germany.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For the initial equity capital injection from a non-resident shareholder, route the funds via SWIFT MT103 from a bank in the shareholder's jurisdiction to the designated EUR account of the UG. The remittance information field must identify the payment as 'Gesellschaftereinlage' (shareholder contribution), reference the notarised contribution resolution, and include the appropriate Deutsche Bundesbank balance-of-payments purpose code. Under German law the mandatory statistical report is due if the amount reaches EUR 12,500; the receiving UG must file the report with the Bundesbank within five business days after the reporting month. The bank will verify the code and may request the corporate resolution, the contribution ledger, and the tax identification number of the company before releasing the funds to the operational account. No special exchange-control permission is needed because the euro area is fully convertible.Trade settlement for the import of cleanroom modular panels, HVAC machinery, and engineering components should be executed through SWIFT MT103 for straightforward payments or through a commercial letter of credit (documentary collection) using MT700/MT707 under UCP600 for large supplier invoices. For payments within the SEPA zone, use the SEPA credit transfer rail with purpose code 'GC'/'EC' or the relevant Bundesbank statistical code. All MT103 field 70 entries must include the underlying invoice reference and the agreement on bank charges (SHA, BEN, or OUR) as contracted.
