Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, BZSt) and the locally competent municipal tax office (Finanzamt); electronic filing portal ELSTER (www.elster.de).

E-government filing portal

After formation and registration in the German Commercial Register, the Unternehmergesellschaft (haftungsbeschränkt) must file the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' with the Finanzamt to obtain the tax number (Steuernummer). For cross-border operations, the UG separately applies to the BZSt for a VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.) through the BZSt online application form. Where a foreign shareholder/director is involved, the UG must appoint a person authorized to receive service in Germany and comply with anti-money-laundering identification requirements; non-resident entrepreneurs engaged in supplies in Germany may be required to appoint a VAT fiscal representative under section 22a UStG.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Germany does not apply a US-style check-the-box regime; the UG is a corporation by operation of statutory law and cannot elect pass-through treatment. Available statutory elections include the German corporate fiscal unity (Organschaft) under sections 14 and 17 KStG, subject to a profit-and-loss transfer agreement and a 95% shareholding, which consolidates income tax and VAT purposes; trade-tax group (Gewerbesteuer-Organschaft) may apply separately. Hybrid mismatch rules under section 4k EStG follow ATAD/multilateral instrument and do not permit elective mismatch positions; mismatches are automatically neutralized.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general economic-turnover threshold for established taxable persons: any independent economic activity in Germany triggers mandatory VAT registration. An optional small-business exemption (Kleinunternehmerregelung) under section 19 UStG is available from 1 January 2025 when previous-year turnover does not exceed EUR 25,000 and estimated current-year turnover does not exceed EUR 100,000. For intra-Community distance sales and certain cross-border electronic services, the special EU-wide registration threshold is EUR 10,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for export supplies under section 6 UStG where the seller proves that movable goods were transported or dispatched to a third-country territory and physically left the European Union within the statutory time limits. Services directly connected to exported goods, including transport and handling services, may also be zero-rated. Cross-border pipeline EPC work on immovable property located outside Germany is generally outside the scope of German VAT; where the supply constitutes a B2B service to a non-resident entrepreneur, the place of supply is in the recipient's jurisdiction, with the recipient liable to reverse-charge VAT. Intra-Community supplies to EU business customers are zero-rated under section 6a UStG only if valid EU VAT numbers are held and EC sales lists are filed.

  • Customs Export Declaration / Export Accompanying Document (Ausfuhrbegleitdokument) generated through the ATLAS customs system
  • Transport documentation evidencing physical exit from Germany and the European Union: Bill of Lading, CMR consignment note, or equivalent air/sea waybill together with the commercial invoice and incoterms contract
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or equivalent bank settlement evidence demonstrating receipt of payment from the non-resident buyer within the statutory period

Germany currently imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, no continuous transaction control, and no digital stamping/API clearance regime. A phased domestic B2B e-invoicing mandate applies under the Wachstumschancengesetz (Growth Opportunities Act): from 1 January 2025, every domestic taxable person must be able to receive structured electronic invoices in XRechnung or ZUGFeRD format; from 1 January 2027, taxable persons with more than EUR 800,000 in taxable turnover in the preceding year must issue e-invoices; from 1 January 2028, the issuance obligation extends to all taxable persons. Export declarations are made electronically through the German customs ATLAS system, and VAT returns must be transmitted electronically to ELSTER.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to strict documentary audit by the Finanzamt and the customs authorities. Failure to produce contemporaneous proof of physical export results in retrospective VAT assessment at 19% on the exported invoice, plus statutory late-payment interest of 0.5% per month under section 233a AO and late-filing penalties. Fraudulent export documentation or deliberate omission triggers criminal tax evasion liability under section 370 AO with imprisonment up to five years and/or fines; reckless conduct may be sanctioned as a misdemeanor. Clawbacks are enforced within the statutory VAT assessment periods, and managing directors are personally liable under sections 69 and 71 AO for underpaid corporate tax and VAT by reason of their representation duty.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany has full current-account and capital-account convertibility under the EU Capital Free Movement Directive (Directive 88/361/EEC) and German Foreign Trade and Payments Regulation (AWV). Profit repatriation, dividends, interest, and loan repayments are unrestricted; there are no exchange controls or prior-approval requirements. Statutory compliance is limited to ex-post statistical reporting for cross-border payments exceeding EUR 12,500, and broader transaction/payment reporting obligations under AWV Sections 11-13 for resident and non-resident counterparties. Sanctions and export-control restrictions modify freedom of transaction only where EU/UN/OFAC or German Bundestag measures apply. Capital injection into a German UG via shareholder loan or equity contribution is fully convertible.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany does not impose export-proceeds surrender, mandatory conversion, or repatriation timelines. Exporters, including UG entities engaged in cross-border pipeline EPC services and equipment supply, may retain all export receipts in any freely convertible currency. The only regulatory requirement is statistical reporting to Deutsche Bundesbank for payments and receivables above EUR 12,500, submitted within seven calendar days via Z1/Z2 reporting process. No foreign currency balances must be exchanged into EUR.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (German Federal Bank) and the Federal Ministry of Finance for Außenwirtschaftsverordnung (AWV) enforcement; primary FX and balance-of-payments statistics are reported to the Bundesbank's External Payments Reporting Office (Außenwirtschaftsstatistik).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

High for transaction banking and FX conversion, Low for primary project clearing and escrow rails. EU-licensed or German-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) can be used for cross-border payments, SEPA/SEPA Instant, and conversion at lower cost and faster onboarding. However, an EMI cannot accept deposits, cannot issue commercial documentary letters of credit, and is generally not acceptable as a receiving bank on sovereign project wire routing or as an issuing/confirming bank under UCP600. For this ISIC 4220 gas-pipeline EPC mandate with sovereign-backed payment rails, an EMI is viable only as a supplementary payment rail, not as the principal treasury account. Legal constraints arise from the German Payment Services Supervision Act and AMLD4/AMLD5, including passporting restrictions after Brexit,

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For equity or shareholder-loan injection into the German UG, use a SWIFT MT103 with purpose code in accordance with German balance-of-payments statistics (AWV). The funds may be denominated in EUR, USD, or any freely convertible currency. Wire instructions must include the UG's account IBAN, a clear end-beneficiary name, and an acceptable purpose code covering direct investment or loan financing. The receiving bank will verify the sender against sanctions and PEP lists and, if the amount exceeds EUR 12,500, the UG must submit a Z1 balance-of-payments return to Deutsche Bundesbank within seven days. No exchange-control authorisation is needed. For sovereign project exposure, the first injection should be in euro and matched pro-rata to the notary-stated share capital contribution and paid-in NPK, ensuring bank confidence in the capital escrow stage.Outbound payments for pipeline equipment, subcontractor services, and construction-site clearing must be routed through the UG's operational account via SWIFT MT103 or, for documentary trade, by an irrevocable commercial letter of credit. For the ISIC 4220 cross-border pipeline, standard practice is to use SWIFT MT700/MT701 for the issue of an irrevocable LC, with MT710/MT705 for amendment, and MT202 for bank-to-bank settlement. German banks will enforce UCP600 for documentary credits and URDG 758 for demand guarantees. Payments should be accompanied by the underlying sales/service contract, packing list or evidencing certificate, inspection certificates, and if applicable, a technical acceptance report.
