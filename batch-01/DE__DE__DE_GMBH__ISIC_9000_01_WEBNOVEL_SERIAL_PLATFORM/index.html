Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A pure virtual office without a physical German address is not permissible as the statutory seat (Sitz) of a GmbH. The registered office must be a real, physical street address in Germany at which service of process can be effected and which appears in the commercial register. A virtual office service can be accepted as a secondary correspondence address and as a customer-care address for the webnovel subscription platform, provided the company also maintains a physical registered office address. The commercial model is digital-only with no statutory license requirement, increasing the practicality of a small service office, but the notary and Registry Court will require evidence of actual availability of the registered office address.

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Commercial Register (Handelsregister) of the Local District Court (Amtsgericht) of the district of the company's registered office; electronic filing executed by the German notary.

Governing statute

German Limited Liability Companies Act (Gesetz betreffend die Gesellschaften mit beschränkter Haftung – GmbHG): Sections 1–2 (formation and articles), 5 (minimum capital), 6–8 (managing directors, application content, capital declaration), 10 (registration) and 11 (legal effectiveness upon registration); procedural rules in the German Commercial Code (HGB) on the Commercial Register.

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarized incorporation deed (Gründungsprotokoll) containing the Articles of Association (Gesellschaftsvertrag) and the application for registration (Anmeldung zur Eintragung) pursuant to Sections 7, 8 and 78 GmbHG, submitted in certified electronic form by the notary.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Minimum stated capital (Stammkapital) of EUR 25,000; at least EUR 12,500 (50%) of each cash contribution must be paid in before registration (100% for a single-shareholder GmbH). No statutory escrow account is required, but the managing directors must declare under Section 8(2) GmbHG that the paid-up capital is at the company's free disposal in a bank account in its name.

Funding & unlock path: No fire-safety or structural-load construction permit is required for digital operations without a production site. Any administrative office must comply with the Workplaces Ordinance (Arbeitsstättenverordnung), the Occupational Health and Safety Act (ArbSchG) and DGUV accident-prevention rules, including escape routes, emergency exits, fire extinguishers, electrical safety and a suitable hazard risk assessment; structural load certificates are relevant only for racking/heavy equipment and are inapplicable to a webnovel subscription office.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt; www.bzst.de) — federal authority issuing the VAT ID (Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer) and administering the EU One-Stop-Shop; decentralized local Finanzamt for corporate income tax, trade tax and VAT assessment; statutory e-filing through the ELSTER portal (www.elster.de).

E-government filing portal

A newly incorporated GmbH must register with the locally competent Finanzamt by submitting the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' via ELSTER within one month of commencing business. The Finanzamt assigns a statutory Steuernummer used for all tax returns. The separate EU-compliant VAT ID (DE + 9 digits) is requested either through the tax registration questionnaire (forwarded to the BZSt) or by direct application to the BZSt's VAT-ID online procedure; the number is activated for VIES confirmation. A foreign entity without a domestic establishment registers for VAT with the Finanzamt allocated under section 21 AO for non-resident entrepreneurs and obtains its VAT ID from the BZSt; alternatively, non-resident digital suppliers may register exclusively under the OSS non-Union procedure via the BZSt for EU B2C e-services.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — a GmbH is a resident corporation subject to unlimited taxation when its statutory seat or place of effective management is in Germany. It bears 15% Körperschaftsteuer, 5.5% Solidaritätszuschlag on corporate tax, and municipal Gewerbesteuer (3.5% federal base rate multiplied by the municipal Hebesatz). It is not pass-through eligible; worldwide income is taxable with ordinary foreign tax credit relief under double taxation treaties; CFC and section 49 EStG extended-closing rules apply for certain foreign-source items. — A newly incorporated GmbH must register with the locally competent Finanzamt by submitting the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' via ELSTER within one month of commencing business. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a GmbH is a resident corporation subject to unlimited taxation when its statutory seat or place of effective management is in Germany. It bears 15% Körperschaftsteuer, 5.5% Solidaritätszuschlag on corporate tax, and municipal Gewerbesteuer (3.5% federal base rate multiplied by the municipal Hebesatz). It is not pass-through eligible; worldwide income is taxable with ordinary foreign tax credit relief under double taxation treaties; CFC and section 49 EStG extended-closing rules apply for certain foreign-source items.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box transparency election exists. Relevant statutory elections include: (i) Organschaft — corporate income tax, trade tax and VAT fiscal-unity election consolidating a German controlling parent with one or more wholly-owned controlled subsidiaries, subject to profit-pooling, financial-integration and uniform-management tests; (ii) EU One-Stop-Shop (Union and non-Union schemes) election filed with the BZSt to centralize VAT compliance for EU distance sales and electronically supplied services; (iii) 'Kleinunternehmer' small-business exemption under section 19 UStG, revocable for five binding years, if prior-year turnover does not exceed EUR 22,000 and the current-year forecast does not exceed EUR 50,000; (iv) trade tax loss carry-back and carry-forward elections under sections 10a and 8c GewStG. Hybrid-mismatch deduction-denial rules under section 4k EStG apply mandatorily (ATAD I/II) and cannot be overridden by election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general economic turnover threshold for resident entrepreneurs: VAT registration duty arises from the first taxable supply upon formation of entrepreneurial status under section 2 UStG. The 'Kleinunternehmer' exemption operates as a registration/deduction reliever at EUR 22,000 prior-year turnover and EUR 50,000 current-year forecast. Non-established foreign digital operators have no threshold: registration in Germany (or EU) is mandatory from the first euro of German/EU B2C electronically supplied services; the EUR 10,000 EU-wide OSS threshold applies only to micro-businesses established in one member state.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports qualify for zero-rating under sections 4 No. 1 and 6 UStG when the goods physically leave the EU customs territory: supplier transport within three months, customer transport within three months extendable to twelve months on application, evidenced per sections 8–9 UStDV. For the core digital webnovel platform: electronically supplied services are located by place-of-supply rules — B2B sales are reverse-charged at the customer's establishment (no German VAT); B2C sales to non-EU customers are outside German VAT scope (effective 0%); B2C sales to EU customers are taxable at the customer's member-state rate and are reportable through the OSS rather than as German zero-rated supplies. Accordingly, the platform's offshore services rely on residence evidence and payment-rail data rather than customs declarations, while any attached tangible merchandise must satisfy the customs-documentation route to claim 0%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Ausfuhranmeldung via ATLAS)
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank payment confirmation proving receipt of foreign consideration)
  • Air Waybill or CMR consignment note where applicable
  • Commercial invoice with complete parties and Incoterms
  • Export accompanying document (Ausfuhrbegleitdokument) or equivalent proof of physical exit from the EU customs territory
  • For offshore e-services: customer residence evidence pack (IP geo-location, billing address, payment processor country code, subscriber device data and bank details) in lieu of customs papers

Mandatory B2B e-invoicing (E-Rechnung): from 1 January 2025 every German B2B taxpayer must be able to receive structured electronic invoices; issuance becomes mandatory from 1 January 2027 for suppliers with prior-year turnover exceeding EUR 800,000 and for all B2B suppliers by 1 January 2028. Accepted formats are XRechnung (XML) and ZUGFeRD/Factur-X, with Peppol transport permitted; data files must be transmitted in a machine-readable structured format. VAT returns and EC sales lists are filed electronically via ELSTER using ERiC/REST-certified interfaces; OSS filings are made through the BZSt. Germany does not impose real-time transaction-by-transaction fiscal clearance or digital stamping on e-invoices; the cash-register TSE obligation (section 146a AO) applies only to electronic cash registers and is not engaged by Apple IAP/Google Play Billing payment rails.

Unsubstantiated or abusive zero-rating is reversed on German tax audit, producing a 19% VAT assessment, denial of associated input VAT recovery, statutory interest, and enforcement seizure. Reassessment windows: 4 years for good-faith returns, 5 years for negligent misdeclaration (section 378 AO), and 10 years for intentional tax evasion (section 370 AO). Late-payment surcharge accrues at 1% per commenced month on unpaid VAT (maximum 10%; section 240 AO); late-filing penalties may reach 10% of assessed VAT (section 152 AO). Negligent tax evasion is finable up to EUR 50,000, and intentional VAT fraud is a criminal offense carrying imprisonment of six months to five years generally, up to ten years in aggravated cases, with fines and confiscation of illicit benefits. The BZSt additionally runs automatic cross-border VAT data exchanges under EU Regulation 904/2010 and DAC 7 digital-platform reporting, increasing audit visibility of webnovel subscription flows processed through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing rails.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany operates a fully convertible currency regime under EU directives and international standards. Both capital and current account transactions are freely convertible with no capital controls. Profit remittances to foreign shareholders are permitted without restriction, subject only to standard corporate tax compliance and anti-money laundering reporting. Transactions above €12,500 require mandatory electronic reporting to the Bundesbank via the eFEX system. Digital service revenues from subscription platforms qualify as current account items and are treated as freely convertible.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender of export proceeds to the central bank applies to service-based revenues from digital platforms. Subscription app receipts denominated in foreign currency may be retained in foreign currency accounts and converted at market rates through authorized banks. Voluntary surrender is available but not obligatory. All receipts must satisfy VAT and corporate income tax obligations with full documentation for audit purposes.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital electronic money institutions require full BaFin licensing under the Payment Services Supervision Act. Cross-border EMI operations for a German GmbH are restricted to partnerships with licensed EU entities. Direct offshore issuance is prohibited without regulatory approval and may conflict with GDPR data protection requirements.
