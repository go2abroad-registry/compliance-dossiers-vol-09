Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A UG must have a domestic business address in Germany under sections 4a and 8(4) GmbHG. A serviced or virtual office may be acceptable if it is a genuine address at which official documents can be delivered and the company can be reached. A mere mailbox, forwarding-only address, or address lacking reliable access may fail the statutory requirement. For online merchandising of fashion jewelry and fine timepieces, a virtual office may be acceptable during formation if the business has separately documented operational substance, such as management availability, inventory or fulfillment arrangements, accounting records, customer-service capability, payment compliance, consumer-law processes, and demonstrable control of goods and funds. Banks, Stripe, PayPal, tax authorities, customs authorities, and AML-obliged professionals may require evidence of actual operations and may reject an address-only structure.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum above EUR 1 (approximately USD 1.10) for the Unternehmergesellschaft (haftungsbeschränkt). Each capital contribution must be paid in full and be at the free disposal of the managing director before the application for registration; a bank credit confirmation is required. No escrow account is mandated for cash contributions, but contributions in kind require a non-cash incorporation report and audit.

Funding & unlock path: The warehouse and office premises must undergo a basic fire safety inspection under the applicable state building code (Landesbauordnung), including compliance with emergency exit signage, fire extinguisher requirements, and evacuation routes. For occupied office and logistics spaces, the Workplace Ordinance (Arbeitsstättenverordnung) and the Industrial Safety Ordinance (Betriebssicherheitsverordnung) apply as to lighting, sanitation, ventilation, and crane or racking safety. A structural load inspection is required only if pallet racking in the warehouse exceeds a total shelf height of 2.50 meters; no special civil-defense license is otherwise required.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum above EUR 1 (approximately USD 1.10) for the Unternehmergesellschaft (haftungsbeschränkt). Each capital contribution must be paid in full and be at the free disposal of the managing director before the application for registration; a bank credit confirmation is required. No escrow account is mandated for cash contributions, but contributions in kind require a non-cash incorporation report and audit.

No specific German industrial licensing authority is triggered because the pure online merchandising of fashion jewelry and fine timepieces is not an industrial manufacturing activity. General supervision remains with the municipal Trade Office (Gewerbeamt) and, where applicable, the Chamber of Industry and Commerce (IHK).

Category: No specialized industrial license. The ordinary trade registration certificate (Gewerbeschein) under section 14 Gewerbeordnung is the only operational authorization required; no separate precious-metal hallmarking or watchmaking license is necessary for retail distribution.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt) – Central Federal Tax Office, together with the locally competent Finanzamt (income/corporate/trade tax registration). Primary e-file portal: ELSTER (www.elster.de); BZSt online portal for VAT ID and foreign-entrepreneur registration: www.bzst.de.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a German UG must submit to the competent local Finanzamt the official 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire), either electronically via ELSTER or in paper form, and disclose all anticipated economic activities, ownership, and bank accounts. The Finanzamt assigns a local tax number (Steuernummer). Subsequently, the UG must apply separately to the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt) for a national VAT identification number (USt-IdNr) using the official application form ('Antrag auf Erteilung einer USt-IdNr') available through the BZSt online portal. Non-resident entrepreneurs with no permanent establishment in Germany may use the special BZSt procedure for foreign applicants and must file a corresponding 'Fragebogen für im Ausland ansässige Unternehmer' (foreign-entrepreneur questionnaire), which simultaneously triggers VAT registration and issuance of a German USt-IdNr where required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

German tax law does not adopt a 'check-the-box' system; a UG is statutorily classified as a body corporate (Kapitalgesellschaft) subject to entity-level corporate income tax and trade tax. No transparent pass-through election is permitted. Available elective mechanisms include: (i) Organschaft (fiscal unity) for corporation tax, trade tax, and VAT under §§ 14–19 KStG and § 2 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UStG; (ii) the small-entrepreneur/VAT small-business exemption under § 19 UStG where gross turnover does not exceed the statutory thresholds and input VAT deduction is waived; (iii) election of annual VAT assessment versus monthly advance returns; (iv) group VAT registration as part of an Organschaft if the controlling and controlled entities meet German fiscal-unity requirements. Hybrid mismatch and pass-through elections are unavailable for a German resident UG.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No registration threshold for non-resident/non-established suppliers; a German USt-IdNr and VAT return obligation arise as soon as any taxable supply is made in Germany. For resident small entrepreneurs, the § 19 UStG small-business exemption applies if previous-year turnover did not exceed EUR 22,000 and current-year turnover is not expected to exceed EUR 50,000. Above those limits, full VAT registration is mandatory for all resident businesses.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% export VAT rate applies under § 6 UStG where the UG, as exporter, dispatches goods directly to a third-country territory (outside the EU) or places them in a customs warehousing procedure. The UG must demonstrate actual physical movement of the jewelry/watches out of EU customs territory and hold verifiable commercial and transport documentation evidencing the exporter and the destination. Intra-Community supplies to EU-business acquirers are exempt from VAT under § 6a UStG if the buyer holds a valid VAT identification number and the goods arrive in another EU Member State. Digital B2B services supplied to a third-country entrepreneur are outside German VAT scope under § 3a UStG place-of-supply rules, while B2C offshore services are generally taxable at destination in the customer's jurisdiction. Export zero-rating is strictly documentary in nature and requires proof of export before the 0% treatment is finalized.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ATLAS electronic declaration / Movement Reference Number – MRN)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR consignment note, evidencing physical export
  • Proof of transport (postal/courier movement evidence or independent carrier records)
  • Export accompanying document (EAD) or re-forwarding/customs transit proof where applicable
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing collection of receivable in foreign currency
  • Customer-validated delivery confirmation showing third-country address

Germany has introduced a phased B2B e-invoicing mandate. From 1 January 2025, every resident entrepreneur must be able to receive structured electronic invoices (XRechnung or ZUGFeRD/Factur-X) for domestic B2B supplies. Issuers with a 2026 calendar-year turnover exceeding EUR 800,000 must transmit structured e-invoices from 1 January 2027, and all resident entrepreneurs must do so from 1 January 2028. Germany does not operate a centralized real-time fiscal clearance or digital-stamping platform; e-invoices are transmitted directly via PEPPOL, EDI, or email using structured XML. Cross-border and export invoices remain governed by § 14 UStG and must ensure authenticity, integrity, and legibility; for exported goods, electronic plain-format invoices remain permissible without prior authorization or government stamping.

Export zero-rating is provisional and subject to tax-office verification. If the required export documentation is incomplete, contradictory, or not produced in time, the Finanzamt retrospectively reverses the 0% rate and assesses VAT at the domestic rate (19%) on the original sales amount, plus late-payment/underpayment interest under § 233a AO (0.5% per commenced month). The normal assessment period is four years, extended to ten years in cases of intentional or reckless concealment. Knowingly presenting false export evidence constitutes tax evasion under § 370 AO and is punishable by imprisonment of up to five years or a criminal fine; it also triggers customs fraud liability under Union Customs Code Article 42. In addition, failure to maintain mandatory VAT documentary records can lead to regulatory fines under § 26c UStG of up to EUR 25,000, denial of the BZSt VAT ID, suspension of the use of export zero-rating, and full retrospective clawback of input VAT previously claimed in connection with the respective goods.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible. Germany applies no exchange controls on either current-account or capital-account transactions, in full alignment with Articles 63-66 TFEU on the free movement of capital. Profit remittances, dividend distributions, loan repayments and royalty outflows are legally unrestricted; the only regulatory burden is the statistical reporting to the Deutsche Bundesbank of every cross-border payment exceeding EUR 12,500. Tax hurdles must not be confused with FX restrictions: a UG profit distribution to a foreign shareholder is subject to 25% German withholding tax plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge, potentially reduced to 0-10% under an applicable double-tax treaty or the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive for qualifying corporate shareholders.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany imposes no export-proceeds surrender obligation and no mandatory conversion of foreign-currency receipts into EUR. The UG may lawfully retain, save and re-use USD, CNY or CHF proceeds in its euro and foreign-currency accounts. Statutory compliance is confined to (i) AWV reporting to the Deutsche Bundesbank of each cross-border receipt or payment exceeding EUR 12,500 within five business days, and (ii) retention of underlying trade documentation (sale/purchase invoices, shipping documents, customs declarations) for at least five years for potential inspection by the Bundesbank, customs authorities and the tax authorities.

Central bank supervision: European Central Bank (monetary authority for the eurozone) and Deutsche Bundesbank (German central bank, competent national authority for the German Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance - Außenwirtschaftsverordnung/AWV - and cross-border payment statistics); banking supervision and anti-money-laundering oversight co-vested with the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin) and the German Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a supplementary settlement rail, never as the sole treasury backbone. BaFin-licensed or EU/EEA-passported Electronic Money Institutions (e.g., Wise, Revolut, Airwallex, Payoneer) may lawfully open multi-currency wallets for the UG under the German Payment Services Supervision Act (ZAG); however, EMIs cannot grant credit or hold interest-bearing demand deposits, and client funds are safeguarded rather than covered by the German statutory deposit protection scheme. Because the target payment rails are Stripe and PayPal, an EMI can accelerate cross-currency sweeps and FX conversion, but payroll, VAT payments and high-ticket fine-watch supplier disbursements must remain on a Tier-1 German credit-institution account to avoid AWV statistical fragmentation and reputational AML flags at the German bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder equity injections should be routed via SWIFT MT103 (non-EUR) or SEPA Credit Transfer (EUR) into the UG dedicated German account, with the remittance information unambiguously stating 'Capital contribution / Einlage'. Foreign-currency receipts are converted by the receiving German bank at the applicable FX telegraphic-transfer rate into EUR; any amount exceeding EUR 12,500 must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank under AWV payment statistics. Each contribution must be evidenced by a signed capital-contribution confirmation (Einlagenbescheinigung) for transmission to the commercial register; shareholder loans must be labelled 'Darlehen' and documented by written agreements to prevent reclassification as hidden equity contributions. The sending bank, the German notary and the tax advisor should be pre-aligned on the source-of-funds narrative to avoid second-line KYC review.Supplier settlements for jewelry and watch inventory should be executed (a) via SWIFT MT103 bank transfer for direct telegraphic payments to overseas suppliers (e.g., Indian stone and artisan suppliers, Swiss watch distributors, Chinese manufacturers); (b) via commercial documentary letter of credit (MT700) for high-value fine-watch purchases requiring payment security; and (c) via SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant for EU suppliers settled in EUR. Marketplace payouts from Stripe and PayPal should be swept in one daily cycle into the UG operational account, converting stale USD/CNY/CHF balances via the bank FX desk rather than relying on processor-imputed conversion.
