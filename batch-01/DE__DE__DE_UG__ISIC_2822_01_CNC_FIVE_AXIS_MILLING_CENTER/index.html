Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt fuer Steuern) for VAT ID issuance and cross-border matters; municipal Tax Office (Finanzamt) for tax registration and audits; official portal ELSTER (www.elster.de) for all tax registrations, returns and certificates.

E-government filing portal

Domestic UG: (1) File the statutory tax registration questionnaire (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung) via ELSTER within one month of business commencement; (2) Finanzamt assigns a local tax number (Steuernummer) for CIT, trade tax and VAT advance returns; (3) apply separately to BZSt for a VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.) through ELSTER. Non-resident foreign entity without domestic PE: (4) apply directly to BZSt using Form 'Antrag auf Erteilung einer Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer' for reverse-charge, intra-EU supplies, distance sales and OSS/import reconciliation, attaching articles of association, commercial register extract and customs/export license evidence; processing typically 4-6 weeks; digital authentication via ElsterX.500 certificate or registered agent (gewerblicher Buergermeister).

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation. A UG (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschraenkt) is a resident capital company (Kapitalgesellschaft) and is transparently ineligible for pass-through treatment. It is fully subject to German corporate income tax (Koerperschaftsteuer) at 15.0% plus solidarity surcharge (Solidaritaetszuschlag) of 5.5% on the CIT amount (effective 0.825%) and municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer), which yields a combined federal-plus-trade effective CIT load of approximately 30-33% depending on the municipal levy factor (Hebesatz, generally 200%-470%). Distributions to shareholders are taxed separately at 26.375% final withholding (Abfgeltungsteuer including solidarity surcharge) or under the partial-income system (Teileinkuenfteverfahren) for corporate shareholders. Territorial scope: worldwide income for resident UG, tax treaty exemption relief for foreign PE dividends permitted under the treaty network. — Domestic UG: (1) File the statutory tax registration questionnaire (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung) via ELSTER within one month of business commencement; (2) Finanzamt assigns a local tax number (Steuernummer) for CIT, trade tax and VAT advance returns; (3) apply separately to BZSt for a VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.) through ELSTER. Non-resident foreign entity without domestic PE: (4) apply directly to BZSt using Form 'Antrag auf Erteilung einer Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer' for reverse-charge, intra-EU supplies, distance sales and OSS/import reconciliation, attaching articles of association, commercial register extract and customs/export license evidence; processing typically 4-6 weeks; digital authentication via ElsterX.500 certificate or registered agent (gewerblicher Buergermeister).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. A UG (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschraenkt) is a resident capital company (Kapitalgesellschaft) and is transparently ineligible for pass-through treatment. It is fully subject to German corporate income tax (Koerperschaftsteuer) at 15.0% plus solidarity surcharge (Solidaritaetszuschlag) of 5.5% on the CIT amount (effective 0.825%) and municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer), which yields a combined federal-plus-trade effective CIT load of approximately 30-33% depending on the municipal levy factor (Hebesatz, generally 200%-470%). Distributions to shareholders are taxed separately at 26.375% final withholding (Abfgeltungsteuer including solidarity surcharge) or under the partial-income system (Teileinkuenfteverfahren) for corporate shareholders. Territorial scope: worldwide income for resident UG, tax treaty exemption relief for foreign PE dividends permitted under the treaty network.

Available election forms

German law does not offer a check-the-box transparency election; the UG is per se a C-corporation. Available elections: (1) VAT grouping (Organschaft) under UStG section 2(2) for financial, economic and organizational integration of subsidiaries; (2) small-business VAT exemption (Kleinunternehmerregelung, UStG section 19) on an annual application basis if prior-year turnover is below EUR 22,000 and current-year forecast below EUR 50,000; (3) option to waive VAT exemption on real estate transactions (UStG section 9); (4) trade tax loss carry-forward election under GewStG section 10a; (5) no hybrid mismatch election - fully aligned with ATAD Germany; (6) group taxation election for CIT (Organschaft) requiring profit-and-loss transfer agreement (Ergebnisabfuehrungsvertrag).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 22,000 prior-year turnover threshold for the small-business exemption (UStG section 19); additionally current calendar-year forecast must not exceed EUR 50,000. Except for the small-business exemption, there is no minimum general registration threshold - any entrepreneurial activity (even a single taxable supply) triggers compulsory VAT registration. For cross-border EU distance sales and OSS B2C scheme the pan-EU registration threshold is EUR 10,000; for CNC machinery exports and B2B supplies registration is mandatory immediately without turnover relief.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% rate applies to direct export supplies (UStG sections 4(1)(a) and 6) where the CNC five-axis milling center is transported by the supplier or on the supplier's behalf to a third-country destination outside the European Union. Qualifying conditions include: (1) timely export evidenced by customs export declaration (Ausfuhranmeldung via ATLAS), customs exit confirmation and transport contract; (2) the buyer must be an entrepreneur (B2B) or the export must be a genuine commercial export; (3) intra-Community supplies to EU customers are zero-rated (UStG sections 4(1)(b), 6a) only if buyer's valid VAT ID is obtained and proper recapitulative statement filed; (4) offshore B2B services (installation, commissioning, maintenance of the CNC centers) qualify for 0% when the recipient is established in a third country and the benefit of the service lies abroad; (5) no input VAT recovery restriction for the exporter, with refund claimable via the regular input VAT deduction or reverse-charge mechanism; prerequisite: the supplier must retain verifiable transactional documents proving actual export within one month of supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Ausfuhranmeldung electronically lodged in ATLAS system)
  • Customs Exit Confirmation (Ausfuhrbegleitdokument with customs office of exit stamp or electronic ITGB receipt)
  • Bill of Lading (Ocean) / Air Waybill (Air) / CMR Waybill (Road) / Railway Consignment Note (Rail)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List reflecting the exact machine serial number and HS code 8457.10
  • Supplier's Export Evidence Report (Belegnachweis nach 'ELugV') including transport instruction and departure date
  • Intra-EU: Customer's Validated VAT Identification Number, recapitulative statement entry, and delivery note with buyer confirmation
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT message evidencing payment from the foreign buyer (for documentary substantiation)
  • For high-risk dual-use machine tools: Export License or End-User Certificate (Endverbleibserklaerung) per EU Dual-Use Regulation 2021/821

Germany does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping regime. Mandatory structures: (1) From 1 January 2025 all businesses must be able to receive structured electronic invoices in EU standard EN 16931 XML (ZUGFeRD/XRechnung) for B2B supplies; (2) issuance of e-invoices becomes mandatory from 1 January 2027 for taxpayers with prior-year turnover exceeding EUR 800,000 and from 1 January 2028 for all taxpayers; (3) B2G e-invoicing to federal authorities has been mandatory since 27 November 2020 (XRechnung/Peppol BIS); (4) no clearance API or stamping - exchange occurs through the Peppol network or agreed EDI channels enabled by IT security standards GS1; (5) real-time declaration obligations apply only to VAT advance returns (Umsatzsteuervoranmeldung) filed via ELSTER by the 10th day after month/quarter-end, plus recapitulative statements (Zusammenfassende Meldung) for intra-EU B2B supplies.

Export zero-rating claims are highly scrutinized by German tax audit and customs authorities through ATLAS data cross-checks, Bundesbank payment statistics and BZSt exchange of information. Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating triggers: (1) retrospective VAT assessment (clawback) at 19% on the full invoice amount plus interest of 0.5% per month (6% p.a.) under AO section 233a; (2) administrative penalty up to EUR 5,000 per faulty invoice under UStG section 26a; (3) criminal tax evasion under AO section 370 - imprisonment from 6 months to 5 years, or up to 10 years in aggravated cases (commercial gang fraud, export carousel schemes); (4) confiscation of machinery and forfeiture of proceeds; (5) denial of input VAT deduction, revocation of VAT ID and removal from the list of registered exporters; (6) for dual-use CNC machines, violations of End-Use certificates additionally invoke the Foreign Trade Act (AWG) with penalties up to EUR 500,000 or imprisonment for 5 years. German audit the export evidence strictly within the 6-month repatriation standard (180 days); lack of hard currency repatriation within 180 days shifts the presumption toward fraudulent circular transactions unless a documented credit/offset agreement exists.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany operates a fully convertible currency regime under EU law and national FX regulations. Both current and capital accounts are open with no restrictions on convertibility or repatriation of profits. Remittance of corporate profits to foreign shareholders is permitted without prior approval, subject solely to standard tax filings, beneficial ownership disclosure, and anti-money laundering compliance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or conversion of export proceeds required. All export transactions must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank for statistical purposes within 30 days of shipment. Free FX trading applies; no statutory conversion ratios or timelines for surrender exist.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% Körperschaftsteuer + 5.5% Solidaritätszuschlag = 15.825% combined federal corporate income tax; plus municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) at Hebesatz-dependent rate of 14–17%, producing a combined effective statutory tax rate of approximately 30–32%.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Statutory withholding rate on distributions to non-resident shareholders: 25% capital income tax (Abgeltungsteuer) plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge on that tax, resulting in 26.375% total withholding where no church tax is applied; church tax is generally not levied on UG distributions to corporate shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

General statutory deadline: 31 July following the fiscal year-end; where a tax adviser prepares the return, the filing deadline is automatically extended to 28/29 February of the following calendar year; quarterly prepayments of corporate income tax and solidarity surcharge are due on 10 March, 10 June, 10 September, and 10 December.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Same statutory deadline as KSt 1: 31 July following the fiscal year-end; extension to 28/29 February if professionally advised; trade tax prepayments are quarterly (10 Feb, 10 May, 10 Aug, 10 Nov) based on the most recent assessment.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 5,000,000 for controlled transactions in goods and EUR 500,000 for controlled services per fiscal year (application of the 2020 BMF Verwaltungsgrundsätze Verrechnungspreise); a Master File is required for the ultimate German parent or group with consolidated turnover exceeding EUR 750 million; transfer-pricing documentation must be contemporaneous and submitted upon official demand within 60 days for cross-border transactions.

Economic substance scope: Germany does not operate an offshore-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) framework; however, the tax administration applies a substance-over-form doctrine under §42 AO and the concept of economic activity (wirtschaftliche Betätigung) as reinforced by §8c KStG and the BEPS MLI. For the German UG (haftungsbeschränkt), statutory corporate substance is mandatory: registered office, actual local management, payroll with skilled personnel, premises and machinery used for CNC precision milling. A UG lacking genuine substance and controlled predominantly from abroad will be treated as having its place of actual management outside Germany, triggering resident company status reclassification, contribution to a loss of treaty entitlement, denial of German R&D and §7g incentives, and the risk of CFC-to-foreign-asset reclassification.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax Evasion (Steuerhinterziehung)

Statutory sanction: Under §369 AO and §370 AO, penalty of up to five years' imprisonment for basic evasion, ten years for especially severe cases (commercial-scale misconduct); financial penalty up to EUR 40,000 per defendant or to the amount of the tax evaded; cumulative surcharge of 0.5% monthly interest on back-taxes; automatic disallowance of trade loss carried forward; seizure of profits and equipment.

Director personal liability: Director a member is liable under §69 AO for penalties and taxes not paid by the UG where non-payment is intentional or grossly negligent; tax evasion triggers personal criminal conviction, asset forfeiture, travel ban, immigration alert, and disqualification from serving as a director for up to 10 years; piercing the corporate veil may order civil restitution to the tax authority for the declared shortfall.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: German Revenue may treat the UG as a 'Briefkastenfirma' without economic substance under §42 AO, adjust the taxable income to reflect economic reality, refuse DTA treaty benefits, apply an immediate assessment with penalty addition of 5% of the adjusted increase under §162 AO (minimum EUR 5,000), and impose a civil penalty under the Anti-Money-Laundering Act (GwG) for false economic purpose declarations, with fines up to EUR 5 million or 10% of total annual turnover.

Director personal liability: The managing director becomes personally liable for all taxes, surcharges, and administrative fines if he or she acquiesced in the sham structure; criminal exposure includes fraud (§263 StGB) and aiding tax evasion; travel bans and denial of residency permits are issued to non-resident directors on pending tax-evasion charges; a court may order the UG's immediate winding-up and personal restitution to the fisc.
Anti-Money-Laundering (AML) Breach for High-Risk Machinery Trade

Statutory sanction: Under the German GwG (Geldwäschegesetz), sanctions include monetary fines up to EUR 5 million for serious intentional breaches or up to 10% of annual global turnover; simultaneously, the export permit authority may revoke the manufacturing licence under § 8 Außenwirtschaftsgesetz, and criminal money-laundering penalties apply under §261 StGB: imprisonment of 3 months to 5 years, imprisonment up to 10 years for qualified money-laundering activities.

Director personal liability: Directors are statutory compliance officers under §§6, 43 GwG; non-compliance leads to personal fines, disqualification from management office, and criminal conviction. For cross-border CNC machine exports involved in sanctions evasion or the use by unauthorised end-users, directors can be personally charged with financing terrorism; travel bans and extradition are available; civil liability extends to full restitution, damages to contractual parties, and loss of the special export licence.
Transfer Pricing Documentation and Substance Adjustment Failure

Statutory sanction: Under §162 Abs. 4 AO, a presumption of income understatement arises and the tax authority may estimate income with an additional tax assessment plus a penalty surcharge of 5% of the amount of the understated income (minimum EUR 5,000); further late penalties under §152 AO and interest of 0.5% per month; in extreme cases, penal fraud proceedings for tax evasion under §370 AO are initiated.

Director personal liability: The director's positive duty to sign the declaration of completeness (Vollständigkeitserklärung) means any false declaration triggers personal criminal liability; the tax authority may pierce the UG veil when the director fails to keep proper TP records, making the director jointly and severally liable for the resulting corporate tax debt and penalties; imprisonment of up to five years and civil restitution for lost tax revenues are common outcomes for repeated or systematic TP misdeclarations.
