Competent corporate registry

Amtsgericht (local court) acting as Handelsregister (Commercial Register) via the gemeinsames Registerportal der Länder; local IHK (Industrie- und Handelskammer) for ancillary notification; Finanzamt for tax registration.

Governing statute

Gesetz betreffend die Gesellschaften mit beschränkter Haftung (GmbHG): §§ 2-11 (formation and registration), § 5a (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschränkt), § 7 (registration prerequisites), § 8 (registration application), § 10 (commercial register entry), §§ 6, 35, 46 (directors and shareholder resolutions); Handelsgesetzbuch (HGB) §§ 8, 9, 12 (commercial register filings); Aktiengesetz (AktG) §§ 41-42 (business premises).

Formation / constitutional filings

Notariell beglaubigte Gesellschafterliste and notarized Gesellschaftsvertrag (Articles of Association), filed via notarized Handelsregisteranmeldung (registration application) with the Commercial Register.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The UG (haftungsbeschränkt) is not subject to the EUR 25,000 minimum GmbH share capital. The share capital must be at least EUR 1.00. Contributions must be paid in full in cash before filing the registration; non-cash contributions are not permitted. The amount paid in must be deposited into a business bank account and confirmed by the bank or by the notary; only after full payment may the registration application be filed (§ 5a GmbHG). A statutory reserve of 25% of annual net profit must be retained until capital reaches EUR 25,000.

Funding & unlock path: For office or warehouse premises, fire safety certification (Brandschutz) may be required by the local Bauamt (building authority); structural load inspections if heavy inventory storage; workplace clearance from the Gewerbeaufsichtsamt for employee health and safety (Arbeitsschutzgesetz). If selling electrical goods, compliance with CE marking and WEEE registration is mandatory.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt) — Federal Central Tax Office, for VAT identification numbers and central taxpayer functions; locally competent Finanzamt for income tax, trade tax and VAT assessment; official digital portal: ELSTER (www.elster.de).

E-government filing portal

A newly incorporated UG must file the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) with the local Finanzamt via ELSTER or prescribed paper form within one month of commencing business, disclosing intended activities, expected turnover and ownership details. The Finanzamt assigns the statutory Steuernummer (tax number), and VAT identification number (Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer) is separately applied for through the BZSt or requested in the registration questionnaire. Non-resident entities without a German seat use the BZSt central application procedure or register with the responsible foreign-seller Finanzamt, appointing a domestic fiscal agent where required for VAT compliance.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — a resident UG is treated as a corporation: subject to corporation tax (Körperschaftsteuer), solidarity surcharge and trade tax at entity level; shareholders are taxed only on actual distributions; no pass-through status is available. — A newly incorporated UG must file the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) with the local Finanzamt via ELSTER or prescribed paper form within one month of commencing business, disclosing intended activities, expected turnover and ownership details. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a resident UG is treated as a corporation: subject to corporation tax (Körperschaftsteuer), solidarity surcharge and trade tax at entity level; shareholders are taxed only on actual distributions; no pass-through status is available.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election and no elective pass-through treatment exists for a UG. Relevant statutory elections include: formation of an Organschaft for corporate income tax, trade tax and VAT grouping; the small-business exemption under §19 UStG where turnover is below statutory thresholds; and expedited VAT refund or reverse-charge reliance where supplied to a German business customer. Hybrid mismatch positions are governed by mandatory anti-hybrid rules, with no elective mismatch override.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No registration threshold for an established UG: VAT registration is mandatory upon commencement of taxable entrepreneurial activity. Small-business relief may exempt turnover under §19 UStG if prior-year turnover does not exceed €22,000 and the current-year turnover is unlikely to exceed €50,000, but the entity remains registered. Cross-border EU distance sales to consumers are covered by the €10,000 pan-EU distance-selling threshold for application of the destination-state rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating for exports under §6 UStG applies where tangible goods are actually dispatched to a third-country destination and physically leave the European Union; the supplier must retain irrefutable evidence of export, including the customs export document, transport documents, invoices and, where applicable, proof that the foreign consignee obtained the right of disposal. For online sales of musical instruments and studio audio gear, the dispatch must match the customer's non-EU delivery address and the transaction must be supported by customary commercial records.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic Ausfuhranmeldung with ATLAS exit confirmation)
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent transport document (CMR consignment note, air waybill or postal export receipt)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank transfer receipt evidencing export payment
  • Commercial Invoice and export contract
  • Proof of receipt or disposal of goods by the foreign consignee where available

Germany does not operate real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping. Mandatory domestic B2B e-invoicing (E-Rechnung) is phased: from 1 January 2025 every German B2B supplier must be able to receive structured e-invoices; issuance of e-invoices becomes mandatory for businesses with turnover above €800,000 from 2027, and for all German B2B suppliers from 2028. Accepted structured formats include XRechnung and ZUGFeRD/Factur-X, with secure transmission by EDI, API, email or portal; there is no national tax-authority API clearance step.

Tax authorities may claw back zero-rating if export evidence is incomplete, inconsistent, or if consideration is not received within the 180-day documentary expectation, re-assessing 19% German VAT on the transaction value, plus interest under §233a AO, late payment surcharges under §240 AO, and penalty charges. Fraudulent zero-rating or forged export proofs constitute tax evasion under §370 AO, carrying fines and imprisonment up to 10 years, with a 10-year assessment period for concealed deliberate evasion and personal liability exposure for managing directors through §§34, 69 AO.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany applies full current-account and capital-account convertibility under EU Treaty rules and the OECD Code of Liberalisation; there are no foreign-exchange approval requirements, no central-bank surrender obligations, no exchange-rate band, and no restrictions on profit remittance for a German UG other than standard corporate tax and withholding-tax compliance; cross-border payments are freely cleared in EUR via TARGET2/SEPA and in foreign currency via correspondent banking

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export-proceeds surrender timelines and no mandatory conversion ratios in Germany; export receipts may be retained in EUR or foreign currency, but all cross-border payments exceeding EUR 12,500 or its equivalent must be reported to Deutsche Bundesbank for balance-of-payments statistical purposes under the Außenwirtschaftsverordnung (AWV)

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (German Central Bank), with the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin) responsible for banking and AML supervision and the Federal Foreign Trade Office (BAFA) administering export controls

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-to-High as supplementary payment-collection and clearing infrastructure only. A BaFin-authorised or EU-passported E-Money Institution (EMI) may legally hold client funds and instruct payments, but it is not a full credit institution and cannot provide classic treasury services such as committed credit lines, documentary letters of credit, or deposit safeguarding in the same way as a commercial bank; an EMI should be used for low-cost multi-currency receivables while ultimately settling through a tier-1 banking partner
