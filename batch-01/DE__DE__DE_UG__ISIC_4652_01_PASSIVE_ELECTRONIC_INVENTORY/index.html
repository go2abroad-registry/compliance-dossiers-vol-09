Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Local Amtsgericht acting as Handelsregister court for the registered office, with electronic filing through the competent notary and the gemeinsame Registerportal der Länder.

Governing statute

Gesetz betreffend die Gesellschaften mit beschränkter Haftung (GmbHG), particularly Sections 1-3 (formation and articles), Section 5 (share capital), Section 5a (Unternehmergesellschaft), Sections 7-8 (registration requirements), Section 10 (commercial register particulars), Section 11 (legal existence), Section 35 (management), and Section 37 (management limitations). Handelsgesetzbuch (HGB), Sections 8-12, governs commercial-register filing and publication.

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarised Gesellschaftsvertrag (articles of association) and notarised Anmeldung zum Handelsregister, together with the Geschäftsführer declaration and required shareholder and beneficial-owner information.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 43 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

A UG may be established with share capital below EUR 25,000, including EUR 1, but the subscribed cash capital must be paid in full before registration. Contributions in kind are not permitted at formation. No statutory escrow account is required; the capital is normally paid into the company's bank account, and the managing director confirms availability to the registry. The company must retain 25% of annual net profit, subject to statutory adjustments, until capital reaches EUR 25,000 under GmbHG Section 5a.

Funding & unlock path: Obtain landlord and, where applicable, building-authority confirmation that the premises use is approved for warehousing and commercial distribution. Maintain fire-risk assessment, escape routes, fire protection equipment, emergency plans, and inspections required by local building and fire authorities. Conduct workplace risk assessments under the Arbeitsschutzgesetz and Betriebssicherheitsverordnung, provide safe racking and structural-load controls, and comply with Arbeitsstättenverordnung, DGUV rules, hazardous-substance rules, and occupational-health obligations. A separate fire-safety certificate is site-dependent rather than automatically required for this business model.

Paid-in capital rule: A UG may be established with share capital below EUR 25,000, including EUR 1, but the subscribed cash capital must be paid in full before registration. Contributions in kind are not permitted at formation. No statutory escrow account is required; the capital is normally paid into the company's bank account, and the managing director confirms availability to the registry. The company must retain 25% of annual net profit, subject to statutory adjustments, until capital reaches EUR 25,000 under GmbHG Section 5a.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No sector-specific industrial licensing board is ordinarily required for wholesale distribution of passive electronic components. The local Gewerbeamt administers the general trade registration, while the local building, environmental, and occupational-safety authorities supervise the premises.

Category: Gewerbeanmeldung under Section 14 Gewerbeordnung (GewO); no separate manufacturing or product-distribution operating permit is ordinarily required for ordinary wholesale distribution of capacitors and resistors.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt) / Bundesministerium der Finanzen; portal: ELSTER (Elektronische Steuererklärung)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a German branch or incorporating a German entity (e.g., UG) must apply for a Steuernummer (tax number) from the local Finanzamt upon registration with the Handelsregister (commercial register). The UG obtains a VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.) by filing the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (questionnaire for tax registration) via ELSTER with the responsible Finanzamt; cross-border EU transactions require an additional USt-IdNr. from BZSt. Non-resident entities with German nexus (e.g., distance sales or warehouse) must register for VAT through the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern's 'VAT Registration for Non-EU Entrepreneurs' procedure, submitting Form USt 1 TB plus supporting articles of association and proof of foreign registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity establishing a German branch or incorporating a German entity (e.g., UG) must apply for a Steuernummer (tax number) from the local Finanzamt upon registration with the Handelsregister (commercial register). The UG obtains a VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.) by filing the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (questionnaire for tax registration) via ELSTER with the responsible Finanzamt; cross-border EU transactions require an additional USt-IdNr. from BZSt. Non-resident entities with German nexus (e.g., distance sales or warehouse) must register for VAT through the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern's 'VAT Registration for Non-EU Entrepreneurs' procedure, submitting Form USt 1 TB plus supporting articles of association and proof of foreign registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Germany does not provide a check-the-box election akin to the U.S. The UG (Unternehmergesellschaft) is a domestic corporation subject to Körperschaftsteuer (corporation tax), Gewerbesteuer (trade tax), and solidarity surcharge; no pass-through election is available. Hybrid mismatch rules under the German Tax Code (Außensteuergesetz §4k) may recharacterize payments or deny deductions, but no affirmative election exists; the UG is per se a taxable entity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 22,000 intra-Community distance sales threshold; EUR 100,000 annual EU-wide cross-border distance sales threshold (OSS scheme); domestic registration mandatory for any taxable person with taxable supplies, irrespective of threshold, if established in Germany. Non-resident suppliers must register when making any taxable supply in Germany unless covered by the OSS.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods (including wholesaling of electronic components) from Germany to third countries qualify for zero-rating (0% VAT) under §4 Nr.1 UStG and §6 UStG. Input VAT is recoverable. Intermediary/dispatched exports require proof that the entrepreneur (or recipient) transported the goods into the Drittland; services linked to exported goods (e.g., logistics) are zero-rated under §4 Nr. 3 UStG only if directly tied to the physical export.

  • Electronic Customs Export Declaration (ATLAS export declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or airway bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (foreign exchange proof; Zahlungseingangsbestätigung)
  • Export-commercial invoice with Incoterms and delivery terms
  • Proof of transport (e.g., CMR / B/L / air waybill) dated to the export
  • Supplier attestation or end-user certificate in applicable controlled goods cases

From January 2025, Germany mandates E-Invoicing (XRechnung/ZUGFeRD format) for domestic B2B supplies; issuers must transmit invoices electronically through a certified platform. From 2027, a mandatory real-time electronic reporting (via E-Invoice under the ELSTER/SePA pilot) will apply, replacing control verification (Belegabruf). No general fiscal stamping or real-time fiscal clearance for standard VAT; transitional periods apply until 2026. APIs are required for incoming e-invoices; the GoBD (Grundsätze zur ordnungsmäßigen Führung und Aufbewahrung von Büchern) mandates digital retention.

Fraudulent zero-rating (behauptete Ausfuhr) triggers VAT reassessment plus interest (6% per annum on the amount), late-payment penalties (Säumniszuschlag of 1% per month), and fines under §370, §378 of the German Fiscal Code (AO). Deliberate evasion implicates criminal liability (including imprisonment up to 5 years). Retroactive clawback may extend up to 10 years for fraudulent acts, 4 years for gross negligence, or 1 year for standard errors. Additionally, the Finanzamt may deny input VAT deduction and disallow the export because the 180-day inward remittance and documentation chain (ATLAS plus bank confirmation) is mandatory evidence.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany operates under a free capital account convertibility regime within the Eurozone. Current account transactions are fully convertible. Profit remittances to non-resident shareholders face no convertibility restrictions but require tax reporting under AbgÄG, beneficial ownership disclosure, and compliance with FATCA/CRS. Hurdles include source-of-funds verification and potential CFC attribution for non-resident owners.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory mandatory surrender of export proceeds. Receipts may be retained in foreign currency or converted at spot rates. Deutsche Bundesbank collects statistical data via Balance of Payments reporting for exports above thresholds. Intra-EU supplies follow reverse-charge VAT without FX surrender.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Corporate cross-border EMI services are restricted; traditional banks are mandatory for full banking licenses. Fintech platforms such as N26 Business or Trade Republic provide limited corporate accounts but lack comprehensive FX clearing and trade settlement capabilities.
