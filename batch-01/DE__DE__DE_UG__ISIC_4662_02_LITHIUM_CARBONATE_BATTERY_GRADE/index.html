Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

A UG may be formed with nominal share capital of at least EUR 1. The subscribed cash capital must be paid in full before registration; contributions in kind are not permitted at formation. A separate statutory escrow account is not required, but the capital must be placed at the company's free disposal, normally through a corporate bank account. The UG must allocate 25% of annual surplus, reduced by carried-forward losses, to a statutory reserve until the capital reaches EUR 25,000.

Funding & unlock path: For an office-only trading operation, no general industrial clearance is required beyond building-use and workplace compliance. A warehouse or handling site must satisfy local building authority requirements, fire-protection and evacuation rules, occupational health and safety requirements under the Arbeitsschutzgesetz, hazardous-substance requirements under the Gefahrstoffverordnung, and, where applicable, fire-service inspection, structural load verification, explosion-protection assessment, storage compatibility review, and workplace risk assessment.

Paid-in capital rule: A UG may be formed with nominal share capital of at least EUR 1. The subscribed cash capital must be paid in full before registration; contributions in kind are not permitted at formation. A separate statutory escrow account is not required, but the capital must be placed at the company's free disposal, normally through a corporate bank account. The UG must allocate 25% of annual surplus, reduced by carried-forward losses, to a statutory reserve until the capital reaches EUR 25,000.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Finanzamt (local district tax office) with jurisdiction over the UG's statutory seat/place of management; Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, BZSt) for the issuance of VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.) and for VIES/ECS data exchange; Federal Customs Service (Generalzolldirektion) for EORI and ATLAS customs clearance; mandatory statutory e-filing portal: ELSTER (www.elster.de).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: notarial incorporation deed (Musterprotokoll) appointing the managing director; Step 2: filing the UG for entry in the Commercial Register (Handelsregister) at the local Amtsgericht; Step 3: Register Court notifies the competent Finanzamt ex officio; Step 4: the Finanzamt transmits the tax registration questionnaire (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung), which the UG must complete and file via ELSTER within one month of commencing business; this questionnaire is the single application for the Steuernummer (corporate income tax, trade tax, VAT); Step 5: the Finanzamt assigns the Steuernummer, typically within 4-6 weeks; Step 6: BZSt issues the DE-prefixed 9-digit USt-IdNr. within approximately 2-4 weeks of notification from the Finanzamt; Step 7: the UG applies to the Generalzolldirektion for a customs EORI number, mandatory for export declarations in ATLAS; Step 8: the UG activates an ELSTER certificate (software/hardware-based) for monthly VAT pre-registrations and, where relevant, EC Sales Lists; annual corporation tax, trade tax, VAT returns and AWV export payment records (Bundesbank statistics, threshold EUR 12,500) are likewise handled through the same infrastructure.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation - the UG (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschränkt) is a resident German capital company with unlimited tax liability on worldwide income based on statutory seat and/or place of management; subject to corporation tax (Körperschaftsteuer) at 15%, solidarity surcharge at 0.825% (5.5% of the corporation tax), municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) at an effective rate of approximately 9.5%-17% depending on the municipal assessment rate (Hebesatz), and VAT; the UG is not a pass-through vehicle; profits are taxed at entity level and shareholder distributions attract German dividend withholding tax at 26.375% (25% plus solidarity surcharge) subject to treaty relief or the domestic participation exemption. — Step 1: notarial incorporation deed (Musterprotokoll) appointing the managing director; Step 2: filing the UG for entry in the Commercial Register (Handelsregister) at the local Amtsgericht; Step 3: Register Court notifies the competent Finanzamt ex officio; Step 4: the Finanzamt transmits the tax registration questionnaire (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung), which the UG must complete and file via ELSTER within one month of commencing business; this questionnaire is the single application for the Steuernummer (corporate income tax, trade tax, VAT); Step 5: the Finanzamt assigns the Steuernummer, typically within 4-6 weeks; Step 6: BZSt issues the DE-prefixed 9-digit USt-IdNr. within approximately 2-4 weeks of notification from the Finanzamt; Step 7: the UG applies to the Generalzolldirektion for a customs EORI number, mandatory for export declarations in ATLAS; Step 8: the UG activates an ELSTER certificate (software/hardware-based) for monthly VAT pre-registrations and, where relevant, EC Sales Lists; annual corporation tax, trade tax, VAT returns and AWV export payment records (Bundesbank statistics, threshold EUR 12,500) are likewise handled through the same infrastructure.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the UG (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschränkt) is a resident German capital company with unlimited tax liability on worldwide income based on statutory seat and/or place of management; subject to corporation tax (Körperschaftsteuer) at 15%, solidarity surcharge at 0.825% (5.5% of the corporation tax), municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) at an effective rate of approximately 9.5%-17% depending on the municipal assessment rate (Hebesatz), and VAT; the UG is not a pass-through vehicle; profits are taxed at entity level and shareholder distributions attract German dividend withholding tax at 26.375% (25% plus solidarity surcharge) subject to treaty relief or the domestic participation exemption.

Available election forms

Germany has no US-style check-the-box election. Available statutory elections/instruments: (1) Organschaft (fiscal unity) and VAT fiscal unity under §§ 14 KStG and 2 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UStG, available where a parent company holds at least 75% of the UG and an executed profit-and-loss pooling agreement (Ergebnisabführungsvertrag) is registered in the Commercial Register; (2) small-enterprise VAT exemption option under § 19 UStG for annual turnover up to EUR 25,000, though commercially inadvisable for a cross-border procurement UG that must recover input VAT; (3) trade-tax group election under § 2 Abs. 2 GewStG; (4) tax-neutral reorganisation elections under the Reorganisation Tax Act (UmwStG); (5) no elective opt-out from ATAD anti-hybrid rules - §§ 4k and 4j EStG apply mandatorily to hybrid and reverse-hybrid mismatch arrangements; (6) fiscal-year election constrained, as German corporations must generally use the calendar tax year absent compelling commercial necessity under §§ 4a EStG, 8b KStG.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

General small-business registration exemption: EUR 25,000 annual turnover under § 19 UStG, effective 1 January 2025 (previously EUR 22,000); however, the UG engaged in active cross-border wholesale trade is VAT-registered from its first taxable supply and cannot rely on the small-business exemption where input VAT recovery is essential; intra-Community acquisition threshold for non-taxable entities: EUR 12,500 under § 1a Abs. 1 UStG, not applicable to an already VAT-registered UG; distance-sales/OSS registration trigger: EUR 10,000 Union-wide remote-sales threshold for EU B2C e-commerce; import VAT (Einfuhrumsatzsteuer) arises at customs clearance on each importation of lithium carbonate at 19%, with a VAT deferral procedure available for established importers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is achieved under the German statutory export-exemption model: (i) export supplies (Ausfuhrlieferung) under § 4 Nr. 1 lit. a and § 6 UStG of lithium carbonate powder (HS 2836.91) purchasers outside the EU are exempt with input VAT credit where the goods are physically dispatched by the UG or on its behalf to a third-country destination and documentary proof is furnished within the applicable filing period; (ii) intra-EU dispatches to a VAT-registered EU customer are exempt under § 6a UStG where both VAT IDs are validated in VIES, the goods reach the other Member State, and a Gelangensbestätigung (arrival confirmation) is obtained; (iii) supplies for the provisioning of seagoing vessels, aircraft, and offshore installations may qualify under § 8 UStG; (iv) ancillary services directly connected to exported goods (transport, loading, freight handling) are exempt under § 6 Abs. 1a UStG; (v) offshore services rendered to non-EU principals generally qualify for exemption when the recipient is established outside the EU; the UG bears the burden of proving all negative conditions of exemption.

  • Customs export declaration (Ausfuhranmeldung) filed in the ATLAS system with Movement Reference Number (MRN), together with the electronic export certificate or customs-stamped paper copy
  • Commercial invoice in English/German describing battery-grade lithium carbonate (HS 2836.91), net/gross weights, Incoterms rule, unit price and total value, with an express VAT export exemption statement and EORI/VAT ID details
  • Packing list, and where the letter of credit prescribes, assay or quality certificate evidencing battery-grade purity and lot traceability
  • Transport document proving departure from EU customs territory: Bill of Lading (ocean), Air Waybill, or CMR consignment note naming the third-country destination
  • Proof of export compilation (Buch- und Belegnachweis) under §§ 8-10 UStDV, comprising customs certifications and, for intra-EU movements, the Gelangensbestätigung or equivalent evidence under § 17a UStDV
  • Inward remittance certificate or SWIFT MT103/bank credit advice evidencing receipt of export sales proceeds into a German bank account, required for AWV statistical reporting and audit verification
  • Underlying sales contract and Incoterms annexes documenting risk, title, and delivery point, with insurance certificates where applicable

Mandatory domestic B2B e-invoicing (E-Rechnung) phased in by the Growth Opportunities Act: from 1 January 2025 every German entrepreneur must be able to receive EN 16931-compliant structured e-invoices (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD 2.0.x, or EDI such as Peppol BIS 3.0); issuance obligation commences 1 January 2027 for businesses with prior-year turnover above EUR 800,000 and extends to all businesses from 1 January 2028; Germany operates no real-time fiscal clearance, API-based continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping regime; compliance is enforced through periodic VAT pre-registrations filed via ELSTER (mandatory monthly for a newly founded UG during the first two operating years, thereafter monthly where prior-year net VAT liability exceeds EUR 7,500, otherwise quarterly), annual VAT returns, and recapitulative statements (EC Sales Lists) to BZSt for intra-EU supplies; e-invoices and underlying export documents must be retained unaltered for 10 years under § 147 AO; intra-EU supplies additionally require VIES validation of the customer's VAT identification number.

The Finanzamt applies strict documentary scrutiny to export evidence in periodic VAT and cross-border merchandise audits; missing or defective documentation triggers retrospective VAT assessment at 19% on the export sales value, plus interest at 6% per annum under § 233a AO, late-payment surcharges under § 240 AO, disallowance of related input VAT credits, and denial of exemption without post-deadline cure; fraudulent or sham export arrangements (fictitious consignees, circular goods flows, missing-trader/carousel involvement) constitute criminal tax evasion under § 370 AO, punishable by imprisonment of up to 10 years, fines, and a 10-year retrospective assessment window under § 169 AO; administrative fines of up to EUR 5,000 per violation may be imposed under § 26a UStG for documentary non-compliance; unscrupulous operators face joint-and-several liability under § 25d UStG and additional foreign-trade administrative sanctions by the Federal Customs Service and BZSt; export refund claims are cross-matched against FX inbound flows, ATLAS cargo records, and underlying trade finance documentation, and discrepancies may additionally lead to AWV enforcement proceedings.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility for both current-account and capital-account transactions. There is no German foreign-exchange control, prior approval requirement, or inward investment screening that impedes the UG's battery-materials arbitrage model. Dividend and profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is permitted without central-bank authorisation, but is subject to German corporate-law distributable-profit and solvency rules, a statutory dividend withholding tax of 26.375% where no treaty or EU Parent-Subsidiary relief applies, and Deutsche Bundesbank statistical reporting under the Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung - AWV).

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, repatriation deadline, or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export receipts. Exporters may keep proceeds in foreign currency, convert them freely, or use them for trade disbursements. The only material compliance obligation is statistical: each cross-border receipt or payment with a non-resident counterparty exceeding EUR 12,500 or the equivalent must be reported to Deutsche Bundesbank under the AWV balance-of-payments reporting framework.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (Germany's central bank and balance-of-payments/AWV statistics authority); Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht (BaFin) supervises banking and anti-money-laundering compliance. Germany has no separate foreign-exchange administration, as the eurozone and EU law guarantee free movement of capital and payments.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM as a primary treasury rail; MEDIUM as an ancillary collection or disbursement account. German-licensed or EEA-passported digital EMI platforms can offer euro IBANs and SEPA/SWIFT payment initiation, but they are not credit institutions under the German Banking Act (KWG) and cannot issue commercial Letters of Credit, confirm trade finance instruments, hold interest-bearing deposits, or execute wholesale FX hedging products. Many EMI virtual-account models also create legal uncertainty as to segregation of client funds and may reject high-value battery-materials trade settlements without enhanced KYC documentation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route inbound equity injections via SWIFT MT103 from an account held in the name of the shareholder. The payment reference must identify the UG, the Commercial Register number, and the purpose as 'capital contribution / Kapitalerhöhung'; where an ISO 20022 purpose code is required, use the bank-defined equity/capital-injection code and never a commodity-import/trade code. The Deutsche Bundesbank AWV filing must classify the flow as a capital contribution, not a trade payment. The UG must retain the notarised shareholder resolution or contribution agreement, source-of-funds documentation, remitter's bank reference, and confirmation that no third-party payments, netting, or cash pooling were used. If the contribution occurs before the UG is fully registered, it must be placed in a dedicated capital escrow/deposit account of the UG and released only after all legal prerequisites are satisfied.Use SWIFT MT103 via gpi for direct supplier wires, commercial Letters of Credit under UCP600 via SWIFT MT700/MT707 for shipment-backed settlements, and SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant for euro payments to EU suppliers. For large same-day euro liquidity movements, TARGET2/T2 RTGS settlement is available. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM as a primary treasury rail; MEDIUM as an ancillary collection or disbursement account.
