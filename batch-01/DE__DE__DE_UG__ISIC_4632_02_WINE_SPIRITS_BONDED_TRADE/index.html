Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

UG statutory minimum share capital is €1.00 per § 5a(1) GmbHG; each cash contribution must be paid in full and be freely available to the managing director before filing. At least 25% of annual net profits must be retained in a statutory reserve until the subscribed capital reaches €25,000. Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: the facility must comply with the Model Industrial Building Directive (Muster-Industriebaurichtlinie); spirits storage areas with fire load densities above 1,200 MJ/m² require an automated sprinkler installation, smoke and heat exhaust ventilation, and a pre-acceptance inspection by the municipal Brandschutzdienststelle (Fire Protection Inspectorate). Occupational clearance: a structural load certificate (standsicherheitliche Prüfung) for pallet racking, explosion protection zoning under ATEX 2014/34/EU for filling and sample-draw areas, mandatory safety instructions under the German Occupational Health and Safety Act (ArbSchG), and a documented workplace risk assessment covering manual handling, forklift traffic and confined-space entry into bonded cellars.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Finanzamt (local tax office) under the Bundesministerium der Finanzen (Federal Ministry of Finance) for corporate income and trade tax; Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt) for VAT ID (USt-IdNr.); Hauptzollamt / Generalzolldirektion for customs and excise supervision; electronic filing and correspondence portal: ELSTER (https://www.elster.de).

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned UG: (1) file Articles of Association and register the UG in the Handelsregister (Commercial Register) via a notary; (2) within 4 weeks of incorporation, file the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung (tax registration questionnaire) with the locally competent Finanzamt, including shareholders, management, and economic activity data; (3) obtain the corporate income tax / trade tax number (Steuernummer) from the Finanzamt; (4) obtain the VAT ID (USt-IdNr.) from BZSt on the basis of the questionnaire, which BZSt issues automatically or on application; (5) complete Gewerbeanmeldung (business registration) at the local Gewerbeamt which is forwarded to the tax authority; (6) if operating a bonded wine/spirits warehouse, file separately with the Hauptzollamt for customs warehouse authorization and excise suspension approval under the Branntweinsteuer / Schaumweinsteuer legal framework; (7) for non-resident shareholders, provide an Ansässigkeitsbescheinigung (certificate of tax residence) to support treaty relief and avoid withholding tax obstacles.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; resident UG (seat or place of management in Germany) is subject to unlimited corporate income tax liability (Körperschaftsteuer) at 15% plus solidarity surcharge 0.825% and Gewerbesteuer (trade tax) at an effective rate of approximately 14-17%; profits are taxed at the entity level and distributions to shareholders are subject to 25% withholding tax (Abgeltungsteuer) or partial-income method if applicable; no pass-through election available for a UG; Territorial_Scope: worldwide income with foreign tax credit / treaty relief; Tax_Exempt_Zone: not applicable in Germany. — For a foreign-owned UG: (1) file Articles of Association and register the UG in the Handelsregister (Commercial Register) via a notary; (2) within 4 weeks of incorporation, file the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung (tax registration questionnaire) with the locally competent Finanzamt, including shareholders, management, and economic activity data; (3) obtain the corporate income tax / trade tax number (Steuernummer) from the Finanzamt; (4) obtain the VAT ID (USt-IdNr.) from BZSt on the basis of the questionnaire, which BZSt issues automatically or on application; (5) complete Gewerbeanmeldung (business registration) at the local Gewerbeamt which is forwarded to the tax authority; (6) if operating a bonded wine/spirits warehouse, file separately with the Hauptzollamt for customs warehouse authorization and excise suspension approval under the Branntweinsteuer / Schaumweinsteuer legal framework; (7) for non-resident shareholders, provide an Ansässigkeitsbescheinigung (certificate of tax residence) to support treaty relief and avoid withholding tax obstacles.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; resident UG (seat or place of management in Germany) is subject to unlimited corporate income tax liability (Körperschaftsteuer) at 15% plus solidarity surcharge 0.825% and Gewerbesteuer (trade tax) at an effective rate of approximately 14-17%; profits are taxed at the entity level and distributions to shareholders are subject to 25% withholding tax (Abgeltungsteuer) or partial-income method if applicable; no pass-through election available for a UG; Territorial_Scope: worldwide income with foreign tax credit / treaty relief; Tax_Exempt_Zone: not applicable in Germany.

Available election forms

Germany has no check-the-box regime; the UG is mandatorily treated as a corporation. Relevant elections and options: (1) Organschaft (fiscal unity) under Sec. 14 KStG and Sec. 2 (2) No. 2 UStG, allowing profit and loss pooling with an integrated German parent and VAT consolidation; (2) option to tax real estate under Sec. 9 UStG to waive VAT exemption; (3) application of the small-entrepreneur rules under Sec. 19 UStG (turnover below €22,000 in the prior year and below €50,000 in the current year) which are practically unavailable for bonded wholesale; (4) VAT reverse-charge treatment for specified inbound services under Sec. 13b UStG; (5) transfer pricing and documentation elections under Sec. 90 AO; (6) hybrid mismatch adjustments under Sec. 4k EStG which leave no elective benefit for a purely domestic UG.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Domestic small entrepreneurs: €22,000 turnover in the previous year and €50,000 expected in the current year (Sec. 19 UStG); a UG is a legal person and is generally taxable as an entrepreneur regardless of turnover; foreign entities without a German seat or permanent establishment are liable to register from the first taxable transaction in Germany with no de minimis threshold; wine and spirits wholesale supplies are always subject to mandatory registration and the standard 19% rate (reduced 7% rate does not apply to alcoholic beverages).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods to destinations outside the EU are zero-rated under Sec. 6 UStG, including goods removed from a customs bonded warehouse, provided the exporter proves that the goods physically left Germany/EU within the statutory evidence period (generally the goods must be moved to the third-country destination and documented with the electronic ATLAS export declaration, Ausfuhr- und Abfertigungsbeleg); intra-EU dispatches to customers holding a valid VAT ID are exempt under Sec. 6a UStG subject to delivery proof and EU Sales List reporting; wine and spirits exports also qualify for excise duty suspension under the Branntweinsteuer, Biersteuer, and Schaumweinsteuer regimes; ancillary services such as freight, handling and warehousing relating to exported goods can fall under the zero-rating of Sec. 4 No. 6 UStG; the bonded warehouse operator must maintain full records of importation, storage, transfer, and export of excise goods to support the 0% treatment and duty suspension.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Elektronische Ausfuhranmeldung via ATLAS, evidenced by the Ausfuhr- und Abfertigungsbeleg / Export Accompanying Document reference)
  • Bill of Lading or CMR consignment note evidencing physical removal of goods from Germany and the EU
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List in a language acceptable to the Finanzamt
  • Proof of payment / Inward Remittance Certificate (Zahlungsbeleg / Devisenbeleg) satisfying foreign exchange control and audit requirements under the Außenwirtschaftsverordnung (AWV)
  • For intra-EU movements: purchaser's VAT ID, a combined delivery note and the EU Sales List (Zusammenfassende Meldung) entry under Sec. 6a UStG

Mandatory B2B e-invoicing (E-Rechnung) is being phased in: from 1 January 2025, all domestic German B2B entrepreneurs must be able to receive structured electronic invoices in XRechnung or ZUGFeRD XML formats; from 1 January 2027, e-invoice issuance becomes mandatory for all domestic B2B transactions (an exemption applies to invoices below €800 and to certain exempt transactions); Germany does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance, pre-clearance API, or digital stamping system — invoices are not approved transaction-by-transaction by the tax authority; taxpayers must retain machine-readable audit files (GoBD compliance) and submit periodic VAT returns via ELSTER; export and excise movements are cleared through the ATLAS customs system (Automated Tariff and Local Customs Clearance System) and EMCS for excise goods.

False export zero-rating or fabricated documentation triggers: (1) retrospective VAT assessment (clawback) of the gross proceeds at 19% plus interest under Sec. 233a AO (0.5% per month); (2) late-payment surcharges under Sec. 240 AO; (3) criminal tax evasion penalties under Sec. 370 AO — imprisonment of up to 5 years or a monetary fine for willful evasion, with increased penalties for tax evasion in the form of smuggling; (4) personal liability of UG managing directors under Sec. 34 and 69 AO where obligations are not met; (5) immediate revocation of the bonded warehouse authorization and excise duty assessment on the goods under the Branntweinsteuer law; (6) customs debt creation under Articles 79 and 84 UCC plus penalty proceedings before the Hauptzollamt; (7) enhanced audit scrutiny and a rebuttable presumption of non-export if the mandatory proof-of-removal documentation is not produced within the statutory evidence windows.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany, as an EU member state, fully liberalised the movement of capital and payments under Article 63 TFEU. There are no exchange controls on profit remittance, dividend distribution, capital injections, loan repayments, trade settlements, or liquidation proceeds. The euro is fully convertible without any notarisation or ministerial approval. The sole legal duty is statistical reporting to Deutsche Bundesbank pursuant to the Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (AWV). The UG may transfer funds abroad without quantitative or qualitative restrictions, but must be able to substantiate the underlying commercial purpose and preserve documentation for customs and excise audits.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory obligation to surrender export proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio exists under German law. Export proceeds may be retained in foreign currency, converted at any commercially available rate, or offset against import invoices. However, payment receipts of EUR 12,500 or more from cross-border transactions are subject to monthly AWV reporting. The exporting UG, or the bank acting on its behalf, must report the transaction to the Deutsche Bundesbank within 7 days of receipt for each goods trade. Special screening under the German Export Control Act (Außenwirtschaftsgesetz) remains applicable for dual-use or strategic goods, but bonded wine/spirits trade is outside the sensitive-goods list.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (competent authority for German foreign exchange statistics and cross-border reporting under AWV; BaFin supervises banking and payment services licensing)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium to High with legally defined constraints. German-regulated EMI platforms (Penta, Qonto, Aion Bank, Solarisbank-powered neobanks) can legally hold a corporate operational IBAN and execute SEPA and SWIFT transactions under a BaFin e-money licence. However, for bonded wine/spirits wholesale trade, these EMIs do not offer dedicated excise-duty suspense ledger accounts, transport-warehouse customs bonds, or Permission for Bonded Warehouse Transfers. They cannot substitute a tier-1 bank for the required customs guarantee (Carnet/CFSP) nor for the reporting of AWV cross-border receipts. Thus the compliant structure is a primary tier-1 bank account for trade and escrow rails, with a supplementary EMI account for operating expenses, payroll, and minor overheads.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Bonded warehouse / excise license legitimacy

Root cause: Bonded warehouse / excise license legitimacy

Defense evidence: Provide the official German customs registration number (EORI), valid excise warehouse approval pursuant to § 1 Branntweinmonopolgesetz or § 4 of the German Spirits Act (if applicable), proof of ownership of the bonded warehouse (lease agreement or copy of warehouse authorisation), and the customs movement reference number for the first import shipment. Include an independent valuation certificate of the stock and an IHK (Chamber of Commerce) registration, plus a sworn oath of the managing director confirming that all duty-suspension facilities are correctly used.
Beneficial ownership opacity

Root cause: Beneficial ownership opacity

Defense evidence: Current extract from the German Transparency Register (Transparenzregister) for the UG showing all UBOs above 25% (natural persons only), plus an organisational chart charting each intermediate entity. Each UBO must provide a certified passport copy, proof of residence, and a short curriculum vitae detailing the business history. Where a UBO is itself a company, provide consolidated group structure documents and an explanation of the source of wealth for the capital injection.
Unusually large cross-border payments for spirits on the SEPA/SWIFT rails

Root cause: Unusually large cross-border payments for spirits on the SEPA/SWIFT rails

Defense evidence: Full underlying commercial contracts (purchase orders, pro-forma invoice, final invoice), the customs bar-coded declaration, bill of lading / air waybill with matching HS codes (22.04 for wine, 22.08 for spirits), and the warehouse movement document LCB/NADOC from the German customs system. Maintain a complete audit trail confirming the physical movement from the bonded warehouse to the buyer. Provide the unique assets and stock list with date of receipt. If payments exceed EUR 50,000, also present the internal transfer pricing policy or a fair market valuation from a certified industry expert.
Trade to high-risk countries (sanctions exposure, proceeds laundering)

Root cause: Trade to high-risk countries (sanctions exposure, proceeds laundering)

Defense evidence: Pre-clearance screening of the counterparty against German, EU, UN, and OFAC sanctions lists plus a signed declaration that the buyer is not affiliated with any restricted entity. Include the commercial terms, stated end-use, and destination customs regime. For exports to non-EU countries, provide the export declaration (Ausfuhranmeldung) and, where applicable, an import certificate from the destination country. For each shipment, maintain a copy of the SWIFT or CIPS/SEPA payment message showing the debit and credit banks. If payable via a letter of credit, provide the full MT700 and MT720 presentations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire transfer protocol: the non-resident shareholder initiates an SWIFT MT103 from the sender's foreign bank to the UG's German bank account. The wire must include the German reference 'Stammeinlage' or 'Kapitalgesellschafts Darlehen' if it is a subordinated shareholder loan. For amounts exceeding EUR 12,500, the receiving German bank will automatically submit the AWV report to the Bundesbank in the appropriate balance-of-payments code (direct investment / intercompany loan). The UG must document the injection via shareholder resolution, a cash contribution confirmation under §§ 5 and 8 of the German Limited Liability Companies Act, and a current securities account statement. For equity increases, the commercial register filing must show the updated share capital prior to wire receipt. No prior governmental approval is required, but if the contribution includes the assumption of an equity premium above the stated share capital, the excess must be booked in the capital reserve. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium to High with legally defined constraints. German-regulated EMI platforms (Penta, Qonto, Aion Bank, Solarisbank-powered neobanks) can legally hold a corporate operational IBAN and execute SEPA and SWIFT transactions under a BaFin e-money licence. However, for bonded wine/spirits wholesale trade, these EMIs do not offer dedicated excise-duty suspense ledger accounts, transport-warehouse customs bonds, or Permission for Bonded Warehouse Transfers. They cannot substitute a tier-1 bank for the required customs guarantee (Carnet/CFSP) nor for the reporting of AWV cross-border receipts. Thus the compliant structure is a primary tier-1 bank account for trade and escrow rails, with a supplementary EMI account for operating expenses, payroll, and minor overheads.
