Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The UG requires stated nominal capital between EUR 1.00 and EUR 24,999 with at least one fully paid-in issued share; contributions in cash must actually be paid before filing and remain at the free disposal of the managing director. No court-managed escrow account is statutorily mandatory, but payment is evidenced by a German bank statement or, alternatively, by a notary's escrow (Anderkonto) or court deposit (Hinterlegung) confirmation.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification in accordance with the Model Administrative Regulation on Technical Building Requirements (Muster-VV TB) and local fire brigade acceptance; structural load and stability verification (Standsicherheitsnachweis) for seawalls and breakwaters by a certified structural engineer (Prüfingenieur); occupational health clearance by the State Factory Inspectorate (Gewerbeaufsichtsamt) based on a workplace risk assessment (Gefährdungsbeurteilung) under the Occupational Health and Safety Act (ArbSchG) and the Workplace Ordinance (ArbStättV); statutory accident insurance coverage with BG Bau for onshore works and BG Verkehr for marine crew operations, including seafarer fitness examinations (G25/G37) and site-safety coordination plans (SiGe-Plan).

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt) for VAT identification numbers (USt-IdNr.) and central tax certification; local Finanzamt (tax office) for Steuernummer (domestic tax number) administration; e-tax portal ELSTER (www.elster.de) for filings and registration.

E-government filing portal

Non-resident German UG: file the electronic 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) with the competent local Finanzamt upon acquiring a permanent establishment, creating an establishment for employer-type obligations, or making VAT-taxable supplies in Germany. The Finanzamt assigns a domestic Steuernummer; for cross-border supplies, the UG must additionally apply to BZSt for an USt-IdNr. under the EU VAT Identification procedure. All applications are processed through ELSTER or official paper forms; no prior foreign registration is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

German law does not provide US-style check-the-box elections. The UG, as a capital company, is exclusively entity-level taxed under Körperschaftsteuer and Gewerbesteuer. Available elections: Organschaft (tax and VAT fiscal unity) may be elected where a controlling entity holds at least 95% of voting rights and the relationship substantiated for at least five years; VAT Organschaft election under UStG §2(2) (unified entrepreneur). Hybrid mismatch elections are not permitted; anti-hybrid rules per ATAD apply mandatorily.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general economic turnover threshold for registration; any taxable entrepreneurial activity in Germany requires VAT registration and the Steuernummer acknowledgement. Small business (Kleinunternehmer) exemption applies only to domestic entrepreneurs with prior-year turnover not exceeding EUR 22,000 (2023–2024) or EUR 25,000 (2025 onward) and current-year maximum EUR 50,000 (currently adjusted); foreign contractors and any entity performing imported services are not automatically exempt and must register.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of goods: zero-rating applies if the physical dispatch from Germany to a third country (outside EU) is proven by an accepted customs export declaration and transport evidence; to claim 0%, the supplier must hold export documentation within the statutory retention period and transmit export taxes correctly. Dredging and seawall construction works performed on immovable property located in Germany are treated as supplies connected with German immovable property and are standard-rated at 19%; services performed on immovable property outside Germany fall outside German VAT scope or are zero-rated subject to foreign place-of-supply rules. Offshore services to recipients with place of residence outside the EU may be zero-rated where the service is not connected to German real estate and the place-of-supply B2B rules (reverse charge or outside-Germany location) are evidenced.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Ausfuhrzollanmeldung via ATLAS/EZT)
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent transport document proving EU exit
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or equivalent bank/immigration proof of foreign-currency repatriation

No real-time clearance or digital stamping regime in Germany. Since 1 January 2025, all domestic B2B suppliers must be able to receive e-invoices in structured XML format (EN 16931 semantics, XRechnung or ZUGFeRD); issue mandates phase in from 1 January 2027 for businesses with >EUR 800,000 annual turnover and from 1 January 2028 for all businesses. Invoices must be transmitted via interoperable APIs or business systems; no centralized government approval or clearance stamp is required.

Export zero-rating is strictly audited by the customs administration and local Finanzamt. Absence of substantiating export documents triggers an immediate VAT reassessment at 19%, monthly default interest of 0.5% per month, and suspension of input VAT credits offset. Where evidence is fabricated or fictitious, German tax criminal law applies under Abgabenordnung §§370, 378: negligent evasion carries fines up to EUR 25,000; intentional evasion carries imprisonment up to five years, increased to up to ten years in severe cases. Additionally, the EU anti-fraud regulation (EU Reg. 904/2010 Art. 47) allows retrospective clawback of uncorrected VAT credits and a 10% administrative penalty on the unpaid VAT amount.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany is a fully convertible current and capital account jurisdiction under EU law, the EU Capital Movements Directive, and the German AWV. There are no exchange controls, surrender requirements, or profit remittance restrictions for EUR and freely convertible currencies. Dividends, royalties, interest, and service remittances may be made without Deutsche Bundesbank approval. Any cross-border payment to or from non-residents exceeding EUR 12,500 or equivalent must be reported to Deutsche Bundesbank under the external trade and payments statistics reporting framework; this is a statistical/balance-of-payments compliance requirement, not a convertibility restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion of export proceeds is imposed by Germany or the European Central Bank. Exporters may freely hold foreign currency in resident accounts. The sole compliance requirement is the AWV payment reporting obligation: export receipts from non-residents of EUR 12,500 or more must be reported to Deutsche Bundesbank no later than the seventh day of the month following the month in which the payment was received. For maritime dredging EPC milestone proceeds, the correct service/goods code must be used to align with the port authority underlying contract.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (central bank) and Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin) for banking and financial market conduct; Deutsche Bundesbank administers cross-border payments statistics and foreign trade reporting under the Außenwirtschaftsverordnung (AWV).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. German-registered or EU-pasported e-money institutions supervised by BaFin may legally open payment accounts and offer ancillary FX services, but they are not credit institutions under the German Banking Act (KWG). EMIs cannot provide fully regulated escrow or blocked-capital accounts, cannot issue commercial letters of credit, and are commonly rejected by public-sector port authorities as treasury counterparties. Core dredging EPC milestone collections and LC disbursements should be handled through a full-license bank; EMIs are acceptable only for auxiliary payroll or subcontracted service payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing for capital injection into the DE_UG is a SWIFT MT103 from the foreign corporate shareholder account to the German receiving bank using the UG's IBAN and the bank's BIC. The payment reference should identify the purpose as a capital contribution ('Kapitaleinzahlung') or shareholder loan. No Deutsche Bundesbank approval is required; however, if the transfer is EUR 12,500 or more, the UG must ensure that the inbound payment report is filed with Deutsche Bundesbank under AWV by the seventh day of the following month.
