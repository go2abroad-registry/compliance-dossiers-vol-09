Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The UG may be formed with a minimum share capital of EUR 1.00, fully paid up in cash and freely at the disposal of the managing director; no escrow account is mandated for cash contributions, but contributions in kind require a reasoned formation report; by law the UG must retain 25% of annual surplus as a statutory reserve until the share capital reaches EUR 25,000, at which time it may convert to a GmbH.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate per the Model Building Regulation (MBO) and state building codes, including proof of escape routes, fire compartments, and hydrant coverage; structural load verification for machine foundations and warehousing by a certified structural engineer; workplace clearance per the Workplaces Ordinance (Arbeitsstättenverordnung) including lighting, ventilation, and emergency exits; occupational safety assessment per DGUV regulations and the Industrial Safety Ordinance (BetrSichV), with periodic inspection of forklifts, compressed-air vessels, and lifting equipment by an accredited inspection body (TÜV or DEKRA); explosion protection documentation if a solvent-degreasing plant is installed.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern / BZSt) for VAT identification numbers and cross-border tax affairs; local tax office (Finanzamt) for corporate income tax, trade tax, VAT and payroll tax administration; electronic filing and communication via the ELSTER portal.

E-government filing portal

After notarisation and entry of the UG in the commercial register (Handelsregister), the managing director must file the tax registration questionnaire (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung) with the competent Finanzamt, either in paper form or via ELSTER. The Finanzamt issues the Steuernummer (statutory tax number) for income and VAT purposes. The BZSt issues the Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer (USt-IdNr.) upon application for intra-Community supplies and export transactions. A tax representative or appointed agent may be necessary where the UG is newly established or has no German residence.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation: the UG is a capital company (Kapitalgesellschaft) subject to corporate income tax (Körperschaftsteuer) at 15.0% plus solidarity surcharge (5.5% of the CIT due) and trade tax (Gewerbesteuer, rate varying by municipality). There is no pass-through entitlement: profits and losses are recognised and taxed at the entity level. — After notarisation and entry of the UG in the commercial register (Handelsregister), the managing director must file the tax registration questionnaire (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung) with the competent Finanzamt, either in paper form or via ELSTER. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the UG is a capital company (Kapitalgesellschaft) subject to corporate income tax (Körperschaftsteuer) at 15.0% plus solidarity surcharge (5.5% of the CIT due) and trade tax (Gewerbesteuer, rate varying by municipality). There is no pass-through entitlement: profits and losses are recognised and taxed at the entity level.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in German law; the UG is a per se corporation. Relevant elections and mechanisms include: Organschaft (corporate income tax and trade tax group consolidation under §§14–19 KStG); VAT fiscal unity (Organschaft for VAT group treatment under §2 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UStG); continuation of tax book values under the Reorganisation Tax Act (UmwStG) for restructurings; and mandatory hybrid-mismatch alignment under §4k EStG, which does not grant elective transparency.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary threshold for mandatory VAT registration: every entrepreneur (Unternehmer) conducting taxable supplies in Germany is obliged to register with the tax office. The small-business exemption (Kleinunternehmerregelung) applies to entrepreneurs with previous-year turnover ≤ EUR 22,000 and expected current-year turnover ≤ EUR 50,000, but is not relevant to an exporting UG manufacturing hydraulic valves.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods to third countries are zero-rated under §6 UStG where the supplier can prove that the goods were actually transported to a non-EU destination. Intra-EU supplies are exempt from VAT under §4 No. 1 letter b and §6a UStG where the purchaser is a VAT-registered business in another Member State and the goods are shipped or transported to that Member State. Services directly related to the export, such as logistics or processing on exported goods, may qualify under §8 UStG. The UG must maintain full documentary and accounting evidence showing the export chain, identity of recipient, and transport from Germany.

  • Electronic customs export declaration lodged via ATLAS, with the issued Movement Reference Number (MRN)
  • Ocean Bill of Lading, CMR international consignment note, or Air Waybill showing the German exporter as the dispatch party and the foreign destination
  • Commercial invoice and packing list referencing the export declaration and the underlying contract
  • Proof of customs registration or, for low-value dispatches, a customs-signed export confirmation
  • Inward remittance certificate, SWIFT MT103/field record, or bank statement evidencing receipt of the export sales proceeds in the UG’s German bank account
  • Intrastat declaration (if applicable) and the EC Sales List (Zusammenfassende Meldung) for intra-EU dispatches

Domestic B2B e-invoicing is being phased in. From 1 January 2025, German businesses must be able to receive structured electronic invoices (EN 16931 format, e.g., XRechnung or hybrid ZUGFeRD). From 1 January 2027, taxpayers with taxable turnover above EUR 800,000 must issue such e-invoices for domestic B2B transactions; the full obligation starts 1 January 2028. There is no real-time central fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or mandatory API clearance authority yet; transmitting via standard interoperable channels, the PEppOL network, e-mail, or a clearing portal is permitted. Future continuous transaction reporting to BZSt is expected pursuant to EU VIDA proposals.

German tax authorities subject export zero-rating to high evidentiary scrutiny. Missing, incomplete or fraudulent export documentation leads to VAT reassessment with output tax at 19% plus statutory default surcharge under §240 AO, late-payment interest under §233a AO at 0.15% per month, and, in severe cases, penalties up to EUR 500,000 or imprisonment up to 10 years for tax evasion pursuant to §370 AO. The BZSt and risk-management systems of the Finanzämter cross-check export declarations, VAT refund claims, and recipient data; falsified documents trigger retrospective clawbacks, disqualification from future zero-rating, and referral for criminal prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany operates a full-convertibility euro regime; both current-account and capital-account transactions are freely convertible under Article 63 TFEU and the Foreign Trade and Payments Act (AWG), with no exchange control licences, no profit-remittance permissions, and no restrictions on non-resident equity ownership or dividend repatriation; the only hard compliance duty is statistical reporting to Deutsche Bundesbank, namely payment reporting for cross-border payments exceeding EUR 50,000 (AWV §67) and reporting of outstanding cross-border receivables, payables and equity positions above the statutory AWV thresholds (currently up to EUR 5,000,000 and EUR 12,500,000 depending on the instrument); dividend and interest remittances trigger German withholding tax of 26.375 percent (including solidarity surcharge) subject to DTT relief and the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender, no mandatory conversion, and no statutory repatriation deadline for export proceeds in Germany; exporters may lawfully retain EUR or foreign-currency balances in resident and non-resident bank accounts; the binding obligations are purely informational and statistical: electronic customs export declarations via the ATLAS system, automatic AWV payment reporting by the receiving bank for receipts above EUR 50,000, Intrastat goods-statistics filings, and, for pressure-specification hydraulic valves with military-relevant characteristics, BAFA dual-use end-use/end-user documentation under EU Dual-Use Regulation 2021/821.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (Central Bank, Frankfurt am Main) as monetary authority and recipient of foreign trade/payment statistics; BaFin (Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht) as banking and EMI supervisor; Bundesministerium für Wirtschaft und Klimaschutz (BMWK) and BAFA as AWG/dual-use export control administrators.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High as an overlay treasury rail, Medium as the primary bank relationship. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity contributions are routed as a SWIFT MT103 from the non-resident shareholder's bank to the UG's German current account, with remittance field 70 referencing the notarial deed and 'Gesellschaftereinlage', and using ISO 20022 payment purpose code 'CAPT' (Capital); for shareholder loans the routing uses purpose code 'LOAN' or the bank-specific AWV code, with a fully executed loan agreement retained; amounts exceeding EUR 50,000 are automatically reported by the receiving bank to Deutsche Bundesbank under AWV §67, while the corresponding cross-border receivable/payable position is reported above the statutory AWV §69 thresholds; the credit is then matched against the UG's registered share capital in the Handelsregister and documented in the annual financial statements; no Bundesbank authorization is required due to full capital-account liberalization.Clearing rails are layered: (i) SWIFT MT103/202 wire transfers for advance payments, down payments, and open-account settlements;
