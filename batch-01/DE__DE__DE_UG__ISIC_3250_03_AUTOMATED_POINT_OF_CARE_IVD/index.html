Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt) / Federal Central Tax Office, together with the local Finanzamt (tax office); central digital portal: ELSTER (www.elster.de)

E-government filing portal

The UG must submit the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) via the ELSTER portal to the competent local Finanzamt. The Finanzamt issues the domestic tax number (Steuernummer). For cross-border and intra-Community transactions, the UG must separately apply to the BZSt for a VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.) using the official national application form. Foreign entities without a domestic seat may apply directly to the BZSt, generally after registration with the local Finanzamt or through a fiscal representative if legally required.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (UG is a German corporate body subject to corporation tax, trade tax, and solidarity surcharge; no pass-through treatment available) — The UG must submit the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) via the ELSTER portal to the competent local Finanzamt. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (UG is a German corporate body subject to corporation tax, trade tax, and solidarity surcharge; no pass-through treatment available)

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists. A UG may elect VAT fiscal unity (Organschaft) with its parent undertaking, and, where materially integrated, corporate-trade tax group status (Steuerorganschaft) via profit and loss pooling. Hybrid mismatch limitations under §4k EStG prevent elective avoidance of double non-taxation consistent with ATAD.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

The German small-business exemption applies where previous-year gross turnover did not exceed EUR 22,000 and current-year expected turnover does not exceed EUR 50,000. Non-established foreign suppliers may become compulsorily VAT-registered irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating for physical exports applies where the IVD goods are delivered by the UG to a non-EU customer or to an EU consolidator without domestic consumption, satisfying the substantive conditions of §4 Nr.1 and §6 UStG. In each case the UG must evidence the export in conformity with the customs export declaration (Ausfuhrnachweis), including the customs office of exit on a valid export declaration. Services, including software/technical support for point-of-care IVD analyzers, may be zero-rated as B2B supplies to non-resident recipients under §3a UStG when the recipient has its seat or permanent establishment outside the EU/EEA.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Ausfuhrnachweis / Abgangs- und Ausfuhrsbescheinigung) covering the goods and declared export office
  • Bill of Lading (for sea freight) or Air Waybill (for air freight) evidencing transport to a non-EU destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank wire confirmation demonstrating the foreign buyer’s payment from outside Germany

Germany does not operate real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or tax-authority API pre-clearance for standard domestic transactions. E-invoicing obligations are phased in from 1 January 2025 for domestic B2B supplies; e-invoices must comply with EN 16931 and be transmitted in structured formats (XRechnung/ZUGFeRD). A valid invoice requiring a VAT ID and sequential invoice number remains mandatory; e-Invoice requirements also apply to B2G supplies without threshold.

Exports are subject to documentary audit by the Finanzamt and customs authorities. Failure to produce the required export proof within the statutory period obliges the UG to self-correct VAT at 19% plus statutory interest (§233a AO); fraudulent or negligent export zero-rating claims expose the UG to VAT reassessment, civil penalties up to twice the evaded tax, and criminal prosecution for tax evasion under §370 AO, together with retrospective clawbacks under falsified or missing documentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Euro operates under full capital account convertibility per EU liberalization directives; no prior approval required for most capital transactions; profit remittances unrestricted subject to standard tax and AML documentation; capital versus current account distinctions do not apply due to harmonized EU FX framework; remittance hurdles limited to beneficial ownership verification and source-of-funds attestations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or forced conversion of export proceeds; companies retain proceeds in foreign currency accounts for free conversion; statistical reporting mandatory via customs declarations (Zoll) and Intrastat for values exceeding thresholds; no statutory timelines or conversion ratios imposed under current EU FX regulations.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for standalone cross-border EMI operations due to BaFin licensing mandates including minimum capital of EUR 350,000, robust consumer protection standards, and integration requirements with licensed banking rails; partnerships with established institutions recommended to navigate regulatory constraints.
