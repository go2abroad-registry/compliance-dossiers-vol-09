Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

UG requires a minimum nominal share capital of EUR 1.00, fully paid in cash or kind before the registration application is filed. The capital must be deposited in a German bank escrow/trust account in the name of the company in formation; the account release is certified by the managing director. The entity must retain at least 25% of annual net profit in a statutory reserve until the issued capital reaches EUR 25,000, at which point conversion into a full GmbH may be considered.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification issued by the local fire brigade (Feuerwehr) after inspection of the installed manifold room and pipe penetration seals. Structural load inspection for anchor points and risers by a certified structural engineer in accordance with DIN 7396 and state building regulations. Site clearance from hospital facility management for entry into sterile or semi-sterile medical areas.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt) — Federal Central Tax Office (national competence for USt-IdNr issuance, OSS/IOSS registrations, and EORI coordination) together with the locally competent Finanzamt (regional tax office at the UG's registered seat) exercising full assessment authority for Körperschaftsteuer, Gewerbesteuer, and Umsatzsteuer; official electronic filing infrastructure: ELSTER portal (www.elster.de) and the BZSt online portal for VAT-ID and EU distance-selling registration.

E-government filing portal

The UG (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschränkt) must file the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung (tax registration questionnaire, form EF/SO) electronically via ELSTER with the Finanzamt of its registered seat within one month of commencing the hospital medical gas pipeline business. The Finanzamt issues the 9-digit Steuernummer (statutory corporate tax number). The UG then applies separately to BZSt for the Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer (USt-IdNr) required for intra-Community transactions and applies for an EORI number (starting DE) for customs declarations. Non-resident ownership is permissible; the UG registers in its own name and cannot rely on any foreign shareholder TIN. A managing director may sign electronically or authorize a Steuerberater by power of attorney; the Finanzamt may set advance VAT and income-tax prepayments based on projected hospital-EPC turnover.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

German law contains no US-style check-the-box election; the UG is mandatorily taxed at entity level under the Körperschaftsteuergesetz (15% corporate income tax plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge plus municipal trade tax — Gewerbesteuer). Available statutory elections: (1) Organschaft — fiscal unity under §§ 14–18 KStG and § 2 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UStG requiring financial, economic, and organizational integration plus a written profit-and-loss pooling agreement (Ergebnisabführungsvertrag), allowing group-level income and VAT consolidation; (2) Real-estate VAT option under § 9 UStG to waive VAT exemptions on property supplies (not applicable to pipeline installation works); (3) Kleinunternehmerregelung under § 19 UStG is statutorily unavailable to a UG because every corporation is a mandatory VAT entrepreneur — the €22,000 small-business threshold does not apply, and standard 19% VAT invoicing is compulsory from the first invoice.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold applies to a German UG: every entrepreneurial activity under § 2(1) UStG obliges VAT registration irrespective of volume. The €22,000 small-business relief (§ 19 UStG) is unavailable to legal persons. For EU distance sales of goods and cross-border B2C digital services, the annual EU-wide threshold of €10,000 triggers registration in other Member States under the OSS scheme; medical gas pipeline installation services are, however, immovable-property services taxed exclusively at the situs of the hospital, so the UG's domestic EPC work is subject to German VAT and no foreign VAT registration arises therefrom.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports: A supply of goods physically transported to a third-country (non-EU) destination is zero-rated under § 6 UStG where the UG, as exporter/consignor, obtains and preserves the official customs export confirmation (Ausfuhrnachweis) in ATLAS with a valid Movement Reference Number (MRN). Intra-Community (EU) dispatches of incorporated pipeline equipment are exempt under § 6a UStG subject to the customer's valid EU VAT ID and submission of the Zusammenfassende Meldung. Services: Hospital medical gas pipeline installation, copper vacuum manifold works, and medical oxygen terminal fitting are services relating to immovable property; under Art. 47 VAT Directive they are deemed supplied where the hospital building is situated. Installation performed in Germany is therefore subject to German 19% VAT at the situs; installation performed on non-German hospitals lies outside the German tax net (foreign reverse charge or territorial non-taxation) and is not 'zero-rated German export VAT' — the UG must invoice such foreign EPC work without German VAT only when the non-German place of supply is documented and, for EU customers, the customer's VAT ID is obtained.

  • Validated German Customs export declaration in ATLAS (Ausfuhranmeldung) bearing the MRN and the UG's EORI number as exporter
  • Transport title: Bill of Lading (Konnossement), Air Waybill, or CMR consignment note showing despatch ex-Germany to the non-EU hospital/consignee
  • Official export confirmation certificate (Ausfuhrnachweis) — electronic ATLAS clearance or AE certificate issued by Hauptzollamt pursuant to §§ 8–12 UStDV
  • Commercial export invoice (Ausfuhrrechnung) with export Incoterms (EXW, FOB, CFR) referencing the MRN, contract, bill of materials, and copper pipeline/terminal manifold line items
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or non-EU bank transfer/telex payment confirmation evidencing receipt of foreign-currency consideration within the repatriation window

Germany imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, no tax-authority pre-approval APIs, and no digital stamping of invoices. Mandatory B2B e-invoicing (E-Rechnung) under § 14 UStG as amended by the Wachstumschancengesetz 2023: (1) since 1 January 2025, every domestic B2B supplier must be able to receive e-invoices in EN 16931-compliant formats (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD/Factur-X, OIOUBL) — paper and PDF issuance remains permitted; (2) mandatory e-invoice issuance from 1 January 2027 for entrepreneurs whose 2026 turnover exceeded €800,000; (3) mandatory issuance for all entrepreneurs from 1 January 2028; EDI interchange under a mutual agreement remains valid through 31 December 2027; e-invoices must be archived in a tamper-proof, GoBD-compliant digital repository for 10 years under § 14b UStG. The UG's hospital-EPC invoices require no prior Finanzamt clearance or digital stamp.

German customs and tax auditors verify MRN/Warenverzeichnis traces and export-certificate authenticity against VAT returns; if the UG fails to produce a valid Ausfuhrnachweis, the zero-rating is retroactively reversed and 19% VAT assessed together with late-payment interest of 0.5% per month (6% per annum) under § 233a AO within the standard 5-year assessment limitation period (§ 169/170 AO), extended to 10 years for evasion. Submission of forged or fictitious export documents to sustain zero-rating constitutes tax evasion under § 370 AO — imprisonment up to 5 years (up to 10 years in aggravated cases), fines under §§ 379–384 AO, confiscation of the evaded VAT under §§ 73–73b StGB, and liability of the managing director via Haftungsbescheid under §§ 34, 69, and 71 AO. BZSt's automated cross-border data-matching (OSS/IOSS exchange, EMS/Customs surveillance) systemically flags mismatched invoices and blocked refunds, and the UG's VAT refund claim remains suspended until the export evidence is authenticated.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany maintains full convertibility for both current and capital account transactions under the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and Section 19 of the German Foreign Trade and Payments Act (AWG). There are no general exchange controls, no surrender or conversion requirements for export proceeds, and no prior exchange control approval for repatriation of capital, dividends, or royalties. Profit remittance is subject only to general German corporate law and tax law: profit distributions by a UG require a shareholder resolution, compliance with the balance sheet test under the German Commercial Code (HGB), and prohibition of distributions that would endanger the company's capital or solvency. Non-resident shareholders may freely receive dividends after deduction of German withholding tax (Kapitalertragsteuer, including solidarity surcharge and, where applicable, church tax), subject to treaty relief under any applicable double tax agreement. Practical hurdles are not FX-related but arise from anti-money-laundering verification of the underlying commercial transaction, sanctions list screening, and banking KYC documentation. As the UG may require initial capital as low as 1 EUR, banks may impose enhanced substance checks on capitalization and business purpose before opening a full-service corporate account.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany does not impose any export proceeds surrender obligation or mandatory conversion ratio. Exporters and service providers may retain foreign currency balances in resident or non-resident accounts. However, under the German Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (AWV), residents must report to the Deutsche Bundesbank all payments received from non-residents, and all payments made to non-residents, within five working days of the month following the month in which the payment was made or received. The reporting threshold applies when the amount exceeds EUR 12,500 or its equivalent in another currency. The report is statistical and does not trigger approval.
