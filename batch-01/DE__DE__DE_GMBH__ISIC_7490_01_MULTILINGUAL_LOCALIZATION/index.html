Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Commercial Register (Handelsregister) maintained by the Local Court (Amtsgericht) in the district of the company's registered office; Registergericht

Governing statute

German Limited Liability Companies Act (GmbH-Gesetz, GmbHG), Sections 1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 10, 35 and 78; German Commercial Code (HGB), Sections 8 to 16 on Commercial Register procedure

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarized Articles of Association (Gesellschaftsvertrag) or Model Protocol (Musterprotokoll) together with the Joint Application for Registration in the Commercial Register (Anmeldung zur Eintragung)

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 30 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Legal minimum share capital is EUR 25,000; before registration no less than EUR 12,500 of the cash contributions must be paid up into a blocked escrow/deposit account (Sperrkonto) held in the company's name and at the free disposal of the future managing director. Contributions in kind must be fully vested and described in the notarized articles.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification: the lessor must provide the building's fire safety and escape-route documentation under the applicable state building code (LBO); if the studio is accessible to the public, a fire protection concept from the local fire safety authority may be required. Structural load inspection: not required for ordinary office use unless the localization studio installs acoustic ceilings, heavy dubbing equipment, or structural modifications. Occupational clearance: the employer must carry out a workplace risk assessment under Section 5 and Section 6 of the German Occupational Safety and Health Act (ArbSchG), and comply with the Workplace Ordinance (ArbStättV) covering ventilation, lighting, emergency exits, and first-aid provisions.

Paid-in capital rule: Legal minimum share capital is EUR 25,000; before registration no less than EUR 12,500 of the cash contributions must be paid up into a blocked escrow/deposit account (Sperrkonto) held in the company's name and at the free disposal of the future managing director. Contributions in kind must be fully vested and described in the notarized articles.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt, Federal Central Tax Office) for VAT ID issuance; local Finanzamt (tax office) for corporate income tax, trade tax, and VAT administration; electronic filing portal ELSTER (www.elster.de)

E-government filing portal

A newly formed GmbH files the statutory 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) via ELSTER after commercial register entry; the local Finanzamt issues the corporate income tax/trade tax Steuernummer, and an application through the questionnaire or BZSt online portal obtains the VAT ID (USt-IdNr); foreign entities establishing a German branch or permanent establishment must follow the same registration procedure under Sec. 138 AO and Sec. 27a UStG.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (GmbH is subject to corporate income tax at 15% plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge and municipal trade tax; no pass-through treatment) — A newly formed GmbH files the statutory 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) via ELSTER after commercial register entry; the local Finanzamt issues the corporate income tax/trade tax Steuernummer, and an application through the questionnaire or BZSt online portal obtains the VAT ID (USt-IdNr); foreign entities establishing a German branch or permanent establishment must follow the same registration procedure under Sec. 138 AO and Sec. 27a UStG.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (GmbH is subject to corporate income tax at 15% plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge and municipal trade tax; no pass-through treatment)

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election; available elections include corporate income tax and trade tax 'Organschaft' tax group via a profit-and-loss pooling agreement (Sec. 14 KStG and Sec. 2 GewStG), VAT grouping 'Umsatzsteuer-Organschaft' (Sec. 2 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UStG), the Sec. 1a KStG option for a foreign corporation to be taxed as a German corporation, and hybrid-mismatch adjustments under Sec. 4k EStG and Sec. 12a Abs. 3 KStG.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general registration threshold for regular entrepreneurs; small-business VAT exemption applies below EUR 22,000 prior-year turnover and EUR 50,000 current-year forecast (Sec. 19 UStG); intra-EU distance-sale and cross-border B2C digital-service threshold of EUR 10,000 for the OSS special scheme

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating with input VAT credit applies to physical exports of goods to third countries (Sec. 6 UStG) and intra-Community supplies to EU VAT-registered business customers (Sec. 6a UStG); for multilingual localization and media dubbing services supplied B2B to non-resident businesses, the reverse-charge place-of-supply rule (Sec. 3a Abs. 2 UStG) removes the supply from German VAT; the supplier must evidence transport/destination and customer business status.

  • Customs export declaration (Ausfuhranmeldung / ATLAS export manifest)
  • Bill of lading, airway bill, or CMR road consignment note evidencing physical export movement
  • Inward remittance certificate or payment receipt evidencing export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and proof of customer VAT ID or business registration for reverse-charge exemption

Mandatory B2B electronic invoices (E-Rechnung) in EN 16931 structured formats (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Peppol BIS) from 1 January 2025, with phased issue obligations between 2027 and 2028; mandatory XRechnung for B2G procurement; no real-time fiscal pre-clearance API or digital stamping; GoBD-compliant electronic books and records are required for VAT audit.

Retrospective VAT assessment, denial of input VAT deduction, late-payment surcharge of 1% per month (Sec. 240 AO), delay penalty of up to 25% of assessed VAT capped at EUR 25,000 (Sec. 152 AO), and interest at 0.5% per month (Sec. 233a AO); criminal tax evasion under Sec. 370 AO carries up to five years' imprisonment (up to ten years in severe cases); managing directors face personal liability for unpaid VAT under Sec. 34 and 69 AO.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany maintains a fully convertible Euro currency regime. Capital account transactions are unrestricted, enabling free capital mobility. Current account transactions, including profit remittances, encounter minimal regulatory hurdles and are processed efficiently via standard banking channels.

Export proceeds surrender rules

German exporters are not subject to mandatory surrender or conversion requirements for export proceeds. Funds may be retained in foreign currency accounts, subject solely to general AML and tax reporting obligations.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: cross-border related-party transactions for goods exceeding €5,000,000 and for services/other transactions exceeding €500,000 per fiscal year. Master File: required when the consolidated group revenue exceeds €100,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Germany does not operate separate Economic Substance Regulations; however, treaty entitlement, beneficial ownership analysis, § 42 AO abuse doctrine, and the ATAD principal purpose test require a genuine local presence, business premises, staff, management functions, and economic activity. A letterbox GmbH without substance will be denied treaty relief and dividend withholding tax reductions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Steuerhinterziehung under § 370 AO)

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment for up to 10 years; recovery of all evaded taxes plus 0.5% monthly late-payment interest; criminal fines and confiscation of illicit gains; suspension or revocation of business licenses.

Director personal liability: Managing directors acting in breach of § 34/§ 69 AO are personally liable for unpaid corporate and trade taxes; criminal conviction can trigger asset seizure, travel bans, denial of exit permits, and imprisonment.
Shell Company / Letterbox Entity

Statutory sanction: Recharacterisation under § 42 AO; denial of treaty benefits; permanent establishment of the foreign parent may be deemed in Germany; retrospective assessment and cancellation of VAT registration.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where directors have structured a sham entity; personal civil restitution to the tax authority and imprisonment for fraudulent abuse of legal forms may follow.
AML and Unlicensed Payment Handling (GwG / § 261 StGB)

Statutory sanction: Fines under the GwG up to €1 million per violation; confiscation orders; criminal prosecution under § 261 StGB with imprisonment up to 5 years; public reprimand and exclusion from payment processing networks.

Director personal liability: Managing directors are personally liable for AML compliance failures; fines apply to both the entity and the individual; repeated or aggravated violations result in personal criminal liability, travel restrictions, and disqualification from managing a GmbH.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure (§ 90 Abs. 3 AO / § 162 AO)

Statutory sanction: Tax assessment by estimation; penalty surcharge of 5%-10% of adjusted income (minimum €5,000); administrative penalty up to €1,000,000 for severe documentation failures; double taxation risk through income reclassification.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly signed deficient TP documentation or instructed false pricing are jointly and severally liable for added taxes and penalties; criminal tax evasion charges can be joined with transfer pricing findings.
