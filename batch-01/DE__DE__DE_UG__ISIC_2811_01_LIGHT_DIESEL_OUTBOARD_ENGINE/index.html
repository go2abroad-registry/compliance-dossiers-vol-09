Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Ministry of Finance (Bundesministerium der Finanzen, BMF); Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, BZSt); locally competent tax office (Finanzamt) for corporate income tax, trade tax, and withholding tax assessments; ELSTER (Elektronische Steuererklärung) is the statutory electronic filing portal

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities and non-resident persons may lawfully establish and own a UG (haftungsbeschränkt) with no minimum share capital. Procedure: (1) execute the articles of association before a German notary; (2) register the UG in the commercial register (Handelsregister) maintained by the local Amtsgericht; (3) after registration and before commencement of business, submit the statutory questionnaire on tax registration (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung) electronically via ELSTER to the competent Finanzamt, which then issues the Steuernummer (income-tax and trade-tax identification number) for the UG; (4) apply separately for the turnover-tax identification number (USt-IdNr) from the BZSt using Form USt 1 TZ or the BZSt online portal, attaching a certified excerpt from the commercial register, the notarized articles, identity particulars of shareholders/managing directors, the precise business description (manufacture of marine outboard engines and compact generators), and projected turnover figures; (5) statutory tax registration must be completed within about four weeks of actual business commencement; a German bank account is not a precondition for obtaining tax numbers

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany maintains a fully convertible currency regime within the euro area. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are unrestricted under EU Treaty free movement of capital and the AWV; profit distribution, dividend remittance, loan repayment, equity injection and liquidation proceeds may be transferred from a German UG to non-resident shareholders without quantitative ceilings, exchange-control approval or profit-repatriation tax clearance. Practical hurdles are limited to (i) EU/UN economic-sanctions screening, (ii) Anti-Money-Laundering Act (GwG) transparency obligations regarding the source of funds and the ultimate beneficial owner, and (iii) mandatory Bundesbank statistical reporting for each cross-border payment exceeding EUR 12,500 and for foreign receivables/payables exceeding EUR 5 million. No consent-based central bank authorization is required for ordinary trade or capital flows; the euro is freely market-convertible.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio in Germany. Exporters of light diesel outboard engines and compact generators may lawfully retain foreign-currency receipts indefinitely in foreign-currency accounts at German or EEA banks without any obligation to convert into euro. Statutory compliance is purely statistical and administrative: every receipt from a non-resident exceeding EUR 12,500 must be reported by the German resident recipient to the Deutsche Bundesbank within five banking days of the value date for balance-of-payments statistics; outstanding foreign receivables and payables must be reported when the aggregate amount exceeds EUR 5 million; and intra-group loans above EUR 5 million are separately reportable. Late or failed reporting constitutes an administrative offence enforceable by the Bundesbank and BaFin, but no conversion ratio, surrender timeline or repatriation condition applies to the marine-engine export business.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (the central bank of the Federal Republic of Germany), operating within the Eurosystem/ECB Single Supervisory Mechanism, together with the German Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin) for banking, payment-institution and securities supervision, the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action (BMWK) for foreign-trade screening, and the Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung, AWV) as the operative legal instrument.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Rating: MEDIUM—VIABLE ONLY AS A SECONDARY OPERATIONAL RAIL. BaFin-licensed or EEA-passported e-money institutions and payment institutions (e.g., Solaris Group via banking-as-a-service, Wise Business, Revolut, and German PSD2/EMD platforms) may lawfully open euro-denominated operational accounts, issue German IBANs and execute SEPA and SWIFT-style cross-border payments for a German UG without holding a full banking licence. These rails are adequate for collecting customer inbound TTs for engine sales and for settling low-to-mid-value supplier invoices. They are NOT permissible or practically viable as the sole treasury infrastructure for: (i) import documentary letters of credit for engine components and marine-generator parts; (ii) high-value SWIFT MT103 flows requiring proprietary correspondent relationships in Africa, Asia or Latin America; and (iii) execution and custody of OTC FX hedging derivatives. Legal constraint under the German Payment Services Supervision Act (ZAG): an EMI is neither a credit institution nor an investment firm; it cannot grant intraday credit and its safeguarding regime differs from deposit-guarantee protection. Offshore digital EMIs domiciled outside the EEA cannot lawfully provide payment accounts to a German UG without BaFin authorisation or a valid EEA passport, and such institutions must therefore be excluded from the approved counterparty list.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Ultimate beneficial owner is a non-resident individual outside the EU/EEA and cannot appear in person at the German branch.

Root cause: Ultimate beneficial owner is a non-resident individual outside the EU/EEA and cannot appear in person at the German branch.

Defense evidence: Apostilled or certified passport copy with medical-grade identification quality; notarised declaration of the UBO under section 3(2) GwG including the full corporate ownership chain; notarised specific power of attorney for the German resident Geschäftsführer; certified German translation of all constitutional documents; economic-nexus evidence (residence permit, employment certificate or business registration in the home jurisdiction); and a notary- or German-consulate-executed specimen signature card.
Initial capital contribution or capital-reserve injection arrives by wire transfer without a transparent source-of-funds trail.

Root cause: Initial capital contribution or capital-reserve injection arrives by wire transfer without a transparent source-of-funds trail.

Defense evidence: Notarial subscription deed (Übernahmeerklärung) identifying the contributing shareholder and the exact euro amount; bank statements of the transferring shareholder covering a continuous six-month trail; a written declaration of origin of funds (Vermögensherkunft) correlated with the shareholder's tax returns; if funds originate from an attorney or notary escrow account, the irrevocable Anderkonto confirmation issued by that custodian; and the final capital-payment confirmation generated by the receiving German bank.
Trade flows involve counterparties or disbursement corridors in higher-risk jurisdictions, including dealers of diesel outboard engines in Africa, the Middle East or Latin America.

Root cause: Trade flows involve counterparties or disbursement corridors in higher-risk jurisdictions, including dealers of diesel outboard engines in Africa, the Middle East or Latin America.

Defense evidence: Complete corporate KYC package for every material counterparty (registration extract, passport or director identification, UBO declaration, recent financial statements); sanitised sanction-list screening printouts from a commercial screening tool; export sales contract and pro-forma invoice; ocean bill of lading and packing list; EU dual-use export classification analysis under Regulation (EU) 2021/821 confirming the outboard engine is not a dual-use item and that no export-licence trigger applies; and a transaction-volume forecast that maps future payment frequencies to the bank's expected pattern.
Sanctions or PEP screening produces a positive or ambiguous hit on a shareholder, director or named customer.

Root cause: Sanctions or PEP screening produces a positive or ambiguous hit on a shareholder, director or named customer.

Defense evidence: Complete identity attributes (full name, date of birth, permanent address, nationality) to distinguish the subject from the sanctioned individual; professional-source declarations and notarised affidavits; a written legal opinion from a German Rechtsanwalt confirming no designation nexus; a letter of comfort from the referring tier-1 bank where the group has an existing relationship; and, where residual ambiguity exists, the formal removal of the affected party from signature authority or transaction approval workflows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire-transfer protocol for equity injections into the UG: (i) before the value date, the notarial capital-increase deed or subscription document must be executed and filed with the Commercial Register (Handelsregister) where a capital increase is involved, and the receiving bank must be given a copy of the notarial reference; (ii) the international remittance is executed via SWIFT MT103 for non-EEA shareholders, or via SEPA Credit Transfer for EEA shareholders, using the bank's pre-agreed standard settlement instruction and the UG's own IBAN; (iii) the remittance information field must state the exact statutory purpose and documentary reference, in the format 'Kapitalerhöhung laut notarieller Urkunde vom [date], Register-Nr. [HRB number], UR-Nr. [notary file number]' and must not include ambiguous wording such as 'loan', 'fee', 'repayment' or 'commission' that could trigger tax recharacterisation or a source-of-funds review; (iv) the bank's ISO 20022 pain.001/SWIFT purpose code should be the one mapped internally to 'capital contribution/equity injection' in the receiving bank's ordering system; if no exact code is available, the free-text notarial reference must be used; (v) a Source-of-Funds disclosure form with the contributing shareholder's six-month banking trail and the UBO declaration must be lodged at onboarding to prevent the injecion being frozen by AML filters; and (vi) for a UG, the subscribed initial capital (plus any premium/agio) need not meet the EUR 25,000 GmbH minimum, but the full amount stated in the articles of association must be covered by the credited funds before registration, and the funds must remain at the free disposal of the legal representative.The UG's outbound trade settlement architecture uses four principal clearing rails: (1) SEPA SCT under pain.001/pacs.008 for euro payments to EEA suppliers of engine components, freight and insurance, ensuring the IBAN/BIC are validated and the purpose code is aligned with the underlying invoice; (2) SWIFT MT103 for non-EEA suppliers, with full UETR tracking, harmonised structured remittance data and mandatory beneficiary-name and address screening against EU Regulation (EU) 269/2014 and United Nations sanctions lists prior to release; (3) documentary commercial letters of credit, both irrevocable sight and usance, opened by the UG's German account bank through a correspondent of the supplier's bank, with documents per UCP 600 and electronic presentation (eUCP) acceptable to the bank, used for high-value marine-engine component imports where the supplier requires payment assurance; and (4) bank-to-bank MT202/MT202 COV for internal liquidity concentration when surplus balances from the local current account are swept to a group treasury hub or to the head-office account of a supporting bank. For the marine-engine business, the trade-finance desk of Commerzbank or Deutsche Bank will require the commercial invoice, bill of lading/airway bill, certificate of origin and inspection certificate to match the L/C terms, and all disbursements must be reconciled to purchase orders in the operational ERP to retain the evidentiary chain for AWV reporting and for the statutory five-day Bundesbank statistical declaration.Outright EUR/USD and EUR/GBP currency forwards (Devisentermingeschäfte) documented under the ISDA 2002 or ISDA 2019 Master Agreement, or under the German master agreement for financial term transactions (Rahmenvertrag für Finanztermingeschäfte), limited to hedging of committed engine-export receivables and component-import payables.Standard FX swaps combining a spot foreign-exchange leg with a simultaneous forward reversal, employed for temporary liquidity matching when USD collections arrive earlier than scheduled EUR component payments.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (cross-currency swaps, CCS) for medium-term matching of long-term USD or GBP revenue streams from generator-unit service contracts against EUR manufacturing and procurement costs, subject to EMIR margining and reconciliation obligations.Non-deliverable forwards (NDF) booked offshore with the third-country branch or affiliate of the German account bank for currencies without full convertibility in London or New York centres, such as USD/CNY, USD/NGN or USD/EGP, used strictly for trade receipts collected through restricted-currency banking channels.OTC EUR/USD plain-vanilla European-style currency options (purchased long positions only) to cap adverse USD payment obligations while retaining the benefit of favourable rate movement, without speculative premium-writing exposure.
