Non-cash contributions require an auditor's valuation. An escrow account is not required for monetary contributions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full de jure and de facto convertibility. Current-account and capital-account transactions are liberalized under TFEU Article 63 (free movement of capital) and the Estonian Foreign Exchange Act (Välisvaluutaseadus). No Eesti Pank authorization, FX license, or mandatory declaration is required for inward or outward remittances, dividend distributions, shareholder loans, royalty payments, trade settlements, or profit repatriation. Non-resident ownership of an OÜ is unrestricted. Residual frictions: (i) EU/UN/Estonia sanctions and asset-freezing screening applied by banks; (ii) automatic statistical reporting of cross-border transactions to Eesti Pank for balance-of-payments purposes; (iii) cash declaration at EUR 10,000 under Regulation (EU) 2018/1672; (iv) the bank's own AML due diligence on profit repatriation, requiring underlying invoices and corporate documentation. Dividends face no FX hurdle; Estonia's 0% undistributed-profit regime taxes profits only upon distribution, and the dividend payout itself is subject to no exchange-control or central-bank authorization.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Estonia imposes no statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation obligation for export proceeds. The localization agency may retain translation and dubbing revenues in the invoiced currency (EUR, USD, GBP) indefinitely in multi-currency current accounts. The euro is the only legal tender, but banks may voluntarily convert idle foreign-currency balances at market rates with no regulatory compulsion. No FX surrender certificate or Eesti Pank approval is required for the ISIC 7490 service-export model.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia), operating within the Eurosystem under the European Central Bank framework. Prudential supervision of credit institutions is delegated to Finantsinspektsioon (Estonian Financial Supervision Authority); AML/CFT supervision is shared between Finantsinspektsioon and the Estonian Financial Intelligence Unit (Rahapesu Andmebüroo). Eesti Pank acts as the statistical authority for cross-border payment data, with resident banks serving as reporting agents. Estonia is a Eurozone member state (euro as sole legal tender since 1 January 2011) and maintains no separate foreign-exchange administration or permit-granting FX office.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High viability as supplementary settlement rails; Medium as the sole treasury anchor. Wise Europe SA (EMI authorized by the Belgian NBB, passported into Estonia under PSD2) provides EUR clearing via SEPA and SEPA Instant with a Belgian IBAN and B2B batch payment capabilities; Payoneer (EU EMI license) offers USD/EUR/GBP receiving accounts with partner-bank settlement. Legal constraints: (i) EMIs are not credit institutions under the Estonian Credit Institutions Act, therefore they cannot certify paid-in share capital to the commercial register — acceptable only because the OÜ may be formed with zero minimum statutory capital; (ii) resident banks increasingly de-risk inbound EMI pay-ins for non-resident-owned OÜs, so the company must retain full invoice and delivery evidence for every EMI-sourced credit; (iii) EMIs provide non-Estonian IBANs (BE/IE style), which may cause counterparty friction and delayed settlement; (iv) no Eesti Pank license is required for the OÜ to hold funds with an EEA-passported EMI. Recommended architecture: LHV or SEB corporate account as the primary treasury anchor, Wise B2B for SEPA vendor and freelancer mass payouts, and Payoneer for USD client collections, with daily or weekly sweeps to the Estonian bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol for equity or loan injection into the Estonian OÜ: (i) no minimum statutory capital applies (Äriseadustik §140 as amended in 2011 — an OÜ may be founded with zero paid-in capital; where the founder elects to capitalize, the traditional EUR 2,500 benchmark remains the standard banking expectation); (ii) for equity: remit via SWIFT MT103 for non-EEA origin or SEPA Credit Transfer for EEA EUR payments, with field 70 reading 'EE [registry code] — aktsiakapitali sissemakse / share capital injection, Commercial Code §140(1)' and EU balance-of-payments purpose code 'P11 – equity capital and shares of investment funds'; no Eesti Pank approval is required; (iii) the receiving bank credits the free current account; for amounts of EUR 20,000 or more, expect automatic sanctions screening and a request for the subscription agreement, board resolution, and valid identification of the remitting shareholder; (iv) the board must record the subscription in the share register (aktsiaraamat) and reflect it in the annual report filed with the Äriregister; (v) for shareholder loans: execute a written loan agreement, use purpose code 'P20 – loans between resident and non-resident companies', and apply an arm's-length interest rate to avoid thin-capitalization and transfer-pricing challenge; (vi) direct capital injections from EMI accounts are discouraged; the founder should use a personal bank-to-bank wire to keep the transaction unimpeachable.Clearing rails for trade settlements: (i) EEA freelancer and vendor payouts up to EUR 50,000 — SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) via ISO 20022 pain.001, referencing 'EE [code] / invoice / PO'; (ii) non-EEA subcontractors (US, UK, Asia) — SWIFT MT103 via the bank's international desk (typical tariffs EUR 6–15 per payment), with beneficiary IBAN and SWIFT/BIC in field 57 and invoice reference in field 72; (iii) high-value studio dubbing or localization packages (EUR 250,000 or more) — Commercial Letter of Credit via SWIFT MT700/MT707 governed by UCP600, with the Estonian OÜ as applicant, requiring invoice, delivery certificate, and media-master acceptance, or a standby LC for performance guarantees; (iv) royalty and license payments to rights holders — SWIFT MT103 marked 'Royalties' with WHT self-assessment under the EU Interest & Royalties Directive 2003/49/EC (0% WHT where the beneficial owner is an EU/EEA resident company); (v) the Estonian bank remains the Eesti Pank statistical reporting agent, eliminating manual balance-of-payments declarations; (vi) when routing through Wise B2B, funds move from the client to Wise's Belgian IBAN and then onward via SEPA or SWIFT to the Estonian account — the Wise payment confirmation must be retained as the VAT and income-tax supporting document; Payoneer USD receipts are settled via ACH/SWIFT sweeps; all EMI rails should be whitelisted in the bank's relationship-manager mandate.Outright EUR/USD and EUR/GBP FX Forwards (1-week to 12-month tenors) on the bank's corporate treasury desk under an IFEMA or ISDA Master Agreement; no central clearing or Eesti Pank approval applies to a non-financial counterparty below the EMIR Refit clearing threshold, and the instrument must hedge a documented commercial exposure from invoiced USD or GBP receivables.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) for exotic or non-convertible currencies (e.g., USD/TRY, USD/INR, USD/RUB subject to EU sanctions screening) cash-settled in EUR, available through SEB or Swedbank international desks; Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 50,000 for controlled transactions (Local File); Master File required if group revenue exceeds EUR 50 million

Economic substance scope: Estonia does not have specific ESR, but applies substance requirements to avoid shell company status; tax residency based on incorporation or place of effective management.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fine up to EUR 1.2 million or 10% of tax evaded; criminal prosecution for willful evasion

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for unpaid taxes if they knowingly participated; travel bans and imprisonment up to 5 years.
Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Reclassification as tax resident elsewhere; denial of treaty benefits; penalty up to EUR 10,000

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for taxes and penalties; potential criminal charges for fraud.
AML Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Fines up to EUR 1.6 million or 10% of annual turnover; license revocation

Director personal liability: Directors may face personal fines and imprisonment up to 3 years for willful AML violations.
Transfer Pricing Misreporting

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable income; penalty up to 50% of the additional tax assessed

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for penalties if they approved the mispricing; potential criminal liability for tax evasion.
