Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The statutory minimum share capital of an Osaühing is €2,500, which must be contributed to a payment account opened in the company's name and verified by the bank before or upon filing. Alternatively, under § 135(6) of the Commercial Code, the company may be incorporated with zero initial share capital ('0-capital OÜ'), in which event no distributions of assets to shareholders are lawful until the share capital has been fully paid to at least €2,500. Share contributions must be effected by bank transfer only; cash or set-off are not accepted by the registry. The e-Business Register automatically verifies the payment through an integrated bank-link confirmation.

Funding & unlock path: If the company occupies a physical office, the employer must submit an Occupational Health and Safety registration to the Labour Inspectorate (Tööinspektsioon) before work commences, perform a workplace risk assessment, and comply with the Occupational Health and Safety Act; fire safety under the Rescue Act (Päästeseadus) requires a written fire-safety audit, functioning detectors/extinguishers, and evacuation instructions, with supervision by the Rescue Board (Päästeamet). Where no physical premises are used, these clearances do not apply; the company may elect a virtual-office address for registry purposes, subject to the service provider's registered business address authorization.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet, MTA); electronic services portal: e-MTA (e-maksuamet/e-toll); company registration: e-Business Register (Äriregister).

E-government filing portal

For a resident OÜ, incorporate through the e-Business Register; the Commercial Register assigns a registry code that serves as the legal person's tax and identification number, and the MTA automatically registers the company for VAT once the statutory threshold is exceeded or an application is submitted. A non-resident entity without a permanent establishment must apply to the MTA through e-MTA for an Estonian VAT identification number; for B2C electronic and digital services, the non-Union OSS scheme may be used instead of a local VAT registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — an Estonian resident OÜ is the taxpayer; accounting profits are not taxed while retained, but taxable distributions and deemed fringe benefits are taxed at the rates applicable on distribution. Foreign permanent establishment profits are generally not taxed until distributed; Estonia does not apply pass-through taxation for OÜs.

Available election forms

Estonia does not operate a US-style Check-the-Box election. Voluntary VAT grouping may be elected by related taxable persons with MTA approval. The regular-dividend mechanism permits a reduced tax rate on recurring profit distributions within statutory historical limits, but this is conditional and not a pure election. Hybrid mismatch rules under the ATAD implementation are mandatory and non-elective.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

22%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 40,000 per calendar year of taxable supplies for Estonian-established businesses; non-resident suppliers of B2C digitally supplied services generally have no micro-threshold and must register from the first supply unless using the non-Union One-Stop-Shop (OSS).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods exported from Estonia to destinations outside the EU where the export is evidenced within the statutory time limits and the goods leave the EU under customs supervision. For cross-border market-research SaaS services in B2B transactions, the place of supply is in the customer's jurisdiction, so Estonian VAT does not apply; for B2C digital services to non-EU customers the place of supply is outside Estonia. Supporting evidence must demonstrate the non-resident status and business character of the customer.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/EAD) cleared by the Estonian Tax and Customs Board
  • Bill of Lading / CMR / air waybill proving physical export from the EU
  • Commercial invoice and underlying contract or purchase order
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit confirmation evidencing repatriation of export proceeds

Estonia does not mandate real-time government clearance, transactional API reporting, or digital stamping. B2G e-invoicing is required through the e-MTA environment using EN 16931/Peppol formats; all VAT returns and transaction listings must be filed electronically through e-MTA. Domestic B2B e-invoicing is accepted and promoted but not yet universally mandatory.

The MTA may conduct post-clearance audits, reject incomplete or falsified export evidence, and retrospectively claw back VAT previously zero-rated or refunded, adding statutory late-payment interest and fines. Intentionally fraudulent documentation may be prosecuted as tax fraud under the Penal Code, leading to criminal sanctions and an extended retroactive reassessment period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia has no currency-exchange control regime. The EUR is legal tender, with full convertibility on both current and capital account transactions under the repealed Foreign Exchange Law framework and Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union Article 63 guarantees on free movement of capital. Profit remittance, dividend distribution, and capital repatriation are not subject to exchange-approval or conversion licensing; the only statutory hurdle is the corporate income tax on distributions (20/80 regular rate or 14/86 on qualifying regular dividends) plus standard withholding obligations if applicable to non-resident payees. An OÜ has no minimum paid-in capital requirement, and capital contributions may be received in EUR or any freely convertible currency and maintained offshore or onshore without restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Estonia does not impose surrender, repatriation, or mandatory conversion ratios on export receipts or cross-border service earnings. Income from ISIC 7320 market-research and consumer-polling contracts may be retained in any currency, converted freely, or used directly for foreign disbursements. The only residual obligation is statistical reporting to Eesti Pank within the balance-of-payments survey where a resident enterprise exceeds the applicable reporting thresholds; this is a data-reporting duty, not an FX-control restriction.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia) as the national central bank and member of the Eurosystem, jointly responsible with the European Central Bank (ECB) for monetary policy and foreign-reserve operations; foreign-exchange conduct rules and payment-license oversight are administered by Finantsinspektsioon (Estonian Financial Supervision Authority) and the Estonian Tax and Customs Board for trade-related currency matters.

PayPal Fit: High

High feasibility. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Preferred rails are SEPA Credit Transfer in EUR from the shareholder's EU account or SWIFT MT103 for non-EU remittances, credited to the OÜ's dedicated capital-collection IBAN at LHV Pank, Swedbank, or SEB Pank. SWIFT field 70 must read 'Capital increase EE-OU [shareholder name] equity contribution' or 'Shareholder loan disbursement'; Estonian law does not mandate a national purpose code, but the reference must match the e-Business Register transcript for the capital increase filed through the e-Notary system. For monetary contributions made before registration, the bank credit confirmation is retained by the notary; for subsequent increases, the deposit is verified digitally. For shareholder loans, a written loan agreement, interest schedule, and fitness certificate are required. The treasury ledger must book the receipt per the Estonian chart of accounts, segregating paid-in capital (account 2130) from shareholder-loan liabilities (account 2199) to support clean audit trails and subsequent dividend or repayment planning.Primary clearing rails for ISIC 7320: (a) SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) for EUR-denominated panelist incentives across the EEA; (b) Wise mass-payout API covering more than 70 local rails with executed FX at mid-market-plus-margin for non-EUR panelists; (c) PayPal Mass Payout as a fallback aggregation channel for territories where SEPA and Wise are unavailable; (d) SWIFT MT103 with charge code OUR or BEN for high-value B2B settlements such as data-licensing vendors, advertising agencies, and subcontract research partners, using in field 70 an UNSPSC code plus the text 'Market research services ISIC 7320' to ease downstream FX clearing and customs classification. Commercial Letters of Credit are not standard for market-research disbursements but may be opened through the domestic bank's trade desk for high-value panel incentives in restricted-currency jurisdictions, settled in EUR. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% on gross distributed profits (20/80 of the net distribution); 0% tax on undistributed and retained profits

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% domestic withholding on dividends to non-resident corporate shareholders holding at least 10% (participation exemption); 7% or 20% for non-resident individual shareholders depending on regular vs non-regular distribution classification; 20% on dividends to non-resident corporate shareholders below the participation threshold unless a DTA reduces the rate

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 250,000 aggregate annual cross-border controlled transactions (goods, services, intangibles, financial); below this threshold a Local File is prepared only upon tax authority request; Master File triggered when the Estonian entity belongs to a group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 50 million; Country-by-Country Reporting applies at group revenue of EUR 750 million

Economic substance scope: No dedicated Economic Substance Regulation code exists in Estonia; however, the place of effective management test (Income Tax Act § 6) and EU/GAAR anti-abuse rules require the OÜ to have real local substance. A foreign-managed OÜ with no Estonian employees, premises, bank accounts, or governance records may be reclassified as tax resident abroad, denied DTA benefits under the Principal Purpose Test, and stripped of the 0% retained-profit privilege. For an e-Residency structure, substance must be evidenced by board minutes, accounting records, contract management, and operational decision-making in Estonia.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Dividend Concealment

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of the hidden distribution at 20/80 (or 14/86 if regular); daily arrears interest at 0.06%; penalty surcharge up to EUR 1,000 for each unfiled declaration; criminal fine for a legal person under Penal Code § 398 of up to EUR 500,000, plus confiscation of proceeds.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid tax and interest under § 61(1) of the Tax Act; intentional evasion is punishable by up to 5 years' imprisonment (§ 389); courts may impose travel bans, asset attachment, and prohibition on serving as a director for up to 3 years.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Ignore the legal personality of the OÜ under the Estonian Supreme Court's abuse-of-law doctrine; reallocate income to the foreign beneficial owner; impose a 20% penalty surcharge plus interest under § 127 of the Tax Act; revoke e-Resident digital ID privileges.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors and beneficial owners can be personally assessed for unpaid CIT and penalties; restriction on leaving Estonia under the Enforcement Act; criminal liability for fraudulent insolvency if the company fails.
AML / Payments Integrity

Statutory sanction: Fines under the Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Prevention Act: up to EUR 400,000 per infringing act for legal persons and up to 10% of the company's audited annual turnover for aggravated breaches; withdrawal of authorisation; confiscation of illegal proceeds.

Director personal liability: Directors exposed to personal fines up to EUR 40,000; culpable participation in AML breaches carries imprisonment up to 5 years; board members may be banned from financial services operations and have their Estonian and EU travel rights restricted during criminal proceedings.
Transfer Pricing and Withholding Misreporting

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment to arm's length; the adjustment is deemed a distribution of profit taxed at 20/80 (with 14/86 unavailable); penalty interest at 0.06% per day; administrative fine for false declaration; potential criminal liability if intentional.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the TSD and TP documentation face secondary liability for the additional tax; if documents are forged, imprisonment up to 3 years; the tax authority may enforce against the directors' personal bank accounts and restrict cross-border movement.
