Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Available election forms

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

22%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

€40,000 taxable turnover per calendar year, unless the entity performs reverse-charge or exempt-with-credit supplies triggering immediate registration

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported from Estonia to a non-EU destination qualify for 0% VAT if the exporter obtains documentary proof of export within 90 days. Certain offshore B2B services, including digital and advisory services, are zero-rated on the basis of the customer's business status and place-of-supply rules. Proof of actual payment and foreign exchange repatriation is expected as part of the documentary chain.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD), Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

Electronic invoicing is mandatory for B2G transactions using nationally standardised e-invoice formats. No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping applies for private-sector invoices. VAT returns and schedules must be filed electronically via e-MTA with structured digital reporting; direct API access is available for authorised software providers.

Fraudulent zero-rating claims are treated as tax evasion. Penalties include a 50% tax surcharge, statutory interest on the underpaid VAT, and material fines under the Taxation Act. Criminal liability and retrospective clawback of the zero-rated amount plus penalties apply in cases involving forged export documents or sham offshore service structures.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia maintains unrestricted current-account and capital-account convertibility as a euro-area member. There is no exchange-control statute, no central-bank approval gate for commercial transactions, and no statutory profit-remittance restriction on dividends, interest, royalties, intra-group loan repayments, or liquidation proceeds payable to non-resident shareholders. The Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union Articles 63-65 guarantee free movement of capital within the Union and with third countries, subject only to prudential and AML safeguards. For this euro-denominated Web3 foundation, the material friction is not statutory FX authorisation but bank-level enhanced due diligence on token-originated funds; conversion between USD/USDC and EUR is executed at freely market-determined rates with the ECB reference rate as the transparent benchmark.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Estonia imposes no statutory surrender ratio, no repatriation deadline, and no mandatory conversion of export proceeds or foreign-currency earnings for any resident entity. As a euro-area member, Estonia applies Regulation (EC) No 184/2005 on balance-of-payments statistics, under which resident banks code cross-border payments using the European standardised purpose-code (SPC) framework for Eesti Pank statistical reporting; however, no central-bank retention, conversion, or repatriation obligation exists.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia), a Eurosystem member central bank operating as the national component of the European Central Bank and, since 1 January 2025, the single supervisory and licensing authority following the merger of the former Finantsinspektsioon (Estonian Financial Supervision Authority) into Eesti Pank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low-to-Medium feasibility. Estonian-authorised electronic-money institutions and payment institutions may lawfully onboard the foundation, and remote account opening with a digital EMI is technically permitted under Estonia's Payment Institutions and E-money Institutions Act. In practice, however, mainstream EU digital EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business, Monese, N26) categorically reject Web3 foundations, token-treasury flows, multisig withdrawals, and crypto-adjacent counterparties under their global risk policies. A digital EMI account is viable only where the provider holds an Estonian or EU e-money licence and maintains an explicit crypto-asset acceptance policy with tiered limits; such an account should be used exclusively as a secondary disbursement and FX-conversion channel and never as the primary escrow, treasury, or clearing rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing for the euro-area entity requires EUR settlement via SWIFT MT103 for external (non-EEA) transfers or SEPA Credit Transfer / SEPA Instant for EU-originating flows, with the Estonian balance-of-payments standardised purpose code (under the ECB/BPM6 classification, reflecting equity capital contribution, intercompany loan, or capital transfer respectively) stated in the payment reference, the entity's KAR registry code included in the remittance information field, and the underlying capital-contribution resolution, grant agreement, or donation deed attached as standing transactional evidence. For USDC/USD inbound capital, the compliant sequence is: (i) transfer into a MiCA-compliant or nationally transitional EU-authorised VASP exchange, (ii) conversion to EUR at transparent market rates, and (iii) onward SEPA Instant credit to the Estonian IBAN. A direct ERC-20 USDC deposit into the foundation multisig is permitted only after a pre-signed treasury resolution and full chain-analytics clearance, with the bank reconciliation attaching the wallet address and transaction hash to the entity balance sheet.Primary clearing rails are SEPA Credit Transfer and SEPA Instant for euro-area operational payments and supplier invoices, SWIFT MT103 for non-EEA and USD-denominated disbursements, and SWIFT MT202 COV for interbank cover where the outbound instruction sits behind a correspondent relay. Commercial Letters of Credit governed by UCP 600 are available for high-value vendor settlement above EUR 100,000 where documentary protection is contractually required. In operational practice, outbound token-rail disbursements are routed VASP-to-bank: an exchange withdrawal with Travel Rule disclosure, conversion of the relevant amount to EUR, and final SEPA/SWIFT transfer to the verified beneficiary. Ancillary protocol-level operational transfers are restricted to the audited multisig vault and must be pre-approved in the banking mandate and reflected in the quarterly transaction monitoring report.Outright EUR/USD forward contracts documented under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with a licensed credit institutionFX swaps (spot-forward combinations) for treasury liquidity repositioning between EUR and USDCross-currency swaps (EUR/USD) for long-dated balance-sheet hedging of USD and USDC-denominated foundation reservesNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) exclusively for settlement exposure in non-convertible or thinly traded emerging-market currencies where onshore deliverable forwards are unavailableListed EUR/USD options for premium-contained downside protection; OTC options permissible only below the applicable EMIR clearing threshold and with internal treasury governance approval

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% on distributed profits (gross-up basis; 25% effective on the net distribution); 0% on retained and reinvested profits; qualifying regular periodic dividends taxed at 14/86 (16.28% effective).
