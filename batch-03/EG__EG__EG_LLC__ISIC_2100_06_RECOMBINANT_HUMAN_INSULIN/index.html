Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) of the Arab Republic of Egypt, Ministry of Finance. Central official portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg; e-filing and unified tax-file portal: https://tax.eta.gov.eg; electronic invoice and receipt clearance portal: https://invoice.eta.gov.eg.

E-government filing portal

An Egyptian-incorporated LLC is formed through the General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI), then must apply to the ETA within the statutory registration window (generally 30 days from commercial registration) to obtain the Unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) and the Tax Card (Bitaka Dharbiya); the same TIN serves as the CIT ID, VAT ID, stamp-duty file and e-invoicing identifier. A foreign entity must first obtain GAFI approval, appoint a resident tax representative, file the ETA registration petition with the certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, board resolution, audited opening balance sheet and proof of Egyptian premises, and complete e-invoicing onboarding; VAT registration is filed on the same unified file once annual taxable turnover exceeds EGP 500,000.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Egypt has no US-style check-the-box election; classification is fixed by legal form, so an EG LLC always files as a juridical person. Available optional regimes: (i) SME presumptive tax under Law No. 176 of 2023, allowing a qualifying manufacturing LLC (revenue below the MSME ceilings) to elect for a five-year term a simplified turnover-based tax instead of normal CIT/VAT accounting; (ii) Suez Canal Economic Zone (SCZone) free-zone licence, which electively achieves Tax_Exempt_Zone treatment (0% CIT and export-oriented zero-rating) versus mainland taxation; (iii) investment incentives under Investment Law 72 of 2017 (tax credits or accelerated depreciation, not income-tax holidays except in special zones). Hybrid mismatch elections are unavailable; Egypt has not enacted OECD hybrid mismatch or GloBE Pillar Two rules, and GAAR plus thin-capitalisation provisions recharacterise artificial debt/equity or transparent structures.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration applies where annual taxable supplies of goods or services exceed EGP 500,000, with registration to be submitted within the statutory registration window after crossing the threshold; voluntary registration is permitted where annual taxable turnover exceeds EGP 150,000. The company must hold VAT status from the commencement of invoicing activity, and export revenue is treated within the scope of the taxable-supplies calculus for threshold purposes as provided in the VAT law and executive regulations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT export zero-rating is claimable for recombinant human insulin and intermediates when: (i) the goods leave Egyptian customs territory under an approved electronic export declaration (SAD) to a non-resident consignee; (ii) the supply is genuinely cross-border, not a free-zone diversion or circular shipment; (iii) commercial and transport documents are consistent; (iv) proceeds are received in freely convertible foreign currency through the Egyptian banking system within the 180-day CBE deadline (or documented exception); and (v) for pharmaceutical output, the Ministry of Health export/GMP certificates accompany the file. Contractual manufacturing or technical services physically performed in Egypt for a foreign principal may also qualify as zero-rated offshore services where the beneficiary and place-of-use tests are met; domestic insulin sales may be VAT-exempt under the medicines schedule, which does not prejudice the export zero-rating claim.

  • Electronic customs export declaration (SAD/AES) validated by the Egyptian Customs Authority, showing HS code 3004.31 or 3004.39 and the pharmaceutical export reason code
  • Commercial invoice and packing list (Arabic/English) fully reconciling with the customs value, the e-invoice XML data and bank payment evidence
  • Clean transport document - signed bill of lading, multimodal bill or air waybill - proving delivery to the named non-resident destination
  • Inward remittance certificate (bank statement or central-bank FX booking) evidencing that export proceeds arrived within the 180-day repatriation deadline
  • ETA-cleared export e-invoice (XML with CIN and QR code) included in the VAT return zero-rated export schedule
  • Regulatory export file: Ministry of Health sanitary/GMP export certificate, batch release certificate and any ETA decision on the VAT schedule treatment of the insulin analog

Mandatory real-time fiscal clearance: all VAT-registered taxpayers must issue invoices exclusively through the ETA Central Invoice Network (CIN), either via direct API integration (JSON/XML) or through an approved billing service provider, with digital signature, and must obtain an ETA-returned unique CIN and QR fiscal stamp before invoice delivery; rejected or unreported invoices have no VAT legal effect and cannot support input VAT deduction or export refunds. The ETA runs automatic integrity and business-rule validation (TIN matching, product HS code, price and VAT reconciliation) and may suspend clearance, issue audit flags, or block refund files on mismatch; e-receipt clearance is additionally mandated for B2C sales by pharmacies and wholesalers.

The ETA reconciles e-invoice clearance records, SAD filings, shipping manifests, insurance files and the banking system's SWIFT/repatriation database before accepting input VAT refunds. For any export claim lacking the complete documentation bundle or where proceeds are late or unrepatriated, the ETA re-classifies the supply as domestic, re-computes VAT at 14%, withdraws the refund and applies delay interest plus an evasion-linked penalty of up to double the tax; the criminal tax-evasion provisions triggered by forged export documents or sham circular shipment schemes carry imprisonment of not less than three years, monetary penalties, suspension of the tax card and disqualification from future zero-rating. As a special-licence high-risk pharma exporter, the entity may also be required to maintain a bank guarantee equal to the annual claimed refund amount during audit.

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime under the Central Bank of Egypt. Current account transactions, including profit remittances, dividends, and import payments, are generally convertible with standard documentation and minimal restrictions. Capital account transactions, such as equity investments and portfolio flows, require prior CBE approval and are subject to repatriation limits and foreign exchange surrender obligations. For a recombinant human insulin manufacturing LLC, profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are permissible subject to CBE notification, beneficial ownership verification, and 20% dividend withholding tax. All foreign currency inflows must ultimately be surrendered to authorized banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export receipts denominated in foreign currency must be deposited into the exporter's bank account within 90 days of shipment and surrendered to the bank for conversion into Egyptian Pounds at the prevailing CBE reference rate. Surrender is mandatory at 100% for most trade transactions. Non-compliance may result in penalties, license suspension, and reporting to the CBE. Supporting documentation including commercial invoices, packing lists, and certificates of origin is required for verification.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt
