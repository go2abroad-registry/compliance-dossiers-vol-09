Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance; official e-Tax portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg; electronic invoice clearance portal: https://einvoice.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must first register commercially in Egypt through a branch or representative office, obtain a commercial registration certificate and legal presence, then apply to the ETA for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and tax card. VAT registration is mandatory where taxable supplies exceed EGP 500,000 per annum; exporters may register voluntarily to claim zero-rating and refunds.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (Egyptian LLC is a resident corporate taxpayer; profits are taxed at entity level; no default pass-through treatment for income tax purposes). — Foreign entities must first register commercially in Egypt through a branch or representative office, obtain a commercial registration certificate and legal presence, then apply to the ETA for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and tax card. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (Egyptian LLC is a resident corporate taxpayer; profits are taxed at entity level; no default pass-through treatment for income tax purposes).

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available under Egyptian tax law. Relevant elections include voluntary VAT registration, VAT tax-group registration for related parties with ETA approval, and selection of the accounting/tax year subject to ETA authorisation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 annual value of taxable supplies of goods and services; businesses making solely zero-rated exports may register voluntarily for refund purposes.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from Egypt, deemed exports used in producing exported goods, international transport and related services, and certain services supplied to non-residents for use entirely outside Egypt. The taxpayer must prove physical export, receive foreign-currency payment through the Egyptian banking system, file the customs export declaration, and submit all supporting documentation to the ETA. Domestic construction of grain silos and elevators inside Egypt is not zero-rated unless performed offshore or supplied under a qualifying export contract.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Egypt has a mandatory electronic invoicing and e-receipt system. VAT-registered taxpayers are phased into the ETA-integrated e-invoice platform; each invoice must be submitted in real time via API in XML/JSON format, validated by the ETA, and issued with a unique invoice ID, digital signature, and QR code. E-receipts are mandatory for business-to-consumer sales.

The ETA may disallow zero-rating, reassess VAT, impose interest, levy penalties up to 100% of the tax due, and initiate criminal proceedings for fraudulent export zero-rating claims. Tax evasion sanctions include imprisonment and fines of up to EGP 100,000, with enhanced penalties for forged documentation and false export declarations. Refund claims remain subject to post-clearance documentary audit and clawback if export evidence or foreign-currency repatriation is not sustained.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Egyptian pound (EGP) is fully convertible for current-account transactions following the CBE's exchange-rate unification on 6 March 2024; the interbank rate is market-driven and banks are free to price FX. Profit and dividend remittances for non-resident shareholders are legally unrestricted under Investment Law No. 72 of 2017, but the remitting bank requires audited financial statements, tax clearance, and a CBE Balance-of-Payments transaction declaration. Capital-account transactions (equity injections, intra-group loans, LC disbursements and hedging) are administratively validated: each transfer needs underlying EPC documentation, source-of-funds evidence, and the bank's FX Position reporting. Practical hurdles include residual interbank USD liquidity stress, 30-to-90-day settlement latency for large remittances during tight windows, heightened UBO verification following Egypt's FATF Increased Monitoring status, and the 4:1 debt-to-equity thin-capitalization ceiling for related-party interest deductibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods export proceeds must be repatriated through the Egyptian banking system within 90 days from the bill of lading date; service export proceeds, including EPC construction milestones billed to non-residents, must be collected within 180 days from the invoice date. The mandatory surrender ratio is nil post-March 2024; exporters may retain 100% of proceeds in foreign-currency accounts, convert at interbank rates, or reuse them for documented import and operational payments. Banks maintain an export-collection log and file CBE event reports when collection exceeds the statutory window; extension requires documented bank-approved rationale.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE), together with the General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI) for foreign-capital instruments and the Egyptian Financial Regulatory Authority for non-bank services. The binding legal anchors are Banking Law No. 194 of 2020, AML/CFT Law No. 80 of 2002 as amended by Law No. 44 of 2022, and Investment Law No. 72 of 2017.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant route: (i) the foreign parent executes SWIFT MT103 in USD/EUR/EGP with Field 70 stating 'Share Capital Contribution to EG_LLC — Board Resolution No. ___ dated ___ — Investment Law No. 72 of 2017'; (ii) the receiving Egyptian bank credits the capital escrow account, issues an Inward Transfer Advice, and files the CBE Balance-of-Payments direct-investment entry categorized as 'Share Capital — Direct Investment Inflow'; (iii) the LLC's certified public accountant verifies the capital receipt and completes the GAFI/Commercial Registry capital-increase amendment within 60 days; (iv) after registration, funds roll into the operational account for mobilization, materials, and payroll. Performance and advance-payment guarantees to the employer are delivered as SBLC MT760 or direct bank guarantee, commonly 10% performance security and 10% mobilization advance recovery.
