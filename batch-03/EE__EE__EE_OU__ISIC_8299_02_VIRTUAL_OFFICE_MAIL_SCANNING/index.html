Applicable AML statute: Estonian Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Prevention Act (Rahapesu ja terrorismi rahastamise tõkestamise seadus; MLTFPA) of 26 October 2017, as amended, implementing EU AMLD4/AMLD5; Commercial Code (Äriseadustik) sections 139.1–139.5 on beneficial-owner identification and register filing; State Fees Act (Riigilõivuseadus); supervisory authority: Estonian Financial Intelligence Unit (Rahapesu andmebüroo, RAB) of the Police and Border Guard Board for AML/CFT licensing and supervision of trust and company service providers (TCSPs); register administration: Centre of Registers and Information Systems (Äriregistri keskus, RIK). Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Permitted with conditions: every Osaühing must have a registered office address in Estonia (Commercial Code section 62); a third-party licensed CMRA/virtual-office address is acceptable for the CMRA company itself provided (i) the address is a physically existing location where legal documents and court mail can be served, (ii) the provider holds a valid RAB-issued TCSP activity licence, and (iii) the company maintains actual local economic substance — leased mail-scanning equipment, defined mail-processing procedures, records of mail receipt, and a responsive registered-agent arrangement. Fictitious addresses and post-office-box-only addresses are rejected by the registrar. As the CMRA business itself is subject to FIU licensing under MLTFPA section 94(31), the licensee must retain client identification records and scanned-mail metadata for five years after termination of the client relationship.

✓ Partner licensed company-secretary channels provide local registered address and government mail forwarding.

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities establishing an Estonian OÜ must register the company in the Estonian Commercial Register (Äriregister) to obtain the 8-digit registry code serving as the statutory TIN. VAT identification number is assigned automatically upon company registration if turnover triggers liability; foreign owners submit incorporation documents, articles of association, and proof of address through the ETCB online portal.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to exports of goods outside the EU and to services supplied to non-EU customers, provided the customer is VAT-registered or non-established in the EU and the service is effectively used outside the EU.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia operates under full capital account convertibility and current account convertibility pursuant to EU acquis and national law. Resident companies enjoy unrestricted profit remittances to shareholders with no capital controls, FX surrender obligations, or repatriation restrictions. Non-resident ownership is permitted without additional FX approvals.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or conversion of export proceeds required under Estonian or EU regulations. Companies may retain and utilize export receipts freely provided full AML/KYC documentation and tax compliance are maintained. No statutory timelines or mandatory conversion ratios apply.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Estonia (Eesti Pank)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Estonia authorizes Electronic Money Institutions under EU Payment Services Directive and national FSA oversight. Cross-border digital EMIs are feasible subject to strict capital, governance, and ongoing reporting obligations. Offshore digital EMI operations remain viable for licensed entities but require enhanced AML/CFT controls and potential local agent arrangements.
