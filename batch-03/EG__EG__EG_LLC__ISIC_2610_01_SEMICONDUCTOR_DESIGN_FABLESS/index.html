Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No fixed statutory minimum capital is prescribed for an LLC under Companies Law No. 159/1981; the authorized capital and the subscribed, allotted shares are stated in the Memorandum of Association. All shares must be fully subscribed; no escrow deposit is mandatory, but the subscription funds or in-kind contribution documentation must be evidenced at registration and confirmed in the first board resolution.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defense Authority fire-safety certificate for all occupied premises, including certified fire extinguishers, emergency exits, smoke alarms, and electrical load compliance; Labor Office workplace-safety clearance under Labor Law No. 12 of 2003 covering interior ventilation, electricity permits, workstation ergonomics, and occupational risk assessment

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance, Cairo; official e-portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg; e-invoicing and e-receipts platform: https://einvoicing.eta.gov.eg with API-based transaction clearance.

E-government filing portal

After commercial registration with GAFI/Egyptian Commercial Registry, the foreign-owned Egyptian LLC must apply for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and statutory VAT ID via the ETA online tax card system within 30 days of commencing activities. The e-application requires: certified copy of the Memorandum of Association, Commercial Registration Certificate, board resolution appointing the tax representative, proof of Egyptian registered office, valid director identification, and bank confirmation of capital contribution. Upon issuance, the TIN/VAT ID is automatically activated for e-invoicing after obtaining a qualified digital signature certificate; VAT registration is filed separately on the same ETA portal upon crossing the statutory registration threshold.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — After commercial registration with GAFI/Egyptian Commercial Registry, the foreign-owned Egyptian LLC must apply for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and statutory VAT ID via the ETA online tax card system within 30 days of commencing activities. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch pass-through election exists in Egypt. An Egyptian LLC is mandatorily a separate taxable resident entity taxed on worldwide income at the corporate rate (22.5% top rate, lower brackets for small business under the simplified regime). Elections available are limited to: (i) the simplified tax regime for qualifying micro/small entities under ETA thresholds; (ii) tax treaty relief through an Egyptian tax residence certificate; (iii) licensing as a free-zone company under GAFI with special VAT/customs treatment; and (iv) selection of accounting method for VAT input deduction, subject to ETA approval.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 annual taxable turnover for goods; EGP 250,000 annual taxable turnover for services; compulsory registration applies once cumulative supply exceeds the relevant threshold; voluntary registration is allowed below the threshold at ETA discretion.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Physical export of semiconductor goods and cross-border fabless services/IP licensing supplied to foreign customers is zero-rated under Article 17 of the VAT Law No. 67 of 2016, provided: (i) tangible goods physically leave Egypt and are declared to customs; (ii) design, R&D, and IP-licensing services are supplied to a non-resident customer with economic use wholly outside Egypt; (iii) consideration is paid in foreign currency and remitted to an Egyptian bank account; (iv) documentary evidence is retained with the ETA; and export proceeds repatriation occurs within the statutory period.

  • Customs Export Declaration (official customs single-window export declaration stating HS code 8542 or relevant semiconductor classification)
  • Commercial Invoice and Export/Licensing Contract with the foreign buyer
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical exit from Egypt
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or CBE bank confirmation of foreign-currency repatriation
  • ETA zero-rated e-invoice batch report bearing the digital QR/UUID stamp

Egypt operates a mandatory real-time e-invoicing/e-receipt clearance regime in phased roll-out for all ETA-registered taxpayers. Every invoice issued by the LLC must be transmitted through an ETA-approved ERP/API integration to the ETA central clearance system, validated within seconds, and digitally stamped with a Unique UUID and QR code. The invoice file must include Arabic content and amounts in EGP; rejected or missing invoices are treated as unlawful and deny the buyer's input VAT deduction. B2B sales, export invoices, and credit/debit notes are all subject to the same fiscal clearance and 5-year archival mandate.

The ETA applies a targeted audit ledger for all zero-rated and refunded exports, cross-checking the customs exit record, logistics movement, e-invoice clearance, and Central Bank of Egypt FX remittance statements. Any false, incomplete, or tardy zero-rating claim triggers full clawback of the VAT refund, statutory interest from the original refund date, an administrative fine of up to 100% of the evaded tax, and, in cases of proved fraud or falsified documentation, criminal penalties including imprisonment of up to 5 years and debarment from the export refund fast-track.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt operates a managed-flexible, market-determined exchange-rate regime following the March 2024 unified float supported by the IMF Extended Fund Facility. Current-account payments for trade, services, IP royalties, and profit repatriation are legally transferable against adequate documentation. Capital-account transactions such as equity injections, shareholder loans, and portfolio flows remain subject to CBE registration, bank-level approval, and practical sensitivity to dollar-liquidity cycles. Profit remittance is legally guaranteed under Article 13 of Investment Law No. 72 of 2017 but in bank practice requires audited financial statements, board-approved dividend distribution, settlement of the 10% non-resident dividend withholding tax, and ETA tax-clearance certification; post-float, documentary-complete remittances are converted and cleared at the unified market rate, although invisible rationing can emerge during severe FX stress.

Export proceeds surrender rules

CBE Circular 117 of 2022, as tightened during 2023, had imposed a mandatory surrender ratio on export proceeds rising from 50% to 100% together with a repatriation window aligned to the standard 180-day term. Following the March 6, 2024 foreign-exchange liberalization, the mandatory conversion-to-bank ratio has been suspended effectively to zero; exporters must nonetheless repatriate the full export invoice value through the collecting Egyptian bank within the statutory 180 days calculated from the bill-of-lading or air-waybill date. The underlying trade contracts, ACI/ACID customs declarations, shipping documents, and SWIFT MT103 advices must be retained for the 5-year bank record-keeping period, and non-compliance exposes the exporter to CBE penalty lists and cancellation of bank credit facilities.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE), operating under the Central Bank and Banking System Law No. 88 of 2003 as consolidated and extensively amended by Law No. 194 of 2020; the CBE is the sole foreign-exchange licensing and monetary authority, while the General Authority for Investment (GAFI) approves FDI registrations under Investment Law No. 72 of 2017 and the Egyptian Tax Authority enforces fiscal clearances precedent to cross-border remittances.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

LOW-MEDIUM as a supplement only: offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business, Payoneer, and Airwallex hold no CBE license, cannot open EGP accounts, cannot issue Egyptian IBANs, and cannot serve as the primary collection account for Egyptian-domiciled receivables. Using them as the principal treasury channel breaches CBE repatriation obligations and the documentary-evidence rule for IP-royalty inflows. They are defensible solely as auxiliary payout rails for foreign foundry subcontractors and global contractor payments, provided the Egyptian operating account at a licensed bank remains the account of record for Egyptian revenue and every EMI drawdown is mirrored by a corresponding SWIFT credit into Egypt within the statutory repatriation period.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The parent or shareholder executes a SWIFT MT103 wire to the Egyptian LLC's Capital Escrow FDI sub-account inside a licensed bank using the BPM6 purpose description 'Direct Investment - Equity Capital Injection (Increase of Capital)'. The bank files the CBE FDI registration on the company's behalf, and the credit is posted as restricted capital until the GAFI certificate of capital contribution is uploaded. The verification bundle comprises board minutes on the capital increase, updated articles of association registered with GAFI, the bank's FDI registration form, a funds-application commitment letter, and, where software tools or intangibles are contributed in-kind, a certified valuation report.For imports of wafer fabrication and design-tool services the compliant rails are: (i) a commercial documentary credit issued under SWIFT MT700 from the Egyptian bank to the foundry's bank; or (ii) following the December 2023 liberalization, direct telegraphic transfer under SWIFT MT103 upon verified FX availability and bank credit and source-of-funds approval. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EGP 500,000,000 (approximately USD 10 million) for local file requirements

Economic substance scope: Mandatory Economic Substance Regulations apply to companies engaged in IP holding, distribution, or certain service activities; requires adequate physical presence, core income-generating activities, and substance in Egypt

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution with fines up to 3 times the evaded tax amount, imprisonment for up to 5 years, and confiscation of assets

Director personal liability: Directors may face personal criminal liability, including imprisonment and disqualification from office
Failure to Maintain Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalties, dissolution of the company, and back taxation with interest

Director personal liability: Personal fines and potential imprisonment for responsible officers
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Fines up to EGP 1 million or 10% of revenue, plus adjustments to taxable income

Director personal liability: Personal liability for fines and potential criminal charges if willful
