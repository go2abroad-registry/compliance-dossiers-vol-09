Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance; unified tax administration and e-services via https://www.eta.gov.eg and the e-invoicing platform https://www.invoicing.eta.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned LLCs must open an ETA tax file and obtain a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) covering CIT, VAT, WHT and stamp duty. Procedure: (1) incorporate with GAFI under Companies Law 159/1981 and Investment Law 72/2017 (no minimum capital, 100% foreign ownership permitted) and obtain the Commercial Register extract; (2) before or promptly upon commencement, in any event within 30 days of incorporation/commencement, file the ETA registration application at the competent tax office (Mamouria), attaching the commercial register, certified and Arabic-notarized constitutional documents of the foreign parent, power of attorney, managers' passports, and proof of Egyptian registered premises; (3) ETA issues the unified TIN valid for all taxes; (4) register for VAT within the statutory deadline after exceeding the EGP 500,000 annual threshold; (5) complete e-invoicing activation and certification of the API/SFTP clearance channel before issuing invoices.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - an Egyptian LLC is a resident juridical person taxed at entity level on worldwide income at 22.5% standard CIT (Law 91/2005 as amended); no pass-through default exists; SCZone and free-zone incentives apply only via separate license.

Available election forms

Egypt has no check-the-box or pass-through election; LLC status is mandatorily taxable at entity level. Available elections: (1) consolidated group taxation for resident companies with 90% or greater direct/indirect ownership (ETA advance approval required); (2) small-supplier simplified VAT accounting for turnovers below the registration threshold; (3) depreciation and inventory method elections made with the first return and changeable only with ETA consent; (4) advance pricing/ruling application for tax classification and transfer-pricing treatment; (5) no unilateral hybrid mismatch election - Egypt applies its domestic rules and the BEPS MLI principal-purpose test.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 annual taxable turnover (mandatory VAT registration threshold); voluntary registration available below the threshold in accordance with ETA rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supplier may claim 0% VAT on: (1) goods physically exported from Egypt with customs exit and transfer of title to a non-resident; (2) services supplied to non-residents where the place of supply is Egypt but the benefit is enjoyed outside Egypt, including services directly related to exported goods; and (3) goods placed in free zones for export. The exporter must prove actual export via customs records, contract, invoices and CBE-compliant receipt of proceeds; input VAT attributable to zero-rated exports remains creditable and is refundable through ETA's VAT refund dossier.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Mandatory clearance-based e-invoicing under ETA Decision 444/2021 and successive phasing: VAT-registered taxpayers must transmit each tax invoice to the ETA platform in XML/JSON via web portal, SFTP or certified API within 24 hours of issuance; ETA validates in near real time and issues a UUID plus QR code digitally stamping the invoice before legal effect; unstamped invoices are void and disallow input VAT recovery. The e-receipt (coded receipt) mandate is phased in for B2C sales of household electrical appliances, requiring near-real-time encrypted transmission from certified cash registers or POS software.

ETA audits every export zero-rating claim by cross-matching the Customs Authority export declaration (Ashal platform) with the bank FX remittance certificate; documentary gaps or delayed remittances trigger reassessment at the standard 14% rate with a 2% monthly late-payment surcharge and 5-year retroactive assessment. Fraudulent or sham export documentation subjects the exporter to clawback of all refunded VAT, administrative penalties and surcharges under Law 188/2022 (TAFITA), suspension of e-invoicing and export clearance privileges, public blacklisting by ETA/customs, and criminal prosecution with imprisonment up to 5 years and/or fines up to EGP 10 million.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Current-account transactions (trade in goods, services, freight, and audited profit dividends) are freely convertible at the unified interbank EGP/USD rate following the March 2024 managed-float reform under the IMF Extended Fund Facility; no prior CBE license is required for bona fide trade remittances. Capital-account flows (foreign equity subscription, intercompany shareholder loans, portfolio inflows, and FX hedging premiums) remain document-intensive: the receiving bank must verify the LLC's GAFI registration file, classify the flow under Egypt's BPM6-based reporting framework, and issue a CBE capital-entry certificate (Shahadat Dukhul Ra's Al-Mal) for eventual repatriation. Profit-remittance friction is moderate: dividends require IFRS-compliant audited financial statements, general-assembly minutes, and an Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) tax-clearance certificate; no separate CBE exchange-control approval is required once the bank's documentary file is complete. If the LLC were instead licensed within the Suez Canal Economic Zone (SCZONE), additional FX waiver regimes and zone-escrow account structures would apply; the present blueprint is built on a standard onshore LLC basis.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The pre-2024 mandatory export-proceeds surrender regime (historically 50-100% conversion at the official rate) has been repealed: since April 2024 EG_LLC may retain 100% of USD export receipts in its foreign-currency account. The statutory repatriation window nonetheless applies: proceeds from non-oil manufactured exports (ISIC 2750 robotic floor-cleaning appliances) must be credited to an Egyptian bank account within 30 calendar days of the transport-document date, extendable to 180 days for capital goods with prior bank approval. The receiving bank must match each receipt with the corresponding Egyptian customs export declaration submitted through the National Single Window (NAFEZA/AFSIS) and report the transaction to the CBE; late repatriation attracts fines and possible suspension of the exporter's commercial-registry file.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE; Al-Bank al-Markazi al-Masri) under Law No. 194 of 2020, acting through its Banking Operations Sector, Foreign Relations Sector, and the CBE Foreign-Exchange Committee; the General Authority for Investment (GAFI) coordinates investment-related FX documentation for foreign-owned LLCs.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW for primary treasury operations. Foreign digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, 3S Money) are not licensed by the CBE under Law No. 194 of 2020 as banks or payment institutions; they cannot hold EGP accounts, issue Egyptian IBANs, settle local trade, or lawfully receive export proceeds on behalf of an Egyptian exporter without breaching CBE export-repatriation reporting. Legally permissible ancillary deployment is limited to offshore concentration accounts for non-Egyptian supplier payments invoiced in USD/CNH, provided the underlying import settlement into Egypt clears through a licensed Egyptian correspondent bank and the EMI does not intermediate any customer-facing Egyptian payment relationship.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing: (1) the non-resident shareholder instructs its overseas bank to execute SWIFT MT103/MT202 to EG_LLC's USD FCA at the receiving Egyptian tier-1 bank; field 70 must read 'EQUITY CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION EG_LLC / Companies Law 4/2022'; (2) the receiving bank validates the LLC's GAFI file and post-incorporation shareholders register, classifies the transfer under BPM6 direct-investment equity (FDI equity) using the local CBE purpose code for capital inflows, and issues the CBE capital-entry certificate on the same value date; (3) for larger equity tranches, the bank requests the general-assembly resolution and a capital-deployment plan covering robotic-vacuum production equipment, tooling, and working capital; (4) any EGP conversion takes place at the unified interbank rate and is reported in the bank's daily CBE FX position statement. The capital-entry certificate is the controlling document for all future dividend and capital repatriation and must be preserved in the treasury archive.Two compliant clearing rails govern EG_LLC's supplier payments: TT Wire and Commercial LC. (1) TT Wire (SWIFT MT103) is used for supplier invoices below USD 50,000 and for repeat low-risk vendors; the narrative must reference the proforma invoice number and ACI/NAFEZA import registration (ACR number) to permit Egyptian customs release; settlement is effected in USD through New York or London correspondents or in CNH through offshore renminbi clearing. (2) Commercial Letter of Credit (SWIFT MT700, UCP600/ISBP745) is the mandatory rail for bulk component shipments from PRC/HK/Taiwan exceeding USD 50,000; the LLC issues irrevocable sight or deferred-payment LCs (up to 180 days tenor) with a 15-30% cash margin, and confirmation by a top-tier PRC or Hong Kong bank is recommended to eliminate documentary-discrepancy and transit risk. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EGP 10,000,000 aggregate related-party transactions (goods, services, intangibles, or financing) triggers the mandatory Local File. Master File is required where the Egyptian LLC belongs to a multinational group with consolidated revenue of at least EGP 100,000,000. CbCR filing applies when group consolidated revenue exceeds EGP 20,000,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Egypt does not apply UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations, but domestic substance is tested through the place of effective management doctrine, the arm's-length principle, and the General Anti-Abuse Rule under Article 103 of Law 91/2005. An Egyptian LLC manufacturing robotic vacuum appliances must evidence real operations in Egypt: board meetings held in Egypt, engineering and assembly activity, employees covered by Egyptian social insurance, inventory, and actual production. A shell with no real substance will be disregarded and transactions recharacterised.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment from 3 to 7 years; fine equal to double the evaded tax; 2% monthly late-payment surcharge on unpaid tax; commercial registration cancellation; tax lien over the LLC's assets.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax where they directed or facilitated the evasion. Courts may issue travel bans, freeze personal assets, and order confiscation of properties purchased with hidden profits.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement / Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: TP recharacterisation plus additional tax at 22.5%, 2% monthly delay surcharge, and administrative penalty of up to 20% of the adjustment. Wilful manipulation can be prosecuted as tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the return with knowledge of false TP certifications are personally liable for the additional tax and penalties. Egyptian courts may pierce the veil and render a personal travel ban until settlement.
Profit Repatriation / Dividend WHT Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Assessment of unpaid WHT plus a penalty equal to 100% of the unpaid amount and 2% monthly overdue surcharge; ETA may attach the LLC's local bank accounts and receivables.

Director personal liability: Directors who vote a dividend while withholding tax is unpaid are personally liable to the extent of the distribution, especially where the LLC is insolvent, and may face travel restrictions pending payment.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Recharacterisation under Article 103 GAAR; disallowance of all deductions; assessment on a deemed profit basis for up to 5 years; penalty equal to double the tax due; possible compulsory dissolution under the Companies Law.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors and shareholding nominees are jointly and severally liable for 100% of the unpaid tax and fines; may be disqualified from board service for 5 years and referred to public prosecution.
AML / Money Laundering through Tax-Offshore Flows

Statutory sanction: Under Law No. 80 of 2002, imprisonment from 3 to 15 years and a fine ranging from EGP 100,000 to EGP 5,000,000; mandatory confiscation of laundered funds and related assets.

Director personal liability: Regulators may freeze personal assets, issue travel bans, and impose 5-10 year directorship disqualification. Directors can be personally ordered to forfeit unexplained wealth connected to the laundering scheme.
