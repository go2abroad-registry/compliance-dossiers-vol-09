Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance - Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA); taxpayer portal: https://portal.eta.gov.eg; official information portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

A foreign LLC must register with ETA before commencing taxable activity. Submit Arabic-translated certificate of incorporation and constitutive documents, board resolution appointing local tax representative, power of attorney, Egyptian trade/commercial registration and office lease, identity documents, foreign currency remittance/payment instrument, and projected annual turnover statement. ETA issues a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) and a tax card; VAT registration is mandatory when annual taxable supplies exceed threshold and can be voluntary after ETA acceptance.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; resident Egyptian LLC is taxed at entity level on worldwide income as a juridical person; non-resident LLC operating through Egyptian PE is taxed on Egyptian-source income only. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; resident Egyptian LLC is taxed at entity level on worldwide income as a juridical person; non-resident LLC operating through Egyptian PE is taxed on Egyptian-source income only. No pass-through status is available for the standard LLC.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election is recognized under Egyptian law. Eligible small/micro enterprises may elect the simplified turnover-based tax regime under the Income Tax Law; a foreign investor may elect investment incentives in Suez Canal Economic Zone (SCZONE) free/tax-eligible zones that suspend taxes/VAT, but such election does not reclassify the LLC as a non-taxable pass-through. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: annual taxable turnover exceeding EGP 500,000; voluntary registration: taxable persons with annual turnover of at least EGP 250,000 may apply; additional ETA e-invoicing/e-receipt stage thresholds require digital registration irrespective of turnover once the taxpayer is selected.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAT-registered exporter may claim 0% VAT on exported goods where goods physically depart Egypt under a valid customs export declaration, sale is to a non-resident customer, proceeds are received in convertible foreign currency through Egyptian banks within the Central Bank of Egypt deadline, and the export sale is correctly declared in the VAT return. Exported services and offshore EPC/steel-structure assembly and engineering services supplied wholly to and consumed by a non-resident outside Egypt may also qualify for zero-rating if backed by contract, foreign currency remittance, and absence of economic use inside Egypt.

  • Final Customs Export Declaration approved by the Egyptian Customs Authority (Export SAD/Bayan) matching the VAT invoice values and HS codes
  • Original or certified copy Bill of Lading / Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Egypt and delivery to the foreign buyer or project site
  • Inward Foreign Exchange Remittance Certificate issued by the Egyptian commercial bank, evidencing receipt of export proceeds in convertible currency
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, sale contract/purchase order and, where applicable, letter of credit or SWIFT MT103 credit advice
  • ETA export supporting file containing the e-invoice export entries and, for related-party exports, transfer pricing documentation if requested

Egyptian Tax Authority mandates real-time e-invoicing for registered taxpayers in phased enrolment waves. B2B/B2G e-invoices and B2C e-receipts must be created via ETA-approved solutions, submitted to the ETA platform through public API in XML/JSON and validated/cleared before the transaction is legally complete; each cleared invoice receives an ETA QR code/digital stamp and unique UUID. Inability to obtain ETA clearance blocks the seller's input VAT deduction and is treated as non-issuance of invoice for VAT settlement purposes.

ETA and Egyptian Customs may jointly audit export files within the five-year VAT assessment horizon. Zero-rating is clawed back if the goods are not exported, proceeds are not repatriated by the deadline, or documentation is false or non-conforming; the clawback assesses output VAT at 14% plus monthly delay surcharge and revokes refunds/input credits. Fraudulent export declarations constitute tax evasion under VAT Law No. 67/2016 and attract criminal fines, imprisonment, automatic suspension of export and tax registration, and publication of assessment before the internal tax appeals committee.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt operates a partial-convertibility regime: the Egyptian Pound (EGP) is not freely deliverable offshore. Current-account flows (genuine import of steel inputs and export proceeds under EPC contracts) are freely convertible through CBE-licensed banks upon presentation of underlying commercial documents, a valid tax card, an import register entry, and an FX declaration. Capital-account flows are restricted: inward equity is open to 100% non-resident ownership under the GAFI investment regime and is registrable for future repatriation; external loans and derivative positions require CBE loan registration or prior authorization. Since the March 2024 exchange-rate liberalization under the IMF Extended Fund Facility, the dual-rate system and the foreign-investor repatriation queue were abolished, and the EGP floats at interbank market prices; genuine commercial FX demand clears within days. Profit remittance hurdles: an LLC must first appropriate the statutory reserve of 10% of annual profits until it reaches 50% of issued capital; distribution then requires audited financials, tax clearance or tax card, UBO and shareholder-registry declarations, and the CBE inward-investment registration certificate. Banks apply standard KYC and sanctions screening; dividends due to a foreign strategic parent are freely transferable once those documents are clean.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory collection window: export proceeds must be repatriated through CBE-licensed banks within 90 days of the bill of lading or airway bill date; an extension to 180 days may be approved by the CBE on documented receivables-financing grounds. Mandatory conversion: the historical fixed surrender ratios (e.g., the 50% surrender applicable before the 2016 float) no longer apply; exporters may retain 100% of proceeds in a foreign-currency account and convert at market when needed. Compliance: the exporting bank submits the proceeds record to the CBE electronic declaration system and reconciles the invoice value against the SWIFT credit; late collection, leakage, or retention outside Egypt triggers supervisory escalation and denial of subsequent trade-finance lines. For the steel prefab EPC model, milestone billings under the EPC contract are treated as export proceeds only where title to goods passes to a non-resident buyer and the goods physically leave Egypt under a customs export declaration.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) — Banking Supervision and Foreign Exchange Sectors, with the Egyptian Money Laundering Combating Unit (EMLCU) and the Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA) for non-bank oversight; statutory mandate under Central Bank and Banking System Law No. 194 of 2020 and AML Law No. 80 of 2002 (as amended).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: LOW as a primary treasury rail and MEDIUM only as an auxiliary collections channel. The CBE does not permit an offshore EMI to hold EGP accounts or issue EGP IBANs to a resident LLC; any payment institution operating in Egypt must be licensed under Law No. 194 of 2020 and the CBE payment-systems framework (Law No. 18 of 2019). A non-resident parent may use an offshore EMI to fund an equity contribution or shareholder loan by inbound wire, but the EGP treasury, tax payments, LC operations, and payroll must run through CBE-licensed bank accounts. Offshore EMIs also raise enhanced-due-diligence flags because intermediated flows obscure the FX declaration trail; settlement into an unregistered intermediary account will be rejected or delayed by Egyptian receiving banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant injection of foreign share capital or shareholder loan: wire via SWIFT MT103 from the non-resident parent's bank to the LLC's designated CBE-licensed account, using an IMF-referenced purpose code corresponding to 'share capital in a limited liability company' (equity injection) or 'intercompany loan'. The receiving bank verifies the GAFI incorporation certificate, the articles of association, the shareholders' ledger, and the board resolution, then issues an inward-remittance certificate and registers the flow on the CBE FX-declaration system. For shareholder loans exceeding USD 1 million, a CBE loan-registration form and drawdown schedule must be filed; conversion of injected USD or EUR into EGP must be executed at a licensed dealer at the prevailing interbank market rate, and the company capital register must be updated with the translated EGP amount. The route must be a direct bank-to-bank transfer free of intermediate offshore settlement accounts to preserve the FX declaration trail and the ability to remit dividends later.Primary rails: (1) irrevocable commercial letters of credit issued by SWIFT MT700 or MT710 for steel raw-material imports and prefabrication equipment, governed by UCP600 and typically on 90-days-sight terms; (2) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for services, freight, insurance, minor spares, and overseas professional fees; (3) documentary collection (DP or DA) only for trusted repeat suppliers. Bid and performance guarantees are issued as SWIFT MT760 against the same collateral line for EPC tenders. Egyptian banks require the underlying import application, customs registration, tax-card validity, and a stated end-use purpose for every outward remittance; FX disbursements are booked at the CBE interbank market rate and must match the approved purchase order to avoid FX declaration mismatches. Bank policy after the 2024 liberalization increasingly requires a matching purchase-order reference and documentary evidence of real trade-backed end use.FX outright forward (deliverable) against USD/EGP or EUR/EGP, tenor up to 12 months, available from CBE-licensed banks only where an underlying trade or receivable exposure is documented; settlement occurs at the agreed forward rate against the interbank fixing.Non-deliverable forward (NDF) on USD/EGP, arranged offshore or back-to-back through the onshore bank's foreign branch, used when EGP deliverability is constrained; final settlement in USD or EUR.Cross-currency swap (USD/EGP or EUR/EGP) matching steel-procurement import liabilities against EPC contract receivables; subject to CBE authorized-dealer review and documented exposure evidence.Plain-vanilla FX options (call or put) at bank discretion for corporate hedging with an approved treasury mandate; structured, exotic, or leveraged products are not permissible for non-bank EGP users.
