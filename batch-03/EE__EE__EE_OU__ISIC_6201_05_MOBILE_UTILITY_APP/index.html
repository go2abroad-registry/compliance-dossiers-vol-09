Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is required; Osaühing may be registered with zero share capital. If the founders nevertheless subscribe a stated share capital, the cash contribution must be paid to a temporary bank account in the company's name before filing, or to an escrow account held by the notary, and non-cash contributions require a valuation report. At least half of the subscribed cash capital may be transferred only after registration.

Funding & unlock path: Office premises must obtain a valid use and occupancy permit under the Building Code; the employer must carry out a workplace risk assessment per the Occupational Health and Safety Act and register the workforce with the Labour Inspectorate (Tööinspektsioon). Fire safety is verified through a self-declaration to the Rescue Board (Päästeamet), including fire evacuation signage and extinguisher placement, with third-party fire safety monitoring in shared commercial buildings.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet, MTA); e-MTA portal (e-maksuamet) at www.emta.ee; Commercial Register (Äriregister) for registration codes.

E-government filing portal

For an Osaühing (OÜ) incorporated in Estonia, the tax identity is the company's registry code, automatically assigned upon entry into the Estonian Commercial Register. No separate TIN application is required. For a foreign entity without a fixed establishment in Estonia, VAT registration is required before commencing taxable Estonian supplies; the application is made through the e-MTA portal using the prescribed VAT registration application, attaching certified copies of incorporation documents, the entity's home-country tax registration evidence, proof of director identity, and appointment of a local tax representative if the MTA requests one. The non-resident VAT ID is issued on the same legal basis as the Estonian registry code after the MTA validates the economic activity and agent details.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; specifically the Estonian distributed-profit territorial model: the OÜ is a legal person subject to corporate income tax on profit distributions, fringe benefits, gifts, transfer pricing adjustments, and non-business costs, while retained and undistributed profits are not taxed at entity level. — For an Osaühing (OÜ) incorporated in Estonia, the tax identity is the company's registry code, automatically assigned upon entry into the Estonian Commercial Register. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; specifically the Estonian distributed-profit territorial model: the OÜ is a legal person subject to corporate income tax on profit distributions, fringe benefits, gifts, transfer pricing adjustments, and non-business costs, while retained and undistributed profits are not taxed at entity level.

Available election forms

Estonia does not provide an IRC § 7701 check-the-box election. No hybrid instrument/entity elective classification exists under domestic law. Statutory elections relevant to an OÜ include: (i) the regular dividend declaration election enabling the 14/86 reduced rate if annual distributions have been paid in every prior tax year; (ii) optional VAT registration before the €40,000 threshold is exceeded; (iii) VAT grouping election under the Value Added Tax Act for closely linked persons; where applicable, EU hybrid-mismatch provision in § 533 of the Income Tax Act is mandatory/anti-abuse rather than elective.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

22%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

€40,000 per calendar year for Estonian-established businesses. Non-resident taxable persons with Estonian taxable supplies must VAT-register without a threshold. Intra-Community distance sales and digital services to non-business EU consumers may be declared via the EU One-Stop-Shop, with a scheme-applicable EU-wide €10,000 distance sales threshold if no other cross-border EU supplies exist.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: 0% VAT applies to supplies of goods physically dispatched or transported from Estonia to a non-EU destination obtained in direct association with the Customs Export Declaration, with transport commencing within the statutory period (in practice within three months of supply) and export proof completed by the customs office of departure. Services: offshore services supplied to a non-EU customer for direct use and benefit outside the EU are either outside the scope of Estonian VAT (where the customer is a non-taxable person resident outside the EU and B2C place-of-supply rules apply) or zero-rated for qualified B2B services under the 'use and enjoyment' rule; pure online mobile utility app sales via app stores to non-EU consumers are generally outside the scope of Estonian VAT and require no 0% refund claim. The recipient's residence, IP-location evidence, app-store financial reports, and signed service contracts substantiate eligibility.

  • Customs Export Declaration (EAD/SAD) with customs office of export validation
  • Bill of Lading / CMR / Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from the EU
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or bank/PSP settlement statement showing non-EU payment receipt
  • Commercial invoice, purchase order, and proof of delivery to the non-EU buyer

No universal real-time fiscal clearance mandate, no SAP approval, and no digital stamping in Estonia. B2G e-invoicing is mandatory for public purchasers: suppliers to Estonian state and municipal bodies must issue structured electronic invoices in the admitted e-invoice format (EN 16931 via Peppol or national portal). B2B e-invoicing is legally accepted and voluntary; the MTA processes e-returns and VAT reports via the e-MTA portal without requiring a continuous transaction-control system.

The MTA can retrospectively deny export zero-rating if the statutory export evidence is not obtained within the prescribed period; the full 22% VAT is assessed on the earlier supply date plus penalty interest of 0.06% per day. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims, forged customs or freight documents, or false B/L/RMC documentation trigger clawback of input VAT deductions associated with the offensively exported goods, a tax penalty up to 100% of the evaded VAT, and criminal liability under Estonian Penal Code including fines and up to five years' imprisonment for aggravated tax fraud. On VAT refund fraud, the MTA may suspend refunds, impose a €2,000 (ordinary) or €4,000 (repeat) penalty as provided in the Taxation Act, and refer criminal prosecution to the Estonian Prosecutor's Office.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia is a full Eurosystem member since 1 January 2011; the euro is the sole legal tender and is freely convertible at floating market rates. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully liberalised under Articles 63 to 66 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, the Estonian Credit Institutions Act, and the Eurosystem legal framework. There are no exchange-control permits, no capital-export licensing, no prior approvals, and no separate non-resident account regimes. Profit remittances, including dividends, interest, royalties, and shareholder loan repayments, may be executed without Eesti Pank approval; only statistical balance-of-payments reporting to Eesti Pank is required. Estonian corporate profit taxation is deferred until distribution (20% regular rate or 14% for regular dividends), so the treasury-relevant control point is the dividend declaration, not an FX clearance mechanism.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Estonia maintains no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation deadline. Export proceeds, including Apple App Store IAP net settlements and Google Play disbursements, may be retained in EUR or any foreign currency in domestic or foreign bank accounts. The only administrative duty is periodic statistical balance-of-payments reporting to Eesti Pank, which is non-intrusive and does not mandate conversion.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia), which since 1 January 2023 performs financial supervision and resolution through its integrated supervisory function (the former Financial Supervision and Resolution Authority, Finantsinspektsioon, is now a department of the central bank); foreign-exchange and euro-area monetary policy competence is delegated to the Eurosystem and the European Central Bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium. EEA e-money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise Plc, Revolut Payments UAB, Monese, Pliant, and Airwallex can onboard an Estonian OÜ remotely via Smart-ID/Mobile-ID and provide IBANs for SEPA receipts plus multi-currency FX wallets. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing requires a SEPA Credit Transfer (ISO 20022 pain.001, EUR) for EEA-origin funds or a SWIFT MT103 for third-country contributions. The beneficiary name must exactly match the OÜ registry name, the beneficiary IBAN must be valid under the EE country code, and the payment purpose must be expressed with ISO 20022 purpose code INVC (investment capital) or EQUI (equity-related) in the Debtor-to-Creditor Information field. The payment narrative must reference the OÜ shareholders' resolution date and, where applicable, the shareholder-loan agreement. The funds may be booked as share capital, share premium, or an interest-bearing shareholder loan; no minimum capital is required under Estonian law and the funds need not be held in an Estonian bank, but domestic booking materially simplifies the bank's source-of-wealth verification. Eesti Pank balance-of-payments statistical notification is required only for aggregated reporting purposes; no central-bank approval is necessary.Outbound treasury clearing for a mobile utility OÜ is dominated by digital-vendor settlement: SEPA Instant and SEPA Credit Transfers for EEA SaaS and cloud providers; SWIFT MT103 for non-EEA marketing platforms, contractors, and infrastructure purchases; and TARGET2 EUR final settlement through tier-1 correspondent banking for large interbank disbursements. Commercial Letters of Credit are generally unnecessary for ISIC 6201 digital services and are reserved for any physical hardware procurement; Apple and Google merchant-of-record payouts typically arrive via SEPA or SWIFT from their treasury entities, requiring no documentary credit architecture.Deliverable EUR/USD outright forward contracts, up to 12 to 24 month tenors, executed under an ISDA 2002 master agreement with an Eesti Pank supervised credit institution or Finantsinspektsioon-authorised investment firm; EMIR clearing and transaction reporting applies.Cross-currency interest-rate and principal swaps, EUR/USD and EUR/SEK, for IFRS 9 hedge accounting of balance-sheet items, subject to the Estonian Commercial Code, EMIR risk-mitigation rules, and central clearing via a recognised CCP or bilaterally margined under a CSA.FX spot and rolling FX swaps for treasury liquidity management, lawful under EMIR; exchange-traded FX options are also permissible but generally not eligible for hedge accounting unless bank-supported.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs): permissible only for illiquid exotic currency pairs in which the counter currency is non-convertible; EUR/USD NDFs are not applicable because the euro is fully deliverable in Estonia and are therefore not a standard Estonian treasury instrument.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium. EEA e-money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise Plc, Revolut Payments UAB, Monese, Pliant, and Airwallex can onboard an Estonian OÜ remotely via Smart-ID/Mobile-ID and provide IBANs for SEPA receipts plus multi-currency FX wallets. Legal constraints are acceptable because the E-money Institutions Act enables EU passporting, but an EMI account is not generally accepted as the sole treasury bank by tier-1 Estonian credit institutions due to source-of-funds opacity and re-KYC contagion. The EMI channel is legally viable as an ancillary payout rail for Apple and Google remittances, contractor disbursements, or foreign-currency wallet conversion; however, EUR-denominated tax payments, statutory audit queries, and investor due-diligence requests should be settled through a licensed Estonian credit institution.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Estonia does not impose a classical withholding tax on the shareholder; it imposes corporate income tax on the distributing OÜ. The domestic effective tax on a dividend equals 20% of gross distribution (20/80 on net distribution), reduced to 14% within the regular-distribution quota. For a non-resident corporate shareholder, the tax is economically borne by the Estonian company before dividend remittance.

The TSD and any associated corporate income tax payment on profit distributions must be submitted and paid no later than the 10th day of the calendar month following the month in which the distribution was made; if the deadline falls on a weekend or public holiday, it moves to the next business day.
