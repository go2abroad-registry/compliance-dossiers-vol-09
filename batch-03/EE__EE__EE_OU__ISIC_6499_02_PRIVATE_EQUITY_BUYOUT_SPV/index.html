Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Commercial Register (Äriregister) operated electronically through the e-Business Register portal (e-äriregister.ee), administered by the Centre of Registers and Information Systems (Registrite ja Infosüsteemide Keskus, RIK); registration decisions are adjudicated by the registry department of the competent county court within the Estonian court system.

Governing statute

Estonian Commercial Code (Äriseadustik, ÄS), RT I 1995, 26, 355; private limited company (osaühing) provisions §§135–177; registration procedure §§61–82; minimum share capital §136(1); management board provisions §§180–190; State Fees Act (Riigilõivuseadus) as the basis for registry state fees.

Formation / constitutional filings

Registreerimisavaldus (Application for Entry in the Commercial Register) filed electronically and digitally signed in the e-Business Register together with the Osaühing Articles of Association (põhikiri).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 54 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Statutory minimum share capital is EUR 2,500 (ÄS §136). No escrow account or pre-filing proof of capital payment is required for online digitally signed e-Residency formations; the capital contribution may be made after registration within the deadline set in the Articles of Association. Non-monetary contributions require an expert valuation. The private equity SPV may adopt a higher capital figure aligned with committed subscription documents.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: the office premises must comply with the general fire safety requirements of the Rescue Board (Päästeamet) and a self-assessment declaration (tuleohutuse deklaratsioon) is filed where applicable. Structural clearance: the leased or owned premises must possess a valid operating permit (kasutusluba) issued by the local municipal authority. Occupational clearance: a workplace risk assessment and health surveillance must be performed in compliance with the Occupational Health and Safety Act (töötervishoiu ja tööohutuse seadus) once any employees are engaged.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable for a passive private equity buy-out SPV in Estonia. Where the SPV would migrate to regulated investment fund management or investment services, the competent authority is Finantsinspektsioon (Estonian Financial Supervision Authority); no manufacturing or industrial licensing board is engaged because the entity operates as an administrative financial holding company.

Category: No operating or manufacturing permit applies to the passive SPV. If the entity undertakes regulated fund management or the provision of investment services, an Investment Fund Management Licence (Fondivalitseja tegevusluba) under the Investment Funds Act or an investment firm authorisation under the Securities Market Act would be mandatory; otherwise the Commercial Register entry is the sole operating charter.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet) / e-MTA portal (emta.ee)

E-government filing portal

For a foreign investor acquiring an Estonian OÜ or establishing a PE SPV: (1) incorporate OÜ via the Estonian e-Business Registry (no mandatory minimum capital); (2) obtain the registry code (Äriregistri kood) and statutory TIN issued by the Estonian Tax and Customs Board; (3) register for VAT through e-MTA using the TIN when taxable turnover exceeds EUR 40,000 per annum or for intra-Community/third-country transactions; (4) for non-resident entities without an Estonian PE, register as a non-resident VAT payer under the special simplified procedure for cross-border supplies. e-Residency provides digital authentication for remote filings.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation on distributed profits. OÜ retains the Estonian corporate income tax exemption on undistributed profits; tax is deferred until profit distribution or deemed benefit. Estonian resident companies are subject to worldwide taxation only on distributions; territorial scope applies to source income of non-residents.

Available election forms

Estonian law does not adopt US-style check-the-box entity election. The OÜ is a fixed tax-resident entity. Available elections are limited to timing/treatment of distributions under the Income Tax Act; hybrid mismatch and CFC rules follow ATAD with no elective carve-outs. VAT grouping is not available.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated exports apply to goods dispatched or transported to a non-EU destination with proof of export, and to goods dispatched to a Member State that are not subject to intra-Community distance-sales thresholds. Certain offshore services (e.g., services related to immovable or movable tangible property outside Estonia, or intangibles supplied to a non-resident with no Estonian economic use) are zero-rated if the recipient is a taxable person in another country. Services that constitute financial intermediation or portfolio management are VAT-exempt rather than zero-rated.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

Estonia mandates e-invoicing for public-sector procurement under EU EN 16931; no real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping regime applies for B2B transactions. Private-sector e-invoicing is voluntary. VAT reporting is filed periodically via e-MTA with XBRL or XML attachments.

Estonian VAT Act § 27 grants the ETCC the power to retrospectively assess VAT, interest, and impose fines up to EUR 7,400 for negligent false zero-rating; intentional tax fraud may attract criminal liability under the Penal Code including imprisonment up to 5 years. The ECJ jurisprudence requires exporters to retain robust transport and payment evidence for 5 years to rebut Revenue rebuttal. For PE buyout SPVs, any export VAT refund claim is scrutinised against round-tripping and transfer-pricing benchmarks.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia is a euro-area member with full current-account and capital-account convertibility. There are no exchange-control approvals, no surrender requirements, no administrative restrictions on capital inflows or outflows, and no preferential exchange-rate categories. Capital movements are liberalized under EU Treaty provisions on free movement of capital. The only practical hurdles are AML/KYC verification, EU and international sanctions screening, and Estonian tax treatment of profit distributions. Profit remittance is not blocked by the central bank: an OÜ may distribute dividends after approval of annual accounts, but the distribution itself triggers Estonian corporate income tax at the entity level, normally calculated as 20 per 80 paid from accumulated taxed profits, with a reduced 14 per 86 calculation available for regular distributions of previously taxed profits under the statutory conditions. Non-resident shareholders are not separately subject to Estonian withholding tax on dividends.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Estonian law imposes no export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no prescribed timeline for converting export receipts into euro. Exporters and trading subsidiaries of the PE SPV may retain receivables in any freely convertible currency, maintain foreign-currency accounts at Estonian credit institutions, and use proceeds directly to settle imports or debt service. Cross-border payment statistical reporting to Eesti Pank and the Estonian Tax and Customs Board may apply for balance-of-payments and tax purposes, but these are information filings, not FX-approval requirements.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia), operating within the European System of Central Banks; monetary policy is delegated to the European Central Bank. The Estonian Financial Supervision Authority (Finantsinspektsioon) exercises prudential supervision over credit institutions and payment services, and the Estonian Financial Intelligence Unit receives AML/CFT reports.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Restricted / Medium for EEA-licensed EMI partners, Low for offshore or non-EEA digital banks. An Estonian or EEA-payment-services-passported EMI may be used as a Treasury-optimization overlay, provided the EMI has registered or passported its services through the Estonian Financial Supervision Authority under PSD2. As a legal constraint, EMIs are not banks: they cannot accept deposits, cannot extend credit, and cannot provide custody of the OÜ or its fund-level assets. They are viable only as pre-funded settlement wallets for low-risk operational payments. Core private-equity buyout funds, acquisition drawdowns, and syndicated loan proceeds should be held at a licensed Estonian credit institution. Numerous EMIs, including many offshore digital EMIs, reject regulated investment-fund, SPV, and private-credit counterparties due to AML risk appetite.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Defense evidence: Full chain-of-ownership chart from ultimate natural persons to the OÜ; fund constitutional pack, including the limited partnership agreement, private placement memorandum, and subscription agreement; UBO declaration per Estonia's Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Prevention Act; investor CIP extracts from the general partner or fund administrator; capital call notices and LP commitment schedules; source-of-wealth statements for the sponsoring PE fund.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity contributions and shareholder loans should be routed through SWIFT MT103 or, if both banks are inside SEPA, SEPA Credit Transfer with the OÜ's IBAN at an Estonian tier-1 bank. EUR funds clear via TARGET2 or SEPA. The wire must state a transparent purpose code, for example 'EQUITY CAPITAL INCREASE' or 'SHAREHOLDER LOAN', matching the Estonian balance-of-payments reporting category and the corporate resolution being implemented. The bank will apply Regulation (EU) 2015/847 remittance-information requirements, so the remitter's full legal name, address, account number, and where applicable LEI must appear. For a private-equity syndicate, final-investor funding from LP capital calls should flow from a regularized fund account into the OÜ's account; an escrow bank at the fund level should issue a reference letter confirming the aggregate investor funding, and the OÜ must retain the capital call notice, funding breakdown, and bank statement as audit evidence.For acquisition consideration, debt repayment, and operational disbursements, the standard routing is SWIFT MT103 for cross-border euro and non-euro transfers, with MT202 COV where allocation to an underlying transaction is required. EUR-denominated domestic and EEA payments clear over SEPA Credit Transfer, which is the most cost-effective rail. For trade-related settlement, a commercial letter of credit is opened by the acquiring operating entity or by the SPV on SWIFT MT700/MT707, with MT798 used for documentary-credit messaging. Loan facility drawings for the LBO may be disbursed directly from the lending syndicate to the OÜ via MT103, while simultaneous onward payment to the seller may be executed through the same settlement bank using a matching allocation instruction. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% on accrued and retained profits; 20% effective CIT on gross distributions (20/80 of the net distributed amount) under Section 50 of the Estonian Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Estonia imposes no statutory dividend withholding tax at shareholder level; the economic charge is the 20/80 distribution tax levied on the distributing OÜ, which is final and exclusive; non-resident shareholders receive the gross dividend without Estonian source deduction.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The TSD and the corresponding tax payment are due by the 10th calendar day of the month following the reporting month; the December return serves as the year-end settlement and is due by 10 January of the following year.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual consolidated return is due by 10 January of the following year together with the December monthly TSD cycle; where the statutory annual report to the Commercial Registry is separately required, the report is due by 30 June following the fiscal year-end.
