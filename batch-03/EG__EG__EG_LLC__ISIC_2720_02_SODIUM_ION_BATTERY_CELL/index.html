Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Under Companies Law No. 194 of 2020 there is no statutory minimum paid-up capital for an LLC; the shareholders determine the capital in the Memorandum and Articles of Association, and each quota must be fully paid in cash or in kind before registration. Cash capital is held in a temporary escrow bank account at an Egyptian licensed bank until the Commercial Registration is issued; after registration the bank releases the funds to the company. In-kind capital requires a certified auditor's valuation and transfer of ownership to the company.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defense clearance (Ministry of Interior) covering fire suppression and alarm systems, accessible exits, hazardous-material storage separation, and emergency response plan. Structural load inspection by the competent governorate engineering administration confirming floor-load capacity for electrode coating, dry-room, and assembly equipment. Occupational Safety and Health clearance by the Ministry of Manpower under Labour Law No. 12 of 2003, including machine guarding, lockout/tagout, chemical hygiene, PPE provision, air monitoring, medical examinations, and safety training records.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance, Government of Egypt; official national tax portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

A foreign-formed entity or Egyptian LLC must first complete Commercial Registry registration (for LLCs, through the General Authority for Investment or the relevant commercial registry office), then submit the registration form to the competent ETA tax office with the company’s commercial registration extract, articles of association, board/shareholder resolution on manager appointment, power of attorney, legal representative identification, and proof of business premises. The ETA issues a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) under the Tax Procedures Law No. 206 of 2020. Separate VAT registration must be completed with the ETA under Law No. 67 of 2016 where annual taxable supplies exceed the mandatory threshold or the entity voluntarily registers before commencing taxable activities.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-formed entity or Egyptian LLC must first complete Commercial Registry registration (for LLCs, through the General Authority for Investment or the relevant commercial registry office), then submit the registration form to the competent ETA tax office with the company’s commercial registration extract, articles of association, board/shareholder resolution on manager appointment, power of attorney, legal representative identification, and proof of business premises. The ETA issues a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) under the Tax Procedures Law No. 206 of 2020. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists for Egyptian LLCs; the LLC is mandatorily taxable as a separate corporate taxpayer. Egypt’s domestic tax law does not provide hybrid mismatch elective classifications. Elective regimes are limited to investment incentives available under Law No. 72 of 2017, which may include tax credits, deductions, free-zone treatment, or investment guarantees where approved, and to optional consolidated tax treatment if the strict affiliation conditions in the Income Tax Law are met.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 per calendar year of taxable supplies of goods and services; a non-resident supplier with an Egyptian nexus, warehouse, agent, or electronic commerce register may be required to register without reference to this threshold if making taxable supplies in Egypt.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The export of goods to a foreign buyer qualifies for 0% VAT if the goods physically leave Egyptian customs territory and the exporter holds evidence of export. Zero-rating also applies to services directly connected to exported goods and to services supplied to non-residents where the benefit is effectively consumed outside Egypt. The taxpayer must prove that the supply is genuinely export-oriented or offshore in substance, and not diverted into the Egyptian market, to sustain the claim.

  • Customs Export Declaration cleared by Egyptian Customs Authority
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing dispatch from Egypt to a foreign destination
  • Commercial invoice and supply contract describing the goods and foreign buyer
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign exchange bank transfer document evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • ETA export invoice record and, where applicable, certificate of origin or proof of actual customs exit

Registered VAT taxpayers issuing business-to-business invoices are required to use the ETA real-time electronic invoicing system. Invoice data must be transmitted in the prescribed JSON/XML format through the ETA e-invoice application programming interface before fiscal clearance; the ETA validates the invoice and issue a unique invoice ID and QR code. Digital stamping and electronic archiving are mandatory for covered taxpayers. Business-to-consumer e-receipt transactions are subject to phased mandatory integration. Failure to obtain real-time clearance or issuance outside the approved e-invoicing channel exposes the taxpayer to fines and suspension of invoice clearance.

The ETA may scrutinise export files and reverse zero-rated treatment if the export documentation bundle is incomplete, the foreign exchange proceeds are not timely repatriated, or the goods or services are found to have been diverted for domestic consumption. Claimed VAT will be clawed back at 14%, with late payment interest, monthly penalties under the Tax Procedures Law, and administrative fines for false declarations. Where fraudulent documents or deliberate evasion are shown, criminal prosecution, additional monetary penalties, and imprisonment may be imposed; tax refunds and future zero-rating entitlements may also be suspended pending full compliance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt operates a unified managed-float exchange regime adopted in March 2024. Current-account transactions (trade settlements, royalties, consulting fees, and dividend remittances) are broadly convertible through CBE-licensed authorized dealers upon presentation of valid commercial documentation. Capital-account operations (equity injections, shareholder loans, disposal proceeds of foreign-owned shares) are permissible under the Investment Law No. 194/2020 and the CBE's facilitated FX regime, but each transfer requires a bank-attested FX purpose declaration, verified GAfI/Fee exemption certificate (if applicable), and audited financials or capital-registration evidence as a backstop. Profit remittance is lawful for foreign shareholders but triggers Egyptian dividend withholding tax (statutory 10% under Income Tax Law No. 91/2005, potentially reduced under an applicable double-tax treaty) and requires evidence of prior settlement of the remittance-related withholding. Practical hurdles arise during episodic EGP liquidity squeezes and bank-level FX prioritization queues, so the entity should maintain a distinct USD and EUR vault balance and route core outflows through pre-funded foreign-currency accounts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under current CBE regulations, export proceeds must be repatriated to Egypt within 30 calendar days from the date of shipment or negotiation of the export shipping document and must be credited to a CBE-authorized bank account in the exporter's name. The historical mandatory surrender/conversion mechanism (which required conversion of a fixed percentage of export dollars into EGP) has been abolished following the March 2024 exchange-rate unification; exporters may retain proceeds in USD or EUR in onshore foreign-currency accounts without a forced conversion ratio. However, all retained balances are subject to CBE periodic reporting requirements and must only be utilized for permissible import settlements, debt-service payments, or repatriation of legitimate surplus with documentary proof.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) - Foreign Exchange Regulation Department

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low - Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, Payoneer, Wise) are NOT viable as a primary Egypt treasury rail. They are not licensed under CBE Law No. 194/2020 to conduct payment business in Egypt, cannot clear EGP, cannot hold a capital escrow balance, and cannot issue documentary letters of credit governed by Egyptian banking regulation. Such EMIs may be used only as an auxiliary pipe for small value overseas collection or vendor payments outside Egypt, provided central treasury records remain on SWIFT and no Egypt-facing settlement is processed through them. Onshore payment-institution licenses (issued by CBE to entities such as local e-wallet operators) are likewise not bank substitutes for trade finance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity must be wired via SWIFT MT103 to the designated Capital Escrow / Investment Account at the Egyptian bank using the foreign investor's remitting bank; the transfer instruction must carry the beneficiary's Commercial Registration number and GAFI file number, with reference narrative 'CAP-INJ [GAFI file no.]' to allow the receiving bank to map the flow to the company's capital table. The receiving bank will record the FX transaction under the CBE's FX declaration regime and may, at its discretion, verify the investor's underlying ownership percentage. If the equity is contributed in-kind (e.g., equipment or technology), no wire occurs; a bank-approved import-settlement-only entry applies and separate CBE in-kind capital filing is required. EGP conversion, if needed, is executed at the interbank rate confirmed by the chosen bank, and the EGP proceeds should remain segregated from operations until GAFI capital registration is complete.Outbound trade settlements should use two primaries: (i) SWIFT MT103 for direct contract payments: prepayments under procurement contracts, engineering service fees, and royalties, with field 72 narrative referencing the underlying proforma invoice and Egyptian import declaration to grant currency-use eligibility; and (ii) Irrevocable documentary Letters of Credit issued per UCP600 (SWIFT MT700) for administered supplier payments to major outsourcers and cathode/anode/precursor vendors in China and Germany, with schedule extension via MT707. The LC can be financed either through an EGP cash-covered account (converted onshore) or pre-funded FCY balances held from export proceeds, which eliminates conversion timing risk; the bank will require each drawdown to be traced to a letter of credit, shipping documents (B/L, packing list, commercial invoice, certificate of origin), and matching customs entry. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

22.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% (final WHT on gross dividend; remitted by Egyptian distributing company)

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within four (4) months after the end of the fiscal year (Art. 59, Law 91/2005); interim quarterly advance payments due in months 3, 6, and 9 of the year

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EGP 15,000,000 related-party transactions (Local File); EGP 50,000,000 annual revenues (Master File); CbC notification at group consolidated revenue threshold per ETA guidelines

Economic substance scope: No standalone ESR statute in Egypt; ETA applies GAAR (Art. 39/40 Law 91/2005) and MLI PPT to disregard transactions lacking commercial substance
