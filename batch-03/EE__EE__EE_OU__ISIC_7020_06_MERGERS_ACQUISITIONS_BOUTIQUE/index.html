Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office and licensed service-provider address are accepted for company registration, statutory correspondence, and receipt of mail in Estonia. However, for an OÜ to be treated as Estonian tax-resident with effective management and control in Estonia, substantive decision-making should take place in Estonia. A virtual address alone does not establish commercial substance for the preferential 0% undistributed-profits tax regime and may be challenged by the Estonian Tax and Customs Board unless real economic activity, management presence, and adequate substance documentation exist.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No mandatory minimum paid-up share capital is required to found an Osaühing (OÜ). The Articles of Association may fix the share capital at EUR 0 (zero-capital OÜ) or at the statutory benchmark of EUR 2,500. If the proposed share capital exceeds the capital actually contributed at registration, the outstanding amount must be paid into a blocked settlement account of a licensed credit institution or evidenced by a bank confirmation; alternatively, the unpaid amount may be recorded as a contribution receivable (sissemakse nõue), payable within one year of registration.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety clearance by the Estonian Rescue Board (Päästeamet) confirming operational smoke detectors, certified extinguishers, illuminated escape routes, and fire-resistance-rated office partitions; structural load certification by an authorized building inspector verifying floor load capacity and safe occupancy; occupational workplace clearance by the Labour Inspectorate (Tööinspektsioon) covering work environment risk assessment, ergonomic workstations, ventilation, and registration of the employer in the Working Environment Register.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet) — e-Maksuamet (e-Tax) portal, emta.ee

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate Osaühing in the Commercial Register (Äriregister) via the e-Business Centre (e-äriregister) using notary or digital e-Residency; receive the registry code (reg. kood) as the TIN. 2) Apply for VAT registration through e-Maksuamet when the statutory threshold is met or activities require; the VAT ID is 'EE' followed by the 9-digit registry code. 3) Non-EU entities without a permanent establishment may be required to appoint an authorised tax representative for VAT registration. 4) For corporate income tax purposes, no separate tax registration is required; entity taxes are managed via e-Maksuamet annual returns.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — 1) Incorporate Osaühing in the Commercial Register (Äriregister) via the e-Business Centre (e-äriregister) using notary or digital e-Residency; receive the registry code (reg. kood) as the TIN. 2) Apply for VAT registration through e-Maksuamet when the statutory threshold is met or activities require; the VAT ID is 'EE' followed by the 9-digit registry code. 3) Non-EU entities without a permanent establishment may be required to appoint an authorised tax representative for VAT registration. 4) For corporate income tax purposes, no separate tax registration is required; entity taxes are managed via e-Maksuamet annual returns.

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 50,000 of annual taxable supplies (excluding capital assets) for resident taxable persons; non-established suppliers of digital/telecom services are registrable from the first euro of Estonian turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: zero-rating applies upon extra-Community export provided the supplier obtains the customs export declaration and transport evidence proving the goods left the EU, normally within three months of supply. Services: M&A advisory services are normally outside the scope of Estonian VAT when the business customer is established in a third country (place of use rule); true zero-rating for services attaches to services directly related to exported goods. Substantiation requires commercial invoices, contracts, bank/remittance evidence, and the recipient's non-EU VAT/ID status.

  • Customs export declaration (SAD/ENS) with electronic validation
  • Bill of Lading (ocean) or CMR (road) or Air Waybill (air)
  • Trade invoice and underlying M&A mandate contract
  • Proof of payment (bank transfer or SWIFT confirmation)
  • Inward remittance certificate (whether issued by bank or exchange control evidence)

Estonia does not require real-time transaction-level clearance or digital stamping. E-invoicing is mandatory for B2G invoices under the EN 16931 standard. All VAT returns, yearly economics declarations and invoice summaries are filed electronically through e-Maksuamet; SAF-T XML data must be produced upon request by the Tax and Customs Board.

Estonian Tax and Customs Board cross-checks export declarations, customs outflows, and banking remittances using risk-based analytics. Unsubstantiated zero-rating claims are retroactively adjusted for the statutory assessment period (3 years standard, 5 years for gross negligence) with arrears interest of 0.06% per day and statutory fines of up to 20% of assessed VAT; deliberate fraud carries criminal liability under Penal Code section 344, punishable by up to 5 years' imprisonment.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia, as a full EU member state, operates under unrestricted capital account convertibility and current account convertibility pursuant to EU Treaty provisions on free movement of capital. Profit remittance faces no capital controls or prior approval requirements; only standard tax reporting and beneficial ownership disclosure apply. Hurdles are limited to compliance with Estonian Tax and Customs Board obligations and EU AMLD/ATAD frameworks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or conversion of export proceeds exists in Estonia. For service-based M&A advisory activities, client fees and advisory disbursements are received directly into corporate bank accounts. Statutory timeline is zero; only post-receipt tax reporting under Estonian corporate income tax rules is required, with no conversion ratios or central bank mandates.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Estonia (Eesti Pank) and European Central Bank (ECB)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High feasibility rating for licensed EU e-money institutions including Wise, Revolut Business, and local operators under Estonian Financial Supervision and Resolution Authority (FSRA) oversight. Legal constraints encompass mandatory registration as an e-money institution or payment institution under PSD2/PSD3, adherence to AMLD5/6 customer due diligence, and prohibition on unlicensed cross-border issuance of electronic money.
