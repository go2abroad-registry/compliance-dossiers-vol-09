Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance; official e-services: ETA Taxpayer Portal (https://www.eta.gov.eg) and the mandatory E-Invoicing and E-Receipts Platform (https://einvoicing.eta.gov.eg).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned EG_LLC must first complete commercial registration to obtain the Commercial Identification Number (CID), then file the statutory tax registration application with the ETA within 30 days of commencing activity; the ETA issues a single unified Tax Identification Number (Tax Card/File Number) covering corporate income tax, VAT, and payroll/stamp taxes, together with the Tax Card. For electronic invoicing, the taxpayer must separately onboard on the ETA E-Invoicing Portal, submit corporate documentation (CID, Tax Card, power of attorney, authorized signatory ID), and obtain API credentials (client ID/client secret) for the E-Invoice and E-Receipt systems.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-owned EG_LLC must first complete commercial registration to obtain the Commercial Identification Number (CID), then file the statutory tax registration application with the ETA within 30 days of commencing activity; the ETA issues a single unified Tax Identification Number (Tax Card/File Number) covering corporate income tax, VAT, and payroll/stamp taxes, together with the Tax Card. For electronic invoicing, the taxpayer must separately onboard on the ETA E-Invoicing Portal, submit corporate documentation (CID, Tax Card, power of attorney, authorized signatory ID), and obtain API credentials (client ID/client secret) for the E-Invoice and E-Receipt systems.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Egypt has no US-style check-the-box election; all domestic LLCs are statutory entity-level taxpayers. Elections that can modify the indirect tax and duty profile: (i) voluntary VAT registration at an EGP 100,000 annual turnover threshold (mandatory at EGP 500,000); (ii) application through GAFI for Investment Law No. 72 of 2017 incentives, including the General Free Zone or Suez Canal Economic Zone (SCZone) regimes under which VAT and customs duties are replaced by alternative levy arrangements for project inputs; (iii) no VAT-group or tax consolidation election is available; hybrid mismatch elections do not exist in Egyptian domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: EGP 500,000 of annual taxable supplies of goods/services; voluntary registration is permitted from EGP 100,000 of annual turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under Article 17(1) of the VAT Law applies to thermoformed dental clear aligner sheets when the goods are sold and physically exported by the Egyptian registered supplier outside Egypt and the supplier retains proof of customs exit and receipt of the export proceeds in Egypt. Offshore services supplied by the Egyptian entity to non-resident beneficiaries outside Egypt may also qualify as zero-rated exported services when the service is consumed entirely outside Egypt; the supplier must maintain contracts, delivery evidence, and banking records demonstrating the offshore nature of the supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration processed through the Egyptian National Single Window (NAFEZA) with export duty/tax clearance stamp
  • Transport document evidencing physical exit: Bill of Lading, Sea Waybill, or Air Waybill
  • Commercial invoice bearing the zero-rated VAT treatment code and the ETA-cleared e-invoice QR code
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by the Egyptian dealing bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds in Egypt

Egypt operates a mandatory real-time electronic invoice clearance system under Ministerial Decrees No. 201 of 2020 (e-invoices) and No. 623 of 2021 (e-receipts). All invoices must be submitted in XML/JSON via secure API to the ETA E-Invoicing Platform, validated against ETA master data (supplier, customer, product codes, VAT rates), and returned with an ETA-assigned UUID and QR code before the invoice becomes legally effective. Integration is a condition for input VAT deduction and for customs release of zero-rated export consignments. Failure to integrate or to issue ETA-cleared invoices disqualifies the 0% export claim, triggers penalties of up to EGP 50,000 per document, and causes denial of input VAT credit.

The ETA may reassess within five years and will claw back input VAT credited on zero-rated exports plus 14% VAT if the customs exit, remittance, or documentation trail is deficient; monthly delay damages accrue at up to 2% per month on the unremitted tax, and e-invoices not cleared by the ETA at the time of export are automatically disallowed. Fraudulent zero-rating claims (including fictitious exports, circular trade, or unrepatriated proceeds) attract criminal penalties under the VAT Law of imprisonment and fines of not less than EGP 50,000, double tax on the falsely zero-rated value, exclusion from the exporters' registry, and mandatory bank-level repatriation enforcement by the Central Bank of Egypt.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Egyptian pound is generally convertible for bona fide current-account transactions, including payment for imported goods and services, subject to bank-level documentary review, sanctions screening, customs and tax documentation, and available foreign-currency liquidity. Capital-account transactions, shareholder funding, intercompany loans, dividends, royalties, management fees, and other profit repatriations are not subject to a single universal statutory prohibition but are controlled through licensed-bank procedures, corporate records, tax clearance or tax-payment evidence, audited financial statements, board or shareholder approvals, and evidence that the underlying transaction is genuine. Foreign exchange access may be affected by market liquidity, CBE directives, bank risk policies, import restrictions, and applicable sector or free-zone rules. The LLC should maintain a registered capital file, shareholder funding ledger, bank advices, customs records, audited accounts, withholding-tax evidence, and transfer-pricing support before requesting dividend or service-fee remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Egypt does not generally impose a universal statutory requirement that every exporter surrender a fixed percentage of export proceeds or convert all foreign-currency receipts into Egyptian pounds. Export proceeds should be collected through authorized banks and reconciled to the relevant commercial invoice, customs export declaration, shipping evidence, and banking or export-control records. Applicable CBE, banking, customs, GOEIC, free-zone, and sector-specific instructions may prescribe reporting, collection, or repatriation periods for particular transactions or exporter categories; no single statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio should be assumed without checking the current bank circulars and the company's export regime. The company should obtain written confirmation from its authorized bank before implementation and retain proof of export, receipt, conversion, and any permitted foreign-currency retention.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE), acting under the Central Bank and Banking System Law No. 194 of 2020, together with the Egyptian Customs Authority, the General Organization for Export and Import Control (GOEIC), and the relevant rules of the Ministry of Finance and Egyptian Tax Authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium for payment collection and expense management, but low as a substitute for an Egyptian corporate bank account. An offshore electronic-money institution or payment service provider cannot independently open a regulated Egyptian deposit account, conduct unrestricted local foreign-exchange dealing, receive capital contributions as an Egyptian bank, or bypass CBE, AML, customs, tax, and import-document controls. Stripe or another platform may be used only if the relevant Egyptian merchant onboarding, acquiring, settlement, product, and current-country availability rules permit it. Settlement to an offshore account may create foreign-exchange, tax, permanent-establishment, consumer-protection, and merchant-of-record issues. The company should use a CBE-licensed Egyptian bank as its primary account, obtain written platform eligibility confirmation, and reconcile platform statements to Egyptian sales, VAT, corporate-income-tax, and foreign-exchange records.
