Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA); official e-portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must register via the ETA online portal, submitting certified constitutional documents (articles of association, commercial registration), a power of attorney, and, where required, appointment of a local tax representative. Upon approval, the ETA issues a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) covering both income tax and VAT obligations. Non-residents may complete remote electronic registration; notarized documentation must be uploaded for biometric verification waiver.

Default tax classification

No check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election exists under Egyptian law. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 annual taxable turnover for mandatory VAT registration; no threshold applies to non-resident suppliers of electronic services; voluntary registration is permitted below threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods are zero-rated if the goods physically leave Egypt and are supported by customs proof. Services supplied to non-residents qualify for zero-rating where the service is rendered and consumed wholly outside Egypt, as evidenced by the commercial agreement, hosting infrastructure location, and absence of any Egyptian resident beneficiary. For PropTech/SaaS, the software must be delivered via cloud infrastructure outside Egypt; advertising or support services delivered to Egyptian residents may disallow zero-rating.

Egypt mandates a real-time e-invoicing clearance system for B2B transactions. VAT-registered taxpayers must integrate their billing/ERP system with the ETA via API, submit invoices in the approved XML scheme, obtain a digital approval code, and include a QR code on each invoice. The system is enforced in phases; failure to obtain real-time clearance can invalidate the invoice and trigger penalties.

Claims are subject to periodic ETA audit. If the export documentation is incomplete, FX is not repatriated within 180 days, or a zero-rated supply is later found to be taxable, the ETA may assess the uncollected VAT plus late-payment interest and fines of up to 100% of the tax. Fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger administrative clawback of refunded amounts and may lead to criminal prosecution and debarment from the e-invoicing clearance system.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations and, after the March 2024 exchange-rate reform, operates a unified, market-determined exchange rate. Current-account transactions (SaaS export receipts, subscription fees, software royalties, and operational payments) are freely convertible and transferable through authorised banks. Capital-account transactions (inbound equity, shareholder loans, and outward profit remittances) are convertible only after bank validation of the underlying investment (GAFI certificate and CBE capital-account registration), confirmation of the source of funds of the original inward remittance, and clearance of Egyptian corporate tax and payroll obligations. Profit remittance hurdles are practical rather than statutory: banks prioritise FX liquidity, may require a tax clearance certificate from the Egyptian Tax Authority, and reject repatriation if the USD/EUR trail does not trace back to the registered capital injection. For a PropTech SaaS exporter, the bulk of revenue will arrive as Stripe/ACH settlements in USD; these must be swept into the Egyptian banking system and matched to the export contract to avoid classification as informal FX circulation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under CBE foreign-exchange instructions implementing Law No. 194 of 2020, all export proceeds for goods and services must be repatriated through the licensed Egyptian banking system: the statutory timeline is 120 days from the bill of lading for goods and 90 days from invoice/service delivery for services, with a six-month extension available upon prior CBE justification. Mandatory conversion ratios are imposed by CBE prudential directive rather than by statute; for service-export receipts (including ISIC 6201 SaaS and cloud-platform licences) the current floor is 0% forced conversion, meaning the LLC may retain, borrow against, or convert its USD receivables at the prevailing market rate, provided 100% of the proceeds pass through the Egyptian bank and are supported by the export contract, digital-delivery evidence, and the CBE transaction declaration. Goods exporters remain subject to a possible CBE-imposed conversion floor (set at 50% during recent external-liquidity stress), but that floor does not apply to software subscription receivables. Late repatriation is an exchange-control violation under Article 20 of Law No. 194 of 2020 and may result in fines, bank-level restrictions, and a compliance flag affecting future treasury operations.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) acting as the sole monetary authority, bank regulator, and foreign-exchange administrator under Law No. 194 of 2020 (the Central Bank and Banking System Law). The CBE's Foreign Exchange Operations Department, together with the Egyptian Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Combating Unit (EMLCU) and the General Authority for Investment (GAFI), supervises currency convertibility, capital-import verification, and profit repatriation.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW as a primary or principal treasury rail; MEDIUM as a supplementary offshore collection node. Non-bank fintech EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex) are not CBE-licensed deposit takers and cannot lawfully hold EGP or provide local current-account services in Egypt. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injections must be wired via SWIFT MT103 to the LLC's licensed Egyptian bank using the exact beneficiary name/BIC/IBAN registered in the CBE capital-account file. The bank opens the inward investment file upon presentation of: (i) GAFI investment registration; (ii) the CBE capital-account declaration; (iii) a shareholder resolution approving the capital increase and share issuance; and (iv) AML clearance evidencing the remitter's source of funds. The remittance must be tagged with ISO 20022 purpose code 'EQUI' for equity or 'LOAN' for shareholder/subordinated loan, and the reference line must quote the GAFI registration number and the Egyptian entity ID. The bank will credit the USD capital account, issue a foreign-currency deposit receipt, and report the inflow under the CBE balance-of-payments reporting file, thereby enabling later profit repatriation. A completed inward capital file is mandatory; without it, a profit/dividend remittance will be administratively blocked.Outbound trade settlements (AWS/cloud fees, international software licences, overseas contractors) are executed through the Egyptian bank's SWIFT MT103 module from the USD/EUR revenue account after submission of the underlying contract, invoice, and a current-account foreign-exchange declaration; no documentary ceiling applies for genuine SaaS operational payments, provided the transaction value matches the commercial agreement. The bank settles via its correspondent network (typically Citibank, JPMorgan, or Standard Chartered) in USD, EUR, or GBP through CHIPS/Fedwire or TARGET2. For on-prem hardware or infrastructure procurement requiring bank undertaking, an irrevocable sight commercial letter of credit is available at NBE/CIB; for digital SaaS delivery, an LC is not advised because the absence of transport documents creates discrepancy risk. The Payment Systems Law No. 18 of 2019 requires all EGP-denominated payment services and clearing to be routed through CBE-authorized banks/SWIFT channels. The viable structure is therefore: Stripe/ACH -> EMI or correspondent USD account -> MT103/MT940 sweep to CIB/NBE -> EGP conversion (if needed) at the CBE market rate.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Egyptian Local File is required when the aggregate value of controlled transactions in a fiscal year exceeds EGP 6,000,000, or where annual business revenues exceed EGP 20,000,000. Master File and CbC report notification are required for multinational groups with consolidated global revenue above EGP 7,000,000,000. A transfer-pricing declaration is mandatory on the corporate income tax return even where documentation thresholds are not crossed.

Economic substance scope: Egypt does not apply a standalone Economic Substance Regulations regime similar to UAE offshore centers. However, Article 32 of Income Tax Law No. 91/2005 embodies substance-over-form: the ETA may disregard artificial or sham transactions and recharacterize arrangements lacking commercial rationale. In treaty-relief cases, the beneficial-ownership test prevents shell holding companies from accessing lower dividend, interest, or royalty withholding rates. A PropTech LLC that is a nominal tombstone company without personnel, management premises, active board conduct, or substantive platform activity will fail anti-abuse and TP review.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Evasion penalty of 100% of avoided tax, 2% monthly late-payment interest, administrative fine up to EGP 200,000, and imprisonment of 2 to 5 years; ETA may additionally impose a 30% concealment addition where accounts have been falsified.

Director personal liability: Directors and authorized signatories who signed or caused the false return are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax; ETA may issue a travel ban, seize personal assets, disqualify the director, and pursue criminal imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Abuse / Synthetic Margin Stripping

Statutory sanction: ETA applies a transfer-pricing adjustment disallowing the non-arm's-length expense, adds 100% of the corrected income, imposes penalty up to 25% of the adjustment, and accrues 2% monthly interest for non-disclosure; failure to maintain TP documentation brings fixed penalties up to EGP 150,000.

Director personal liability: Directors approving such arrangements without substance face a personal tax assessment for unpaid CIT attributable to the pricing adjustment, administrative penalties on the underpaid tax, and cross-border enforcement through asset freezing and travel restriction.
Dividend-Stripping / Treaty Shopping

Statutory sanction: ETA denies the DTA benefit, imposes the full 10% statutory dividend WHT, adds a penalty of up to 100% of the under-withheld tax, 2% monthly late-payment interest, and may block further dividend remittances.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for unremitted WHT on distributions; failure to withhold and remit is a criminal default carrying personal fines, travel bans, and imprisonment of up to three years.
Money Laundering / Shell Corporation

Statutory sanction: Under Egyptian Anti-Money Laundering Law No. 80 of 2002 and counterterrorist financing amendments: imprisonment of 3 to 7 years, fines of EGP 100,000 to EGP 10,000,000, confiscation of funds and property, and suspension of licenses.

Director personal liability: Directors with knowledge or willful blindness bear joint and several criminal liability; personal assets are frozen, travel bans issued, restitution of laundered amounts required, and imprisonment to seven years may be imposed.
Failure to Withhold on Royalties and Interest

Statutory sanction: ETA grosses up the underlying payment, assesses the unpaid WHT against the Egyptian LLC, imposes a 100% non-withholding penalty, adds 2% monthly late-payment interest, and disallows the underlying expense as non-deductible.

Director personal liability: Officers who control payments are personally liable for the unremitted WHT and cannot escape liability through liquidation; ETA may freeze the operating bank account and issue personal travel bans until the WHT is fully settled.
