Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance of the Arab Republic of Egypt; principal official portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg; integrated e-invoicing/e-receipt clearance portal: https://einvoicing.eta.gov.eg.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Egyptian LLC: (1) submit corporate registration documents (Articles of Incorporation, Commercial Registry extract) and the contract of incorporation to the ETA's Unified Tax Registration facility; (2) file the prescribed tax identification application with certified Arabic translation, board resolution appointing the manager or resident tax representative, a certified copy of the company's commercial license, authorized signatory IDs, and power of attorney; (3) register for the Unified Tax Identification Number (UTIN) and obtain the statutory tax card; (4) undergo ETA review and potential physical inspection of the registered office; (5) after issuance, file for VAT/e-invoicing registration under the same UTIN and obtain the e-invoicing code-signing certificate before commencing taxable activity.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; an Egyptian LLC is a juridical person resident in Egypt by virtue of incorporation under Egyptian law and is subject to corporate income tax on worldwide income under Law No. 91 of 2005, as amended, with non-resident shareholders having no direct pass-through status. — For a foreign-owned Egyptian LLC: (1) submit corporate registration documents (Articles of Incorporation, Commercial Registry extract) and the contract of incorporation to the ETA's Unified Tax Registration facility; (2) file the prescribed tax identification application with certified Arabic translation, board resolution appointing the manager or resident tax representative, a certified copy of the company's commercial license, authorized signatory IDs, and power of attorney; (3) register for the Unified Tax Identification Number (UTIN) and obtain the statutory tax card; (4) undergo ETA review and potential physical inspection of the registered office; (5) after issuance, file for VAT/e-invoicing registration under the same UTIN and obtain the e-invoicing code-signing certificate before commencing taxable activity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; an Egyptian LLC is a juridical person resident in Egypt by virtue of incorporation under Egyptian law and is subject to corporate income tax on worldwide income under Law No. 91 of 2005, as amended, with non-resident shareholders having no direct pass-through status.

Available election forms

Egypt does not operate a US-style check-the-box regime or provide separate hybrid mismatch elections. Statutory elections available to an Egyptian LLC include (a) consolidation of affiliated taxable groups into a single corporate tax return with ETA approval when legally permitted; (b) VAT registration and the small-taxpayer/simplified VAT regime for turnover below statutory thresholds; (c) election to use exemption treatment for qualifying long-term security and participation holdings where Egyptian tax law confers capital-gains/dividend relief; and (d) available tax-loss carryforward utilization elections subject to ETA supervision and Egyptian GAAP accounting records.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT registration is mandatory when annual taxable turnover of goods exceeds EGP 500,000 or when annual taxable turnover of services exceeds EGP 250,000; non-resident suppliers of electronic services or digital services to Egyptian consumers may be subject to VAT registration without reference to these thresholds under subsequent ETA directives.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for (a) tangible goods physically exported outside Egypt under a valid customs export declaration showing the Egyptian exporter as the legal consignor; and (b) services supplied to a non-resident client and consumed entirely outside Egypt, including offshore immigration and study-abroad consultancy services when the benefit is realized abroad. The claim requires a complete documentary trail evidencing performance abroad, foreign recipient status, and receipt of export consideration in convertible foreign currency into an Egyptian licensed bank account.

  • Customs Export Declaration (final clearing copy approved by Egyptian Customs Authority, including container/vessel identifiers and invoice/EDIFACT reference)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing the definitive movement of goods outside Egyptian territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by an Egyptian licensed bank confirming collection of the export proceeds in foreign currency

Egypt mandates B2B e-invoicing and B2C e-receipt clearance through the ETA's national fiscal platform. Issuer ERPs must integrate through the ETA's REST/JSON API, authenticate with OAuth/roles, sign XML/JSON invoice files using a private code-signing certificate, transmit each invoice or e-receipt for ETA validation in real time, and obtain an ETA-issued UUID plus QR fiscal stamp before delivering the invoice to the counterparty. The system applies mandatory staged onboarding by annual turnover; all newly registered LLCs must have an active code-signing certificate, a secure digital transaction ledger, and must accept ETA clearance rejection or failure notifications.

The ETA, with the Egyptian Customs Authority, audits zero-rated export claims under strict evidentiary standards. Any missing, forged, or inconsistent export documentation causes immediate reversal of zero-rating, reassessment of VAT at 14%, collection of late-interest and delay penalties, and reversal of claimed input VAT credits. Fraudulent zero-rating or deliberate document falsification exposes the LLC to monetary penalties of no less than EGP 20,000 and up to double the recovered tax amount, disqualification from future e-invoicing clearance privileges, and criminal liability including imprisonment for tax evasion and forgery; foreign-owned LLCs also risk cross-border escalation and banking restrictions under Central Bank of Egypt regulations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Egyptian pound (EGP) is generally convertible for current-account transactions such as profit repatriation, visa/immigration service fees, and trade payments, subject to documentary verification and licensed-dealer settlement. Capital-account remittances, direct foreign investment entry/exit, and cross-border financing require CBE registration, licensed-bank intermediation, and source-of-funds justification. Dividend/profit remittance by a non-resident parent of an Egyptian LLC is permitted after audited financial statements, corporate tax clearance and CBE-approved dealer conversion; no pre-clearance fee is charged, but FX liquidity, bank capacity and anti-money laundering checks can delay settlement. The unified official rate environment reduces the premium of parallel-market conversion but maintains tight purpose-code and negative list controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under CBE FX regulations applicable to exporters (including entities with mixed services/exports), export receipts must be repatriated through an authorized bank within 180 days from the bill-of-lading/date of shipment and at least 50% of the foreign-currency export proceeds must be surrendered/converted into EGP at the official bank rate unless a CBE sectoral exemption is granted. For services falling under ISIC 7810, fees for study-abroad and immigration-consulting services are treated as service exports and must be evidenced by a service contract and invoice; receipt is required no later than 180 days after invoice date, with a mandatory 50% conversion in the absence of a CBE waiver. Documents supporting the transaction must be retained for five years.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE), acting in coordination with the Ministry of Finance as the unified foreign-exchange administrator under Banking Law No. 194/2020 and CBE FX circulars.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for purely offshore EMI entities. The CBE restricts deposit-taking and foreign-currency execution to licensed banks; non-bank EMI or payment-facilitator arrangements require a CBE one-stop authorization for payment services and are severely constrained for inward-direct-investment or escrow purposes. Subject to legal-structure review, an offshore EMI can serve as an ancillary payment rails connector for expense disbursements but cannot act as the primary treasury/escrow depository for Egyptian LLC capital or dividends.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

22.5% on net taxable income of juridical persons under Article 47 of Law No. 91 of 2005 as amended by Law No. 30 of 2023.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% domestic withholding tax on dividends because Egyptian resident companies distribute already-taxed profits under the classical exemption in Article 44 of Law No. 91 of 2005; however, distributions made out of tax-exempt Free Zone profits are subject to a 10% tax levied on the distributing company, creating an effective 10% withholding-equivalent cost on the repatriated dividend.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing and final payment are due within four months after the close of the fiscal year (e.g., by 30 April for a 31 December year-end); the fiscal year may follow the company's Articles of Association subject to ETA approval.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EGP 15 million aggregate annual controlled transaction value triggers mandatory preparation and filing of the TP Disclosure Form annexed to Form No. 1 and the Local File; a Master File is required where the Egyptian LLC is controlled by a non-resident group whose consolidated revenue exceeds the ETA threshold, currently aligned with the OECD Country-by-Country Reporting de minimis of EUR 750 million equivalent.

Economic substance scope: Egypt does not apply the GCC-style Economic Substance Regulations; instead, the general anti-avoidance rule in Article 90 of Law No. 91 of 2005 and Tax Procedure Law No. 206 of 2020 allow the ETA to disregard artificial arrangements lacking commercial purpose and beneficial ownership. The study-abroad and golden-visa consultancy must demonstrate substantive presence through licensed immigration consultants on payroll, a physical registered office, executed client contracts, escrow records, and economic control over its revenue, otherwise the company may be treated as a shell, expenses disallowed, and treaty benefits denied.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion through False Accounting

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment up to five years and a fine equal to two to five times the evaded tax under Articles 112-113 of Law No. 91 of 2005; the ETA also imposes the monthly surcharge up to 90% of the tax under Law No. 206 of 2020, and GAFI may revoke the commercial registration.

Director personal liability: Directors and the designated tax signatory are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, fines, and surcharges; criminal conviction carries imprisonment, automatic bank attachment, and a travel ban until full restitution.
