Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an LLC; the Articles of Association fix the subscribed capital and quotas. The agreed capital must be paid in cash into a temporary corporate bank account before submission, and the bank's capital deposit certificate must be delivered to GAFI. In-kind contributions require a licensed valuator's report. The company is formed upon GAFI's registration decision.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defense Certificate from the Ministry of Interior for the registered office/warehouse after inspection of fire extinguishers, evacuation routes, electrical integrity and separated storage of resin containers (flammable/irritant class). For premises above the enforcement threshold, a licensed engineering office must furnish a structural load certificate. Occupational safety registration under Labor Law No. 12/2003 is needed before the first employee reports; social insurance enrollment is carried out through the GAFI OSS or Social Insurance Authority.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an LLC; the Articles of Association fix the subscribed capital and quotas. The agreed capital must be paid in cash into a temporary corporate bank account before submission, and the bank's capital deposit certificate must be delivered to GAFI. In-kind contributions require a licensed valuator's report. The company is formed upon GAFI's registration decision.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA / الهيئة المصرية العامة للضرائب), an autonomous authority reporting to the Minister of Finance; official digital portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg/; e-invoicing and e-receipt clearance platform: https://srv-invoicing.eta.gov.eg/; complementary single-window incorporation and investment licensing via the General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI): https://www.gafi.gov.eg/.

E-government filing portal

Domestic EG LLC route: (1) incorporate the LLC through GAFI's one-stop shop with notarized Memorandum of Association and obtain the Commercial Registry extract (Sijill Tujari); (2) file the ETA Tax Card application within the statutory post-incorporation window to obtain the corporate Tax Identification Number (TIN); (3) complete VAT registration under Article 78 of Law 67/2016 as amended when annual taxable supplies reach or exceed EGP 500,000, using Form 1 and supporting commercial registration, board resolutions, and identity documents of shareholders and directors; (4) enroll in the ETA mandatory e-invoicing system within the applicable phase window, including procurement of the entity digital signature certificate and ERP/API credential onboarding. Non-resident foreign entity with no Egyptian permanent establishment: apply directly to the ETA for a non-resident VAT/TIN account, appoint a resident tax representative with a formal notarized mandate, and submit equivalent incorporation/tax residency certificates; the tax representative assumes joint liability for issuance, filing, and e-invoicing compliance.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — the Egyptian LLC is a standalone juridical person subject to corporate income tax under Income Tax Law 91/2005, with no shareholder pass-through attributes; standard CIT headline rate is 22.5% (reduced to 15% for holders of certain license-type investment status subject to conditions; 32.5% applies to Suez Canal Authority, EGPC, and banks, which are irrelevant to this retail scope); resident juridical persons with Egyptian management and incorporation are taxed on Egyptian-source income under territorial sourcing principles, with foreign-source income of resident LLCs subject to additional provisions of Article 14 as applied in practice; distributions to shareholders are separately taxed, and no consolidation or flow-through regime is available. — Domestic EG LLC route: (1) incorporate the LLC through GAFI's one-stop shop with notarized Memorandum of Association and obtain the Commercial Registry extract (Sijill Tujari); (2) file the ETA Tax Card application within the statutory post-incorporation window to obtain the corporate Tax Identification Number (TIN); (3) complete VAT registration under Article 78 of Law 67/2016 as amended when annual taxable supplies reach or exceed EGP 500,000, using Form 1 and supporting commercial registration, board resolutions, and identity documents of shareholders and directors; (4) enroll in the ETA mandatory e-invoicing system within the applicable phase window, including procurement of the entity digital signature certificate and ERP/API credential onboarding. Non-resident foreign entity with no Egyptian permanent establishment: apply directly to the ETA for a non-resident VAT/TIN account, appoint a resident tax representative with a formal notarized mandate, and submit equivalent incorporation/tax residency certificates; the tax representative assumes joint liability for issuance, filing, and e-invoicing compliance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the Egyptian LLC is a standalone juridical person subject to corporate income tax under Income Tax Law 91/2005, with no shareholder pass-through attributes; standard CIT headline rate is 22.5% (reduced to 15% for holders of certain license-type investment status subject to conditions; 32.5% applies to Suez Canal Authority, EGPC, and banks, which are irrelevant to this retail scope); resident juridical persons with Egyptian management and incorporation are taxed on Egyptian-source income under territorial sourcing principles, with foreign-source income of resident LLCs subject to additional provisions of Article 14 as applied in practice; distributions to shareholders are separately taxed, and no consolidation or flow-through regime is available.

Available election forms

Egyptian law contains no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election. Available statutory elections relevant to a private LLC: (a) Small and Medium Enterprise (SME) income tax regime under Law 152/2020 for annual turnover not exceeding EGP 20 million — irrevocable 5-year election replacing CIT with deemed-profit rates (0.5% of commercial/industrial turnover, 4% of professional services turnover) and cash-basis accounting, capped against actual profit; (b) voluntary VAT registration election for persons with annual taxable supplies of EGP 50,000 or more but below the EGP 500,000 mandatory threshold; (c) choice of filing basis as a VAT small taxpayer with a simplified 5% scheme where expressly available under ETA guidance; (d) no group relief, consolidated filing, or tax-transparency election is permitted; each LLC must file separately as an independent taxable person.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 of annual taxable supplies for mandatory VAT registration under Article 78(1) of Law 67/2016; voluntary registration available where annual taxable supplies equal or exceed EGP 50,000; non-resident providers of electronic/digital commerce services are required by ETA practice to register for VAT without a local turnover threshold where Egyptian-source supplies are made.

Mandatory real-time fiscal clearance through the ETA e-invoicing engine under Decree 618/2021 and Tax Procedures Law 38/2022: every VAT-registered seller must issue invoices in the ETA XML/JSON schema, transmit each invoice through certified ERP/API integration to the ETA clearance service before issuance to the buyer, obtain an ETA-registered UUID and validation code, embed a digital stamp/signature issued on the taxpayer's electronic certificate, and send only the cleared invoice to the counterparty; invoices lacking a clearance code are inadmissible as evidence and disallow input VAT recovery. The e-receipt (fiscal receipt) mandate also applies in phases to all retail and B2C sales, covering the online maker-hardware channel. Penalties for non-integration, unauthorized serials, or issuance without clearance range from EGP 50,000 to EGP 2,000,000 per violation, plus VAT evasion sanctions when non-compliance is aimed at tax suppression.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Egyptian pound is a partially convertible managed-float currency. Current-account transactions, including e-commerce export proceeds, freight, royalties, and technical service fees, are broadly permitted through authorized banks subject to underlying documentation. Capital-account flows, including inbound equity injections and outbound investment liquidation proceeds, require a substantive CBE bank review, a valid underlying investment registration, and a direct investment declaration in the bank's CBE reporting template. Profit remittances by a non-resident shareholder are not automatically blocked, but are treated as outbound capital transactions and require audited accounts, proof that all Egyptian taxes and statutory withholdings have been settled, and a CBE-approved transfer of foreign currency. The CBE prioritises import settlement, debt servicing, and evident trade flows when allocating scarce foreign currency, so unrestricted convertibility is not available; all conversions must be executed at the interbank market rate.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the CBE's export receipt regime, all export proceeds realised by an Egyptian-licensed LLC must be collected through an authorized Egyptian bank. For physical exports of desktop 3D printers, resin, and consumables, the proceeds must be remitted into the exporter's Egyptian foreign-currency account within 60 calendar days from the bill-of-lading or e-commerce shipment date. The general statutory position is a mandatory 100% repatriation and surrender of documented export proceeds to Egypt, subject to narrow retention rights where the exporter can demonstrate to the bank an immediate permissible use in foreign currency, such as payment for imported filament, spare parts, or trade finance. Banks apply a deemed conversion ratio of 100% of each invoice for reporting purposes; if the proceeds are not used within the permitted retention period, the bank may require conversion into Egyptian pounds at the prevailing interbank rate. E-commerce digital income from app or downloadable software would be treated similarly and categorised as services exports, requiring the same repatriation fingerprint.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) is the sole foreign exchange control and monetary authority under Law No. 194 of 2020. The CBE exercises FX administration through its Banking Supervision and Monetary Policy Departments, with delegated authority to all licensed Egyptian commercial banks to execute trade, investment, and profit remittance payments. The General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI) co-administers direct investment entry records and capital registration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium, conditional. An offshore digital EMI may be used only as a card-acquisition sweep account and never as the primary treasury or settlement account. Under Egyptian Fintech Law No. 22 of 2022 and CBE Payment System regulations, any institution that opens an account or handles e-commerce settlement for an Egyptian-domiciled merchant must be licensed by the CBE or act through a CBE-licensed payment aggregator. A foreign EMI cannot lawfully be the direct counterparty for the LLC's e-commerce payouts or FX settlement; it may receive Stripe or Shopify payouts as a technical bridge if proceeds are auto-swept immediately to a CBE-licensed local bank account, and all underlying transactions are reported to the CBE bank. The LLC should avoid retaining overseas funds for longer than the settlement cycle, as delayed repatriation could be treated as disguised export proceeds holding and trigger AML/FX penalties.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Mismatch between declared e-commerce activity and payment gateway settlement behaviour, particularly high card-not-present volumes or sudden spikes in monthly turnover inconsistent with a new LLC.

Root cause: Mismatch between declared e-commerce activity and payment gateway settlement behaviour, particularly high card-not-present volumes or sudden spikes in monthly turnover inconsistent with a new LLC.

Defense evidence: Full 12 to 24 months of Stripe and Shopify Payments settlement statements, merchant descriptor records, card scheme reference numbers, order IDs, payout reports, customer invoice log, domain SSL certificate, shipping and tracking evidence, courier airway bills, customs declarations for physical 3D printers, supplier invoices, and a written business model narrative that reconciles order volume with bank receipts.

Root cause: Inbound capital injections from a foreign shareholder routed as one-time large-sum remittances without clear economic substance or from loosely documented source-of-funds channels.

Root cause: Frequent or high-value related-party disbursements to China-based resin and consumable suppliers without adequate import documentation or a commercial rationale that matches the bank's transaction profile.

Use of personal accounts, family offshore EMI accounts, or third-party payment channels for treasury, payroll, or settlement of trade payables, creating an intentional or inadvertent commingling red flag.

Root cause: Use of personal accounts, family offshore EMI accounts, or third-party payment channels for treasury, payroll, or settlement of trade payables, creating an intentional or inadvertent commingling red flag.

Defense evidence: Written treasury policy prohibiting commingling of personal and corporate funds, complete corporate bank statements for every treasury channel, payroll processing through the corporate account with signatory matrix, ledger records of all shareholder loans, audited financial statements or annual management accounts, and a letter of explanation certified by the directors together with any corrective action plan already implemented.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity or shareholder-loan capital must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the investor's own bank to the Tier 1 CBE-licensed Egyptian bank. The wire instruction must include clear purpose syntax, normally /PURP/INVS/ plus a free-text reference 'DIRECT INVESTMENT IN EGYPTIAN LLC - EG__EG__EG_LLC'. The receiving bank will reconcile the remitter identity, collect a foreign currency / Egyptian pound account number, and demand the LLC's capital increase or initial contribution documents, CR extract, tax card, and board resolution. The bank obtains a CBE direct-investment declaration and a GAFI registration check before crediting the funds to a segregated capital escrow sub-account. Capital escrow funds cannot be debited for operational expenses until the capital verification certificate is issued and the bank allows movement to the operational account. SWIFT gpi is recommended to provide an end-to-end tracker, and all conversion of the received USD or EUR into EGP must be executed at the interbank rate on the receipt date or on the value date selected by the client, with a written conversion instruction and a mirroring CBE record.For import settlement of resin, filament, and 3D printer components, the LLC may use the following rails: (a) open-account SWIFT MT103 payment for values generally below USD 50,000 to 100,000, with purpose code 'trade settlement' and the underlying customs import declaration reference included in field 70; (b) irrevocable documentary Commercial Letter of Credit issued via SWIFT MT700, complemented by MT707 amendments, for larger or supplier-required transactions, with beneficiary in the exporting country and documents covering commercial invoice, packing list, full-set original bill of lading or airway bill, certificate of origin, and final quality certificate; and (c) for local EGP obligations, vendor disbursements are cleared through Egypt's real-time gross settlement system and InstaPay rails. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory when aggregate annual controlled transactions exceed EGP 16 million; Master File required when the MNE group's consolidated turnover exceeds EGP 250 million; CbC report required when the group's consolidated turnover exceeds EGP 500 million. The ETA also requires a TP disclosure schedule wherever controlled transactions exceed EGP 5 million.

Economic substance scope: No standalone Economic Substance Regulations statute as in the UAE; however, Egypt applies substance-over-form and general anti-avoidance rules (GAAR) plus beneficial-ownership tests requiring actual management, premises, staff, and commercial decision-making in Egypt. A letterbox LLC acting solely as an invoicing conduit for non-resident principals is recharacterized, denied treaty benefits, and subject to retroactive assessment and penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax Evasion (online revenue suppression)

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment of up to 5 years and a fine of at least EGP 5,000 and up to the larger of EGP 50,000 or an amount equal to the evaded tax (Article 133, Law No. 91 of 2005); automatic issuance of a default assessment plus the 3% monthly delay surcharge capped at 24% of the unpaid tax

Director personal liability: Directors and the ultimate foreign beneficial owner are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax; the Commercial Enforcement Court may impose travel bans and asset freezes on the managers and signatories; criminal imprisonment may be enforced personally against the directing mind
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Primary TP adjustment of taxable income, recalculation of the CIT at 22.5%, delay surcharge on the additional tax, and an administrative penalty of up to EGP 50,000 under Law No. 86 of 2022; repeated non-compliance triggers an enhanced audit risk across all open years

Director personal liability: Directors who sign inaccurate Form 516 declarations or TP disclosures incur personal fines and may be held jointly liable for the adjusted tax; ETA may pursue their Egyptian personal assets to settle the corporate liability
Shell Company / Sham Commercial Substance

Statutory sanction: ETA recharacterization of the intermediary structure, denial of the DTA claim on dividends, application of the 10% dividend WHT plus additional tax of 20% of the adjusted amount, and criminal referral for false bookkeeping

Director personal liability: Corporate veil piercing permits civil recovery directly against the controlling shareholders and shadow directors; travel bans and seizure of personal bank accounts are available under Egyptian execution law where the entity has no separately realizable capital
Dividend Withholding Tax Default

Statutory sanction: The LLC remains liable for the unwithheld tax, the 3% monthly delay surcharge (max 24%), and a separate penalty equal to the amount of tax not withheld; no statute of limitation applies where the distribution was made without a declared withholding

Director personal liability: The directors and the officer who executed the dividend mandate are jointly and severally liable for the uncollected WHT; an asset freeze takes effect automatically upon issuance of a formal ETA notice of deficiency
AML / Sanctions Compliance Failure (payment rails)

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment and a fine of up to EGP 1,000,000 under Law No. 80 of 2002 (as amended); Stripe and Shopify may terminate the merchant account, and the ETA may revoke the LLC's tax certificate and freeze trade registrations

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly operate the undisclosed payment structure face personal prosecution, travel bans, confiscation of proceeds, and personal civil restitution to the Egyptian Central Bank's compensation fund
