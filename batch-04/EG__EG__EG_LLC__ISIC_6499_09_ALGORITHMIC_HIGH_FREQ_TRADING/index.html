Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Not permissible as a final registered office. Egyptian law mandates a physical, identifiable and accessible registered office in the jurisdiction for LLC incorporation under Companies Law No. 72 of 2023. For the intended proprietary high-frequency trading activity falling under Capital Market Law No. 95 of 1992, the FRA additionally requires a dedicated physical head office in Egypt with on-site trading and compliance infrastructure, low-latency data-network or co-location arrangements, business-continuity facilities, and resident compliance officers. A virtual office may only serve as a temporary provisional address during the pre-incorporation and pre-licensing phase; it must be replaced by a fully verifiable leased physical premises before the FRA license dossier is submitted.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is mandated for an LLC under Law 159/1981; however, bank escrow account confirmation of at least EGP 50,000 (or such amount specified in the Articles) is required to evidence capital availability, with funds released upon issuance of the automatic tax ID and commercial registration certificate.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defense clearance certificate is required for the office/data facility, including fire safety certification for server rooms, automatic fire suppression system testing, emergency egress inspection, electrical load stability review and structural load certification for raised floors and equipment racks. Occupational health and safety clearance is issued by the local governorate labor office confirming appropriate workplace ergonomics, air-conditioning and 24/7 trading-floor risk management.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) – official portal: www.eta.gov.eg; e-invoicing clearance portal: https://einvoicing.eta.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must register with the ETA to obtain a Tax Card (TIN) and VAT registration certificate. Procedure: submit Form 1 (tax registration) to the competent tax office or use the Investor Services Center; for e-invoicing, complete separate registration via the ETA's e-invoice portal with authentication and digital signature setup. Law No. 91 of 2005 and Law No. 67 of 2016 (VAT).

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Foreign entities must register with the ETA to obtain a Tax Card (TIN) and VAT registration certificate. Procedure: submit Form 1 (tax registration) to the competent tax office or use the Investor Services Center; for e-invoicing, complete separate registration via the ETA's e-invoice portal with authentication and digital signature setup. Law No. 91 of 2005 and Law No. 67 of 2016 (VAT).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or entity classification election under Egyptian law. LLCs are per se taxable entities. Statutory elections may be available under the Income Tax Law for depreciation methods, inventory valuation, or loss carryforward, but no hybrid mismatch election is currently codified.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 annual taxable turnover; mandatory for persons whose taxable supplies per calendar year exceed this threshold. Any foreign entity with a fixed establishment in Egypt performing taxable activities must register regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to exports of goods and services (including qualifying exported financial services) provided the supplier is VAT-registered, the destination is outside Egypt, and the supply satisfies proof of export or offshore service consumption. For algorithmic trading services supplied to non-residents, the exporter must evidence that the recipient is outside Egypt and that the service is attributable to foreign markets under Economic Substance and VAT guidelines.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

Mandatory real-time e-invoicing and e-receipt clearance through the ETA's e-invoice portal. All B2B invoices must be submitted in XML/JSON format via authorized APIs with ETA-issued digital certificates; the ETA validates and assigns an invoice code (QR stamp) in real-time. Non-compliance blocks the transaction or triggers penalties.

Fraudulent export zero-rating claims or refund applications trigger a penalty of 100% of VAT evaded, a minimum fine of EGP 5,000, plus interest (statutory late-payment rate). The tax may be retroactively clawed back for up to five years. Criminal penalties include imprisonment. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days reverses the zero-rating and creates a VAT liability with surcharges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt maintains a managed floating exchange rate with partial capital account convertibility. Current account transactions are convertible upon submission of supporting documentation. Capital account items, including equity contributions and profit repatriation for foreign-owned LLCs, require CBE prior approval and are subject to repatriation limits and documentation verification. Profit remittances are permitted after corporate tax clearance but remain subject to enhanced AML monitoring.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Egyptian entities must surrender 100% of export proceeds to authorized banks within three months of collection. Conversion to Egyptian pounds occurs at the official CBE rate. Algorithmic trading activities classified as financial services are subject to analogous forex surrender protocols upon receipt of any foreign exchange earnings, with full documentation of underlying transactions required.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility rating. CBE regulations prohibit unlicensed cross-border electronic money issuance and require all digital payment services to be conducted through licensed Egyptian banks or approved fintech partnerships.
