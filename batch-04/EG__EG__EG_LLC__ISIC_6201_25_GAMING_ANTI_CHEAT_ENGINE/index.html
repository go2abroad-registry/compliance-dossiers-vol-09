Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance; official e-tax portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg; e-invoicing platform: https://einvoicing.eta.gov.eg; e-receipts and unified taxpayer services accessible through the ETA Integrated Tax System (Misr Digital).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Egyptian LLC must file its corporate tax registration dossier at the competent ETA tax office for the head office within 30 days of commercial registration. The LLC submits: (1) an application for the unified Tax Card on ETA forms; (2) certified copies of the Commercial Registry certificate, Memorandum of Association, board resolution appointing the tax representative, directors'/UBO passports, head-office lease, and bank account evidence; (3) the ETA then issues the permanent Tax Identification Number (TIN) used for CIT, VAT, PAYE and stamp tax; (4) VAT registration is made with the same TIN, obligated within 30 days after taxable supplies exceed EGP 500,000 in any rolling 12-month period; (5) the LLC must activate its e-invoicing profile, obtain an ETA digital certificate and API integrator credentials before issuing any invoice.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-owned Egyptian LLC must file its corporate tax registration dossier at the competent ETA tax office for the head office within 30 days of commercial registration. The LLC submits: (1) an application for the unified Tax Card on ETA forms; (2) certified copies of the Commercial Registry certificate, Memorandum of Association, board resolution appointing the tax representative, directors'/UBO passports, head-office lease, and bank account evidence; (3) the ETA then issues the permanent Tax Identification Number (TIN) used for CIT, VAT, PAYE and stamp tax; (4) VAT registration is made with the same TIN, obligated within 30 days after taxable supplies exceed EGP 500,000 in any rolling 12-month period; (5) the LLC must activate its e-invoicing profile, obtain an ETA digital certificate and API integrator credentials before issuing any invoice.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None for entity classification: Egypt has no check-the-box or pass-through election; an LLC is mandatorily a resident taxpayer on worldwide taxable income at the 22.5% corporate rate. Practically available elections are: (a) voluntary VAT registration below the EGP 500,000 threshold; (b) the small-business presumptive gross-receipts regime under Article 52, Income Tax Law No. 91 of 2005, as amended, for qualifying taxpayers below the ministerial turnover ceiling; (c) investment incentive elections under Investment Law No. 72 of 2017 for qualifying projects; (d) advance tax rulings on hybrid financial instruments; no domestic hybrid-mismatch or fiscal-transparency election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 of annual taxable supplies in a rolling 12-month period (mandatory registration; voluntary registration permitted below the threshold).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Law No. 67 of 2016, exported goods and services qualify for 0% VAT. For the LLC's kernel-level anti-cheat middleware, software licenses, SaaS subscriptions and related technical support: zero-rating requires (i) the customer is non-resident with no Egyptian fixed establishment; (ii) the contract, license and support services are supplied from Egypt but consumed/used wholly outside Egypt; (iii) the supply is evidenced by a signed contract, ETA-cleared e-invoice, electronic delivery/activation logs, and an incoming foreign-currency remittance; (iv) the proceeds are repatriated to an Egyptian bank within 180 days from the supply/shipment date. Services consumed in Egypt (e.g., local gaming operators with an Egyptian establishment) remain standard-rated at 14%. Input VAT attributable to zero-rated exports is creditable and refundable under ETA refund procedures.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document) for physical goods
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill evidencing physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign-currency credit advice from an Egyptian bank
  • Signed commercial contract / purchase order identifying the non-resident customer
  • ETA-cleared zero-rated e-invoice bearing the ETA QR stamp
  • Electronic delivery and service-activation logs for software middleware
  • FX repatriation confirmation evidencing receipt within 180 days

Mandatory ETA e-invoicing and e-receipts (effective phases from November 2020; Law No. 67 of 2016 and Cabinet/ETA decrees): every VAT-registered business must issue invoices through the ETA central fiscal-clearing platform using the approved XML/JSON schema, sign with the ETA digital certificate, transmit each invoice in near real time through the ETA REST API or certified ERP connector, obtain the ETA validation response, and print/embed the ETA-generated QR stamp. A non-cleared or QR-less invoice is non-compliant, bars the customer's input-VAT deduction, and attracts sanctions.

ETA audits examine export claims within the 5-year statutory limitation period, focusing on missing customs/transport evidence, absent FX remittance, related-party circular structures, or services demonstrated to be received/consumed in Egypt. Consequences of rejection: the 0% rate is reversed and 14% VAT is assessed with statutory delay interest; administrative fines run from EGP 5,000 to EGP 100,000 per non-compliant invoice and may reach 100% of the tax (up to 300% where intent to evade is proven); criminal referral to the Economic Criminal Court is possible for fraudulent export declarations, with imprisonment of responsible officers; further consequences include denial of VAT refunds, suspension of the TIN and e-invoicing profile, and personal liability of directors/shareholders in fraud cases.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt operates a unified, market-driven exchange-rate system following the March 2024 liberalization. Current-account convertibility is unrestricted through authorized dealer banks for service fees, software royalties, dividends, and profit remittances, subject to documentary verification comprising audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates, the Foreign Investment file registered under Law No. 72 of 2017, and underlying service contracts. Capital-account transactions, including share-capital repatriation in liquidation, require prior registration with the CBE via the authorized bank's investment application file and are executed at market rates. Practical remittance frictions include bank-level FX rationing during EGP liquidity stress, extended compliance review for indirect beneficial owners, and the 10% withholding on dividend profit remittance. For a pure software exporter with no physical goods, the risk of current-account blockage is rated Low-Medium; maintenance of a USD-denominated retention account at an authorized Egyptian bank is the recommended structural mitigation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under CBE foreign-exchange monitoring regulations applicable to software and services export proceeds, export receipts must be repatriated into Egypt within 5 months from the date of service performance or invoice maturity, whichever is later. Following the complete abolition of the March 2022 mandatory 100% conversion-to-EGP surrender decree as part of the 2024 exchange-rate unification, there is currently no statutory mandatory conversion ratio; exporters may retain up to 100% of export proceeds in foreign-currency accounts with authorized banks. Each receipt must be supported by the export service contract, commercial invoice, and the CBE's electronic export declaration recorded with the servicing bank. Non-repatriation within the 5-month statutory window constitutes a violation subject to CBE fines, bank account restrictions, and inclusion in the banking-sector AML exception reporting. Exporters may apply to the CBE for a one-time extension if the foreign counterparty's payment is contractually deferred, provided the deferral is commercially reasonable and documented.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE), acting through the General Department of Foreign Exchange Operations and the Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Combating Unit (MLCU), under Law No. 194 of 2020 (Central Banking and Financial Regulations Law) and Law No. 80 of 2002.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity financing must travel exclusively through an Egyptian authorized dealer bank (ADB). Protocol: (i) submit the LLC's incorporation certificate, Articles of Association, and GAFI investment registration to the ADB; (ii) open a dedicated USD Capital / Investment Account linked to the CBE's investment-record system; (iii) the foreign subscriber wires via SWIFT MT103 naming the LLC as beneficiary and using the IMF/BPM6 Purpose Code 'Direct Investment — Equity Capital' (FDI own funds) with the GAFI certificate and ISIC 6201 description in the narrative; (iv) the ADB files the CBE inward-remittance declaration, assigns an investment reference number, and either converts to EGP at the market rate or credits the USD retention account per the equity plan; (v) register the capital receipt in the LLC's share ledger and obtain the bank's confirmation to maintain the 'registered FX investment file' that is the legal precondition for future profit remittance and liquidation repatriation. Compliance verification requires the MT103/MT910 confirmation, the notarized board resolution approving the capital call, and the GAFI investment certificate. The LLC must avoid parallel-market conversion or any cash deposit of foreign currency, as unregistered inbound capital cannot be legally repatriated.Outbound disbursements for the anti-cheat engine are service-dominated: cloud-hosting fees, middleware tool licenses, platform subscriptions, subcontracted research-and-development services, and inter-affiliate royalties. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

22.5% of worldwide taxable profits for the Egyptian resident LLC (Article 6, Law No. 91 of 2005, as amended by Law No. 2 of 2018).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% gross final withholding tax on dividends and profit distributions paid by the Egyptian LLC to a non-resident shareholder (Article 57, Law No. 91 of 2005).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

On or before four months after the fiscal year-end (Article 135, Law No. 91 of 2005); for a 31 December year-end, the filing and payment deadline is 30 April. A written discretionary extension of up to 60 days may be granted by the ETA upon request before the original deadline.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EGP 10 million: annual aggregate value of related-party transactions (goods, services, IP royalties, interest, guarantees, and cost-sharing) that triggers mandatory Local File and Master File preparation under the ETA Transfer Pricing Guidelines (2021). Below this threshold, the taxpayer must still apply the arm's length principle and retain contemporaneous evidence. The Transfer Pricing Disclosure Schedule embedded in Form 19 must be filed whenever any controlled transaction exists, irrespective of the quantum.

Economic substance scope: Egypt does not maintain a separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute comparable to Gulf Cooperation Council jurisdictions. However, the general anti-avoidance rule in Article 116 of Law No. 91 of 2005, reinforced by Egyptian court doctrine, empowers the ETA to disregard any transaction, structure, or arrangement lacking genuine commercial substance and whose main purpose is tax avoidance. For a non-resident-controlled Egyptian LLC in the gaming anti-cheat sector, substantive local presence must be demonstrable through real employees under Egyptian labor contracts, payroll, actual physical office premises, technical decision-making records, board meetings in Egypt, and complete statutory books. Absent such substance, the ETA may recharacterize profits, deny treaty relief, and impute income to the economic owner.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Under-Filing

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment of two to five years plus a criminal fine of EGP 50,000 to EGP 5,000,000 under Article 144 of Law No. 91 of 2005 (as amended); mandatory recovery of the evaded CIT with the 2% monthly delay surcharge capped at 100% of the tax; asset seizure and freezing orders issued by the competent criminal court; the tax authority's right of civil recovery survives the criminal proceeding.

Director personal liability: Directors, de facto managers, and signatories to the false returns are jointly and severally liable in personam for the evaded tax, the surcharges, and criminal fines. Conviction triggers automatic travel bans, passport seizure, placement on the ETA/CBE blacklists, and imprisonment. Egyptian courts pierce the LLC veil where the corporate form was used to commit fraud or evade tax collection.
Shell Company / Sham Entity

Statutory sanction: Disregard of the shell under Article 116 GAAR; full recharacterization of the income as attributable to the de facto beneficiary; denial of all deducted costs and losses; CIT at 22.5% on the recharacterized profit; delay surcharge of 2% per month; commercial register cancellation under Companies Law 159/1981; disqualification from contracting with Egyptian public bodies.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors face civil liability for the full reassessed tax, administrative debarment from board positions under the Companies Law, entry into the ETA restricted-persons registry, and travel-ban orders where the sham entity fails to settle the liability.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Default

Statutory sanction: ETA upward adjustment of the Egyptian taxable income to the arm's length value; automatic monthly delay surcharge of 2% on the differential tax (maximum 100%); administrative fine for the TP disclosure schedule deficiency; the burden of proof shifts heavily against the taxpayer when no contemporaneous documentation exists, leading to default estimation of the TP adjustment.

Director personal liability: No criminal exposure for a pure documentation default, but the TP tax deficiency becomes a personal enforcement claim against directors if the LLC is dissolved without paying; travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed at the collection stage.
Cross-Border Payment Characterization Fraud

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization by the ETA with full re-assessment of the under-withheld WHT at the statutory 20% or 10% rate; 2% monthly delay surcharge; a fine of up to EGP 1,000,000 for non-compliance with withholding obligations under Article 148(4) of Law 91/2005; interest and inflation adjustment of the tax liability.

Director personal liability: Withholding agents, including the managing director and CFO, are personally liable for the full tax that should have been withheld at source. Forged TRC or benefit certificates trigger criminal exposure with imprisonment up to five years, travel bans, and ancillary asset seizure.
AML & Exchange Control Non-Compliance (Payment Rails)

Statutory sanction: Criminal penalties under AML Law No. 80 of 2002, as amended by Law No. 154 of 2021, providing for imprisonment of three to fifteen years and a fine of EGP 100,000 to EGP 5,000,000; forfeiture of the bank accounts and the connected proceeds; CBE license revocation for payment intermediaries; blacklisting of the company and beneficial owners from the formal banking sector.

Director personal liability: Directors and the compliance officer bear individual criminal responsibility for AML failures; they face independent imprisonment and fines even if the LLC also pays sanctions. Conviction causes automatic travel bans, removal from registered beneficial-owner lists, and lifetime prohibition from serving in any Egyptian financial institution.
