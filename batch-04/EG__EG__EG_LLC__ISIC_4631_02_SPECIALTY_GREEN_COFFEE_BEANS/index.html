Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for Limited Liability Companies in Egypt. The founders set the capital in the Articles of Association. All issued capital must be fully paid in cash or in kind prior to incorporation; cash capital must be deposited in a temporary corporate bank account with a licensed Egyptian bank and restricted until issuance of the commercial registration certificate.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) — official portal: www.eta.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing an Egyptian LLC must apply to the Egyptian Tax Authority for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration. The application is filed through the ETA's unified registration system using the company's commercial registration certificate, constitutional documents, power of attorney for the legal representative, and proof of the appointed tax accountant. Upon completion, the ETA issues a Tax Card (identifying the entity for income tax and VAT purposes) and, where applicable, a separate VAT certificate for goods destined for export.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; Egyptian LLCs are treated as separate taxable persons subject to corporate income tax on worldwide income if considered resident; export activities are within the territorial scope of Egyptian VAT but may be zero-rated. — A foreign entity establishing an Egyptian LLC must apply to the Egyptian Tax Authority for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Egyptian LLCs are treated as separate taxable persons subject to corporate income tax on worldwide income if considered resident; export activities are within the territorial scope of Egyptian VAT but may be zero-rated.

Available election forms

No general check-the-box regime exists under Egyptian law. An LLC must file the annual corporate income tax return under the deemed entity-level taxation model. Foreign entities may elect to register for VAT voluntarily even below the mandatory threshold, and may apply for the ETA's advance ruling mechanism with respect to zero-rating of export supplies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 annual taxable turnover of goods and services (mandatory VAT registration threshold; voluntary registration permitted below this level).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of physical goods to a destination outside Egypt and services supplied to a non-resident (with services physically performed outside Egypt or otherwise qualifying as exported services under the Egyptian VAT law) are eligible for the 0% VAT rate. The exporter must be the registered VAT payer, retain evidence of the actual export, and ensure payment is received through the banking system in the designated foreign currency.

  • Customs Export Declaration (proved by the ETA electronic export statement)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
  • Commercial invoice issued under e-invoicing rules
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Bank transfer received from abroad)
  • Certificate of Origin (if required by the importing country)

Egypt operates a mandatory e-invoicing system administered by the ETA. VAT-registered entities must integrate with the ETA's real-time invoice clearance platform via approved API connections. Each invoice must be submitted for fiscal clearance before issuance; the ETA validates and returns a digital stamp (QR code / Unique Identifier) which must appear on the final invoice. There is also a phased e-receipt mandate covering business-to-consumer sales. Non-compliance can trigger blocking of invoice clearance and administrative penalties.

The ETA may retrospectively reverse zero-rated treatment and assess VAT at the standard rate plus late-payment penalties for any export found not to satisfy the documentary and foreign-currency repatriation requirements. Fraudulent zero-rating claims or falsified export documents can result in fines up to 3 times the tax due, imprisonment under tax evasion provisions, and compulsory seizure of assets. The exporter bears the burden of proof for physical export and receipt of foreign currency within 180 days.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The EGP operates under a flexible exchange-rate regime following the March 2024 liberalization supported by the IMF Extended Fund Facility. Current-account transactions (imports of goods, trade-related services, freight, and insurance) are freely convertible for CBE-licensed banks. Capital-account operations are partially convertible: non-resident equity injections and profit/dividend repatriation are permitted but are conditioned on (i) prior registration of the investment with GAFI under Investment Law No. 72 of 2017, (ii) an accredited audit report confirming the distributable profit, and (iii) a valid tax clearance certificate. A single corporate bank account with a licensed bank is the mandated channel; all FX transactions must be reported to the CBE.

Export proceeds surrender rules

If the LLC undertakes re-exports or earns export proceeds from the distribution of specialty green coffee beyond Egypt, CBE regulations require 100% repatriation of export proceeds to a bank account in Egypt within 4 months from the date of shipment (bill-of-lading date), extendable to 6 months with prior CBE approval. There is no mandatory conversion ratio: the exporter may retain the full foreign currency balance in a USD/EUR account at a licensed Egyptian bank. Late or incomplete repatriation subjects the exporter to administrative fines, suspension of banking facilities, and inclusion on the CBE watchlist.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) and its Foreign Exchange Operations and Compliance Directorate, acting under the Central Bank and Banking System Law No. 194 of 2020 and the Foreign Exchange Law No. 38 of 1994 (as amended).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low as a primary treasury rail. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer) do not hold CBE banking licenses and cannot maintain EGP IBANs, execute CBE-reportable FX conversions, or issue Egyptian import purpose codes. They are legally permissible only as ancillary collection vehicles for non-EGP inbound receivables; all settlement for coffee import transactions must be executed through a CBE-licensed bank under Law No. 194 of 2020 to maintain regulatory auditability.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Non-resident shareholders transmit USD/EUR via SWIFT to the LLC's capital escrow account at NBE, Banque Misr, or CIB using the stated purpose 'Share Capital Subscription of Foreign Investment under GAFI Law No. 72 of 2017'. The receiving bank verifies the Commercial Registry extract, Memorandum of Association, GAFI investment certificate, and the CBE statistical purpose code for capital transfers. On receipt, the bank credits the escrowed foreign currency (or converts to EGP at the prevailing interbank rate), issues a Foreign Currency Deposit receipt for audit, and releases funds to the operational account after the CBE's onward reporting is generated.Supplier payments for specialty green coffee are cleared through two compliant rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 for post-shipment and cash-against-documents settlements, and (ii) Commercial Letter of Credit (MT700) at sight for pre-shipment cover. All import transactions must first be registered on the National Window / Advanced Cargo Information platform (CargoX) to obtain the ACI number, without which the bank will not approve FX disbursement. LCs are issued by the Egyptian bank in USD or the supplier's currency against corporate documentary cover, and disbursements are matched to the presented documents (commercial invoice, B/L, certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate, and insurance).Deliverable USD/EGP FX forward with tenor up to 6 months, executed through a CBE-licensed bank against a verifiable coffee import contract and reported under CBE foreign-exchange operations guidelinesBack-to-back cross-currency swap with the same LC-issuing Egyptian bank, converting the coffee payable into EGP matched to the LLC's operating cash-flow cycleTrade-backed FX option collar purchased through a CBE-licensed bank for inventory price protection; offshore USD/EGP non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not legally permissible for Egyptian resident entities
