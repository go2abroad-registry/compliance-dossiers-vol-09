Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No general statutory minimum capital is prescribed for an Egyptian LLC. The partners determine capital in the incorporation contract; capital contributions must be paid as required by the applicable company regime and GAFI procedures. The capital is not subject to the joint-stock-company bank deposit certificate procedure. Confirm any current GAFI payment evidence requirements before filing. The amount above is not a representation that government filing charges are zero; charges are assessed in Egyptian pounds and vary by filing and service.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) under the Ministry of Finance; unified digital compliance portal at https://www.eta.gov.eg, with e-filing at https://www.incometax.gov.eg and e-invoicing/e-receipt clearance at https://einvoicing.eta.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

Foreign-invested LLC must (1) incorporate under Investment Law No. 72 of 2017 via GAFI or under Companies Law No. 4 of 2018; (2) obtain commercial registration from the Commercial Registry; (3) file GAFI single-window 'golden license' data; (4) submit the ETA unified tax registration form (with notarized memorandum of association, commercial register extract, board resolution appointing the legal representative, power of attorney for the tax agent, and proof of office address); (5) receive the TIN, VAT account and electronic Tax Card (issued in approximately 10 business days). The TIN activates e-filing and e-invoicing accounts before the first taxable supply.

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists; classification is statutory. Only relevant corporate election is fiscal-unity consolidated filing for an Egyptian parent owning at least 95% of Egyptian subsidiaries (ETA pre-approval, minimum five-year commitment, and application within three months of the relevant tax period end). VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration when annual taxable supplies exceed EGP 500,000; voluntary registration for turnover between EGP 100,000 and EGP 500,000 (VAT Law No. 67 of 2016, Art. 15). De facto unconditional for an LLC holding commercial registration because an active ETA tax file and VAT account are prerequisites for lawful invoicing.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Article 23 of VAT Law 67/2016 zero-rates exported goods and services supplied by a registered taxpayer where (i) the recipient is a non-resident outside Egypt; (ii) the service is legally and economically consumed/benefited outside Egypt; (iii) the supply is evidenced by export contracts, proof of delivery, and ETA-cleared zero-rated e-invoices; and (iv) consideration is received in foreign currency. 3D game art, sculpting and rigging services performed in Egypt but delivered to overseas game studios qualify as offshore services if no economic benefit manifests inside Egypt, supported by the service agreement, milestone acceptance, IP assignment and FX remittance records. Output VAT at 14% applies to any local portion or misuse of the exemption.

  • ETA-cleared zero-rated export e-invoice (QR-coded, accepted by the platform)
  • Customs export declaration (only for physical media/source-file shipments; for pure digital services substitute a digital delivery log with SHA-256 hashes and FTP/cloud transfer reports)
  • Signed international service contract evidencing the offshore recipient and place of beneficial use
  • Proof of acceptance and delivery (milestone checklist, email acceptance, rig/asset handover log)
  • Foreign-currency inward remittance certificate from a licensed Egyptian bank
  • Valid tax card and VAT registration certificate (with updated activity code)

Mandatory national e-invoicing regime (phased from 15 Feb 2021, universal for all taxable persons from 15 Oct 2022): every B2B invoice must be transmitted in real time to the ETA e-invoicing platform in XML/JSON via REST/SOAP APIs; the ETA validates, assigns a UUID and single-use hash, returns an accepted cleared version, and each printed copy must carry a QR code. E-receipts (B2C) are likewise cleared (from Jan 2022). Paper or unsynced invoices are denied input VAT deduction and are deemed non-issued; digital stamping links customs import entries to the invoice registry.

ETA export-audit desk reviews XML e-invoice records, customs declarations, IP/delivery logs, bank statements and FX certificates. Non-receipt of export proceeds within 180 days triggers retroactive assessment of 14% VAT on the grossed-up service value plus delay interest and double-tax penalty; fraudulent zero-rating claims additionally attract administrative fines up to EGP 100,000, imprisonment, and a criminal fine up to five times the VAT evaded (Law 67/2016 Arts. 66-68), re-opening of prior tax periods up to six years (Income Tax Law 91/2005), and deactivation of the e-invoicing account.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt operates a managed-float regime. Current account transactions, including trade-related payments and service fees, are generally freely convertible through official banks subject to standard documentation and anti-money laundering checks. Capital account transactions, including inbound capital injections and outbound profit remittances, are permitted but require express licensing, declaration via the CBE's electronic platform, and compliant routing through a licensed domestic bank. Dividends and branch profits may be remitted after audited financial statements and proof of tax clearance. Foreign ownership of an LLC is permitted, but capital repatriation hinges on registration of the investment with the General Authority for Investment (GAFI) and obtaining a certificate of compliance with the Investment Law No. 72 of 2017. Non-trade capital flows remain subject to de facto administrative review, which can create timing friction; however, for permitted activities such as service exports under ISIC 7490, current account settlement is reliable.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be received into an Egyptian banking account within 90 days of the date of shipment or customs clearance. Upon crediting, the exporter is required to surrender a designated percentage of the proceeds, currently 50%, to a licensed bank for conversion into Egyptian pounds at the prevailing market rate. The remaining 50% may be held in a foreign currency account or sold at the exporter's discretion. To trigger the receipt window, the bank must be provided with a valid customs declaration, an export contract, and the pro-forma invoice; exceptions require prior CBE approval. For service and game-art outsourcing contracts, the underlying invoice and a signed service agreement are the core evidentiary documents for the 90-day collection timeline.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) acting as the sole monetary and foreign exchange authority under Law No. 194 of 2020 and CBE Exchange Control Regulations

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. Offshore digital electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Revolut Business, Airwallex, or Payoneer cannot provide a primary operational account for the Egyptian LLC without obtaining a CBE license or registering as a payment service aggregator with the CBE's FinTech and Innovation Unit. The CBE Law No. 194 of 2020 and the payments regulatory framework require any entity handling client funds in Egypt to be licensed as a bank, specialized payment service operator, or a third-party processor supervised by the CBE. Practical utility is limited to incoming international receipts via PayPal or similar RIFF-facilitated channels, but these cannot substitute for the mandatory local bank account used for FX surrender and tax remittance procedures. The safest treatment is to treat offshore EMIs as remote collection venues only after the underlying export service invoice has been raised and the proceeds are subsequently repatriated to a local account within the 90-day window.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound foreign direct investment or initial capital funding into the Egyptian LLC must be routed through a licensed Egyptian bank using the unified investment transaction purpose code. For equity capital injections, the transfer should carry Purpose Code 'C.F.' (capital formation) or the designated code set by the CBE for inward direct investment, typically '01' for issuance of shares or '03' for shareholder loans. The bank will require the GAFI-issued Investment Certificate, the notarized and legalized incorporation documents, a board resolution approving the capital increase, and an undertaking that the funds will be deposited in the capital account before conversion or restructure. The receiving bank will credit the funds to the LLC's foreign-currency capital account and, if the operating currency is EGP, execute a mandatory conversion into Egyptian pounds at the prevailing interbank rate. Non-resident shareholders must ensure the wire is denominated in USD, EUR, or GBP and includes the Company CR number in the beneficiary field; funds sent to personal accounts or unrelated third-party payees will be rejected.Outbound routing for trade and supplier settlements must be performed through the corporate account using the SWIFT global payment ecosystem. For low-value non-documentary service transactions (e.g., paying freelance riggers in Pakistan or Ukraine), the standard rail is a SWIFT MT103 (customer credit transfer) with the appropriate remittance field (72 or /INT/) indicating invoice settlement and with tax reference numbers. For high-value physical or IP transfer-backed production milestones, the intended rail is either an MT103 issued unilaterally or a commercial documentary letter of credit (LC) opened by the Egyptian bank for the benefit of the overseas seller. For the 3D art outsourcing company, the primary outbound use is dividend repatriation to non-resident shareholders and payments to subcontracted artists; both require documentary proof of the underlying contract, relevant withholding tax certificates, and annual corporate tax filings. Egyptian banks offer a compliant outbound wire service via CBE's centralized settlement system, but banks will require Submission of the 'MT103 Cover' instruction, the supporting invoice, and the tax declaration where applicable. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

22.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends distributed by an Egyptian LLC to its shareholder, whether resident or non-resident, unless reduced or exempted under an applicable double tax agreement.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 4 months after the close of the accounting fiscal year. A 31 December year-end therefore requires filing and final payment by 30 April; a 30 June year-end requires filing by 31 October.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EGP 10 million in aggregate related-party transactions triggers mandatory Local File preparation; Master File becomes obligatory where the consolidated group turnover exceeds EGP 200 million. The documentation must be submitted within 30 days of the Egypt Tax Authority's request.

Economic substance scope: Egypt has not enacted UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations, but the Egyptian Tax Authority applies substance-over-form, controlled-foreign-company and beneficial-ownership rules under Law No. 91/2005 and the Tax Procedures Law. A game 3D art outsourcing LLC must maintain actual Egyptian management, payroll, hardware, software, contracts, and delivery capacity; a letterbox company with no real staff is exposed to PE, anti-abuse, and DTA denial.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment for up to 5 years and a fine of up to EGP 500,000, plus payment of evaded tax, late-interest, and a civil penalty calculated on the evaded amount.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns may be jointly and severally liable for the corporate tax debt, subjected to travel bans and asset seizure, and criminally prosecuted for tax fraud.
Transfer Pricing Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable profit, disallowance of the excessive intercompany deduction, interest on underpaid CIT, and penalties of up to 100% of the tax under-assessment under Egyptian TP rules.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally assessed for the adjusted tax if they executed the intercompany agreements and intentionally caused the mispricing; professional negligence claims are available against external TP advisors.
Shell Company / No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Re-characterization of revenue, denial of treaty benefits, deemed permanent establishment status for the foreign parent, retroactive CIT, WHT and penalties.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors may be personally exposed for fraudulent asset protection and are also subject to immigration travel bans during tax enforcement.
Dividend and Royalty WHT Evasion

Statutory sanction: Withholding tax is recovered from the Egyptian LLC together with late-payment interest, fines of up to 100% of the withheld amount, and criminal prosecution for breach of Tax Procedures Law No. 27/2021.

Director personal liability: The directors responsible for the remittance are jointly liable for the un-withheld tax and may face personal criminal sanctions, including imprisonment.
AML / Financial Compliance Breach

Statutory sanction: Asset freezing, fines under the Egyptian Anti-Money Laundering Law and Central Bank regulations, confiscation of proceeds, and referral to the Public Prosecutor.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and compliance officers face personal criminal liability for failing to identify beneficial owners and report suspicious wire or PayPal transactions, including imprisonment and personal fines.
