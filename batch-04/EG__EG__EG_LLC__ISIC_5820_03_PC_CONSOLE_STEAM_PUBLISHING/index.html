Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt maintains a unified, market-determined exchange rate following the 6 March 2024 CBE liberalisation and the elimination of the prior multi-rate system. Current-account convertibility is effectively full: service-export receipts from Steam (Valve) and PlayStation Store may be retained in USD/EUR at a licensed Egyptian bank and converted into EGP at interbank rates; outward payments for imported services and platform royalties are permissible after submission of the underlying agreement, invoices, and withholding-tax receipts. Capital-account convertibility remains transaction-driven: equity injection and repatriation of subscribed capital, dividends, and shareholder loans require GAFI registration, a valid commercial-registry extract and tax card, audited financial statements demonstrating distributable reserves, and final tax clearances from the Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA). Article 8 of Investment Law No. 72 of 2017 guarantees the transfer of investment proceeds and principal after payment of taxes, but practical hurdles persist in the form of periodic interbank USD/EUR liquidity tightness, bank-level documentary scrutiny, and CBE prioritisation of essential imports. All conversion and remittance must be executed through licensed banks; parallel-market or kerb-side dealing is unlawful under Law No. 80 of 1947.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Egypt imposes a repatriation discipline rather than a mandatory cash-surrender regime. Under the CBE framework in force after the March 2024 float, proceeds from goods exports as well as services exports, including digital-game publishing and Steam/PlayStation royalties, must be credited to an authorised bank in Egypt within the statutory window: goods proceeds within six months from shipment/customs clearance, and services export proceeds within three months from the invoice date or the month-end settlement statement for platform sales. There is currently no fixed percentage of proceeds that must be converted into EGP; exporters may hold 100% of receipts in foreign currency in an Egyptian bank-account, and any conversion must occur at the prevailing interbank rate with a licensed counterparty. The CBE monitors compliance through the bank's trade-finance and reporting systems; late repatriation triggers watchlisting, restriction of import facilities, and administrative penalties under Law No. 194 of 2020. The EG LLC must therefore maintain a repatriation register matching each Valve payout advice and PlayStation Store remittance statement to SWIFT credits entering Egypt within the applicable deadline.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE), operating under Law No. 194 of 2020 (Central Bank and Banking System Law) read together with Foreign Exchange Law No. 80 of 1947 (as amended). The CBE is the sole monetary and foreign-exchange regulatory authority; its Banking Supervision Sector, Foreign Operations Sector, and licensed dealing banks administer all FX approvals, reporting, and market interventions. The General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI) is the parallel authority registering the EG LLC's foreign capital, maintaining the investment file, and certifying capital-account entries.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: Medium, with material legal constraints. Law No. 194 of 2020 reserves the business of taking deposits and executing payment services to CBE-licensed banks and authorized non-bank payment institutions. Offshore digital EMIs and virtual-account providers unlicensed in Egypt (such as certain multi-currency electronic-money platforms) may not lawfully serve as the EG LLC's principal treasury or operative bank account, as they would be engaging in regulated banking activity without a CBE licence. They may, however, be used as auxiliary collection and aggregation rails for Steam/Valve and PlayStation Store payouts (obtaining USD payment details/IBAN statements and automating reconciliation), provided that funds are swept immediately to the Egyptian licensed bank within the repatriation window and the EMI maintains no Egyptian establishment or customer-facing presence. Locally licensed fintech rails such as Fawry and Masary electronic-payment solutions are CBE-compliant for domestic collections and payouts but do not extend to offshore platform-store settlements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injection from the foreign shareholders into the EG LLC must be executed by SWIFT MT103 into the company's capital-formation escrow account at a licensed Egyptian bank. Protocol: (1) notarise the LLC's incorporation filings and register with GAFI and the Commercial Registry, recording the subscribed capital and foreign-ownership ratio; (2) open a temporary capital-deposit account presenting the registry extract, account-resolution of the managers, and share-subscription declarations; (3) the offshore shareholder instructs its remitting bank to wire the exact subscribed amount in USD/EUR with payment reference 'EG LLC - share capital subscription - GAFI investment file no. [___]'; (4) the receiving Egyptian bank verifies the inbound wire against the GAFI registration, credits the capital account, and issues the statutory capital-deposit certificate required for the commercial-registry formalities; (5) upon publication of the commercial registry, the temporary account is converted into the LLC's permanent operational account or into a distinct paid-in-capital ledger line; (6) any shareholder loan (debt injection) must be supported by a CBE declaration, a notarised loan agreement, and market-comparable interest-rate evidence to prevent profit-stripping reclassification. All inbound investment wires must carry the appropriate CBE purpose/statement code for 'direct investment in Egypt'; conversion to EGP occurs only upon explicit instruction through the interbank market.The LLC's outward treasury settlements clear primarily via SWIFT MT103 direct wires for digital royalties and services; SWIFT MT700 irrevocable documentary letters of credit (UCP600) apply only where physical console-media manufacturing, packaging, or fixed-asset procurement requires a documentary trade instrument. Workflow: (a) platform/engine royalties to Valve, Sony, Epic, or Unity are remitted by MT103 from the USD/EUR operational account following upload of the underlying licence, the supplier's tax invoice, and the ETA withholding-tax certificate (20% gross WHT on royalties/services to non-residents, subject to double-tax-treaty reduction); (b) EGP operational disbursements use the CBE-run Instant Payment Network and RTGS rails; (c) any EGP/USD conversion for outbound wires is booked with the corporate bank under a documented purchase ticket with the CBE reporting code; Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

22.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final withholding/distributed-profits tax on dividends remitted by the Egyptian LLC to its non-resident shareholder (Article 58, Law No. 91 of 2005). No additional branch remittance tax applies to Egyptian permanent establishments.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within four months after the fiscal year-end (Article 51, Law No. 91 of 2005). For a 31 December year-end: due 30 April; for a 30 June year-end: due 31 October. Quarterly advance tax installments are payable during the year and credited against the final liability.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EGP 1 million aggregate annual related-party transactions triggers the mandatory TP Declaration; EGP 10 million aggregate controlled transactions triggers the full Local File; MNE groups with worldwide consolidated revenue exceeding EGP 2 billion must also prepare the Master File (Cabinet Decree No. 228 of 2021).

Economic substance scope: No GCC-style ESR code exists in Egypt, but substance is enforced indirectly: (i) Article 74 GAAR allows the ETA to disregard artificial transactions lacking business purpose; (ii) treaty benefits require beneficial ownership and real presence; (iii) CFC rules impute undistributed foreign profits of controlled low-tax companies; (iv) the GAFI UBO registry requires the LLC to file its ultimate beneficial owner. An LLC booking Steam/PlayStation publishing revenue must have local staff managing Steamworks and PlayStation Partner accounts, its own Egyptian bank accounts, board meeting minutes in Cairo, and complete game-asset agreements; otherwise the ETA may re-characterize the vehicle as contractual arrangements of the parent and tax all revenue at 22.5%.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Concealed Digital Revenue

Statutory sanction: ETA reassessment of the full 22.5% CIT on undeclared revenue plus 2% monthly delay interest (capped at 100%); evasion fine of 100% to 300% of the evaded tax; criminal referral to the economic courts; e-invoice and e-tax record infractions attract separate administrative fines up to EGP 500,000.

Director personal liability: Criminal conviction carries imprisonment up to five years and a court-ordered travel ban; the Public Prosecutor may freeze the directors' personal bank accounts; directors who signed false financial statements are jointly and severally liable for the corporate CIT debt and civil restitution.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: TP documentation penalties of EGP 100,000 to EGP 1,000,000; the ETA adjusts taxable profit to the arm's length result, disallows excess deductions, and assesses 22.5% CIT on the add-back plus 2% monthly interest; the tax clearance certificate is suspended.

Director personal liability: Directors who certified false TP documentation face personal fines for false attestation and criminal liability for willful misdeclaration; non-payment blocks their exit from Egypt through travel-ban procedures and subjects them to attachment over personal assets.
Shell Company / GAAR Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Article 74 GAAR re-characterization: the ETA disregards the entity or transactions, imputes revenue to the real economic owner, disallows costs, and reassesses CIT at 22.5% plus 2% monthly interest; denied treaty benefits trigger additional 10% (dividends) or 20% (royalties) withholding re-collection with a 100% under-withholding penalty.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficiaries must personally restitute the unpaid taxes and penalties; willful concealment in a sham structure is prosecuted as tax evasion with imprisonment up to five years; travel bans and asset-freezing orders may be issued pending the criminal trial.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under Law No. 80 of 2002 as amended by Law No. 194 of 2020: fines up to EGP 2,000,000 and imprisonment for money-laundering predicate offenses; GAFI may deregister the LLC and the ETA may suspend its tax card; bank accounts are frozen by order of the Public Prosecutor.

Director personal liability: Directors with knowledge of laundering activities are criminally liable in personam and subject to restitution orders and civil damages to harmed minority shareholders; the commercial registration is canceled by court order, triggering personal winding-up liability of directors.
