Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI) — Investor Services One-Stop-Shop, Cairo, Egypt; final commercial registration through the Commercial Registry under the Ministry of Investment.

Governing statute

Egypt Companies Law No. 159 of 1981 (as amended), provisions governing Limited Liability Companies (Articles on LLC formation, share subscription, managers, and registration); Investment Law No. 72 of 2017; Executive Regulations of the Investment Law (as amended); Law No. 151 of 2020 on Data Protection as applicable to software activities; Egyptian Civil Code rules on notarization and mandate.

Formation / constitutional filings

Incorporation Application (Model LLC-01) together with the Notarized Contract of Formation (Memorandum and Articles of Association) executed before the Egyptian Notary Public.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 21 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Egypt Companies Law No. 159 of 1981 for an LLC; founders may declare nominal share capital sufficient for the business purpose. Escrow deposit is not mandatory for registration; if bank deposit is used, a certificate from a licensed Egyptian bank is needed. No escrow account rule applies unless the company seeks Investment Law project incentives requiring a capital transfer certificate.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety and civil defense inspection certificate required for the registered office and any server-hosting room, issued by the Egyptian Civil Defense Authority before occupancy; occupational health and safety registration with the Ministry of Manpower; structural load verification from the premises owner or municipality if heavy battery arrays or generators are installed; workers' compensation enrollment with the National Organization for Social Insurance.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Egypt Companies Law No. 159 of 1981 for an LLC; founders may declare nominal share capital sufficient for the business purpose. Escrow deposit is not mandatory for registration; if bank deposit is used, a certificate from a licensed Egyptian bank is needed. No escrow account rule applies unless the company seeks Investment Law project incentives requiring a capital transfer certificate.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) — official registration and compliance portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Egyptian LLC must register with the Egyptian Tax Authority by filing the standard tax registration form through the ETA portal or at the competent tax office. Required documents include the certified incorporation certificate, commercial registry extract, chamber of commerce certificate, board resolution or power of attorney identifying the appointed local tax representative, and copies of managers' passports or national IDs. The ETA issues a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) that doubles as the VAT identification number. Non-resident shareholders are permitted, but a resident tax representative may be required for notices and audits.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-owned Egyptian LLC must register with the Egyptian Tax Authority by filing the standard tax registration form through the ETA portal or at the competent tax office. Required documents include the certified incorporation certificate, commercial registry extract, chamber of commerce certificate, board resolution or power of attorney identifying the appointed local tax representative, and copies of managers' passports or national IDs. The ETA issues a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) that doubles as the VAT identification number. Non-resident shareholders are permitted, but a resident tax representative may be required for notices and audits.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists under Egyptian tax law. An Egyptian LLC is automatically treated as a resident corporate taxpayer subject to entity-level corporate income tax on worldwide income. No hybrid entity election is available for domestic LLCs; anti-hybrid and controlled-foreign-company rules under Law No. 91 of 2005 and related ministerial decrees apply. Certain investment incentives and free-zone regimes require separate application to the General Authority for Investment and are not default elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration is triggered when annual taxable supplies exceed EGP 500,000. Voluntary registration is permitted below this threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated VAT at 0% applies to tangible goods physically exported from Egypt to outside the customs territory. For offshore services, zero-rating is available when the services are supplied to a non-resident recipient, the benefit or economic consumption occurs entirely outside Egypt, and the consideration for the services is received in foreign currency through an Egyptian bank channel. The exporter must retain contemporaneous documentary evidence demonstrating the overseas destination and actual receipt of consideration in Egypt.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Executed service contract or purchase order for offshore service exports
  • ETA-approved e-invoice bearing the validation QR code

Egypt operates a mandatory electronic invoicing and e-receipt system administered by the Egyptian Tax Authority. VAT-registered taxpayers above the phased turnover thresholds must integrate their ERP/billing systems with the ETA's e-invoice platform using authorized APIs and XML/JSON data formats. Each invoice is submitted in real time, validated fiscally by the ETA, and returned with a unique QR code and digital stamp. Issuance of unvalidated invoices, delayed clearance, or non-integration can result in blocked invoice generation, restrictions on tax registrations, and statutory penalties.

The Egyptian Tax Authority and customs authorities may retrospectively audit zero-rated export transactions. If the statutory export documentation, foreign-currency receipt, or offshore consumption evidence is deficient, the claimed zero-rating is disallowed and the transaction is recharacterized as taxable at the standard 14% VAT rate. Late-payment fines, delay interest, and administrative penalties apply from the original due date. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims or falsified inward remittance or customs documentation may give rise to criminal liability, monetary penalties, customs confiscation, and exclusion from the electronic invoice clearance system.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The EGP operates under a market-driven flexible exchange rate regime formally adopted on 6 March 2024 within the IMF Extended Fund Facility framework. Current account transactions (cross-border service fees, SaaS subscriptions, DevOps/API monitoring revenues) are effectively convertible and require no prior CBE approval, only underlying invoice/contract documentation. Capital account transactions (foreign equity subscriptions, shareholder loans, dividend repatriation, loan repayments) are freely transferable in the same foreign currency for entities under Investment Law No. 72 of 2017 (GAFI-registered projects), subject to bank-level documentary checks and CBE-related transfer registration. Residual practical hurdles include: (i) strict inward remittance declaration forms, (ii) bank balance-sheet foreign-currency liquidity management during periods of exchange pressure, and (iii) enhanced OFAC/FATF counterparty screening on beneficiaries, particularly for cloud infrastructure payments routed through correspondent banks in the US and EU.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds from digital and cloud services (SaaS subscriptions, API monitoring, database infrastructure) must be repatriated to a CBE-authorized bank account in Egypt within 180 days from the invoice date or the contractually agreed settlement date, whichever first occurs. Under the prevailing post-2024 flexible exchange regime there is no mandatory conversion requirement: the exporter may retain 100% of collected foreign currency in an onshore Foreign Currency Account (FCA) at an authorized bank. However, each credit entry must be routed through a licensed Egyptian FX dealer and must be accompanied by an export invoice (faturah), the underlying service contract, and a foreign currency source declaration form. In practice banks require the matching SWIFT MT103 narrative to reference the commercial invoice number and the LLC's Commercial Registry number, and the transaction must be reflected in the annual audited financial statements to preserve future dividend repatriation eligibility.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE), exercising exclusive regulatory jurisdiction under Law No. 194 of 2020 (the Central Bank and Banking System Law) over foreign exchange dealings, monetary policy, licensing of authorized banks and FX dealers, and cross-border capital movements in the Arab Republic of Egypt.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-Medium. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Mercury, Wise Business, Revolut Business) cannot act as the primary settlement bank for the Egyptian LLC because CBE foreign-exchange rules require 100% repatriation of export-derived proceeds to an account held at a licensed Egyptian bank within 180 days. EMIs, however, may be deployed as supplementary collection rails for high-frequency US SaaS billing (e.g., Stripe Billing, Mercury ACH sweeps) provided that: (i) the EMI account is denominated in USD and owned by the Egyptian LLC; (ii) all accumulated balances are swept via SWIFT MT103 to the primary Egyptian bank; and (iii) no EGP-denominated liabilities are incurred offshore. Legal constraints specific to Egypt: unlicensed e-money operations targeting Egyptian residents are prohibited under Law No. 18 of 2019; offshore EMIs have restrictive country-risk policies concerning Egyptian-registered legal entities, making onboarding materially slower and subject to enhanced EDD.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign-equity capital injections must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 to the LLC's USD operational account at the primary Egyptian bank. The SWIFT narrative must clearly state: 'Capital Subscription for LLC Commercial Registry No. [number], GAFI Certificate No. [number] (if applicable), Tax ID No. [number]'. The receiving bank will request: (i) the LLC's Commercial Registry extract listing the foreign shareholders; (ii) the board/general assembly resolution approving the subscription or capital increase; (iii) the share allotment ledger; (iv) the inward remittance FX declaration form (CBE form 402/FX source declaration); and (v) the investor's incorporation certificate and board resolution authorizing the transfer. The bank records the funds at the prevailing dealing rate in USD or converts to EGP only upon explicit board instruction; matching of the credit to the company's capital file must be completed within 5 business days to enable future dividend and liquidation repatriation rights. Loans from shareholders are permitted and should be labeled 'Shareholder Loan Agreement dated [date]' with a full loan agreement on file; loan repayments are triggered only after CBE-required documentation is approved by the bank.Outbound clearing is divided among four rails: (1) local EGP operational outflows (payroll, local suppliers, office rent) via the Egyptian Instant Payment Network (IPN), Fawry, or direct debit at the clearing bank; (2) international software and cloud infrastructure disbursements (AWS, Stripe processing fees, global subcontractors) via SWIFT MT103 with pro-forma invoices, contracts, or subscription statements attached in the bank's trade file; (3) procurement of hardware or capitalized cloud infrastructure (if any) via Commercial Letters of Credit (MT700) issued by CIB/HSBC with transport/insurance documents only when physical goods are involved, otherwise by T/T MT103; and (4) corporate distributions on capital (dividends) to foreign shareholders via the same SWIFT rails, subject to CBE dividend-transfer application, audited financial statements, general assembly dividend minutes, tax withholding certificate (with treaty relief forms where applicable), and confirmation that underlying capital was repatriable under Investment Law 72/2017.Onshore deliverable USD/EGP FX forward contracts with authorized banks, maximum tenor 360 days, requiring an underlying current-account exposure (SaaS export invoices or incurred hosting costs) to be evidenced at trade inception.USD/EGP FX swaps executed with the primary clearing bank for term mismatches between USD current-account cash inflows and EGP tax/operating obligations.Offshore EGP non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) executed through the international branch or correspondent dealer of an Egyptian authorized bank, used solely for hedging non-resident group-level exposures and eligible for no-delivery settlement against CBE official exchange rate reference.Cross-currency swaps (USD/EGP) for matched asset-liability funding of long-term intercompany loans, subject to CBE approval and submission of the underlying financing agreement.
