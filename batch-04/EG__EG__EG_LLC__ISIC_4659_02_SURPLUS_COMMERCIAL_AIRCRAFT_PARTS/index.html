Cash capital must be deposited into an Egyptian bank account in the prospective LLC's name; in-kind contributions require an independent valuation. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) – official portal: www.eta.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must appoint a local tax representative in Egypt and apply for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration certificate through the Egyptian Tax Authority's registration portal. The application requires the commercial registry extract, power of attorney, incorporation deed, passport copies of managers, proof of office address, and the completed ETA registration forms in Arabic. After submission, the ETA validates the documentation, performs a physical inspection of the registered office, and issues the statutory TIN/VAT ID.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Foreign entities must appoint a local tax representative in Egypt and apply for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration certificate through the Egyptian Tax Authority's registration portal. The application requires the commercial registry extract, power of attorney, incorporation deed, passport copies of managers, proof of office address, and the completed ETA registration forms in Arabic. After submission, the ETA validates the documentation, performs a physical inspection of the registered office, and issues the statutory TIN/VAT ID.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and services are zero-rated under Egyptian VAT if the following conditions are met: the supplier is a registered VAT taxpayer; the goods are physically exported from Egypt within the legally prescribed period; the supplier retains documentary proof of exportation; and the consideration is paid in convertible foreign currency through a bank that has received the export proceeds. Service exports are eligible if the service is supplied to a non-resident for use entirely outside Egypt.

Egypt operates a mandatory electronic invoicing (e-invoice) and e-receipt system administered by the Egyptian Tax Authority. Businesses exceeding the turnover threshold must issue invoices in XML format through the ETA e-invoice portal, obtain a unique QR code from the authority's digital stamping system, and transmit invoices in real-time for fiscal clearance. The system uses API-based submission, schema validation, and digital signature requirements; failure to comply triggers automatic rejection and fines.

The Egyptian Tax Authority conducts strict desk and field audits of export zero-rating claims, cross-matching customs export records, bank receipts, and VAT returns. Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims result in immediate levy of 14% VAT, late payment penalties, administrative fines up to 200% of the tax due, potential imprisonment under Law 67 of 2016 and the Penal Code for tax evasion, and retrospective clawback of refunds with compound interest. Repatriation of export proceeds beyond 180 days without a Central Bank exemption leads to cancellation of zero-rating and automatic VAT reassessment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt maintains current-account convertibility under IMF Article VIII; the CBE unified the EGP exchange rate in March 2024 under the IMF Extended Fund Facility, removing the parallel-market premium. Capital-account transactions remain controlled: foreign direct investment must be registered with GAFI; portfolio inflows require prior CBE approval. Profit remittance (dividends and branch profits) is legally permitted for foreign investors after corporate tax clearance, audited financials, CBE FX declaration, and source-of-funds verification; however, USD liquidity pressure can create settlement queues of 2-8 weeks for non-priority sectors. The Company should hold a USD liquidity buffer and maintain USD credit lines with at least two relationship banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory conversion of export proceeds to EGP is imposed. Per CBE Executive Regulations under Law No. 38 of 1994, exporters must repatriate proceeds through an authorized Egyptian bank within the maturity agreed on the CBE export declaration (market practice: 180 days from the bill of lading or air waybill for L/C sales, extendable to 360 days for open account with bank consent) and credit them to a foreign-currency account. Proceeds can be retained in USD or sold to the bank. Non-repatriation or late repatriation without documented underlying export transactions triggers CBE reporting sanctions, facility suspension, and penalties under Law No. 38 of 1994 and Law No. 194 of 2020.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) - Foreign Exchange Administration. Primary instruments: Exchange Control Law No. 38 of 1994 and Executive Regulations; Central Bank and Banking System Law No. 194 of 2020 (replacing Law No. 88 of 2003); CBE circulars and Board decisions. Only CBE-licensed banks may execute foreign exchange operations and report FX transactions to the CBE.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

22.5% (standard corporate income tax rate for juridical persons under Income Tax Law No. 91 of 2005; higher sector-specific rates may apply to banks, petroleum and telecommunications activities).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final withholding tax on dividend distributions made by Egyptian juridical persons to shareholders; treaty-resident shareholders may claim reduced rates under an applicable double-tax agreement.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Form No. 50 must be submitted within four months after the close of the fiscal year; final tax due must be paid by the same deadline unless an approved instalment arrangement exists.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when annual aggregate related-party transactions with Egyptian operations equal or exceed EGP 8,000,000; Master File is mandatory when the worldwide consolidated turnover of the ultimate parent equals or exceeds EGP 20,000,000,000. Documentation must be prepared before the Form No. 50 due date and submitted to the ETA within 30–60 days of request.

Economic substance scope: Egypt does not impose a standalone Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations statute, but the Income Tax Law and Investment Law require a genuine Egyptian presence, a licensed aviation trading warehouse or distribution capacity, payroll and board substance, and real inventory risk. A shell LLC used solely to route surplus aircraft parts invoices or to access SCZone tax benefits will be disregarded, with all treaty and zone incentives recaptured.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines of 100%–300% or more of the evaded tax, imprisonment for up to 5 years, seizure of inventory and bank accounts, revocation of the Egyptian tax card and civil aviation distribution license, and disqualification from public-sector aviation tenders.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or direct the false return are personally, jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, penalties and surcharges; courts will pierce the LLC veil for fraud and impose travel bans and asset freezes.
Shell Company / Sham Substance / Treaty Shopping

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of the entity's separate tax personality, retroactive denial of DTA and free-zone tax benefits, reallocation of profits to the real economic owner, strike-off from the commercial register and payment of tax plus a 100% non-deduction penalty.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for abuse of legal personality; directors and significant shareholders are personally liable for the resulting corporate tax liability, with criminal imprisonment applicable for forged residency certificates or false beneficial-ownership declarations.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure and Artificial Financing

Statutory sanction: ETA transfer-pricing adjustment, recharacterization of excess payments as deemed dividends, nondeductibility of interest/royalties, monthly 2% late-payment surcharges and penalty additions up to 100% of the adjusted tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly submit incomplete TP disclosures or authorize artificial invoicing are jointly liable for corporate tax deficiencies; criminal referral can result in imprisonment for up to 5 years and confiscation of the profit margin earned from the manipulated transactions.
