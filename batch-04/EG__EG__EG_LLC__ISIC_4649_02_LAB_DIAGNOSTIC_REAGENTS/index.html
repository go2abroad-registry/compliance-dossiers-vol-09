Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) – official portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg; electronic tax filing and registration services accessible via the ETA e-services portal and the unified Egypt Tax Platform.

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities establishing an Egyptian LLC must first complete commercial registration at the General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI) or the Ministry of Trade and Industry; thereafter, the LLC must apply to the Egyptian Tax Authority for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) by submitting the certificate of incorporation, commercial register extract, power of attorney for the legal representative, audited opening balance sheet where required, lease or ownership proof of premises, and valid national ID/passport copies of directors; the ETA issues a unified tax card that consolidates income tax, VAT, payroll tax, and stamp tax registration; a separate VAT registration certificate is issued where taxable turnover exceeds the mandatory registration threshold; foreign shareholders may also be required to provide a tax domicile certificate and beneficial ownership declaration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt operates a managed-float exchange rate regime. As of current practice, the EGP is convertible for current-account transactions (trade in goods and services, remittances, dividends) under CBE licensing. Capital-account transactions (direct equity injection, shareholder loans, exit proceeds) remain subject to case-by-case CBE approval and must be registered with the General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI) to secure future profit repatriation. Repatriation of profits and capital requires an audited financial statement, tax clearance, and confirmation that all statutory banking obligations (e.g., export proceeds rule) are satisfied. Practical delays may occur during FX liquidity stress; companies should pre-arrange clearing with an authorized bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export proceeds from Egyptian entities must be repatriated through an authorized bank within 120 days of the bill-of-lading or air-way-bill date. Proceeds must be credited to the exporter's foreign-currency account held in Egypt, and the CBE requires mandatory conversion of the full amount to EGP upon receipt (unless the exporter holds a valid foreign-currency retention license granted under CBE regulations). For a diagnostic reagent distributor, import operations must demonstrate that export proceeds used for trade settlement are routed through the same banking channel to avoid penalty.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low feasibility. Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Wise, Payoneer, Revolut Business) are not licensed by CBE and cannot hold EGP accounts or execute local/domestic payouts. Their utility is limited to non-Egyptian treasury side transactions (e.g., receiving funds in USD/EUR from vendors outside Egypt), but all settlements to or from Egypt must pass through a CBE-licensed bank. Legal constraints include foreign exchange control regulations and AML/CFT Law No. 80 of 2002, which prohibit local businesses from maintaining operational bank balances with unlicensed foreign virtual account providers.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound foreign capital for the LLC must be routed via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the overseas investor to a CBE-licensed bank, with payment narrative citing 'CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION' or 'EQUITY CONTRIBUTION' and referencing the GAFI registration number. The bank will complete CBE Form 20 (statement of capital movement) and issuance of an import exemption certificate if the capital will be utilized for goods. All inbound transfers require pre-clearance with the bank's treasury desk; funds cannot be released to the operational account until a CBE compliance approval and GAFI capital verification are completed. Transfer fees typically range USD 10-50 for incoming wire plus 0.2% CBE charge.For import of laboratory diagnostic reagents, clearing must be executed via SWIFT MT103 (TT payment) or an Irrevocable Documentary Letter of Credit (LC) opened through a commercial bank in Egypt. MT103 payments should specify the Customs HS Code and import declaration number to avoid document mismatch. LC issuance requires a 100% cash margin for a newly formed LLC or a stand-by letter of credit guarantee. The supplier's bill will be paid only after the presenting bank submits compliant documents (bill of lading, certificate of origin, insurance, commercial invoice) to the CBE-based receiving bank. Alternative clearing rail: Payment in Installments through approved factoring/confirming houses using the 'Banker's Acceptance' instrument is permitted but carries additional interest.
