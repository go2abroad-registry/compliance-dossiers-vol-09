Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI) – One-Stop Investor Services Center (Shatt El-Maaref), Commercial Registry Department of the Ministry of Trade and Industry, and the Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA)

Governing statute

Law No. 194 of 2020 Promulgating the Companies Law, as amended by Law No. 4 of 2023, Articles 664-704 (Limited Liability Companies); Executive Regulations issued by Decree No. 96 of 2021; and Law No. 34 of 1976 on the Commercial Registry, as amended. Single-family office holding activity requires no special-purpose financial license under the Capital Market Law No. 95 of 1992, provided it administers only private family assets.

Formation / constitutional filings

Electronic Incorporation File (Mulaff El-Taasis) comprising the duly executed Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA) of the LLC, submitted through the GAFI e-Services portal or in person at the Investor Services Center

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 22 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on LLCs under Law No. 194 of 2020; declared nominal capital of as low as EGP 1,000 is permitted. The declared capital must be fully subscribed and fully paid at formation, evidenced by a certificate of bank deposit in a corporate account with a licensed Egyptian bank; no escrow or notarized transfer account is required for cash contributions. In-kind contributions require a certified appraiser's report attached to the MOA.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defense certification is required for the office premises: obtain a fire-safety inspection and fire-extinguishing system certificate from the Civil Defense Authority, ensure the building's structural load capacity and electrical installation are certified by the competent municipality, and maintain a valid occupancy/operating license issued by the local Sheyakha/Mahalla; workplaces with 10 or fewer employees require only a self-certified health and safety clearance, but the SFO must keep an adequate first-aid kit, emergency exit plan, and an annual fire-alarm test record.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on LLCs under Law No. 194 of 2020; declared nominal capital of as low as EGP 1,000 is permitted. The declared capital must be fully subscribed and fully paid at formation, evidenced by a certificate of bank deposit in a corporate account with a licensed Egyptian bank; no escrow or notarized transfer account is required for cash contributions. In-kind contributions require a certified appraiser's report attached to the MOA.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED, that (1) the incorporation of the Company as an Egyptian limited liability company under Law No. 194 of 2020 is ratified and the declared share capital of EGP 1,000 is fully subscribed by the founding members; (2) the registered office at 17 Corniche El-Nil, Cairo, Egypt is adopted for statutory notices and service of process; (3) [Manager A] and [Manager B] are appointed as managers and joint authorized signatories; (4) the managers are authorized to open corporate bank accounts, complete commercial registration, tax, social insurance, and all administrative filings; and (5) the Company shall confine its operations wholly to the private single-family office holding and asset administration mandate.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable – no industrial board exercises jurisdiction over an office-based single-family office; administrative clearances only are issued through GAFI's Licensing Unit and the local municipal authority (Mahalla/Sheyakha office)

Category: Not applicable – SFO is a non-industrial, non-commercial holding and family-asset administration vehicle; no statutory operating permit is required, but the company must hold a valid Tax Card, Commercial Registry extract, and proof of the registered office's building-compliance certificate

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance — National Tax Portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg; E-Invoicing Portal: https://einvoicing.eta.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must apply to the ETA for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) using the unified tax registration form. Required documents include legalized certificate of incorporation and memorandum, commercial register extract, board resolution appointing a local tax representative, notarized power of attorney, proof of Egyptian registered office address, and passport copies of officers. The application is submitted online through the ETA portal or via an accredited tax consultant; the ETA issues a single 10-digit TIN that also functions as the VAT identification number when VAT registration is compulsory. The process typically takes 5–10 business days. Separately, the entity must complete e-invoicing registration by obtaining a digital signing certificate and API credentials from the ETA’s clearance system.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity must apply to the ETA for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) using the unified tax registration form. Required documents include legalized certificate of incorporation and memorandum, commercial register extract, board resolution appointing a local tax representative, notarized power of attorney, proof of Egyptian registered office address, and passport copies of officers. The application is submitted online through the ETA portal or via an accredited tax consultant; the ETA issues a single 10-digit TIN that also functions as the VAT identification number when VAT registration is compulsory. The process typically takes 5–10 business days. Separately, the entity must complete e-invoicing registration by obtaining a digital signing certificate and API credentials from the ETA’s clearance system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or pass-through election is available under Egyptian law; an LLC is mandatorily taxed as an entity separate from its shareholders. Relevant statutory elections include: (i) consolidated corporate tax filing for groups with at least 95% ownership of subsidiaries under Article 61 of Income Tax Law No. 91/2005; (ii) free-zone or special economic zone tax exemption election for qualifying investment activities; and (iii) certain ETA-approved simplified VAT accounting/cash-basis arrangements for small taxpayers. No hybrid mismatch election exists under domestic law; OECD hybrid mismatch rules may indirectly apply through tax treaties or MLI provisions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 in annual taxable turnover for mandatory VAT registration; non-resident suppliers making taxable supplies in Egypt may be required to register from the first supply even if below this threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated export treatment is available where goods are physically transferred outside Egypt under a valid customs declaration, title passes to a non-resident buyer, and the sale consideration is received in freely convertible foreign currency and repatriated to an Egyptian bank account within 180 days. For services, zero-rating applies where the service is supplied to a non-resident, is wholly consumed or used outside Egypt, and the consideration is denominated and received in foreign currency and remitted to Egypt within 180 days. The taxpayer must maintain full documentary evidence acceptable to the ETA demonstrating the export nature, the foreign recipient, and the foreign-currency settlement.

  • Customs Export Declaration (certified by the Egyptian Customs Authority)
  • Bill of Lading (or Airway Bill / equivalent transport document evidencing physical export)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank confirmation of foreign currency receipt
  • Commercial invoice and export contract (supporting the transaction’s commercial substance)

E-invoicing is mandatory for all VAT-registered persons under the ETA’s phased roll-out. Invoices and credit notes must be generated in XML/JSON format, digitally signed, and submitted in near-real-time to the ETA’s Invoice Clearance Center via REST API. Only cleared documents receive a transaction validation code, and each final invoice must display a QR code containing the TIN, invoice values, VAT amount, and clearance timestamp. Uncleared invoices are legally invalid, may be excluded from input VAT deduction, and may trigger penalties ranging from EGP 20,000 to EGP 100,000.

The ETA may conduct pre-refund and post-refund audits, with a five-year statutory limitation period. If a zero-rated claim is fraudulent or unsupported, the ETA will claw back the VAT amount understated, impose late-payment interest and administrative penalties (up to EGP 100,000 per violation), and may refer the matter for criminal prosecution. Tax evasion penalties can reach 100% of the tax evaded, in addition to imprisonment under Articles 72–76 of VAT Law No. 67 of 2016.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt operates a flexible exchange-rate regime (IMF-supported unification effective March 2024). The Egyptian pound is fully convertible for current-account transactions through an authorised dealer bank without prior CBE approval, subject to documentary due diligence (invoices, tax clearances, audited financial statements, underlying contracts). Capital-account movements - equity injections, profit remittances, foreign-loan repayments - are transferable in practice but require the corporate resolution, tax clearance certificate issued by the Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), and CBE transaction reporting; no separate CBE licence is required for a Single-Family-Office holding entity (ISIC 6430/NAICS 525920). The entity is not a regulated capital-market company, so Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA) approval is not required, but banks will conduct enhanced source-of-funds verification given the Medium-Risk-Enhanced tier.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory conversion ratio applies to export proceeds after the 2016 foreign-exchange float; however, exporters must repatriate the full invoice value to an authorised Egyptian bank account within 90 days from the bill-of-lading date for goods (90 days from completion for services). Goods exports must be pre-registered on the ACI/Nafeza platform to obtain the customs ACID number, and proceeds may be retained in foreign-currency accounts or converted at the interbank rate. This SFO has no exporting activity, so the obligation is limited to maintaining zero-activity declarations and keeping foreign-currency accounts compliant with CBE periodic reporting.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) - Foreign Exchange and International Dealings Directorate, Banking Supervision and AML Unit, operating under the Central Banking and Monetary System Law No. 194 of 2020, the Foreign Exchange Decree-Law No. 97 of 1976, and the AML/CFT Law No. 80 of 2002 as amended.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low as a primary clearing rail; ancillary use only. Egypt requires CBE licensing for e-money issuance and electronic-payment aggregation; offshore digital EMIs without a CBE licence, local branch, or bank-agent agreement cannot hold Egyptian client funds, access the Instant Payment Network (IPN), or clear EGP obligations. Such EMIs may be engaged solely for FX-rate aggregation, reporting visualisation, and data consolidation, never as the legal repayment or collection channel for onshore SFO treasury flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All injections must arrive via SWIFT MT103/MT202 through the nominated relationship bank, with the beneficiary account title matching the Egyptian LLC name exactly and the remitter matching the registered shareholder name. The banker's 'purpose of remittance' field must cite the capital call / equity-increase category used for CBE inward reporting. The file must be pre-delivered to the bank: notarised capital-increase resolution, updated GAFI/commercial-registry extract, shareholder identification, and provenance statement; funds are credited after KYC/AML dashboards clear, booked to the capital-escrow sub-ledger, and reported to CBE in the monthly external-position statement. Use one single entry bank to avoid duplicated CBE reporting; correspondent routing via New York or European clearers is standard for USD and EUR legs.Outgoing treasury payments use SWIFT MT103 executed through the relationship bank with strict beneficiary-name matching and purpose codes (management fees, advisory fees, securities settlement). Trade settlements and operational disbursements above the de-minimis threshold require underlying documentary support (contract, pro forma invoice, tax forms); commercial letters of credit (sight or usance, MT700/MT707) and documentary collections are the permitted rails for goods and commodities, with the ACI/Nafeza ACID number mandatory for import customs clearance. Profit and dividend remittances to non-resident shareholders require an ETA withholding-tax clearance certificate and the bank's profit-transfer declaration, subject to the applicable domestic dividend withholding tax (generally up to 10%, with treaty relief available). All outbound FX is executed at interbank rates with no surrender obligation.Onshore deliverable USD/EGP forward contracts (tenor up to 12 months) against documented commercial or treasury exposure, executed through the CBE-licensed relationship bank under prudential limits.Offshore USD/EGP non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for portfolio-level hedging where onshore limits are exhausted, booked only through a non-resident counterparty.USD/EGP FX swaps (spot/deliverable) for liquidity-mismatch management between capital-escrow and operational sub-ledgers.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/EGP) for offshore asset-liability matching executed via the securities custodian or prime broker.
