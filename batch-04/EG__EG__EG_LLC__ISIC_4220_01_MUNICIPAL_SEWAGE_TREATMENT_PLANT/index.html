Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual office is not permissible for the intended ISIC 4220 municipal sewage treatment EPC activity. Egyptian commercial registry practice and the Public Works Tender Rules require a physical, identifiable registered office in Egypt where the commercial register plate is displayed, corporate records are maintained, and authorized representatives are available during business hours. A transitional arrangement using a licensed lawyer's or corporate service provider's office may be accepted during pre-incorporation and tax-card registration, but an operational office with project engineering, water-quality records, HSE manuals, and personnel files must be established before the entity enters into the municipal or World Bank-financed contract.

Governing statute

Law No. 159 of 1981 on Joint-Stock Companies, Partnerships Limited by Shares and Limited Liability Companies, as amended; Executive Regulations issued by Prime Ministerial Decree No. 3426 of 1982; Investment Law No. 72 of 2017 applicable through the General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI).

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarized Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association (Aqd Al-Ta'sis) submitted on GAFI OSS Form LLC-01, leading to issuance of the Certificate of Incorporation and Commercial Register Extract

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The limited liability company must have a subscribed capital divided into equal quota shares. The founders must fully subscribe the capital stated in the Articles of Association and deposit the entire subscribed amount in a corporate escrow bank account before registration. No universal fixed minimum is imposed for LLCs under Investment Law 72/2017, but the capital must be sufficient to cover the planned sewage treatment EPC works and must be released only upon GAFI approval.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification issued by the Civil Defense Authority under Law 377/1956 and gazetted executive regulations; structural-load inspection certificate for lifting stations, tanks, and treatment units; occupational health and safety clearance under Labour Law 12/2003, including confined-space entry protocols, chemical hazard inventory, personal protective equipment, and emergency-response signage.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) under the Ministry of Finance; unified online portal at www.eta.gov.eg and e-invoicing/e-receipt digital platforms

E-government filing portal

A foreign legal entity must register for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) at the Egyptian Tax Authority. For VAT purposes, the entity completes VAT registration through the ETA online portal after obtaining an economic activity certificate, notarized constitutional documents and power of attorney; non-resident entities can register directly or through a tax representative, and the TIN is issued within approximately 5-10 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign legal entity must register for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) at the Egyptian Tax Authority. For VAT purposes, the entity completes VAT registration through the ETA online portal after obtaining an economic activity certificate, notarized constitutional documents and power of attorney; non-resident entities can register directly or through a tax representative, and the TIN is issued within approximately 5-10 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box pass-through election exists; corporations are taxed at entity level. Availability of limited statutory elections: choosing instalment payment of corporate tax, consolidating related entities under joint filing, and optional VAT registration below the threshold for exporters. Hybrid mismatch elections are not recognised in domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 annual taxable turnover of goods and services sold in Egypt; registration is compulsory within 30 days of crossing the threshold

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to (i) direct export of goods with physical departure from Egypt, (ii) services supplied to non-resident recipients, wholly performed outside Egypt and used abroad, and (iii) international transport and related services. Claim requires verifiable export proof, customs exit records, and foreign currency proceeds credited into an Egyptian bank account.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ACD/ECS) showing HS code, quantity, FOB value and port of exit
  • Original Bill of Lading / air waybill with Egyptian port of loading and foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank confirmation of foreign currency proceeds received

Mandatory e-invoicing for all B2B sales by VAT-registered taxpayers pursuant to ETA Law No. 67 of 2016. Invoices must be electronically signed, submitted in real time to the ETA E-Invoice platform via API integration, and validated by automatic fiscal clearance before issuance to the buyer; QR code stamping and XML/JSON structure are mandatory.

False zero-rating and VAT refund claims are audited by the Central Administration for VAT Refunds. A claim can be retrospectively clawed back with interest at the statutory rate, plus administrative penalties of up to 30% of tax due; intentional fraud or forged export documentation triggers criminal penalties including imprisonment under the VAT Law and Income Tax Law.
