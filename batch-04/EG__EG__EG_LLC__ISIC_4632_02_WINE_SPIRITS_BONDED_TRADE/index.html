Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance — central portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg; mandatory e-invoicing platform: https://invoicing.eta.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

Register under the Unified Tax Procedures Law No. 90 of 2021 within 30 days of commercial registry issuance. A foreign-owned Egyptian LLC must submit the notarized and Arabic-translated Memorandum of Association, Commercial Register extract, power of attorney for the legal representative, passport copies of shareholders/managers, and proof of the licensed bonded warehouse premises. The ETA then issues the Tax Identification Number (TIN) and digitized Tax Card. Separately, the entity must file VAT registration once annual taxable supplies exceed EGP 500,000 (mandatory) or EGP 50,000 (voluntary), and must complete e-invoicing activation in the ETA roll-out wave applicable to wholesale bonded trade.

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Egypt does not provide a US-style check-the-box election. Statutory elections include: (i) fiscal unity / tax group consolidation for wholly-owned Egyptian subsidiaries under Law No. 90 of 2021; (ii) Investment Law No. 72 of 2017 election between accelerated depreciation and investment-cost deduction for eligible licensed projects; (iii) elective carryforward or refund treatment of input VAT credits under Law No. 67 of 2016; and (iv) election to treat inventory and bonded stock under the customs duty-suspension regime rather than local free circulation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration when annual taxable supplies exceed EGP 500,000; voluntary registration from EGP 50,000. Non-resident suppliers of digital or offshore services to Egyptian consumers are registrable irrespective of turnover. A bonded wholesale operator additionally requires a Customs bonded-warehouse license and registration on the Customs Authority's ACI/CargoX platform for cross-border movements.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under Article 31 of VAT Law No. 67 of 2016 when: (1) the wine/spirits are physically exported outside Egypt and title passes to a non-resident buyer; (2) the exporter holds a valid Egyptian TIN and VAT registration; (3) export proceeds are credited to an Egyptian bank account in freely convertible foreign currency; (4) the e-invoice is cleared by the ETA and the customs export declaration is finalized by the Customs Authority; and (5) goods depart from the bonded warehouse without entry into local free circulation, under a Customs re-export or transfer authorization within the statutory shipping period. International transport and directly related export services may also be zero-rated. Exports from bonded storage are relieved from VAT, customs duty, and excise-equivalent charges, subject to full documentary traceability.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Declaration filed via the Customs Authority/ACI system)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Egypt
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing foreign-currency export proceeds
  • Bonded warehouse release / re-export authorization issued by Egyptian Customs for the specific consignment
  • ETA-validated electronic export invoice bearing the UIC and QR digital stamp
  • Certificate of Origin where required by the destination customs regime

Mandatory electronic invoicing applies to all VAT-registered taxpayers on a phased roll-out. Each invoice must be created in the ETA-specified JSON/XML format, transmitted in near-real time via the ETA invoicing portal or certified API, validated by the ETA fiscal clearance engine to obtain a Universal Identification Code (UIC), and printed with a digital stamp / QR code. Rejected or untransmitted invoices are non-deductible and expose the taxpayer to escalating daily administrative fines, suspension of the TIN and e-invoicing account, and loss of input VAT credit in audits.

The ETA and Customs Authority conduct joint targeted audits that cross-match e-invoice data, customs export declarations, warehouse movement records, and bank forex receipts. Fraudulent export zero-rating, forged transport documents, or non-repatriated proceeds trigger: a fine of 100% to 300% of the VAT evaded under Law No. 67 of 2016; full retrospective clawback of refunds with monthly late-payment interest; imprisonment for willful evasion under Law No. 90 of 2021; suspension or cancellation of the TIN and bonded-warehouse license; and personal liability of directors and legal representatives. Refund claims are risk-scored and selectively subjected to document-integrity validation before any credit or cash settlement.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt maintains current-account convertibility consistent with IMF Article VIII obligations, but capital-account transactions remain subject to CBE and GAFI registration and approval. For the bonded wine and spirits wholesale business, imported goods are treated as normal merchandise trade once the customs declaration, certificate of origin, commercial invoice, and excise-duty requirements are satisfied. Profit remittances by the Egyptian LLC to foreign shareholders are permitted through a licensed bank, provided the shareholder records are updated at GAFI, audited financial statements are filed, corporate tax returns are assessed, and a valid tax clearance certificate from the Egyptian Tax Authority is available. Following the 2024 unified exchange-rate reform, interbank rates apply to inward remittances and trade settlements; no parallel exchange premium is recognised for banking purposes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no current mandatory conversion ratio in force for export proceeds; the exporter may retain 100% of export receipts in a foreign-currency account maintained with a CBE-licensed bank. Export proceeds must be repatriated to Egypt within the payment term approved in the export documentary collection order or sales contract, generally no later than 180 days from the bill-of-lading date for wine and spirits consignments, unless the bank and CBE approve an extended open-account period. The bank is required to match incoming proceeds against the export customs release and report collection to the CBE. Optional conversion to EGP is executed at the prevailing interbank rate; parallel-market sales and transit-account netting are prohibited.

Central bank supervision: The Central Bank of Egypt (CBE), acting as the national monetary authority and foreign-exchange regulator under the Central Bank and Banking System Law No. 194 of 2020.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

LOW - Egypt does not grant standalone Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licences to foreign or offshore digital banks. An overseas EMI cannot hold a CBE-regulated nostro account, issue an Egyptian IBAN, or settle bonded-warehouse customs transactions without a fully licensed local Egyptian bank sponsor. Such platforms may function only as a front-end payment instrument for customer journeys, while the actual cash clearing, FX conversion, and escrow must sit with a licensed tier-1 Egyptian bank. For this medium-risk bonded trade, treasury should not treat offshore EMI balances as eligible for capital escrow, customs guarantees, or excise settlements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital for the Egyptian LLC must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 from the foreign shareholder's bank directly to the designated Egyptian licensed bank corresponding account in USD or EUR. The SWIFT payment must identify the Egyptian LLC and use a clear purpose clause such as 'CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION - EG_LLC - GAFI REGISTRATION NO.'; the receiving bank will capture the CBE foreign-investment identification code and report the transaction to the Central Bank of Egypt. The required verification bundle includes: certified certificate of incorporation and commercial registry extract; GAFI registration or investment appraisal approval; notarised English/Arabic board resolution authorising the capital call; share subscription documents; audited financial statements or source-of-funds evidence for the remitting shareholder; and bank KYC forms. Upon receipt, the bank may hold the funds in a capital escrow account until the LLC's capital declaration and CBE paperwork are completed, after which the funds are released to the operational account.International supplier settlements for aged scotch, bordeaux wine, and warehouse replenishment will run through standard SWIFT clearing rails: MT103 for open-account supplier invoices, MT700/MT760 letters of credit for documentary purchases and standby guarantees where the winery requires payment security, and MT202 for interbank covering instructions when same-day value is needed. Local Egyptian bonded transfers are cleared through the CBE RTGS system in EGP where the seller and buyer maintain domestic accounts, accompanied by customs bonded-warehouse transfer forms and excise movement documentation. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EGP 10,000,000 of annual controlled related-party transactions (per ETA Decree No. 146/2019 and the ETA Transfer Pricing Guidelines) triggers mandatory preparation of the Local File and Master File, available within 30 days of a formal request; Form No. 21 must be filed with the annual CIT return regardless of the threshold; Country-by-Country Reporting is required when the ultimate parent's consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Egypt does not maintain a standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute comparable to the UAE; however, functionality-equivalent substance obligations arise from the GAAR in art. 90 of Law No. 91/2005, treaty beneficial-ownership rules, and the Investment Law incentives. A bonded wine and spirits LLC must demonstrate a licensed and operating customs warehouse in Egypt, permanent staff and payroll, physical premises, board decisions taken in Egypt, and active inventory control; otherwise the ETA and the General Authority for Investment can recharacterize the entity as a mere shell, deny treaty benefits and free-zone incentives, impose deemed profits, and recommend license cancellation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate tax evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine of EGP 10,000 to 100,000 and imprisonment of 6 months to 5 years under art. 121 of Law No. 91/2005; confiscation of assets; recovery of the entire unpaid tax plus the 2% monthly delay surcharge, capped at 100% of the tax; permanent closure of the bonded facility and revocation of the alcohol trading license.

Director personal liability: Directors, general managers, and the tax accountant are jointly and severally liable for the tax debt and civil restitution; travel bans, seizure of personal assets, and criminal sentences of up to 5 years are imposed when the evasion is attributable to their direction.
Transfer pricing documentation failure

Statutory sanction: ETA re-determination of the arm's-length price with full add-back of the adjusted margin to taxable profits; assessed interest at 2% per month on the additional CIT; administrative penalties and, in repeated cases, the same criminal evasion sanction as fraudulent understatement.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed the inaccurate return or directed the mispricing are personally liable for the additional tax, interest, and penalties; they may be prosecuted for complicity in tax evasion and prohibited from leaving Egypt pending settlement.
Bonded customs regime and excise smuggling

Statutory sanction: Customs fines up to 100% to 1,000% of the evaded duty under Customs Law No. 207/2020, confiscation and destruction of goods, revocation and permanent closure of the bonded warehouse license, and criminal penalties for smuggling with imprisonment from 1 to 7 years.

Director personal liability: The warehouse licensee, board members, and the managing director are jointly and severally liable for the evaded customs duty and excise; personal fines equal to the total evaded amount, asset freezes, and imprisonment sentences are routinely imposed.
