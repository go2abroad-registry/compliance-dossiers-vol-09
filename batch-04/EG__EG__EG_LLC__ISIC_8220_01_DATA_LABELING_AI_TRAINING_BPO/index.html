Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) of the Ministry of Finance — unified authority for income tax, VAT (Law 67/2016), stamp duty, and e-invoicing/e-receipts; national single-window tax portal at www.eta.gov.eg; e-invoicing platform at invoicing.eta.gov.eg; customs administered separately by the Egyptian Customs Authority (ECA).

E-government filing portal

The EG_LLC must obtain a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) before commercial operations: (1) incorporate through GAFI single window (Commercial Registry/Qayd) and obtain a company certificate; (2) file the ETA unified tax registration application online (www.eta.gov.eg) attaching certified MOA/assembly minutes, commercial register extract, head-office lease, manager legal IDs, and non-resident owners' apostilled passports/Power of Attorney; (3) ETA issues the Tax Card and assigns a single TIN covering CIT, VAT, and stamp tax within approximately 10–20 working days; (4) VAT registration applies in the same file: mandatory if annual taxable supplies exceed the EGP 500,000 threshold, and optional (recommended for exporters) below the threshold to recover input VAT; (5) for digital-export BPO operations, the LLC must also activate ETA e-invoicing credentials (PKI/Token and API channel) and obtain a CBE inward remittance account for the export settlement currency.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — EG_LLC is a resident juridical person taxed separately from shareholders at the 22.5% corporate rate (no pass-through; Egypt has no SAS/check-the-box regime); territorial scope = worldwide income for residents with foreign tax credit, Egyptian-source-only for non-residents; default location is the standard national regime, not a tax-exempt zone (SCZONE/free-zone export incentives require a separate license and election).

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections for LLCs; statutory options relevant to an export AI/BPO LLC: (i) election to operate under the Investment Law free-zone/SCZONE export regimes (subject to GAFI/ETA licensing and annual fees) with export VAT deeming and customs relief; (ii) voluntary VAT registration to neutralise input VAT on export/zero-rated activities; (iii) election to appoint a resident tax representative/fiscal agent to centralise ETA filings; (iv) where applicable, joint/consortium filing structures may be chosen at incorporation but remain entity-level taxpayers.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 (annual taxable supplies of goods and services; exporters may register voluntarily below the threshold to obtain input VAT credits and zero-rating refund rights)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to (i) exported goods evidenced by a valid customs export declaration leaving Egypt; (ii) sale of goods/services to free-zone or SCZONE operators as deemed exports; and (iii) exported services (including ISIC 8220 data labeling/RLHF services) supplied to a non-resident principal where the service is not used or exploited in Egypt, the contract assigns the deliverable/IP offshore, and the price is payable from abroad in foreign currency. Claim requirements: the LLC must hold a valid VAT certificate, issue a fully ETA-cleared e-invoice with the 0%-rated export line code (indicating 'export of services'), maintain the signed PSA/Master Data Agreement describing unit pricing, and evidence genuine offshore consumption; standard OECD place-of-supply and anti-fragmentation rules apply so that services effectively rendered and benefited in Egypt are recharacterized as domestic taxable supplies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan) — for goods; for BPO services the ETA accepts the zero-rated e-invoice + export-of-services certificate in lieu
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill — for physically exported goods (or digital delivery/GitHub-managed delivery reports for AI/RLHF datasets)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by the LLC's Egyptian bank proving receipt of the export price in foreign currency
  • Signed PSA / Master Services Agreement with the non-resident client showing per-unit pricing (bounding-box annotation / RLHF token rates)
  • ETA-cleared XML e-invoice with 0% VAT code, QR stamp, and payment reconciliation schedule

Mandatory e-invoicing for all VAT-registered persons: B2B invoices must be transmitted in real-time to the ETA e-invoicing platform (invoicing.eta.gov.eg) via REST API in ETA XML/JSON schema (with OAuth2/PKI client-certificate authentication); the ETA validates fields, recomputes VAT lines, and returns a Unique Invoice Identifier (UUID) and a 2D QR stamp that must be printed/embedded on the invoice; invoices not cleared in the ETA data lake are legally deemed non-issued and deny the buyer's input-VAT deduction. E-receipts (B2C face-to-face sales) are being phased in with equal real-time clearance duties. The phase-based rollout has reached all registered taxpayers, including newly incorporated LLCs, which must be e-invoicing-enabled from the moment of VAT activation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Since 6 March 2024, Egypt operates a unified market-clearing exchange rate with no dual-tier pricing or parallel market. Current-account convertibility is unrestricted for bona fide service imports, software licensing, data-processing outsourced fees, and professional services provided underlying contracts and invoices are presented to a licensed authorized dealer with the proper CBE balance-of-payments (BOP) purpose code. The capital account remains administered: foreign direct equity injections, foreign-currency borrowings, and profit repatriation must flow through licensed banks, be tagged with the applicable CBE BOP code, and be supported by the corporate registry and tax card. Profit and dividend remittance to a non-resident shareholder is permissible after: (i) payment of the 5% withholding tax on cash dividends under Income Tax Law 91/2005, (ii) submission of audited financial statements plus board distribution resolution to the paying bank, (iii) a tax clearance certificate, and (iv) completion of the CBE capital and profits repatriation documentation. Standard, properly documented dividend flows do not require prior CBE approval; the price discovery for EGP conversion is left to the interbank market and bank net-open-position constraints.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under consolidated CBE FX guidelines in force through 2024-2025: (1) 100% of proceeds for exported services, including AI annotation, RLHF data curation, and BPO services, must be credited to a resident licensed bank account in Egypt; invoices must be settled no later than 30 days from invoice due date and with an absolute ceiling of 120 days from service delivery. (2) The mandatory percentage-based EGP surrender ratio has been abolished following the 2024 float; the LLC may retain USD or EUR export proceeds in a designated retention account, but such balances may only be used for permitted trade payments or sold to a licensed bank at the interbank rate. (3) Each export receipt must be declared through the bank electronic export-receipt system and recorded in the LLC export register for CBE and General Organization for Export and Import Control inspection. (4) Late or non-repatriation triggers denial of new Form 4 import or debit facilities, placement on the CBE caution list, and counter-party freeze under Articles 115-116 of Banking Law 194/2020.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE); the CBE operates the unified interbank FX system under Law No. 194 of 2020 (Central Bank and Banking System Law), with the Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA) supervising non-bank payment systems adjacent to this treasury plan.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW as treasury clearing core; MEDIUM as ancillary collection rail. Wise holds no CBE payment-institution license under Title IV of Law 194/2020 and does not support Egyptian corporate multi-currency balances or EGP payout corridors; therefore Wise cannot be used as the primary bank. PayPal Egypt Inc. is the one major CBE-licensed foreign PSP and can be used only as a USD aggregator for mass micro-payouts to foreign annotators; PayPal Business balances must be withdrawn to the Egyptian bank account in EGP unless the LLc maintains a USD sub-account and presents PayPal settlement statements to book the USD. Routing material customer funds through an unlicensed offshore EMI would breach Egyptian AML Law 80/2002 sanctions and CBE FX-avoidance enforcement. Recommended treasury architecture: keep PayPal as a micro-collection rail, sweep balances weekly via SWIFT MT103 to CIB or NBE, and maintain all liquidity buffers in onshore USD and EGP accounts.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire protocol: (1) foreign shareholder bank sends USD or EUR via SWIFT correspondent to a tier-1 Egyptian bank with the LLC Commercial Registry number as the last element of field 59 beneficiary identifier and field 70 containing the CBE BOP equity code 5281 for foreign direct investment. (2) The receiving bank verifies the CBE capital-inflow declaration, credits the LLC under-incorporation subscription account, issues the Certificate of Capital Inflow on the CBE BOP system, and either retains the FX in a designated USD account or converts at the daily interbank rate into EGP. (3) All local formation expenses, notary fees, municipal registrations, and initial payroll clear in EGP from the operational account to minimize FX conversion layers. (4) The verification stack includes the original bank certificate of exchange, the SWIFT MT103 copy, corporate registry filings evidencing paid-in capital, and a match between the remitter legal name and the shareholder schedule; never permit PayPal, Wise, or a merchant of record to appear as remitter for equity capital.Primary rail is SWIFT MT103 clean service payment, not documentary letters of credit, because AI data-training flows are non-goods services. Each MT103 embeds the CBE BOP purpose code in field 70: 263 for cloud, GPU, and AI hosting; 251 for royalties and license fees; 269 for other business services; and 1560 for profit distribution. The presenting bank requires the underlying contract, invoice, and proof of receipt for service imports, or board minutes, audited statements, and withholding tax receipts for dividends. Recurring vendor payments under USD 100,000 may be pre-authorized as standing instructions; GPU and cloud-intensive procurement from United States or European suppliers should be documented through three-party contracts with the ultimate AI client. Profit remittance to the non-resident shareholder is executed on the 20th day after board approval of financials, supported by the audited annual pack, withholding tax certificate, and the CBE capital and profits repatriation confirmation. All outbound wires are screened against CBE and international sanctions lists; the LLC must provide a benefit-owner confirmation letter for each beneficiary and must not split transactions to evade BOP threshold reporting.Deliverable USD/EGP foreign-exchange forwards with tenors of one to nine months offered by CIB, NBE, Banque Misr, and HSBC Egypt; permitted only where the LLC holds an underlying export receivable or BPO service contract and the bank receives the client service order with an invoice schedule.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on USD/EGP executable off-balance-sheet with licensed international banks when the onshore deliverable market dislocates; booking requires an underlying export-receipt cycle and CBE net-open-position reporting through the Egyptian counterparty bank.Cross-currency swaps in USD/EGP fixed-for-fixed for AI-infrastructure lease-in or subcontract structures above USD 1 million; subject to the bank asset-liability committee credit underwriting and CBE supervision.European-style FX options, specifically USD call and EGP put, available on a case-by-case basis from licensed banks for advance purchases of data-center equipment or formal export floors; not recommended as a standing tool for a BPO balance sheet.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility: LOW as treasury clearing core; MEDIUM as ancillary collection rail. Wise holds no CBE payment-institution license under Title IV of Law 194/2020 and does not support Egyptian corporate multi-currency balances or EGP payout corridors; therefore Wise cannot be used as the primary bank. PayPal Egypt Inc. is the one major CBE-licensed foreign PSP and can be used only as a USD aggregator for mass micro-payouts to foreign annotators; PayPal Business balances must be withdrawn to the Egyptian bank account in EGP unless the LLc maintains a USD sub-account and presents PayPal settlement statements to book the USD. Routing material customer funds through an unlicensed offshore EMI would breach Egyptian AML Law 80/2002 sanctions and CBE FX-avoidance enforcement. Recommended treasury architecture: keep PayPal as a micro-collection rail, sweep balances weekly via SWIFT MT103 to CIB or NBE, and maintain all liquidity buffers in onshore USD and EGP accounts.
