Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance – e-Tax and e-Invoicing portals available via www.eta.gov.eg and the ETA electronic invoicing system at einvoice.eta.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

A foreign-incorporated shareholder establishes the Egyptian LLC through the General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI) or the Commercial Registry, then applies to the Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) for the unified Tax ID and Tax Card. The LLC must submit its audited memorandum of association, commercial registration certificate, articles of incorporation, valid passports/POAs of shareholders and managers, lease/contract for the registered office, and other ETA-prescribed forms. Non-resident shareholders without a permanent establishment may register separately only for VAT and withholding purposes if making taxable supplies of digital services or deriving Egyptian-source payments.

14%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for goods physically exported from Egypt, for international transport services connected therewith, and for services supplied to a non-resident recipient to the extent the service is provided from Egypt and effectively used and consumed outside Egypt. The supplier must hold statutory export documentation in compliance with Law 67 of 2016 and ETA issuing rules.

  • Valid Customs Export Declaration cleared by the Egyptian Customs Authority through the automated customs system
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure of the goods from Egypt
  • Inward Foreign Remittance Certificate or Egyptian bank statement proving receipt of export proceeds in convertible foreign currency
  • Commercial invoice, underlying sales contract, and VAT return annexure referencing the export customs declarations

Mandatory real-time electronic invoicing with integrated ETA clearance: qualifying taxpayers must transmit invoices in a simplified digital format to the ETA e-invoicing system through approved APIs, receive an ETA clearance number, and imprint a machine-readable QR code on each issued invoice. B2C transactions are progressively covered by the e-receipt system. Non-compliance with e-invoicing/clearance requirements triggers administrative penalties and customs/vAT refund delays.

The ETA may refuse or claw back any zero-rated VAT benefit where the exporter fails to prove physical export, the services are not effectively consumed outside Egypt, or the foreign currency consideration is not repatriated within 180 days. Non-compliant claims are reassessed at the standard 14% VAT rate, with statutory late-payment additional tax of 2% per month or fraction thereof, plus potential doubling of tax liability, denial of VAT refunds, criminal fines and imprisonment for fraudulent evasion, and extension of the statute of limitations in cases of fictitious exports or forged documentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt operates a unified market-clearing floating exchange-rate regime for the EGP introduced on 6 March 2024, replacing the crisis-era rationed FX system and unifying the official and parallel rates. Current-account payments are broadly freely convertible consistent with Egypt's IMF Article VIII obligations accepted in 2009. Capital-account convertibility is conditioned on proper banking-channel documentation: dividend, profit, interest and capital-gains remittances require completion of underlying bank verification, evidence of FDI registration with the General Authority for Investment (GAFI) under Investment Law No. 72 of 2017, and in most cases an Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) tax-clearance certificate confirming settlement of applicable corporate and distribution taxes. Practical profit-remittance hurdles are procedural rather than quantitative: banks require source-of-funds documentation, updated commercial-register extracts, a board resolution authorising the distribution, evidence that the LLC has filed annual tax returns, and clearing of any related-party transfer-pricing reporting obligations; overseas payments are then routed via SWIFT without CBE prior approval. Companies should further ensure the foreign shareholder is recorded in the GAFI/e-investment registry and the capital has been fully paid-in through banking channels to avoid blocking of reinvestment or dividend outward remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under CBE policy, export proceeds from goods and services must be repatriated into Egypt through an Egyptian banking channel within 90 days from the date of the bill of lading for goods or from the invoice/service-completion date for services; CBE approval can extend the window for documented justified delay. Following the March 2024 FX liberalisation, the mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts is effectively 0%: exporters may retain 100% of proceeds in foreign-currency accounts at Egyptian banks, and the previous crisis-era surrender-to-EGP requirement has been abolished. The operative regulatory duties are (i) full repatriation through authorised banks, (ii) truthful attribution of the BOP purpose code and underlying trade documentation, and (iii) consistency between the invoiced value, shipping documents and the credited amount; unrepatriated proceeds or mismatched invoices will be treated as an AML/CFT compliance breach and may trigger bank account suspension.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) - Al-Bank Al-Markazi Al-Masry

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM. Egyptian law, principally CBE Law No. 194 of 2020 on Fintech and the CBE payment-systems framework, requires any payment-service, digital-wallet or closed-loop clearing activity to be performed by a CBE-licensed provider; an offshore EMI (e.g., a Wise/Payoneer-style digital bank) has no autonomous authorisation to open bank accounts, clear payments, or perform FX conversions inside Egypt and is not recognised by the CBE as a settlement counterparty. Foreign EMIs may integrate only as a technical sub-processor or as the offshore collection entity of a licensed Egyptian PSP. For this esports platform, domestic EGP collections should be cleared through a licensed PSP such as Paymob, fawry, Tap Payments or Mo'amalat, while offshore USD/EUR collections can be received via a non-resident affiliated entity or the Egyptian bank's own correspondent network; reliance on a pure offshore EMI without a local licensed PSP will trigger adverse bank KYC review and is not a viable treasury rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: the foreign shareholder executes an SWIFT MT103 wire to the LLC's Egyptian bank account with narrative 'Capital contribution - Investment Law No. 72 of 2017 - [LLC Name]'. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

22.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - dividends distributed by an Egyptian LLC are exempt from income tax in the hands of shareholders under Law 91/2005 Art. 51; Egypt does not impose a dividend withholding tax at the corporate distribution level, and there is no additional branch-remittance tax on profit repatriation.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 4 months after the close of the tax year; calendar-year taxpayers must file by April 30 of the following year.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Instalments are due by the end of the 3rd, 6th, and 9th months of the tax year; each instalment equals one-quarter of the previous year's final tax assessment (or 0.6% of annual gross revenue where no prior-year liability exists).

The Egyptian LLC is the sole taxpayer on its own profits; payments or distributions to the foreign founder are not deductible. Any balance due must be settled via ETA treasury account transfer and proven by bank receipt. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: 30000000

Economic substance scope: No standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute is enacted in Egypt; substantive equivalent is enforced through the General Anti-Avoidance Rules (Unified Tax Procedures Law 174/2020 Art. 57 and Law 91/2005 Arts. 40, 57) and the beneficial-ownership doctrine. An Egyptian LLC owning the esports platform IP or booking intercompany prize-pool fees without an Egyptian office, Egyptian employees, active management, and genuine risk assumption may be disregarded by the ETA, which can reattribute income to the foreign parent, reject the 0% dividend exemption, and assess at 22.5% plus the 2% monthly delay surcharge.
