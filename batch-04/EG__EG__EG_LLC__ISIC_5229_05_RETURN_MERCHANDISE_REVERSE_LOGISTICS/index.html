Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance, Arab Republic of Egypt; digital operations through the ETA tax-services portal, e-invoicing portal, and e-receipt platform.

E-government filing portal

(1) Register the LLC with the General Authority for Investment and Export Development (GAFI) or the local Commercial Registry and submit certified articles of association; (2) obtain a Commercial Registration Certificate and open a tax file at the Egyptian Tax Authority; (3) file Tax Registration Form No. 1, attaching constitutional documents, board resolution, manager identity/power of attorney, and proof of place of business; (4) receive the ETA Tax Card and statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN); (5) where annual taxable supplies meet the threshold, complete VAT registration through the ETA e-invoicing portal and obtain a VAT certificate; (6) register the TIN/VAT number in the ETA electronic-invoicing registration ledger.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — (1) Register the LLC with the General Authority for Investment and Export Development (GAFI) or the local Commercial Registry and submit certified articles of association; (2) obtain a Commercial Registration Certificate and open a tax file at the Egyptian Tax Authority; (3) file Tax Registration Form No. 1, attaching constitutional documents, board resolution, manager identity/power of attorney, and proof of place of business; (4) receive the ETA Tax Card and statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN); (5) where annual taxable supplies meet the threshold, complete VAT registration through the ETA e-invoicing portal and obtain a VAT certificate; (6) register the TIN/VAT number in the ETA electronic-invoicing registration ledger.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Egypt does not provide a check-the-box election or hybrid-mismatch elections under the Income Tax Law. Available statutory elections include depreciation method selection (declining balance versus straight-line), bad-debt deduction elections, five-year loss carry-forward utilization, investment incentive elections and tax holidays under the Investment Law, and Advance Pricing Agreements for transfer-pricing certainty. Non-resident entities may appoint an approved tax representative for procedural filings and service of notices.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 of annual taxable turnover, excluding zero-rated exports; foreign and remote suppliers conducting taxable supplies in Egypt through e-commerce platforms, payment channels, or local warehouses are also required to register irrespective of local establishment.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated where they physically leave Egypt and are delivered to a non-resident buyer, supported by customs export declarations, transport documents, and foreign-currency receipt. For return-merchandise and reverse-logistics operations, returns, re-graded goods, and refurbished items may qualify for zero-rating only if they are physically re-exported without local sale, use, or consumption in Egypt. Services supplied to non-residents are zero-rated where the place of supply is outside Egypt and the benefit is not received by an Egyptian person; documented by service agreement, bank receipt, and absence of an Egyptian VAT invoice.

  • Customs Export Declaration / NAFIZA final clearance release certificate issued by the Egyptian Customs Authority
  • Signed Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export from Egypt to a foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by an Egyptian bank showing receipt of foreign currency in Egypt
  • Export VAT invoice bearing the ETA e-invoicing UUID and zero-rate reason code
  • Quality-grading report, return/disposition record, and certificate of origin for returned merchandise, where applicable

Mandatory real-time fiscal clearance: registered taxpayers must integrate their enterprise resource management system with the ETA e-invoicing API, submit invoices in JSON/XML to the ETA before issuance, and obtain a validated UUID and 2D quick-response code, which must be printed on the invoice. B2C receipts are subject to the phased e-receipt mandate with live clearance. An invoice without ETA clearance is legally invalid for VAT deduction purposes. Digital stamping applies to excise and specified imported products; ordinary invoices do not require physical fiscal stamps.

The ETA conducts export forensic review through NAFIZA, bank-loop confirmation, e-invoicing ledger matching, and customs-exit verification. If zero-rating is claimed without timely hard-currency repatriation or with fictitious export documentation, the ETA will disallow the zero rate, reassess VAT at the standard 14% rate, and add monthly additional tax of 2% of the unpaid tax. Financial penalties range from EGP 5,000 to EGP 1,000,000, and tax evasion may attract a fine of up to three times the tax due, asset seizure, suspension or cancellation of the exporter's license, and criminal sanctions against directors and legal representatives under Law No. 67 of 2016 and the Central Bank of Egypt foreign-exchange rules.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt maintains a broadly liberalised current account convertibility regime for trade and service transactions subject to documentary evidence. Capital account inflows are permitted only through authorized corporate bank accounts with a completed investment declaration to the General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAIT). Profit remittances by foreign-owned LLCs are allowed after submission of audited financial statements, statutory tax clearance, and declaration on the CBE's market rate platform. In practice, temporary hard currency liquidity constraints can create prioritization queues in scarce FX periods, but the formal legal framework contains no restrictions on lawful profit transfer once regulatory filings are completed.

Export proceeds surrender rules

CBE directives require that all export proceeds be repatriated through an authorized Egyptian bank within a statute-based deadline not exceeding 120 days from the date of shipment documentation. A mandatory percentage of the repatriated hard currency, currently 50%, must be sold to the Egyptian banking sector at the prevailing interbank market rate within 30 days of receipt; the remainder may be credited to the exporter's foreign currency account for lawful operating disbursements. Percentages and timelines are periodically amended by CBE circulars and must be verified at the time of compliance.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) – the monetary, banking, and foreign exchange supervisor, operating under Banking and Central Bank System Law No. 194 of 2020

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low – offshore digital electronic money institutions are not licensed by the CBE to operate as deposit-taking banks in Egypt. Any offshore digital EMI, including platforms commonly used for global payment routing such as Airwallex, may only function as a payment initiation or collection agent that is layered on top of a fully licensed Egyptian commercial bank account. Direct use of a non-bank EMI for statutory export proceeds surrender or capital injection is not viable and would violate CBE requirements prohibiting the creation of unfettered foreign currency mobility outside the formal banking system.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions from the foreign parent must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 from a bank account held in the parent's corporate name to the LLC's designated foreign currency account at an authorized Egyptian commercial bank. The wire narrative must cite the CBE purpose code for inward direct investment and the corresponding GAIT project registration number. On receipt, the receiving bank is required to verify the copy of the notarized share transfer / capital increase resolution, the form of the foreign direct investment declaration, and the issuance of the electronic investment certificate obtained through the GAIT one-stop database. Conversion into Egyptian pounds occurs automatically at the prevailing interbank FX market rate on the date of receipt, and the resulting local currency amount is credited to the capital account.Trade settlements for importation of reverse logistics equipment, quality-grading machinery, or third-party freight handling services are executed using SWIFT MT103 for non-documentary provider payments, MT700 for secured documentary commercial letters of credit, and MT760 for standby-letter-of-credit-backed performance security. For export-related refunds to non-resident counterparties on return merchandise flows, the LLC must issue a substantiated credit instruction supported by the original debit note, customs export declaration, and CBE-approved reversal justification. In-country supplier payments and local currency refunds are cleared through the Egyptian national ACH system; cross-border payable/receivable clearing can be aggregated through an approved payment service provider, such as Airwallex, only where that provider has and maintains a local banking sponsorship arrangement and does not act as an independent settlement entity.Onshore deliverable FX forward contracts with licensed Egyptian commercial banks, supported by real trade invoices and underlying foreign currency exposure documentationOffshore nondeliverable forwards (NDFs) executed by non-resident treasury counterparties under an ISDA master netting arrangement, used for economic hedging of EGP depreciation riskFX swaps denominated in lawful currency pairs conducted through an authorized Egyptian bank's treasury desk for rolling over short-term foreign currency obligationsCross-currency swaps executed in the Egyptian interbank market under the CBE's local ISDA master terms, solely for managing long-term hard currency funding costs
