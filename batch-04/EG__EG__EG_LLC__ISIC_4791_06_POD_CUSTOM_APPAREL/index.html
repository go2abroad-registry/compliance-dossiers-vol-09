Competent corporate registry

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Under Companies Law No. 159/1981, LLC founders may fix the share capital in the Articles at any amount; there is no statutory minimum paid-up capital. Paid-up capital is evidenced by the notarized subscription schedule; no escrow account is mandatory. Cash contributions are credited to the corporate bank account after opening, while in-kind contributions are valued and described in the Articles.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance – official portal: https://www.eta.gov.eg/

E-government filing portal

A non-resident LLC must apply to the ETA via the tax portal to obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN) by filing the Tax Card application. Required documents include certified constitutional documents, commercial register extract, proof of Egypt address/lease, power of attorney to a local tax representative, and foreign entity registration certificates. VAT registration is made separately under Law 67/2016, either voluntarily or mandatorily within 30 days of exceeding the EGP 500,000 annual taxable turnover threshold; the ETA issues a VAT certificate linked to the TIN.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A non-resident LLC must apply to the ETA via the tax portal to obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN) by filing the Tax Card application. 14%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of goods physically leaving Egypt, including POD custom apparel, is subject to 0% VAT when the exporter holds an official customs export declaration and the sales proceeds are received in an acceptable foreign currency. Direct services associated with exported goods may also qualify if performed abroad and consumed by a non-resident. The taxpayer must retain contracts, customs declarations, shipping documents, and bank evidence proving the foreign trade and movement.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Egypt operates a mandatory e-invoicing regime for phased-in taxable persons. Qualified taxpayers must integrate their ERP systems with the ETA e-invoice platform via API and issue QR-coded invoices that pass real-time validation before use. A parallel mandatory e-receipt system applies to retail business-to-consumer transactions. For an online POD retailer, every export sale must be reported digitally to the ETA; failure to obtain a valid UUID/QR code may block registration and result in administrative penalties.

Unsubstantiated export zero-rating is retroactively reversed, converting the supply to taxable at 14% VAT, with late-payment charges and fines of up to EGP 50,000 per invoice/document. Fraudulent documentation or failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days may trigger criminal penalties under Law No. 207/2020 on Tax Evasion and the Unified Customs Law, including imprisonment, confiscation, and suspension of the exporter's tax registration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Egypt observes IMF Article VIII current-account convertibility for trade in goods, services, and profit remittances, yet capital-account convertibility remains administratively restricted. Inward and outward capital movements, including LLC equity injections and dividend distributions, must be channelled through CBE-authorized dealer banks, with documented underlying investment, audited financial statements, and tax clearance. Since the October 2024 flexible exchange-rate reform, the CBE no longer publishes a rigid official parity; USD/EGP pricing is determined in an interbank market operated under CBE oversight with managed-float characteristics. Profit remittance is permissible but subject to bank-level documentary review and the availability of foreign currency in the banking system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be repatriated into an Egyptian licensed bank within 180 days from the date of shipment unless CBE agrees otherwise. The exporter may retain the foreign currency in a retention account or convert to EGP at the prevailing interbank rate. The mandatory conversion ratio is currently nil under the CBE's export-support and FX-liberalization circulars; however, the CBE reserves the right to impose a surrender ratio or temporary retention rules during balance-of-payments stress or emergency exchange-control measures.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) - Foreign Exchange Operations Sector

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium when used as a supplementary collection/disbursement rail; Low when proposed as the primary treasury settlement account. Offshore digital electronic-money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise, Payoneer, or similar platforms may support card collection and international payouts, but the principal corporate account and FX treasury must reside at a CBE-licensed bank. The Egyptian payment-services regulatory framework under Law No. 18 of 2019 prohibits unlicensed non-bank entities from carrying out e-money issuance or payment-system activities in Egypt; therefore, using an offshore EMI for primary settlement would be legally constrained and inadvisable. A bank-integrated payment-orchestration model is the compliant structure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The compliant route is a SWIFT MT103 transfer from the non-resident investor's bank to the Egyptian LLC's capital-cum-operational account held at a CBE-authorized dealer bank. The wire instruction must clearly state the underlying purpose as 'Foreign Direct Investment / equity subscription in an Egyptian LLC' and include the LLC's commercial registration number. The receiving bank will require the Investment Law certificate or economic-licence evidence, board and shareholders' resolutions approving the capital injection, constitutional documents, and completion of the CBE's inward foreign investment reporting form. Conversion into EGP, if desired, is executed at the interbank market rate announced by the CBE on the value date.For low-value POD raw-material or service payments, SWIFT MT103 documentary remittance is the standard clearing rail, with invoices and purchase orders submitted through Egyptian bank channels as a current-account transfer. For large or bulk apparel procurement, a UCP600-compliant commercial documentary letter of credit issued by an Egyptian licensed bank and advised or confirmed by a foreign correspondent bank is recommended. Settlement is cleared over the SWIFT network and the Egyptian interbank foreign-exchange clearing system. Following the CBE's post-2024 foreign-exchange reforms, outbound trade disbursements no longer require CBE pre-approval or the former import-finance registration protocols, provided the transfer is supported by genuine trade documentation and the account has a compliant source of foreign currency.Interbank deliverable forward EGP/USD contracts, typically available to Egyptian onshore corporate entities through authorized-dealer banksNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in EGP/USD contracted with international counterparties, used where onshore liquidity or bank limits do not support physical deliveryCross-currency swaps between EGP and USD matched to the underlying trade-finance or capital-injection exposureOption strategies on tracked non-deliverable forwards to cap the EGP cost of projected cross-border settlements, subject to bank treasury credit approval
