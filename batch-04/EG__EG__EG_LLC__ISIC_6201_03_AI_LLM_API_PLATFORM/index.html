Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum stated capital under Law 159/1981; however, the capital must be fully subscribed, with at least 25% of the nominal value of cash quotas paid up and deposited in an escrow capital deposit account at an Egyptian bank before filing. The bank-issued Capital Deposit Certificate is mandatory for the GAFI filing; funds are released only after Commercial Registry entry.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety certificate from the Civil Defence Authority (Ministry of Interior) covering alarms, sprinklers, escape routes, and extinguishers; structural load and occupancy certification from the governorate engineering administration; and workplace clearance from the Ministry of Manpower Occupational Health Department for workstations, electrical loads, and employee welfare areas — all conditions precedent to the final municipality operating license.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum stated capital under Law 159/1981; however, the capital must be fully subscribed, with at least 25% of the nominal value of cash quotas paid up and deposited in an escrow capital deposit account at an Egyptian bank before filing. The bank-issued Capital Deposit Certificate is mandatory for the GAFI filing; funds are released only after Commercial Registry entry.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA), Ministry of Finance — official portal https://www.eta.gov.eg; electronic invoicing platform https://platform.einvoice.gov.eg

E-government filing portal

After incorporation of the LLC in the Commercial Registry, the ETA issues a Tax Identification Number (TIN) manifesting as a tax card. The foreign applicant must file notarized constitutional documents, board resolution, power of attorney, and proof of address, and appoint a resident tax representative. Separate registration for VAT is compulsory once the annual taxable turnover exceeds EGP 500,000, or earlier for the specific transactions or voluntary registration. Payroll, contract, and stamp tax registrations are obtained simultaneously through the ETA one-stop registration window.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — After incorporation of the LLC in the Commercial Registry, the ETA issues a Tax Identification Number (TIN) manifesting as a tax card. The foreign applicant must file notarized constitutional documents, board resolution, power of attorney, and proof of address, and appoint a resident tax representative. Separate registration for VAT is compulsory once the annual taxable turnover exceeds EGP 500,000, or earlier for the specific transactions or voluntary registration. Payroll, contract, and stamp tax registrations are obtained simultaneously through the ETA one-stop registration window.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists in Egypt. An LLC is mandatorily treated as a separate taxable juridical person under Law No. 91 of 2005 and Law No. 67 of 2016. Pass-through treatment is unavailable to LLC members. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available; the Egyptian Domestic and DTT framework follows the BEPS hybrid mismatch rules on a statutory, non-elective basis.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

14%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EGP 500,000 annual taxable turnover from taxable goods or services

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods qualify for 0% VAT where the goods are physically exported pursuant to a valid customs export declaration and the foreign-exchange proceeds are received within the statutory period. Offshore services, including AI/LLM API services supplied to a non-resident business customer, qualify for zero-rating where the recipient is outside Egypt, the service is consumed outside the Egyptian customs and tax territory, payment is received from foreign currency, and the Egyptian provider is not making a separate domestic VAT supply. The zero-rating claim must be supported by written contracts, service delivery documentation, and the customer's non-resident status evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (C52 or equivalent electronic export declaration)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / directly applicable transport document
  • Commercial Invoice and custom value declaration
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or Central Bank of Egypt foreign exchange receipt evidencing repatriation of export proceeds

Egyptian e-invoicing is mandatory for VAT registrants above the ETA-designated revenue thresholds. Invoices must be generated through approved B2B/B2C systems and transmitted in XML or JSON to the ETA platform in real time. The system performs automated schema validation, fiscal stamp application, and issuance of a unified invoice number with a QR-code-based digital stamp. Unscrupulous manual invoices or rejected submissions are legally treated as non-issued invoices and trigger penalties.

The ETA audits zero-rating and refund claims under a risk-based e-filter. A taxpayer failing to substantiate delivery, export, or repatriation within 180 days will have the zero-rating reversed at 14% VAT, with input VAT recovered by way of recapture. Additional penalties include monthly late-payment interest, administrative fines of up to 200% of the tax evaded, and, in cases of intentional fraudulent documentation or forged customs declarations, criminal sanctions including imprisonment, licence deregistration, and retrospective clawback up to five years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Egyptian Pound (EGP) is largely convertible for current-account transactions under the Foreign Exchange Law No. 194 of 2020, while capital-account movements remain subject to CBE prior-approval or referral rules. Profit remittances are permitted after satisfying Egyptian tax clearance, settlement of trade finance obligations, and submission of audited financial statements. Following the March 2024 exchange-rate float and IMF-backed reform, no mandatory surrender ratio applies to export receipts, and FX availability is governed by the interbank market supply and demand framework.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under CBE Circular No. 65 of 2022 (as amended), export proceeds must be repatriated to Egypt within 90 days from the date of shipment unless the exporter's bank grants an approved extension. The previously applicable mandatory conversion percentages (e.g., 50% for exporters) were abolished with the March 2024 exchange-rate regime; export proceeds may be credited to foreign-currency accounts, and conversion to EGP is conducted at the prevailing interbank market rate when settlement obligations require EGP.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Egypt (CBE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Revolut Fit: High

Low — Offshore fintech EMIs (e.g., Payoneer, Revolut Business) are not licensed as authorized dealers under Egyptian banking law and cannot operate as CBE-compliant settlement institutions for the mandated treasury flows. They may be used only as merchant collection gateways on the customer-facing side, but all final FX settlement, capital injection, and export proceeds repatriation must route through a licensed Egyptian bank under the CBE open-market exchange regime.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

22.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final WHT on dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders; the paying Egyptian LLC is the withholding agent. The domestic 10% rate applies to distributions from profits; treaty rates commonly range from 5% to 15% depending on shareholding structure.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 4 months after the close of the fiscal year. For a 31-December fiscal year-end: filed and final balance paid by 30 April of the following year; taxpayers with an approved different fiscal year file within 4 months of their own year-end.
