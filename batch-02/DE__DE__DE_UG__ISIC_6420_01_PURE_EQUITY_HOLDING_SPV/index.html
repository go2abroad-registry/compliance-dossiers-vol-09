Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office providing a physical German street address, mail acceptance, and document service may be tolerated by the commercial registry in most districts for a passive equity-holding SPV. However, for the UG to maintain German tax residence and commercial substance, the seat of actual management must be in Germany. A pure mailbox/letterbox arrangement with no local books, no local management decision-making, and no physical substance creates material risk of tax reclassification, denial of treaty benefits, and anti-money-laundering scrutiny. For this passive holding industry, a virtual office should be supplemented by a local registered agent, German bank account, factual minutes, and substance documentation.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The UG (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschränkt) may be formed with a nominal share capital as low as EUR 1; there is no statutory minimum capital. Nevertheless, all cash contributions must be fully paid in before registration and placed at the free disposal of the managing director. No statutorily mandated escrow account applies; the register court may request bank payment confirmation. Under GmbHG §5a(3), the UG must retain 25% of annual net distributable profits in a statutory reserve until the share capital reaches EUR 25,000.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Local Finanzamt (Tax Office) with jurisdiction over the UG's registered seat — responsible for corporate income tax (Körperschaftsteuer), solidarity surcharge (Solidaritätszuschlag), trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) and VAT assessment; Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt, Federal Central Tax Office) allocates the VAT identification number and administers cross-border information exchange, DAC/EU administrative assistance and e-invoicing data access; Bundesministerium der Finanzen (BMF) is the central policy authority. Filing portals: ELSTER (www.elster.de) and BZSt Online-Portal (www.bzst.de).

E-government filing portal

Procedure for a foreign-founded UG: (1) notarise the articles of association and register the company in the Commercial Register (Handelsregister) at the local Amtsgericht; (2) file the statutory tax registration questionnaire (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung, form 15a/15b) with the competent Finanzamt within one month of formation, electronically via ELSTER; (3) obtain the Steuernummer (tax number) and advance VAT filings (Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldungen); (4) apply for the Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer (VAT ID, format DE123456789) with BZSt through the ELSTER-BZSt interface; (5) for non-resident shareholders, submit certified passport/ID copies, UBO register evidence under the Geldwäschegesetz and proof of registered office; non-EU founders must additionally provide notarially certified corporate documents. No apostille is needed for EU founders; a foreign parent carrying on German VAT-exposed activities must also file form USt 1 TG.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — the UG is a German corporation (Kapitalgesellschaft) with statutory resident status and unlimited worldwide taxation under § 1 KStG; headline burden: 15% corporate income tax plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge (aggregate 15.825%) and municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) with an average effective load of approximately 14% to 17%. A pure equity holding SPV benefits from the 95% exemption for dividends under § 8b KStG (5% deemed non-deductible expenses) and, where the shareholding equals at least 15%, a full trade tax exemption for dividends under § 9 Nr. 7 GewStG. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the UG is a German corporation (Kapitalgesellschaft) with statutory resident status and unlimited worldwide taxation under § 1 KStG; headline burden: 15% corporate income tax plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge (aggregate 15.825%) and municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) with an average effective load of approximately 14% to 17%. A pure equity holding SPV benefits from the 95% exemption for dividends under § 8b KStG (5% deemed non-deductible expenses) and, where the shareholding equals at least 15%, a full trade tax exemption for dividends under § 9 Nr. 7 GewStG. Pass-through treatment is not available for a UG; no territorial-limitation election and no tax-exempt-zone regime applies in Germany.

Available election forms

Germany does not operate a check-the-box election. Available statutory elections: (1) Organschaft (corporate fiscal unity) under §§ 14–17 KStG and § 2 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 GewStG, allowing a German parent holding at least 75% of the UG and executing a profit-and-loss pooling agreement to consolidate corporate income tax, trade tax and VAT results; (2) the option under § 9 UStG to waive VAT exemption for real-estate leases; (3) conversion of the UG into a regular GmbH under § 5a Abs. 3 GmbHG once registered capital reaches €25,000. Hybrid mismatch consequences are non-elective and apply automatically under § 4k EStG (ATAD transposition), denying deductions where the counterparty jurisdiction grants non-taxation or double deduction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no general turnover threshold for VAT registration — any entrepreneur carrying on taxable supplies must register. However, the small-business exemption (Kleinunternehmerregelung) under § 19 UStG applies where prior-year turnover did not exceed €25,000 and current-year expected turnover does not exceed €100,000 (thresholds raised from €22,000/€50,000 as of 1 January 2025). For intra-EU distance sales of goods and certain B2C digital services, the annual cross-border threshold is €10,000, above which the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) or local foreign registration is required. A passive equity holding UG realising only dividend income is generally outside the VAT registration net because the holding of shares is exempt under § 4 Nr. 8 UStG without input VAT recovery.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

An entrepreneurial UG may claim the export exemption (steuerfreie Ausfuhrlieferung — economically zero-rated with full input VAT deduction) under § 4 Nr. 1a UStG and § 6 UStG where the supplier or the non-EU recipient dispatches the goods from Germany to a third-country destination outside the EU, actual exportation is proven, and the documentary and bookkeeping evidence (Beleg- und Buchnachweis) complies with §§ 8–13 UStDV. Intra-EU supplies to business customers are exempt under § 6a UStG upon presentation of the customer's valid VAT ID and transport evidence. B2B services supplied to non-EU entrepreneurs are outside German VAT pursuant to the place-of-supply rule in § 3a Abs. 2 UStG, qualifying as exempt export services. A passive equity holding UG generally has no export goods and no taxable input chain; its shareholding activity is VAT-exempt under § 4 Nr. 8 UStG, and any intercompany management charges are subject to the reverse-charge mechanism.

  • Customs export declaration (Ausfuhrbegleitdokument) validated electronically through the ATLAS customs system, plus Intrastat declaration where relevant
  • Transport evidence: Bill of Lading (sea), Air Waybill (air) or CMR consignment note (road), naming the exporter and the non-EU destination
  • Commercial invoice (Handelsrechnung) and packing list specifying quantities, HS codes and Incoterms
  • VAT identification number of the EU customer for intra-Community supplies exempt under § 6a UStG
  • Inward remittance certificate (Zahlungseingangsbeleg) evidencing collection of the foreign-currency sales proceeds within the 180-day repatriation benchmark

Germany has mandated structured B2B e-invoicing (E-Rechnung) effective 1 January 2025 under the Wachstumschancengesetz. All businesses must be able to receive e-invoices in XRechnung or ZUGFeRD format; until 31 December 2026, paper, PDF and EDI invoicing may continue with recipient consent. From 1 January 2027, suppliers with annual turnover exceeding €800,000 (reference year 2026) must issue e-invoices; from 1 January 2028, all businesses must issue e-invoices. The regime does not require real-time fiscal clearance, government digital stamping or mandatory API pre-validation; compliance is achieved through secure electronic transmission and tamper-proof archiving under GoBD (Grundsätze zur ordnungsmäßigen Führung und Aufbewahrung von Büchern), with BZSt access to invoice data during statutory audits.

Export zero-rating is scrutinised by the local Finanzamt with data exchange through Zoll (German customs) and the BZSt. Where documentary proof is deficient or goods are illicitly diverted into the EU, the Finanzamt reverses the exemption and assesses output VAT at 19% (or 7% reduced rate) on the full supply value, adds interest at 0.15% per month (1.8% per annum) under § 233a AO for interest periods after 31 December 2018, and imposes late-payment surcharges of 1% per month under § 240 AO. Administrative negligence fines under § 378 AO range from 10% to 30% of the evaded tax; intentional evasion constitutes a criminal tax offence under § 370 AO, punishable by imprisonment of six months to ten years or an income-based fine, with a ten-year limitation period under § 169 AO. Retrospective clawback includes recapture of any input VAT credits previously refunded, denial of the § 8b KStG dividend exemption in abuse cases under § 42 AO, and adverse DAC6/Amtshilfe information exchanges to counterpart EU member states.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible; Germany maintains neither capital account nor current account exchange controls. Profit remittances, dividends, and interest may be freely transferred to non-resident shareholders without approval. The only material obligations are (i) statistical payment reporting to the Bundesbank for every cross-border payment or receipt exceeding EUR 12,500 and (ii) FDI screening under the AWV if the SPV expands into sensitive sectors. For a passive equity holding SPV with no operational activity, no specific convertibility restriction applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany imposes no mandatory surrender, no minimum conversion ratio, and no statutory repatriation deadline for export receipts. The exporter may retain foreign currency in any licensed bank or exchange it freely. The sole statutory requirement is the monthly reporting of individual cross-border transactions in excess of EUR 12,500 to the Deutsche Bundesbank, generally due by the fifth working day of the following month, including the nature and counterparty of the payment.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank, Frankfurt am Main, exercising oversight under the German Foreign Trade and Payments Act (AWV) and the EU sanctions framework, with BaFin (Federal Financial Supervisory Authority) supervising payment institutions and securities compliance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low to Medium for a pure equity holding SPV. German-licensed EMIs and digital banks (e.g., current-account fintechs under BaFin or ZAG) are legally able to open corporate accounts, but most decline passive holding structures due to insufficient operational substance and enhanced AML requirements. Offshore digital EMIs holding a European passport are legally admitted after a BaFin notification, but they generally cannot provide the degree of documentary support required for a Cape-Delaware-style holding SPV and tax certificate issuance; a full relationship with a tier-1 commercial bank is therefore recommended.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The parent or the ultimate beneficial owner must instruct a SWIFT MT103 wire transfer to the UG's beneficiary account at a German bank. The wire must clearly reference the legal basis, e.g., 'Kapitalerhöhung bei DE_UG', and include the German purpose code for capital investment (Zahlungszweck 'E01' or 'Kapitalanlage') where the bank requires a classification. For payments from non-EU jurisdictions, the remitter's commercial invoice or a notarised declaration of the bank purpose is indispensable. The receiving German bank will automatically include the amount in the Bundesbank's official statistics if it exceeds EUR 12,500. To avoid blocking, the UG must also present a notarised shareholder resolution approving the capital increase and, if the contribution is made in kind, an audited valuation.For a pure equity holding SPV, trade-related outbound payments are exceptional, but the standard rails are: (i) SWIFT MT103 for direct contractual payments, covering intercompany loans, dividends, and professional fees; (ii) Commercial Letters of Credit issued under ICC UCP 600 for import transactions if the SPV is ever used as a procurement or settlement entity; and (iii) standard SEPA Direct Salary/Wire for domestic operations. Every outbound disbursement must be mapped to an underlying contract, subject to transfer pricing documentation if the counterparty is affiliated, and reported to the Bundesbank under the AWV when above EUR 12,500. For dividend remittances to the parent, the wire should include the purpose code 'DIV' and the WHT reclaim/exemption certificate reference to secure the Directive treatment.Deliverable FX outright forward (Devisentermingeschäft) with a maturity of up to 12 months, concluded under an ISDA 2002 or a conservative German master agreement, hedging a specific, documented exposure (e.g., future dividend in foreign currency).Non-deliverable forward (NDF) limited to currencies that cannot be cleared on an onshore basis (e.g., CNY, RUB, TRY) and only when supported by a genuine commercial exposure or a liquid market quotation; must be booked through an EU-authorised bank and evidenced by an underlying exposure report to the Bundesbank and tax advisor.Cross-currency swap (FX swap rolling / long-term forward) used to hedge the effective EUR value of a future acquisition or dividend stream, with the duration matching the foreign subsidiary's distribution plan.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Low to Medium for a pure equity holding SPV. German-licensed EMIs and digital banks (e.g., current-account fintechs under BaFin or ZAG) are legally able to open corporate accounts, but most decline passive holding structures due to insufficient operational substance and enhanced AML requirements. Offshore digital EMIs holding a European passport are legally admitted after a BaFin notification, but they generally cannot provide the degree of documentary support required for a Cape-Delaware-style holding SPV and tax certificate issuance; a full relationship with a tier-1 commercial bank is therefore recommended.
