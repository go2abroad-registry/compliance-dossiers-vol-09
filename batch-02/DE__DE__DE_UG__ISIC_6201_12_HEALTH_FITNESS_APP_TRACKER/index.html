Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

For a UG (Entrepreneurial Company), the minimum nominal share capital is €1.00 under GmbHG §5a. The full subscribed capital must be paid in cash to a bank account in the company's name before registration; non-cash contributions are prohibited. The bank's certificate (Bankbescheinigung) must evidence that the funds are at the company's free disposal. Until registered share capital reaches €25,000, 25% of each year's net profit must be allocated to the statutory capital reserve.

Funding & unlock path: For any occupied office, the UG must comply with the Occupational Health and Safety Act (ArbSchG), workplace regulation (Arbeitsstättenverordnung), and building fire safety rules. A fire safety certificate from the local fire protection authority is required for premises with employees; structural load certificates and equipment inspection records must be maintained and presented to the Berufsgenossenschaft or municipal authorities upon inspection.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, BZSt) for VAT identification and central tax matters; local Tax Office (Finanzamt) for income, trade and VAT administration; electronic filing portal ELSTER (www.elster.de) and BZSt online services (www.bzst.de).

E-government filing portal

A German-resident UG obtains its Steuernummer by filing the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung (statutory tax registration questionnaire) via ELSTER with the locally competent Finanzamt based on its registered seat/place of management. The UG then separately applies to the BZSt for a Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer (VAT ID) through the BZSt online procedure once entrepreneurial activity begins. Non-resident entities without a German establishment are directly registered for VAT through the BZSt/competent Finanzamt procedure; a local tax representative may be required where no domestic seat exists.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A German-resident UG obtains its Steuernummer by filing the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung (statutory tax registration questionnaire) via ELSTER with the locally competent Finanzamt based on its registered seat/place of management. The UG then separately applies to the BZSt for a Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer (VAT ID) through the BZSt online procedure once entrepreneurial activity begins. Non-resident entities without a German establishment are directly registered for VAT through the BZSt/competent Finanzamt procedure; a local tax representative may be required where no domestic seat exists.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or pass-through election is available; German tax classification follows domestic corporate law, making the UG fully taxable at entity level. Contractual elections that may apply include the conclusion of a profit-and-loss transfer agreement for Organschaft (corporate income tax, trade tax and VAT fiscal unity) and the option under §9 UStG to waive VAT exemption for real-estate transactions. Hybrid mismatch rules are applied under German implementation of ATAD and prevent deduction/non-inclusion mismatches without elective relief.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 10,000 intra-EU distance-sales/OSS threshold for cross-border electronic services and distance sales; no general VAT registration threshold for non-resident suppliers; domestic small-business exemption available up to EUR 25,000 prior-year turnover and EUR 100,000 current-year turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0%/exempt-with-input-credit VAT treatment applies to (i) goods physically exported from the EU customs territory, evidenced by a customs export declaration and proof of physical exit; (ii) intra-EU supplies to VAT-registered recipients supported by a valid EU VAT ID; and (iii) services supplied to non-EU recipients where the place of supply is outside Germany. For digital health and fitness app sales, German VAT is driven by place-of-supply rules: B2B electronic services follow the customer's establishment; B2C electronic services to EU consumers fall under the EU One-Stop-Shop destination-VAT mechanism; B2C supplies to non-EU consumers generally fall outside German VAT without invoking a customs-type export zero-rating procedure.

  • Customs export declaration (Ausfuhranmeldung/Export accompanying document) via ATLAS
  • Proof of physical export from EU customs territory
  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Bill of lading or air waybill
  • Inward remittance certificate or payment evidence

Germany does not operate real-time tax-authority clearance, continuous transaction controls or digital stamping. Mandatory e-invoicing for domestic B2B supplies took effect on 1 January 2025 on receipt using the EN 16931 formats XRechnung/ZUGFeRD; phased issuance obligations apply from 2027 for large taxpayers and from 2028 for all taxpayers. VAT returns, advance VAT returns and recapitulative statements are filed through ELSTER; no API-based fiscal fiscal-clearance system is currently in force.

Fraudulent export zero-rating or forged export evidence triggers retrospective VAT assessment plus default interest at 6% per annum, reversal of input VAT deductions, and penalty charges. Tax evasion under §370 AO carries imprisonment up to five years, or up to ten years in aggravated cases, together with monetary fines. The extended assessment period of ten years applies to tax-evasion cases, and the responsible directors, tax agents and beneficial owners may incur joint and several liability. Export evidence and customs dossiers are audited under §§147, 193 AO and subject to retrospective clawback if falsified.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany maintains fully liberalized current and capital account convertibility under TFEU Articles 63–66 and AWG § 4. The euro is freely convertible; there are no exchange controls, no licensing requirements for inward capital contributions into a UG, no restrictions on dividend distributions, royalties, or exit proceeds. Profit remittance by a UG is subject only to (i) German corporate-law capital-preservation tests (GmbHG §§ 30–31 and the UG-specific statutory reserve build-up under GmbHG § 5a(3), which restricts distributions until share capital plus reserves reach EUR 25,000), and (ii) German withholding tax of 25% plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge on the 25% (i.e., 26.375% effective), reduced where an applicable DTT or the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive applies. The only material FX compliance duty is the Bundesbank statistical external-statistics reporting under AWV §§ 60–67: any resident-to-non-resident or non-resident-to-resident payment (including app-store payouts, license fees, and capital increases) must be declared when the aggregate per-counterparty cross-border flows reach EUR 12,500 per calendar year.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Germany imposes no surrender obligations, no repatriation deadlines, and no mandatory conversion ratios for export receipts or digital-service revenue. USD receivables from Apple/Google U.S. storefront settlements may be retained indefinitely in a non-EUR account without penalty or forced sale. For euro-zone intuitive treatment, the UG may elect voluntary conversion through its clearing bank; the sole statutory requirement for non-EUR receipts is the AWV EUR 12,500 cross-border reporting threshold, plus correct macro-statistical classification of the underlying service revenue code.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (Central Bank of the Federal Republic of Germany, Frankfurt am Main), acting within the European System of Central Banks; regulatory banking and AML supervision by the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht – BaFin); foreign trade and payments legal framework administered jointly with the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action under the Foreign Trade and Payments Act (Außenwirtschaftsgesetz – AWG) and the Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung – AWV).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as auxiliary rail, Low as sole primary account. BaFin-licensed German e-money institutions and payment institutions (EMIs/PIs) may lawfully provide the UG with merchant acquiring, virtual IBANs, multi-currency wallets, and API-driven cross-border payouts; however, an offshore digital EMI (Lithuanian, Irish, or UK e-money licence) cannot present itself as a German deposit-taking bank, cannot issue a truly attachment-proof and creditor-reference-compliant German IBAN for SEPA creditor identifier purposes, and creates friction in Apple/Google payout VAT validation (USt-IdNr) and Bundesbank statistical reporting. The treasury blueprint therefore uses a baFin-regulated EMI only for segregated pre-funding corridors and mid-month USD/EUR conversion facilities, while all legal-entity balances, UBO-linked compliance, and statutory records remain in a German tier-1 bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Statutory routing for non-resident shareholder equity injection: SEPA Credit Transfer (EUR) or SWIFT MT103 (USD) to the UG's German operative account; the remittance information (Verwendungszweck) must specify 'Einlage Stammkapital UG – [Handelsregisternummer] – Kapitalverkehr' to enable correct Bundesbank external-statistics classification. Where the contribution is paid in cash into the German account before the UG is registered in the commercial register, the receiving bank should be instructed to hold the funds on a Girokonto 'für Rechnung der Vorgesellschaft' (pre-incorporation account); immigration-controlled authorization is not required for EU shareholders under TFEU Art. 63, while non-EU shareholders likewise need no German Office for Economic Affairs approval because a UG used for digital-app software licensing is not a sensitive-sector investment under AWG Annex 1. All Apple/Google app-store payouts are treated as current-account service export revenue; each payout batch must bear the XML/beneficiary reference containing the app identification and Apple/Google payout IDs, with the German bank receiving the corresponding AWV Z5 declaration automatically where the EUR 12,500 aggregate threshold is exceeded.(1) SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) and SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) for euro payouts to EU subcontractors, cloud service providers, and German end-customer refunds, including direct debit (SEPA Core/B2B) reconciliation of app-store chargebacks. (2) SWIFT MT103 (field 70 remittance information) for non-euro worldwide vendor settlements, particularly USD, GBP, and CHF software licensing fees; MT103 must be accompanied by UETR identification and OFAC/EU consolidated sanctions screening. (3) Commercial Letters of Credit under UCP 600 (issuance via SWIFT MT700, settlement via MT740/MT756) for high-value data-infrastructure contracts, plus standby LCs where contractual performance security is demanded by a non-resident distributor. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium as auxiliary rail, Low as sole primary account. BaFin-licensed German e-money institutions and payment institutions (EMIs/PIs) may lawfully provide the UG with merchant acquiring, virtual IBANs, multi-currency wallets, and API-driven cross-border payouts; however, an offshore digital EMI (Lithuanian, Irish, or UK e-money licence) cannot present itself as a German deposit-taking bank, cannot issue a truly attachment-proof and creditor-reference-compliant German IBAN for SEPA creditor identifier purposes, and creates friction in Apple/Google payout VAT validation (USt-IdNr) and Bundesbank statistical reporting. The treasury blueprint therefore uses a baFin-regulated EMI only for segregated pre-funding corridors and mid-month USD/EUR conversion facilities, while all legal-entity balances, UBO-linked compliance, and statutory records remain in a German tier-1 bank account.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% corporate income tax (Koerperschaftsteuer) on taxable profits plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge on the assessed corporate tax, yielding an effective headline rate of 15.825%; municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) at typical local rates of 14%–17% brings the combined statutory burden to approximately 30%–33% depending on the municipality.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

The statutory domestic rate is 25% Kapitalertragsteuer plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge, aggregating to 26.375%. Treaty and EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive relief can reduce or eliminate the withholding where beneficial ownership is established; the residual rate may be 0%–15% depending on the applicable double taxation agreement.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The corporate income tax return must be filed within five months after the end of the fiscal year. For calendar-year taxpayers with a 31 December year-end, the return is due by 31 May of the following year. When filed by a qualified German tax adviser, the deadline is automatically extended to 31 July; further discretionary extensions may be granted by the responsible Finanzamt under Section 109 AO.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File documentation is mandatory in Germany when the annual aggregate value of cross-border controlled transactions with related parties exceeds EUR 100,000 per transaction category; a Master File is required where the multinational group's consolidated global revenue exceeds EUR 750,000,000. Tax authorities may demand contemporaneous documentation at audit even below these administrative thresholds where the facts indicate potential income shifting.

Economic substance scope: Germany does not maintain a separate UAE-style Economic Substance Regulation filing, but substantive economic presence is enforced through Section 42 AO (abuse of law), Section 50d Abs. 3 EStG (treaty and conduit abuse), and German CFC rules (AStG). A UG must maintain a physical office, resident management, payroll, independent records, local bank accounts, and actual strategic decision-making in Germany. A letterbox structure will be recharacterised, the corporate veil may be pierced, and treaty relief will be denied.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Incorrect Corporate Return

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine and imprisonment up to 5 years under Section 370 AO; aggravated cases involving amounts exceeding EUR 50,000 or false documents carry imprisonment of 6 months to 10 years. The tax claim accrues at 0.5% monthly interest and is enforced by seizure and distress.

Director personal liability: The managing director acts as statutory tax representative under Section 34 AO and is personally liable for intentional misconduct. Criminal conviction results in civil restitution of under-collected taxes, confiscation of personal assets, travel bans, and imprisonment.
