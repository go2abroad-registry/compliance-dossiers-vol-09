Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet, MTA); online portal e-MTA at emta.ee; company formation via e-Business Register (ariregister.rik.ee) including e-Residency digital ID channel.

E-government filing portal

OÜ is incorporated in the e-Business Register; the Commercial Register assigns an 8-digit registry code which is the statutory corporate TIN, and MTA is notified automatically. For VAT, a resident OÜ applies in e-MTA once EUR 40,000 taxable turnover is exceeded, or earlier voluntarily. A non-resident entity without an Estonian seat must register for VAT from its first taxable supply in Estonia; EU-based entities apply via e-MTA, non-EU entities through an approved tax representative. The VAT ID (EE + 9 digits) is generally issued within 10 working days.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation: OÜ is a separate CIT taxpayer with full deferral — undistributed annual profits are taxed at 0%; 20% tax (14% reduced rate for regular dividends within prior three-year average; 22% for non-regular distributions under 2025 reform) arises solely on distributions and deemed distributions (fringe benefits, gifts, non-business costs). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: OÜ is a separate CIT taxpayer with full deferral — undistributed annual profits are taxed at 0%; 20% tax (14% reduced rate for regular dividends within prior three-year average; 22% for non-regular distributions under 2025 reform) arises solely on distributions and deemed distributions (fringe benefits, gifts, non-business costs). No branch remittance tax; territorial scope does not apply to retained profits; non-resident shareholders may face withholding on distributions.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Estonian law; OÜ classification is fixed by the Commercial Code. Available elections: (i) voluntary VAT registration before the threshold; (ii) VAT group registration with MTA consent; (iii) OSS/IOSS registration for EU distance sales; (iv) Advance Pricing Agreement for transfer pricing; (v) regular-dividend treatment to access the 14% reduced rate; (vi) ATAD anti-hybrid rules apply — no elective hybrid mismatch classification regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

22%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 40,000 taxable turnover per calendar year for resident entities; nil threshold for non-residents (registration from first taxable supply in Estonia); EUR 10,000 EU-wide threshold for distance sales of goods via OSS.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT under VAT Act § 15 for goods physically exported outside the EU within 90 days, including new ocean container vessel hulls and installed equipment. VAT Act § 16(2) zero-rates supplies of goods and services for equipping, repairing, maintaining or chartering seagoing vessels engaged in international traffic — covering shipyard construction, repair and conversion. Cross-border B2B services to non-EU shipowners are zero-rated or outside scope where the place of supply is outside Estonia. All claims require export evidence retained by the OÜ.

  • Customs Export Declaration (EAD) electronically lodged and released by MTA customs
  • Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill evidencing third-country discharge port
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 proof of foreign-currency payment received
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and vessel delivery/acceptance protocol
  • International sales contract with INCOTERMS
  • Ship's exit certificate or customs office confirmation showing departure from EU customs territory

No legacy real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping. Phased e-invoicing mandate: from 1 July 2025 all businesses must be able to receive structured e-invoices (EN 16931); B2B e-invoice issuance becomes mandatory from 1 January 2026 with full private-sector scope from 1 January 2027, using Peppol-compatible service operators and e-MTA reporting. MTA applies risk-based real-time analytics and will align with EU ViDA digital VAT reporting.

MTA audits export zero-rating by cross-checking EAD, Bill of Lading, port exit records and FX receipt trail. Unproven exports are reassessed at 22% VAT plus default interest of 0.06% per day under Taxation Act § 129, with risk of VAT registration revocation. Intentional false zero-rating or forged export documents constitute tax fraud under Penal Code § 397: fine and/or up to 5 years' imprisonment (up to 7 years for large-scale criminal groups). Reassessment window is 3 years (5 years for misdeclaration), and is unlimited in criminal fraud cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia is a Eurozone state (EUR since 1 January 2011) with full current-account and capital-account convertibility under Articles 63-65 TFEU. No foreign-exchange control, conversion licence or central-bank approval applies to capital imports, trade payments or profit repatriation. The only material remittance hurdle is the Estonian Corporate Income Tax Act: an OÜ's earnings are tax-deferred until distribution, and a profit distribution triggers 20/80 corporate income tax (14/86 reduced rate available for regular distributions). This is a tax event, not a currency-control event; dividends and loan repayments can be converted from EUR to any permitted currency freely.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion exists in Estonia. There is no statutory timeline for repatriation, no minimum conversion ratio and no bank duty to convert export receipts into EUR. Export proceeds may be retained in EUR or any freely convertible currency on an Estonian bank account; the only related obligation is statistical balance-of-payments reporting under the Eesti Pank Act, which the domestic bank performs at a transaction-level threshold.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia), an integral member of the Eurosystem and the European System of Central Banks; since 1 January 2023, prudential supervision of Estonian credit institutions is consolidated inside Eesti Pank (formerly the independent Finantsinspektsioon).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility for ancillary operational flows, Low as a primary LC or escrow rail. EEA-licensed electronic-money institutions (including Estonian EMI licensees) may lawfully open payment accounts, execute FX conversions and issue e-money under EMD2/PSD2, but they are not credit institutions and cannot issue or confirm irrevocable Commercial LCs, cannot legally discharge a capital-escrow mandate under the Estonian Law of Obligations for shipbuilding instalments, and fall short of the maritime counterparty-acceptance standards of most prime shipowner banks. The mandatory pattern for this industry is therefore: EMI accounts only for small outflows and payroll-side rails, while instalment LCs, escrow and major USD receipts sit exclusively with a full credit institution chartered under the Estonian Credit Institutions Act.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Fragmented, third-party-funded or unexplained equity injections into the OÜ inconsistent with the shipyard business plan; funds originating from high-risk or aggregating payment providers.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Use SWIFT MT103 or a TARGET2/SEPA credit transfer naming the OÜ's IBAN, the deposit account class and purpose text 'equity injection / direct investment / BOP equity' so the receiving bank can apply the statutory Eesti Pank balance-of-payments classification. The OÜ must first pass a board resolution and file the capital decision in the e-Business Register; funds must originate from the investor's own regulated account (no third-party aggregation); for injections over EUR 100,000 the bank will require an enhanced source-of-funds questionnaire and the notarized capital decision. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required if cross-border controlled transactions exceed €50,000 (single transaction) or €250,000 (aggregate) per year; Master File required if group consolidated revenue exceeds €50 million

Economic substance scope: Estonia does not have specific Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) but requires genuine economic activity for treaty benefits; anti-abuse rules (e.g., GAAR) apply; shipyard must demonstrate operational substance (employees, premises, equipment) to avoid treaty denial

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fine up to €1.2 million or 300% of evaded tax; criminal prosecution; asset seizure
