Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up share capital is required for an OÜ under Estonian law; the articles of association may fix the share capital at EUR 0 or at any higher amount chosen by the founders. No escrow account is imposed by the Commercial Code for the zero-capital route. If positive share capital is chosen, the corresponding contribution must be paid or contributed as provided in the articles, and the capital amount must be recorded in the shareholders' register.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety compliance under the Rescue Act is mandatory: the production premises must have appropriate extinguishers, smoke detection, safe storage of flammable resins and hardeners, and an emergency evacuation plan; the Rescue Board (Päästeamet) may inspect without prior notice. Under the Occupational Health and Safety Act, the workplace must be registered with the Labour Inspectorate (Tööinspektsioon), employees must receive chemical-agent risk training, and ventilation or extraction systems must be periodically verified. Structural load inspection is not ordinarily required for tabletop dice casting unless the premises are modified, heavy storage racks are installed, or the building use classification is changed.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up share capital is required for an OÜ under Estonian law; the articles of association may fix the share capital at EUR 0 or at any higher amount chosen by the founders. No escrow account is imposed by the Commercial Code for the zero-capital route. If positive share capital is chosen, the corresponding contribution must be paid or contributed as provided in the articles, and the capital amount must be recorded in the shareholders' register.

(2) The registered office of the Company is fixed at the address stated in the articles, which shall serve as the Company's official seat and principal place of administration in Estonia. Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Consumer Protection and Technical Regulatory Authority (Tarbijakaitse ja Tehnilise Järelevalve Amet, TTJA) — applicable for product-safety surveillance only; no general industrial operating licence is required for small-batch ISIC 3240 resin casting of tabletop dice.

Category: Not applicable — no statutory operating or manufacturing licence is required for non-hazardous artisan manufacture of polyhedral dice under Estonian law; product safety and CE-marking obligations apply if the dice fall under an applicable EU product-safety directive, in which case an EU Declaration of Conformity must be prepared.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia operates under full capital account convertibility as an EU member state. Profit remittances, dividends, and interest payments face no statutory restrictions or convertibility hurdles for resident OÜ entities. All cross-border transfers are subject solely to standard AML/KYC verification and reporting thresholds set by Eesti Pank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or forced conversion of export proceeds applies in Estonia. Export receipts must be reported to the Bank of Estonia for statistical purposes if exceeding EUR 50,000 per transaction or annually, but voluntary conversion to euro is permitted without fixed ratios or penalties.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Estonia (Eesti Pank) and European Central Bank (ECB)

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% of gross profit distributions (20/80 of net dividend, i.e. 25% of net cash payment); 0% tax on undistributed retained earnings

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% shareholder-level withholding tax; the distributing Estonian OÜ pays the 20% corporate distribution tax on gross profits distributed (20/80 of net dividend, i.e. 25% of net cash payment)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 50,000 aggregate annual cross-border controlled transactions trigger transfer pricing documentation and disclosure obligations in Estonia; a Master File and Country-by-Country Report are required where the ultimate parent group has consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750 million.

Economic substance scope: Estonia does not maintain separate Economic Substance Regulations comparable to Caribbean OECD jurisdictions; however, substance is embedded in the legal definition of Estonian corporate residence (actual place of effective management) and in the OECD Principal Purpose Test. An e-Residency OÜ that is managed from abroad, has no Estonian board meetings, and lacks any operational substance in Estonia may be treated as non-resident, causing a deemed distribution of retained profits and 20% corporate distribution tax. There is no statutory minimum capital or minimum employee requirement, but the registered address and real decision-making management in Estonia are decisive for tax treaty access.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Artificial Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Deemed loss of Estonian tax residence and taxation of worldwide assets as a deemed distribution at 20/80; administrative re-assessment plus penalty interest at 0.06% per day; fines up to EUR 32,000; revocation of the OÜ's registration if the registered address is found artificial

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors are jointly and severally liable for the re-assessed corporate tax when they deliberately misuse the corporate form; Estonian enforcement authorities may impose travel bans, freeze personal bank accounts, and pursue disqualification orders against board members in EU jurisdictions
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of the controlled price to the arm's length price; re-characterization of the excess margin as a taxable deemed distribution subject to 20/80 corporate income tax; additional surcharge of 50% of the unpaid tax in cases of gross negligence; penalty interest accrual from the original due date

Director personal liability: TP documentation preparers and directors may be held personally liable for the additional tax if they knowingly certified false functional or risk analyses; criminal price-fixing charges may arise if cross-border margin manipulation is tied to tax evasion
Tax Evasion and Payment-Rail AML Abuse

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax evasion under the Estonian Penal Code punishable by a fine up to EUR 12,500 and/or imprisonment up to five years; mandatory deregistration of the OÜ and cancellation of the e-Residency digital ID; confiscation of assets derived from undeclared sales

Director personal liability: Shareholder-directors bear personal criminal liability; managing directors are subject to pre-trial travel bans, passport surrender, and asset seizure; convictions may be transmitted across the EU through mutual recognition frameworks, blocking the director from holding corporate positions in any EU member state
