Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No mandatory minimum paid-up capital; an OÜ may be registered with zero share capital. If the subscribed capital is below €2,500, the registry marks the company as a low-capital OÜ, and restrictions on dividend distributions and shareholder loans apply until the capital is increased. There is no statutory escrow account; the subscribed funds are deposited in the company's own contribution bank account or an unnamed deposit account.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification: obtain a Fire Safety Use Permit (kasutusluba) from the Rescue Board after inspection of escape routes, fire extinguishers, and electrical installations. Structural load approval: the injection molding press and drying equipment must be certified with a civil engineering structure load assessment for the production floor. Occupational clearance: register the workplace with the Labour Inspectorate (Töönspektsioon), perform a statutory workplace risk assessment, and ensure compliance with the Occupational Health and Safety Act, including machine guarding, PPE, and annual staff training.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet, 'MTA'); primary digital portal is e-MTA at emta.ee, along with the Commercial Register / Company Registration Portal (ariregister.rik.ee) for entity codes and e-Residency onboarding; EORI number issuance is integrated into the MTA customs module.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity first obtains an Estonian registry code through the Commercial Register, using the e-Residency digital ID where applicable. For VAT purposes, the entity must submit a preliminary VAT registration application (form INF12, or a regular application under Section 193 of the Estonian VAT Act) through e-MTA; upon approval the MTA issues a VAT identification number in the format EE + 9 digits, a tax account number, and if required an EORI number. A non-resident entity engaged in taxable supplies in Estonia without a fixed establishment must register with the MTA before the first taxable supply unless the supply is covered by reverse charge or OSS/IOSS; processing time is normally five business days from a complete filing.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity first obtains an Estonian registry code through the Commercial Register, using the e-Residency digital ID where applicable. For VAT purposes, the entity must submit a preliminary VAT registration application (form INF12, or a regular application under Section 193 of the Estonian VAT Act) through e-MTA; upon approval the MTA issues a VAT identification number in the format EE + 9 digits, a tax account number, and if required an EORI number. A non-resident entity engaged in taxable supplies in Estonia without a fixed establishment must register with the MTA before the first taxable supply unless the supply is covered by reverse charge or OSS/IOSS; processing time is normally five business days from a complete filing.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Estonia does not operate a US-style check-the-box election; every legal entity is taxed at entity level under the Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus). Statutory elections available include: (i) voluntary VAT registration below the EUR 40,000 turnover threshold under Section 4(1) of the VAT Act; (ii) retroactive/one-off VAT registration with MTA consent under Section 193(4) of the VAT Act; (iii) group VAT registration for economically integrated entities under Section 8 of the VAT Act; (iv) the dividend distribution timing and dividend-in-kind elections under Section 50 of the Income Tax Act, including the regular 20% rate and the 14% reduced rate where certain conditions are met; and (v) hybrid mismatch disclaimers and CFC elections required under ATAD (EU Directive 2016/1164), filed via the e-MTA portal for arrangements with non-EU affiliates.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

22%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 40,000 annual taxable turnover for an Estonian-established taxable person; a non-resident entity without a permanent establishment in Estonia is required to register from the first taxable supply of goods or services in Estonia, with no turnover threshold, unless the supply is subject to reverse charge, OSS, or IOSS; voluntary registration is permitted once any taxable activity is commenced.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating at 0% VAT applies to supplies of goods dispatched or transported by the seller to destinations outside the customs territory of the European Union, provided the goods leave the EU within the statutory evidence window (generally 90 days from dispatch or before the VAT return deadline for the relevant period) and the seller holds documentary proof of export. Customs mechanical keyboard keycaps manufactured in Estonia and sold to non-EU retail or distribution customers qualify for zero-rating when the goods are physically exported under customs supervision. Related services directly linked to the export transport (international carriage, loading, handling) and services physically performed outside the EU are also zero-rated where the customer is established outside the EU.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD / EX-10 procedure lodged electronically) with electronic proof of exit (EX-1).
  • Transport documentation evidencing physical departure from EU customs territory: CMR road consignment note, Air Waybill, or Bill of Lading.
  • Commercial invoice and export sales contract showing INCOTERMS delivery terms.
  • Packing list with HS-classification breakdown of keycaps (HS 3926.90 plastic articles and HS 8473.30 keyboard parts).
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank SWIFT MT103 or bank confirmation evidencing receipt of export consideration in Euro or foreign currency).

Estonia mandates receipt and processing of structured electronic invoices (e-arve) for all operators in the Commercial Register since 2019, using the EU standard format (EN 16931, PEPPOL/UBL). There is no government-operated real-time fiscal clearance or tax-stamping system comparable to the Italian SDI; however, exporters and e-commerce sellers must transmit EC Sales Lists, Intrastat declarations, OSS/IOSS returns, and periodic VAT returns through the e-MTA portal or via MTA's REST API and X-Road data exchange. Cash-register operators with fiscal memory are subject to digital fiscal recording requirements for face-to-face sales. Electronic invoices must be archived for seven years and must be made available to MTA on request.

The MTA may retrospectively disallow zero-rating and assess output VAT at the standard rate together with statutory tax-default interest, calculated under the Taxation Act (MKS Section 104) as the six-month EURIBOR average plus a 7 percentage-point premium. Where export documentation is missing, forged, or not produced within the 90-day proof window, the exporter must repay the VAT refund or input credit previously claimed, plus interest. Deliberate misstatement or documentary falsification constitutes tax fraud under Sections 153 and 154 of the Estonian Penal Code, exposing a legal person to criminal fines up to EUR 105,000, confiscation of the refunded amounts, withdrawal of authorisation for simplifications (e.g., AEO status), and imprisonment for responsible individuals of up to five years. The standard retroactive assessment limitation period is five years under Section 107(4) of the Taxation Act, extending to ten years in aggravating circumstances involving artificial cross-border arrangements.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia has adopted the euro as its sole legal tender and maintains unrestricted current-account and capital-account convertibility pursuant to TFEU Articles 63-66 and the EU Capital Movements Directive. No exchange-control authorisation, FX conversion quota, or central-bank approval is required for profit remittance, dividend distribution, loan repayment, or capital repatriation by an OÜ. The only tax consequence of distributions is the Estonian profit-distribution tax at 20/80 (or 14/86 on regular qualifying distributions) of the gross dividend amount, which is not an FX control. Practical remittance hurdles are limited to bank-level documentary checks: shareholder resolution, filed annual report, beneficial-ownership declaration, and the recipient's valid tax-residency certificate.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. As a full Eurozone member with free capital movement, Estonia does not impose any statutory surrender, mandatory conversion, repatriation deadline, or retention ratio on export proceeds. USD-denominated settlements from Shopify Payments and Stripe may be held offshore, converted at free market rates, or redeployed at the OÜ's discretion. The only compliance duty is balance-of-payments reporting: any single or cumulative cross-border payment exceeding EUR 50,000 per month must be reported to Eesti Pank via the servicing bank using the statistical direct-investment/trade-in-goods codes; transfer-pricing documentation must support all intra-group export invoicing.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia), acting within the Eurosystem of the European Central Bank; prudential supervision of credit institutions is performed by Finantsinspektsioon (Estonian Financial Supervision Authority) and AML/CFT supervision by the Rahapesu Andmebüroo (Estonian Financial Intelligence Unit) under the Police and Border Guard Board.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

High as a supplementary settlement rail, with strict balance caps. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% on distributed profits (equivalent to 25% of the net dividend); 0% on retained/undistributed profits

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% at shareholder level (Estonia levies no dividend withholding tax; the distributing OÜ bears corporate income tax on the gross distribution at 20%, or 14% for qualifying regular distributions, equivalent to 25%/16.28% of the net dividend)

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

By the 10th day of the month following the calendar month in which the profit distribution was made (no separate annual CIT return; annual financial report to the Commercial Register is a separate obligation)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 50,000 aggregate related-party transactions per fiscal year generally; EUR 20,000 for transactions with related persons resident in low-tax jurisdictions; Country-by-Country Reporting applies where the group consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 750 million

Economic substance scope: Estonia does not operate a Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulation. However, Estonian tax residence and access to the 0% retained-profit regime require genuine local substance: physical or virtual board with real management in Estonia, local records, actual business function, and payroll. The Estonian GAAR (Income Tax Act § 1(2)), ATAD CFC rules and place-of-effective-management criteria deny benefits to artificial shell arrangements

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Misuse of Deferred CIT

Statutory sanction: Retroactive deemed distribution assessment, 20%/14% CIT on the hidden amount, daily penalty interest of 0.06%, criminal fines for the company, and possible forfeiture of assets under the Estonian Penal Code

Director personal liability: Directors jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax; travel bans and disqualification from management; imprisonment of up to 7 years for aggravated tax fraud where acts are intentional and large-scale
Shell Company / Artificial Tax Residence

Statutory sanction: Commercial Register strike-off or forced deletion; the company reclassified as non-resident or as a foreign PE of the beneficial owner; retroactive taxation of undistributed profits at 20%/14%; VAT registration revocation; administrative fines

Director personal liability: Director's veil-piercing for taxes and penalties due to fraudulent undercapitalization; travel restrictions during tax proceedings; criminal conviction for false business declaration up to 7 years' imprisonment
Transfer Pricing / Intercompany Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Arm's-length adjustment increasing taxable distributions; additional CIT of 20%/14% on the adjusted amount; penalty interest of 0.06% per day; separate documentation penalties of up to EUR 10,000; possible penalty tax up to 50% of the deficiency

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly approved non-arm's-length arrangements face civil restitution, disqualification, and criminal liability for tax fraud with imprisonment up to 3 years
AML / Payment Integration Abuse

Statutory sanction: AML/CFT fines for the company up to EUR 1.2 million; additional tax on undeclared deemed distributions at 20%/14%; asset forfeiture under money laundering statutes; exclusion from payment processing platforms

Director personal liability: Board members personally fined up to EUR 120,000 and imprisoned up to 7 years for money laundering; travel bans and restitution orders issued against personal assets
