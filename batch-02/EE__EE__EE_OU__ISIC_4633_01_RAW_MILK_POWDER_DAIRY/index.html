Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum share capital; an OÜ may be incorporated with EUR 0 capital under Commercial Code Section 140(2). If voluntary share capital is established below EUR 2,500, contributions must be made in money only, and cash contributions must be paid into the company's escrow or bank account prior to filing (or within the payment term stated in the Foundation Agreement, up to 10 years for delayed contributions). Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet, MTA); official digital processing portal: e-MTA (e-Maksuamet) at https://www.emta.ee. The MTA is the unified single-window national authority covering corporate income tax, VAT, customs declarations, Intrastat, social tax, and the VAT refund regimes for EU and third-country traders, and is the issuing authority for tax certificates, VAT numbers, excise authorisations, and e-MTA electronic signature credentials.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident establishing an Osaühing (private limited company, OÜ) receives its unique Estonian business and tax identification code (registrikood) automatically at compulsory Commercial Register (äriregistri) entry; this code is the entity's statutory TIN for all tax types. For VAT purposes, the OÜ (including a fully e-Residency-incorporated entity) must submit the VAT registration application (avaldus käibemaksukohustuslasena arvelevõtmiseks, e-MTA form KAT) electronically through e-MTA using ID-card, Mobile-ID, Smart-ID, or the e-Residency digital ID of a director or authorised signatory; mandatory filing must occur within 10 days after the calendar month in which taxable supplies exceed the €40,000 annual threshold, with registration effective from the statutory date set by law. On acceptance, the MTA issues a VAT certificate and an Estonian VAT number in the format EE + 9 digits (e.g., EE100123456). Voluntary early registration is expressly permitted and is strategically recommended for an export-oriented OÜ in order to zero-rate exports and to recover input VAT on procurement, cold-chain storage, and international freight. Foreign entrepreneurs without an Estonian establishment making only distance sales into Estonia register through the One-Stop-Shop (OSS) regime in their home Member State rather than obtaining a domestic Estonian VAT number.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation - Estonia operates a territorial, distribution-based corporate income tax under the Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus): undistributed and unrepatriated profits of an OÜ are subject to 0% corporate tax until distributed or deemed distributed; on ordinary dividend distributions, tax is imposed at 20% of the gross amount (the 20/80 rate applied to the net dividend), with a reduced 14/86 rate available only where the distribution stream satisfies the statutory continuity and holding-period conditions. No pass-through election is recognised for an OÜ; fiscally transparent treatment is confined to general partnerships (täisühing) and limited partnerships (usaldusühing) under the Commercial Code. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - Estonia operates a territorial, distribution-based corporate income tax under the Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus): undistributed and unrepatriated profits of an OÜ are subject to 0% corporate tax until distributed or deemed distributed; on ordinary dividend distributions, tax is imposed at 20% of the gross amount (the 20/80 rate applied to the net dividend), with a reduced 14/86 rate available only where the distribution stream satisfies the statutory continuity and holding-period conditions. No pass-through election is recognised for an OÜ; fiscally transparent treatment is confined to general partnerships (täisühing) and limited partnerships (usaldusühing) under the Commercial Code. The OÜ remains an Estonian tax resident on the basis of incorporation and, where central management is exercised in Estonia, on place-of-management principles.

Available election forms

The OÜ may elect, at the level of each dividend decision, between ordinary dividends taxed at 20/80 (20% tax on gross / 25% on net) and reduced-rate regular dividends taxed at 14/86 to the extent the distribution is sourced from profits already subjected to the distribution-layer corporate tax, as defined under the Income Tax Act continuity rules; no advance ruling is required beyond satisfactory documentary substantiation. Estonia does not operate an IRS-style check-the-box or any fiscal-transparency election for limited companies; entity-level classification is conclusive and cannot be varied by contract. Automatic relief is available under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive (0% domestic withholding on dividends paid to qualifying EU/EEA corporate shareholders holding at least 10%), and the hybrid mismatch rules transposing ATAD operate through the Income Tax Act to disallow deductions on hybrid instruments, recharacterise hybrid entity payments, and override any foreign entity classification that would otherwise create a deduction without a corresponding taxable income inclusion.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia is a eurozone member; the euro (EUR) is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions. There are no exchange controls or restrictions on profit remittance, dividend distribution, or repatriation of capital for non-resident investors. Estonia adheres to EU free movement of capital provisions. The only formalities are standard reporting to the Tax and Customs Board (MTA) for statistical purposes and compliance with anti-money laundering (AML) regulations for large cash or suspicious transactions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Estonia imposes no mandatory surrender or conversion of export proceeds. Exporters may retain all foreign currency earnings in any bank account, domestic or foreign, without any statutory timeline for conversion. However, for accounting and tax purposes, transactions must be recorded in EUR using the official exchange rate of the European Central Bank (ECB) on the transaction date. There is no requirement to convert export receipts within a specific period.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia) and the Estonian Financial Supervision Authority (Finantsinspektsioon) for banking and AML oversight

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium. Estonia's e-Residency program allows non-residents to open accounts with licensed fintech EMIs (e.g., Holvi, Monese, or specialized B2B platforms like Payoneer or Airwallex) for cross-border payments. However, for a dairy commodity trading entity with high transaction volumes and LC requirements, traditional banks are preferred due to their robust trade finance infrastructure. EMIs may lack full SWIFT correspondent banking capabilities for LC issuance and may impose lower transaction limits. Legal constraints: EMIs must hold an EU e-money license (e.g., from the FCA or BaFin) and are subject to Estonian AML supervision if they operate via a branch or agent. They cannot provide credit or trade finance products. For this entity, EMIs are viable for ancillary payments but not as primary treasury rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For equity injection or shareholder loans from non-resident investors, use SWIFT MT103 wire transfers with purpose code 'G' (for direct investment) or 'L' (for loans) as per the ECB's Balance of Payments (BoP) classification. The receiving bank will require a copy of the share subscription agreement or loan agreement. For capital escrow accounts, the bank may require a notarized resolution from the board. All inbound transfers must be reported to the MTA for statistical purposes if they exceed EUR 10,000. The bank will verify the source of funds and may request the underlying contract.For trade settlements (e.g., payment to milk powder suppliers), use SWIFT MT103 for telegraphic transfers (TT) or commercial letters of credit (LC) via SWIFT MT700. For LC transactions, the bank will require the underlying sales contract, proforma invoice, and shipping documents. For TT payments, the bank will require the commercial invoice and proof of goods (e.g., bill of lading). All outbound payments must be accompanied by a purpose code (e.g., 'P' for goods trade) and may be subject to AML review if the amount exceeds EUR 10,000. The bank may require a copy of the import/export declaration from the customs authority.FX Forwards (deliverable) for EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, and other major currencies, with a tenor up to 12 months, available from major Estonian banks (Swedbank, SEB, LHV) for corporate clients with a credit line.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) for exotic currencies (e.g., RUB, CNY) if the counterparty is in a restricted market; these are available via international banks with a presence in Estonia, but require a special approval.Currency Swaps (cross-currency interest rate swaps) for long-term hedging of both principal and interest, typically used for larger trade finance facilities; available from tier-1 banks with a strong credit relationship.
