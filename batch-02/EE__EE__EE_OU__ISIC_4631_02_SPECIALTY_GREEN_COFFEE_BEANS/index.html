Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet, MTA) - official portal: e-MTA (https://e-mta.ee) which is the single digital gateway for VAT registration, tax declarations, customs declarations and e-invoices; the e-Commercial Register (Äriregister, https://ariregister.rik.ee) is the nominally competent authority for incorporation and issue of the statutory registry code.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Incorporate the Osaühing (OÜ) in the e-Commercial Register; the company receives an 8-digit national registry code (registrikood) which serves simultaneously as the statutory TIN for all Estonian tax purposes. Step 2 - Register as a VAT taxable person with MTA via e-MTA (Form KPR / online e-service) when annual taxable domestic turnover reaches the statutory threshold or, on a voluntary basis, earlier; a non-resident entity with no Estonian seat must obtain VAT registration before making the first taxable supply in Estonia and will receive a VAT number in the format EE + 9 digits (registry code plus control digit). Step 3 - Where customs operations occur on imported or exported green coffee, obtain an EORI number through e-MTA and, if documentary letters of credit are used, ensure the EORI is linked to the OÜ's e-MTA account. Step 4 - No separate corporate income tax registration is required: the OÜ is automatically within the Estonian distributed-profit corporate income tax regime, and annual tax computations (Form TSD with annexes) are filed electronically even where no distributions occur in a given tax year.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation - the Estonian resident OÜ is a separate limited-liability legal person taxed under the Estonian distributed-profit model: 0% corporate income tax on retained and reinvested profits; upon distribution of profits by way of dividends, deemed benefits or privileged distributions, corporate income tax of 20% is payable by the company on the grossed-up amount (20/80 of the net dividend). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the Estonian resident OÜ is a separate limited-liability legal person taxed under the Estonian distributed-profit model: 0% corporate income tax on retained and reinvested profits; upon distribution of profits by way of dividends, deemed benefits or privileged distributions, corporate income tax of 20% is payable by the company on the grossed-up amount (20/80 of the net dividend). Foreign shareholders are generally exempt from dividend withholding under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive or treaty relief; the same 20% entity-level tax applies to distributions from an Estonian permanent establishment of a non-resident company.

Available election forms

Estonia has no US-style check-the-box election; the characterisation of an OÜ as a corporate tax subject follows conclusively from commercial law. The relevant statutory fiscal elections and special mechanisms are: (1) the former reduced 14% corporate income tax rate on regular profit distributions was abolished with effect from 1 January 2025, leaving a uniform 20% rate on all distributions; (2) an eligible non-resident company may elect to treat profits attributed to its Estonian permanent establishment as distributed and taxed in Estonia in order to obtain treaty credit protection; (3) reorganisations within the scope of the EU Merger Directive may be executed tax-neutrally without prior approval; (4) Estonian domestic law incorporates ATAD hybrid-mismatch rules in force since 2019 which automatically neutralise deduction/non-inclusion mismatches arising from hybrid financial instruments, hybrid entities or dual-resident structures; no elective opt-in exists for these anti-hybrid provisions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

22%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 50,000 of annual taxable domestic turnover (threshold raised from EUR 40,000 with effect from 1 January 2025); voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold; non-resident persons supplying taxable goods or services in Estonia are subject to compulsory registration without local threshold before commencing supplies; distance sales of goods and electronic/digital services may use the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) to avoid domestic registration; import VAT on inbound green coffee is payable at the customs border and may be declared via the import VAT credit procedure available to registered importers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supply of specialty green coffee beans from Estonia to a destination outside the European Union qualifies for the 0% VAT export rate provided that: (i) the goods are physically consigned to a third-country buyer and leave Estonia no later than 180 days after the time of supply; (ii) the supply is evidenced by a customs export declaration accepted in the EU Export/EXT system with an MRN and an exit confirmation from the border office; (iii) transport documents (bill of lading, CMR or air waybill) are issued in the name of the Estonian exporter and show the ultimate third-country destination; (iv) the foreign buyer is identified and the sale consideration is received in the exporter's bank account within the statutory 180-day repatriation period; (v) the tax invoice clearly indicates the zero export rate and the legal basis. Intra-Community dispatches to an EU VAT-registered counterparty are exempt under the intra-Community supply rules subject to production of the buyer's valid EU VAT number and EC Sales List reporting, which is not an export-zero-rating scenario but requires equivalent documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/EE-EXT) containing the MRN, commodity code 0901.11, net weight of coffee and declared export value
  • Exit confirmation or equivalent 'goods departed' message from the EU Export/ICS system evidencing physical departure from Estonia
  • Bill of Lading (ocean), CMR (road) or Air Waybill naming the Estonian exporter as consignor and the third-country consignee
  • Commercial invoice stating 0% export VAT, Incoterms and coffee lot identification
  • Packing list, container seal records and the green coffee lot certificate / phytosanitary certificate required by the destination country
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 credit confirmation proving receipt of foreign currency proceeds in the exporter's bank account within the 180-day repatriation window

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance chain is in force in Estonia (there is no national digital platform equivalent to Italy's Sistema di Interscambio or Spain's SII). MTA mandates electronic filing of the VAT return (Form KMD) and the safety/sales lists through the e-MTA portal by the 20th day of the month following the tax period, using monthly or quarterly frequency according to turnover. B2B invoices relating to coffee export must comply with Articles 80-94 of the Estonian VAT Act; structured e-invoices (e-Arve, Peppol BIS, UBL 2.1, Factur-X) are widely accepted; invoices must be issued no later than the 15th day of the month following the month of supply; no digital stamping or government seal is required.

MTA subjects coffee exporters to targeted documentary audits and data cross-checks between the customs export system, e-invoices and banking receipts. If the export evidence bundle is incomplete, the goods have not left Estonia within 180 days, or foreign proceeds are not repatriated within the 180-day deadline, MTA retroactively recharacterises the supply as taxable at the standard 22% VAT rate and assesses the VAT due together with statutory late-payment interest of 0.06% per day (approximately 21.9% per annum) from the original due date. Under section 99 of the General Part of the Tax Code the ordinary reassessment period is three years, extended to seven years where intentional concealment or tax fraud is established. Deliberately false export declarations or fraudulent VAT refund claims constitute a criminal tax offence under sections 347-349 of the Penal Code, punishable by up to five years' imprisonment and fines of up to EUR 27,200 for natural persons or up to EUR 1,600,000 for legal persons; ancillary consequences include director disqualification, suspension or blocking of VAT refund entitlements, revocation of AEO/export authorisation and, in the case of customs violations, additional fines of up to EUR 32,000 under the Customs Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia is a full euro-zone member with no exchange-control restrictions. Both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible in EUR and freely convertible foreign currencies. Profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is not subject to FX approval or an Estonian bank-level withholding process; an OÜ distributes dividends as gross amounts, with the corporate income tax accounted for on the distributing company under the Estonian Income Tax Act. Practical clearance hurdles are limited to bank KYC/AML verification, EU and international sanctions screening, and the delivery of valid tax residence documentation by the foreign shareholder.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender rule and no mandatory conversion ratio. Export and trade receivables may be held in EUR or any foreign currency without a deadline. For balance-of-payments statistics, resident companies report cross-border transactions using the official BPM6 and EU coding framework. For green coffee imports, the bank may request the bill of lading, customs import declaration and commercial contract to verify that the inbound/outbound wires correspond to genuine goods movement.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia) as the euro-area central bank and national competent authority for monetary stability and balance-of-payments reporting; foreign exchange administration is liberalised under Article 63 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and Regulation (EC) No 184/2005; prudential supervision of credit institutions is exercised by Eesti Pank together with the Estonian Financial Supervision and Resolution Authority (Finantsinspektsioon).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium. Authorised EU/EEA e-money institutions such as Wise, Payoneer or Bankera can provide IBAN accounts, low-cost EUR conversion and faster cross-border transfers, but they are not appropriate as the sole clearing hub for high-value green coffee container disbursements or for the issuance of documentary letters of credit. A full domestic commercial bank is required for L/C confirmation, trade-document checks, guarantees and correspondent-banking liquidity. Non-EEA EMIs are excluded unless they hold a valid Estonian e-money licence or an EU/EEA passport under the E-Money Directive 2009/110/EC.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder equity injections into the OÜ's Estonian bank account are executed by SEPA Credit Transfer in EUR or SWIFT MT103 in USD or third-country currencies. The reference field must identify the capital nature of the transfer using a clear statement such as 'DIRECT INVESTMENT — CAPITAL INCREASE' plus the OÜ registry code. The bank verifies the notarised e-Business Register board resolution, the shareholder register and the ultimate beneficial ownership declaration. A segregated Capital Escrow Account is recommended so that funds intended for trade-finance collateral are not miscoded as operating revenue. Source-of-wealth documentation and passport identification are required for larger injections.For imported specialty green coffee, outbound disbursements are settled primarily through the SWIFT correspondent-banking network. Documentary letters of credit are opened via MT700 through the Estonian LC-issuing bank, amended via MT707 and settled at maturity through MT103 or MT202. The presentation bundle must include original bill of lading, commercial invoice denominated in USD, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin and a weight/quality certificate. For open-account batches, direct SWIFT MT103 remittances are made with purpose code 'GOODS TRADE — IMPORT' referencing the contract number.
