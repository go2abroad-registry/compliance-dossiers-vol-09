Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet, MTA) — online portal e-MTA at emta.ee

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity that is obliged to register for VAT in Estonia must submit an electronic application via e-MTA. Non-EU residents appoint a fiscal representative unless an exemption applies. The application requires proof of incorporation, director identity, description of Estonian taxable activities, and expected turnover. Upon approval, MTA issues an Estonian VAT registration number (KMKR) and a business registry code when applicable. For corporate income tax, foreign companies without a permanent establishment are taxed by withholding at source and do not require a general CIT registration; a permanent establishment or taxable presence triggers a registration obligation with the Estonian Commercial Register and tax registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity that is obliged to register for VAT in Estonia must submit an electronic application via e-MTA. Non-EU residents appoint a fiscal representative unless an exemption applies. The application requires proof of incorporation, director identity, description of Estonian taxable activities, and expected turnover. Upon approval, MTA issues an Estonian VAT registration number (KMKR) and a business registry code when applicable. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Estonia does not operate a US-style check-the-box regime. Available elections include the VAT grouping election under the Value Added Tax Act and the election for a non-resident to apply for full VAT registration rather than operating through a fiscal representative. Under EU Anti-Tax Avoidance Directive implementation, hybrid mismatch adjustment elections apply to structured arrangements; no general elective entity classification is permitted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

22%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

€40,000 annual taxable turnover (VAT Act §3); non-resident suppliers of goods or services physically supplied in Estonia are generally required to register without a turnover threshold if the place of supply is Estonia.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero percent VAT applies to supplies of goods dispatched or transported by the supplier or the customer to a destination outside the European Union, where the supplier retains contemporaneous commercial and transport evidence. Intra-Community dispatches to another EU Member State also qualify for zero rating provided the purchaser's EU VAT identification number is obtained and recorded, the goods leave Estonia within required time limits, and a compliant EC Sales List is submitted. Plush toys OEM manufacturers may claim zero rating when the goods are directly exported or dispatched for installation in a third country. Offshore services (e.g., subcontracting production services to non-EU clients) are zero-rated if the customer is a taxable person in a non-EU jurisdiction and the place of supply rules under the VAT Act support the destination principle.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document SAD / ESS electronic export declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill/CMR transport document evidencing dispatch to third country
  • Commercial Invoice or pro-forma invoice correlating to the export declaration
  • Proof of payment or other commercial evidence confirming remuneration for the exported goods
  • Customs release / exit confirmation message from ECS (Export Control System)

Estonia mandates electronic filing of VAT returns, customs declarations and statistical reports through e-MTA, but does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping system. Public sector bodies must receive invoices as structured PEPPOL e-invoices; B2B and B2C e-invoicing remains optional except where the invoice is addressed to a public-sector buyer. No government real-time clearance API or fiscal printer reporting is in force for standard VAT transactions.

Claims for export zero rating are subject to documentary audits by MTA; failure to produce admissible transport or customs evidence within the audit period triggers VAT re-characterisation as a domestic taxable supply, payment of 22% VAT, arrears interest calculated from the due date, and a penalty that may reach 10% of the understated tax. Fraudulent or deliberate zero-rating claims constitute tax fraud under the Penal Code (Karistusseadustik), punishable by imprisonment up to five years and confiscatory fines up to €1,000,000 in corporate cases. Retrospective clawback extends five years from the end of the tax period for negligent misdeclaration and indefinitely in cases of intentional concealment.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full capital account convertibility and current account convertibility; unrestricted profit remittances and dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders with no capital controls or prior approval requirements

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender of export proceeds; companies may retain foreign currency earnings or convert freely to EUR at prevailing market rates without statutory timelines or conversion ratios

Central bank supervision: Bank of Estonia (Eesti Pank), operating within the Eurosystem and under the oversight of the European Central Bank
