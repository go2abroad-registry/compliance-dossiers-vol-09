Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The UG may be formed with share capital below EUR 25,000, but the statutory minimum nominal capital is EUR 1 and the cash contribution must be fully paid and freely available to management before registration. No formal escrow account is prescribed; the managing director must ensure the capital is at the company's unrestricted disposal and the bank deposit confirmation is submitted with the Handelsregister application. If share capital reaches EUR 25,000, ordinary GmbH rules apply.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification for the leased office must be confirmed under the local state building code; escape routes, emergency lighting, and fire extinguishers must comply with Arbeitsstättenverordnung. No structural load inspection is required for ordinary commercial office use unless the building permit imposes one. Electrical equipment used by staff must be inspected under DGUV V3, and the employer must complete a workplace risk assessment and register employees with the statutory accident insurance body.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

GmbH and UG are subject to entity-level corporate income tax (Körperschaftsteuer) and trade tax (Gewerbesteuer); no US-style check-the-box election exists. Available elections include: Bulgarian/USt small business exemption under §19 UStG (turnover thresholds), option for tax liability on real estate transfers under §9 UStG, intra-group VAT unity election (Organschaft) under §2 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UStG, and compliance with hybrid mismatch rules under § 4k EStG / § 8b KStG (ATAD implementation), limiting mismatch-bloc arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 25,000 (small-business exemption threshold under §19 UStG for domestic resident enterprises; non-resident suppliers have mandatory VAT registration has no turnover threshold — any B2B or B2C supply to Germany with place of supply in Germany requires VAT registration)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods to non-EU destinations (§4 No.1 letter a, §6 UStG) and intra-Community supplies to EU VAT-registered recipients (§4 No.1 letter b, §6a UStG) are zero-rated. Cross-border B2B services (including data analytics SaaS) fall under B2B place-of-supply rules, where the service is subject to reverse charge in the recipient's country rather than German VAT; zero-rating is available only if the recipient is a business established outside Germany and the service is for non-business purposes (limited). Proof of export or cross-border service provision must be substantiated by documentary evidence as required under §§8–9 UStDV.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Zollausfuhranmeldung) with EBZ number
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical dispatch from Germany to non-EU territory
  • Commercial Invoice and delivery note with Incoterms
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank transfer confirmation) proving payment from foreign customer
  • Contract for export of digital services with recipient's business profile and VAT evidence

B2B e-invoicing mandate effective 1 January 2025: all resident taxable persons must receive XML-format e-invoices (XRechnung or ZUGFeRD/Factur-X) electronically, with full issuance mandate phased for 2027 onwards for larger taxpayers and 2028 for smaller ones; no continuous transaction control/clearance or digital stamping API; returns and payments are submitted via ELSTER.

Export zero-rating is subject to stringent documentary retention (10 years) and can be clawed back via corrected VAT assessments if the exporter fails to produce conclusive proof within the legal audit window. Penalties for fraudulent zero-rating claims under §370 AO extend to imprisonment of up to 10 years, monetary fines, interest at 6% per annum (§233a AO), and the reversal of VAT exemption with late-payment surcharges. On-site procedures include Umsatzsteuer-Sonderprüfung and unannounced Umsatzsteuer-Nachschau, with retrospective corrections under §27 UStG and statute of limitations of up to 10 years for VAT fraud cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany and the Eurozone operate full current- and capital-account convertibility under Art. 63 TFEU and the German Foreign Trade and Payments Act (Außenwirtschaftsgesetz, AWG), without approval gates, exchange quotas, or forced holdings in EUR. Profit and dividend remittance from the UG to non-resident shareholders is free of exchange-control authorization; the practical hurdles are fiscal: distributions bear German withholding tax of 25% plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge (aggregate 26.375%), reducible under the US-German tax treaty, EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive, or a BaFin-certified withholding-relief certificate; management fees, royalties and interest payments are likewise freely transferable and are exempt from statutory withholding, subject to anti-abuse and treaty-shopping tests. The sole surviving administrative control is the AWV statistical reporting duty for each cross-border payment or receivable equal to or exceeding EUR 12,500.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany imposes no surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no retention window for export or SaaS-license receipts. All USD and third-currency proceeds of the SEO analytics tool may be held offshore, retained indefinitely, or liquidated at market rates. Only the AWV information return applies: receipts from non-residents above EUR 12,500 must be notified to the Deutsche Bundesbank with the correct statistical purpose code, either directly by the UG or through the collecting bank, and must be supported by underlying invoice and contract evidence retained for six years.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (the national central bank of Germany) as the monetary and foreign-exchange statistics authority, in conjunction with the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin) for bank licensing and supervision and the Federal Office for Economic Affairs and Export Control (BAFA) for foreign-trade control; cross-border payment reporting (AWV statistics) is exclusively filed with the Deutsche Bundesbank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Revolut Fit: High

High as a secondary settlement layer; medium as principal treasury channel. BaFin-licensed and EEA-passported electronic money institutions (Penta, Qonto, Revolut Business) can open remote IBAN accounts for a UG, but mainstream EMIs generally require German economic substance and often decline fully foreign-owned single-shareholder UGs. EMIs further lack direct ECB TARGET2 participation, issue no bank-grade Einzahlungsbestätigung for commercial register filing, and are not AWV-reporting intermediaries, shifting the reporting burden back to the taxpayer. The blueprint thus uses EMIs only for receivables concentration under the sponsor bank sweeping liquidity framework.
