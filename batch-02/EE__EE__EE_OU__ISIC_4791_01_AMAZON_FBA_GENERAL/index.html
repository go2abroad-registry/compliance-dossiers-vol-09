Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Maksu- ja Tolliamet (Estonian Tax and Customs Board); e-MTA portal (emta.ee/e-mta); company registration through e-äriregister (ariregister.rik.ee)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned OÜ is incorporated in e-äriregister and obtains an Estonian business registry code (Registrikood), which functions as the corporate TIN. VAT registration is then effected via e-MTA; registration is mandatory for an established taxable person when taxable turnover reaches EUR 40,000 in a calendar year, and for non-established taxable persons upon any taxable supply in Estonia unless the reverse charge or distance-sales regime otherwise applies. The Tax and Customs Board assigns a KMKR number (EE VAT ID) for EU cross-border operations and e-Toll/customs declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — Estonian OÜ is taxed at entity level on profit distributions rather than on accrued annual profits; undistributed profits are exempt from income tax. A distribution-based territorial scope applies, with participation-exemption relief for qualifying foreign dividends and capital gains and ATAD-aligned CFC rules.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election is available under Estonian corporate income tax. Available statutory electives include VAT grouping (forming a single taxable person with other Estonian-established persons), the reduced 14/86 regular-dividend rate election for recurring distributions from retained profits, and application of the participation exemption. Hybrid mismatch adjustments apply in accordance with EU ATAD implementing provisions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 40,000 annual taxable turnover for resident taxable persons; no threshold for non-resident taxable persons supplying taxable goods or services in Estonia; EU-wide EUR 10,000 distance-sales threshold for intra-EU distance sales of goods and electronically supplied services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate applies to goods physically dispatched or transported by the OÜ or on its behalf to destinations outside the European Union (third countries/territories), provided the export takes place within the statutory period and is evidenced by a customs export declaration in the exporter's records. Intra-EU B2B supplies to a taxable person with a valid VAT ID are also zero-rated if dispatch to another Member State is substantiated. Sales of goods located in Estonia to EU consumers through Amazon Marketplace are distance sales and are not export zero-rated unless an applicable threshold or special scheme reallocates the place of supply.

  • Customs export declaration (EXS/SAD) lodged via e-Toll
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or CMR road consignment note
  • Commercial invoice
  • Proof of physical departure from EU (customs exit confirmation or cargo manifest)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/bank statement evidencing receipt of payment in EUR
  • Amazon Seller Central export/order reports and payment settlement statements

No mandatory real-time invoice clearance, digital stamping, or per-invoice API filing obligation exists in Estonia. E-invoicing in the national e-arve format or European EN 16931 (Peppol) standards is legally accepted and encouraged. VAT returns (KMD), EC Sales Lists (KMD INF), Intrastat declarations and customs declarations are submitted through e-MTA/e-Toll in aggregated periodic filings, not through a transaction-level government clearance system.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to documentary inspection by Maksu- ja Tolliamet. If the required export proof is missing or insufficient, VAT is clawed back at 20% with statutory default interest (0.06% per day from the due date), and administrative fines up to EUR 1,300 may be imposed. Intentional fraudulent zero-rating can trigger criminal tax fraud liability under the Estonian Penal Code and extension of the standard three-year assessment period to six years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies. Estonia is a euro-area Member State, an OECD member, and an EU Member State; both current and capital account transactions are liberalised under Articles 63-65 TFEU and subsidiary Estonian banking and market legislation. There are no domestic approvals, quotas, licenses, or surrender requirements for conversion of EUR, USD, GBP, or other freely tradable currencies. Profit remittance is unrestricted from an FX perspective; dividend distribution is subject only to Estonian corporate income tax rules, not to central-bank authorisation. Financial sanctions under binding EU CFSP decisions and UN embargoes are the only practical blocking framework; they operate separately from FX convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Estonia imposes no statutory timeline for repatriation of export proceeds, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no requirement to sell export currencies for EUR. Cross-border payment statistics may be collected by Eesti Pank through commercial banks for balance-of-payments purposes, but this is purely statistical and does not create an obligation to surrender or convert export receipts. Foreign-currency balances may be held indefinitely.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia) — National Central Bank of the Republic of Estonia; monetary and foreign-exchange reserve functions are delegated to the European Central Bank within the Eurosystem. There is no separate foreign-exchange control administration.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For compliant equity injection, the non-resident shareholder wires EUR through SWIFT MT103 or SEPA Credit Transfer directly to the OÜ’s dedicated Estonian IBAN. The commercial bank must see a clear payment purpose code — typically ISO 20022 ‘EQUI’ for equity or the bank-specific code ‘S010’ for direct investment/share-capital movements — and a narration stating ‘[Shareholder name] — OÜ share capital contribution’ or ‘equity injection into [OÜ registry code]’. There is no Eesti Pank approval or authorisation gate for such an injection, but the receiving bank performs sanctions screening and AML/KYC checks. For amounts exceeding approximately €10,000, the bank should be notified in advance and may require a board resolution, updated shareholder register, UBO declaration, and evidence of the shareholder’s source of funds. The equity leg must never be routed through a merchant-settlement account or a treasury account used for Amazon payouts.For FBA supplier payments, the standard clearing rails are SWIFT MT103 for non-SEPA beneficiaries, SEPA Credit Transfers for EU/EEA suppliers, and SEPA Instant Credit Transfers for urgent intra-EU payments where the beneficiary bank supports SCT Inst. Payment narratives must include the supplier invoice number and the purpose code ‘TRAD’ (trade settlement) or ‘PURP’ (recurring procurement) as appropriate. Commercial letters of credit are permissible and are offered by Estonian banks, but they are operationally disproportionate for high-volume, low-value Amazon FBA parcel trade; they should be reserved for bulk wholesale purchase orders from non-EU suppliers. For Estonian VAT payments to the Estonian Tax and Customs Board (MTA), the OÜ should use a domesticated SEPA Credit Transfer or the MTA’s dedicated payment service with the official reference code, never mixing VAT payments with commercial disbursements. Amazon marketplace payouts arrive as automated credits from Amazon’s payment entities and may be denominated in EUR, USD, GBP, or other currencies; household FX conversion and sweeps should be executed through the same regulated banking or fintech partner to preserve a clean audit trail.Outright deliverable FX forwards (EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, EUR/PLN, EUR/SEK) with a licensed credit institution or regulated EU/EEA fintech, executed under an ISDA 2002 or standard FX Master Agreement; the forward must be linked to a firm FBA settlement or supplier-payment exposure and not used for speculative positions.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) only where the underlying currency is non-convertible or cannot be settled through a local deliverable market in the relevant jurisdiction; for freely convertible EUR, USD, and GBP crosses, NDFs are not the appropriate instrument and may be rejected by Estonian banks as speculative in nature.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) for medium-term balance-sheet hedging of non-EUR intercompany debt or shareholder loans, subject to EMIR reporting/clearing thresholds where applicable and MiFID II suitability assessment by the investment firm.Plain-vanilla FX options and option collars for hedging forecast FBA revenue or supplier payables in USD/GBP; trades must be entered with an FICC dealer under the ISDA documentation suite, material risks disclosed to management, and premiums recorded as treasury expenses in the OÜ’s audited financial records.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium-High for revenue settlement and treasury operations; not a substitute for a fully regulated Estonian payment account in all tax, licensing, and banking workflows. Wise Business, Airwallex, Payoneer, Revolut Business, and Stripe may be used where they hold a valid EU/EEA payment-service or electronic-money institution licence and exercise passporting into Estonia; their IBANs are often foreign (Lithuanian, Belgian, UK, or other EEA) but are legally usable for FBA marketplace payout consolidation. Legal constraints: the OÜ must remain fully registered under the Estonian registry and maintain Estonian tax substance; a foreign EMI does not exempt the OÜ from Estonian AML, VAT, or accounting obligations; Estonian banks may treat EMI top-up flows as high-risk and request the entire source-of-funds trail. To avoid blockers, use segregated client-money accounts, never commingle personal expenses, and preserve the Amazon settlement statements supporting each EMI credit.
