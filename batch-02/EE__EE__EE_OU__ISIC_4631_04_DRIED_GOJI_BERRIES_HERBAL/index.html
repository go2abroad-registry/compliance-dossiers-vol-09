Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Minimum statutory share capital of an Osaühing is EUR 1 (one euro). Where share capital is below EUR 2,500, the full capital must be paid in cash before submission of the registration application; the funds must be held, unimpeded, on a bank or notary escrow account established for the OÜ pursuant to Commercial Code § 136(4). In-kind contribution is not permitted below the EUR 2,500 threshold.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate issued by the Rescue Board (Päästeamet) for the warehouse and repacking premises, including fire class conformity, evacuation plan and smoke detection systems; building use permit (kasutusluba) issued by the local municipal building control department confirming structural load of racks and storage shelves; and occupational health clearance by the Labour Inspectorate (Tööinspektsioon) covering workplace risk assessment, hygiene segregation of foodstuffs from non-food materials, lighting, ventilation, and occupational health surveillance scheduling.

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum statutory share capital of an Osaühing is EUR 1 (one euro). Where share capital is below EUR 2,500, the full capital must be paid in cash before submission of the registration application; the funds must be held, unimpeded, on a bank or notary escrow account established for the OÜ pursuant to Commercial Code § 136(4). In-kind contribution is not permitted below the EUR 2,500 threshold.

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

Non-resident commercial entities that carry out taxable activities in Estonia must register for Estonian VAT/TIN. Obtain a missing registration code from the Commercial Register (for a branch) or, if no Estonian presence, request a special non-resident VAT operator number in e-MTA. Submit the standard VAT registration form via the self-service portal after completing the hierarchical data control list; TIN for tax purposes is the Estonian registry code (registrikood) and VAT number is EE+9 digits.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box election for Osaühing; the corporate territorial system applies mandatorily (profits taxed only upon distribution); VAT grouping election available under VAT Act; ATAD hybrid mismatch and interest limitation rules apply; calendar-year tax period is fixed with no alternative fiscal-year election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

€40,000 per calendar year for established taxable persons; non-established non-resident suppliers generally must register before making any taxable supply in Estonia, unless reverse charge applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of dried Lycium (goji) berries and herbal botanicals to a destination outside the EU, including the surrounding customs territory, is exempt with the right to deduct input VAT (effective 0% VAT) when evidence shows dispatch/transport out of the EU within the statutory period (generally 3 months from dispatch, or 12 months under certain transport conditions) and the exporter retains the export documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/EX8/electronic EMS)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR transport evidence
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/TT transfer evidence
  • Proof of physical export: customs-verified departure proof ('A' stamp or electronic equivalent)
  • Sales invoice and contract specifying the non-EU destination buyer

No real-time fiscal clearance / digital stamping; mandatory structured e-invoicing in B2G transactions; VAT returns and registrations are filed electronically via e-MTA with API/X-Road integration; invoices must be issued in a format that ensures authenticity and integrity (receipt by the tax administration is not required).

Export zero-rating claims are subject to Tax and Customs Board (MTA) national and EU analytical risk checks; lack of documentary proof triggers reversal of the zero rate, resulting in a supplementary VAT assessment plus default interest (0.03% per day) and penalty up to 40% of the underpaid tax in case of gross negligence; intentional fraudulent documents expose the taxpayer and board to criminal sanctions under the Penal Code (fine up to €45,000 or imprisonment up to 5 years) and extension of the statute of limitation to 6 years after discovery.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility as a euro-area member and OECD member. There are no Estonia-specific exchange controls, no approval or currency budget for imports, exports, distributions, or third-country capital movements, and no distinction between residents and non-residents for euro conversion. Profit remittance is not subject to FX approval but is subject to the Estonian distributed-profit corporate income tax; distributions must be filed with the Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet), and withholding may apply to non-resident shareholders unless reduced by an applicable tax treaty or the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Estonian law does not impose mandatory export-proceeds surrender, minimum conversion ratios, or time limits for repatriation of export receipts. Export proceeds may be held in EUR or foreign currency, provided the flows are reflected in the OÜ's accounting records and reported to Eesti Pank through the resident credit institution for balance-of-payments statistical purposes using the relevant goods and direct-investment transaction codes.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia), the central bank of the Republic of Estonia and member of the Eurosystem; since 1 January 2025 the Estonian Financial Supervision Authority (Finantsinspektsioon) has been consolidated within Eesti Pank, making Eesti Pank the single financial supervision authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a complementary payment rail, Low as the sole treasury platform. Estonian-licensed e-money institutions and payment institutions, and EU e-money institutions passporting into Estonia, provide remote onboarding, multi-currency IBANs and inexpensive FX conversion; however, they are legally prohibited from taking deposits or granting credit, must safeguard client funds, and are not a substitute for a Tier-1 bank for large agricultural export flows. Such providers may restrict high-volume commodity settlements without a bank guarantee or an established wholesale trade relationship.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing is a SWIFT MT103 wire from the sole corporate shareholder, never cash and never third-party paper, to the OÜ's designated EUR account in an Estonian Tier-1 bank. The remittance must state the payment purpose as a non-resident direct investment capital contribution and reference the notarized shareholder resolution; the EUR amount is credited immediately without FX conversion. Payments in USD or CNY should be converted at the receiving bank's FX desk only after a source-of-funds questionnaire has been approved. Eesti Pank balance-of-payments statistics are transmitted automatically by the bank under the direct investment equity capital code. The client must preserve the wire advice, shareholder resolution, UBO declaration, and ledger entry evidencing the contribution for the annual report.Primary clearing rail for EUR-denominated settlements is SEPA Credit Transfer; for high-value and third-country settlements the rail is SWIFT MT103/MT202. For initial or high-value goji berry export lots, a confirmed irrevocable documentary letter of credit issued via SWIFT MT700 under ICC UCP 600 is preferred; the beneficiary must present compliant documents, including invoices, phytosanitary certificates, bills of lading, certificates of origin, and insurance certificates, with proceeds credited to the OÜ settlement account. For ongoing open-account trade, payables to growers and logistics providers are disbursed via MT103 to matched counterparties only, with explicit invoice and contract references; no cash payments or commingled third-party disbursements are permitted.Deliverable OTC FX forwards in EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, and EUR/CNY with an Estonian or EU Tier-1 counterparty under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement and CSA, tenor 1 to 12 months, limited to estimated underlying export flows.Plain-vanilla EUR/USD and EUR/CNY cross-currency swaps used to hedge medium-term trade finance facilities or shareholder-loan exposure, subject to ISDA/CSA and EMIR margin and reporting rules.Non-deliverable forwards for currencies that cannot be delivered into Estonia because of central-bank convertibility restrictions, contracted with a regulated EU investment firm and retained strictly for hedging forecast export receipts.European-style FX options for protective downside risk on large USD or CNY invoiced shipments, permitted where the bank grants an FX derivative line and the strategy is documented as a hedge under the board-approved treasury policy.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

22% of net profit distributions, generally calculated as 22/78 of the net amount distributed, effective from 1 January 2025. Undistributed and retained accounting profits are not subject to Estonian corporate income tax merely because they are earned.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Dividends are generally subject to Estonian corporate income tax at 22/78 of the net distribution from 1 January 2025; this is a company-level tax, not generally a withholding tax imposed on a non-resident corporate shareholder receiving an ordinary dividend. A 7% withholding obligation may apply to specified dividends taxed under the transitional 14/86 regime when paid onward to an individual.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

TSD and tax due are generally due by the 10th day of the month following the month in which the taxable payment or distribution was made. The annual report is generally due within six months after the financial year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Estonian detailed transfer-pricing documentation requirements generally apply to specified categories of taxpayers, including a company with at least 250 employees, annual turnover exceeding EUR 50 million, or balance-sheet total exceeding EUR 43 million, and specified non-resident permanent establishments and group situations. Separate master-file obligations may apply to qualifying multinational groups, including groups meeting the applicable EUR 750 million consolidated-revenue criterion. These thresholds do not exempt smaller taxpayers from the arm's-length principle or from producing evidence when requested.

Economic substance scope: Estonia does not operate a general standalone Economic Substance Regulations regime equivalent to certain offshore jurisdictions. Estonian and EU anti-avoidance rules remain applicable, including the arm's-length standard, general anti-abuse principles, controlled foreign company rules where relevant, and applicable EU ATAD measures. Maintain genuine decision-making, personnel, premises, records, and commercial capacity consistent with the OÜ's actual functions and risks; e-Residency alone does not establish tax residence, management substance, or treaty entitlement.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax fraud and false reporting

Statutory sanction: Assessment of unpaid tax and interest, recovery measures, and administrative or criminal penalties depending on intent, amount, and statutory elements. Criminal liability may include a fine or imprisonment under Estonian law; exact sanctions depend on the offence and court findings.

Director personal liability: Directors may face personal criminal liability for their own intentional conduct and may incur civil or tax-related liability where statutory grounds are met. Limited liability does not protect a director from personal criminal responsibility or liability for an independently established breach of duty.
Non-arm's-length related-party transactions

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may adjust taxable amounts, assess corporate income tax, interest, and applicable penalties, and require supporting transfer-pricing documentation. Tax treatment depends on the transaction and applicable rules.

Director personal liability: A director may be liable for personal misconduct or a proven breach of management duties; an adjustment does not automatically pierce the OÜ's limited liability.
Unlawful dividend or disguised distribution

Statutory sanction: The payment may be treated as a taxable distribution or other taxable outflow, with tax, interest, and penalties. An unlawful distribution may also be recoverable under company-law rules.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize unlawful payments may face company-law liability and potential repayment claims. Personal liability depends on the director's conduct, applicable statutory duties, and causation.
Unlicensed or non-compliant food business

Statutory sanction: Competent authorities may order corrective action, restrict or suspend operations, withdraw or recall products, and impose administrative or other statutory sanctions. Requirements depend on the product, activity, and destination market.

Director personal liability: Directors may face personal consequences for their own offences or breaches of duty; corporate non-compliance alone does not automatically remove limited liability.
Money laundering and sanctions violations

Statutory sanction: Potential account restrictions, asset freezing, regulatory measures, substantial fines, and criminal prosecution, including imprisonment where statutory elements are met. The applicable sanction depends on the conduct and relevant sanctions regime.

Director personal liability: Directors and other individuals may be prosecuted for their own participation, knowing facilitation, or legally defined failure to perform applicable duties. Travel restrictions or asset measures may apply only where authorized by law and a competent authority.
Shell or sham-company arrangements and misrepresentation of management

Statutory sanction: Tax residence, permanent establishment, treaty, and transaction consequences may be reassessed under applicable domestic and treaty rules; additional tax, interest, penalties, and foreign-jurisdiction exposure may follow. There is no automatic Estonian penalty solely for having a low-substance company.

Director personal liability: Directors may face liability for false statements, fraud, or breach of duty where established. Incorporation through e-Residency does not confer immunity or automatically establish tax residence.
