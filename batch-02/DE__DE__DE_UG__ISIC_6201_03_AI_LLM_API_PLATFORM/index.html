Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Commercial Register (Handelsregister) maintained by the local District Court / Register Court (Amtsgericht - Registergericht) in the district of the UG's registered office. Submission is made electronically through a notary public. Tax registration is handled separately by the competent local tax office (Finanzamt).

Governing statute

German Limited Liability Companies Act (GmbH-Gesetz, GmbHG), especially §§ 1, 3, 5a, 7, 8, 35-53; German Commercial Code (HGB) §§ 8, 8a, 106, 161; German Notarisation Act (Beurkundungsgesetz); Money Laundering Act (GwG) beneficial-ownership rules.

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarised formation deed (Errichtungsurkunde) incorporating the Articles of Association or statutory model protocol (Musterprotokoll), together with the application for entry in the Commercial Register (Anmeldung zur Handelsregistereintragung).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 21 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

A UG (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschränkt) may be formed with a statutory share capital of at least €1 but must remain below €25,000 until profit increments are used to build up a regular GmbH capital reserve. All cash contributions must be paid in full before registration and must be at the managing director's free disposal; capital contributions in kind are prohibited for the UG. No formal escrow account is required by statute, but a designated business bank account with a capital-payment confirmation is the standard evidence for the registration declaration.

Funding & unlock path: Not mandatory for a pure online service company with no physical manufacturing plant or public-access facility. If a German office is opened, the employer must obtain workplace safety clearance under the Workplace Ordinance (Arbeitsstättenverordnung), including verification of fire-safety equipment, escape routes, emergency exits and safe electrical installations, and evidence of a building fire-safety completion certificate from the local building authority (Bauaufsicht). Civil-defence clearance is not required except for critical-infrastructure premises, in which case the municipal civil-protection office must approve the building's resilience and emergency-response plans. For remote-first operation, document an office-equivalence assessment and include it in the occupational health and safety file.

Paid-in capital rule: A UG (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschränkt) may be formed with a statutory share capital of at least €1 but must remain below €25,000 until profit increments are used to build up a regular GmbH capital reserve. All cash contributions must be paid in full before registration and must be at the managing director's free disposal; capital contributions in kind are prohibited for the UG. No formal escrow account is required by statute, but a designated business bank account with a capital-payment confirmation is the standard evidence for the registration declaration.

(5) Formation expenses are approved and all first-board resolutions are entered into the corporate book. Unanimously adopted.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No German industrial licensing board is responsible for a pure AI/LLM API service company. The local trade authority (Gewerbeamt) handles mandatory trade registration and the Chamber of Industry and Commerce (IHK) administers compulsory membership. If the company elects to own a physical server data centre, the competent authority would be the State construction and immission-control authority (Bauaufsichtsbehörde/Immissionsschutzbehörde).

Category: Gewerbeanmeldung trade-registration certificate and IHK membership notice. No separate industrial operating permit is required for cloud-based generative-AI platform services. Further approval such as a telecommunications licence under TKG or a Federal Network Agency registration is not applicable to a pure API/software platform unless the company provides public telecom-network services.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax administration is primarily handled by the competent state-level Finanzamt. A UG generally registers through the Finanzamt responsible for its registered office using ELSTER (the German tax administration portal). The Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, BZSt) issues VAT identification numbers (Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer, USt-IdNr.) and administers certain cross-border VAT procedures through its online services.

E-government filing portal

After commercial-register registration and commencement of business, submit the electronic Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung through ELSTER to the competent Finanzamt. The Finanzamt assigns a domestic tax number and assesses corporate income tax, trade tax, and VAT registration requirements. Apply separately to the BZSt for a USt-IdNr., ordinarily after or in connection with tax registration. A foreign entity without a German establishment applies to the competent German tax office for non-resident taxpayers; the responsible office depends on the circumstances and applicable allocation rules. Provide incorporation and registry evidence, registered-office and management details, beneficial-owner information, business activities, expected turnover, bank details, and relevant evidence of German taxable activity. EU cross-border transactions may also require participation in the applicable recapitulative-statement and VAT reporting procedures.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

A German UG is a separate taxable corporation and is not eligible for a US-style check-the-box election to be treated as a partnership or disregarded entity. It is generally subject to corporate income tax, solidarity surcharge, and municipal trade tax. Germany has no general entity-classification election that converts a UG into a pass-through entity. Hybrid mismatch rules may deny deductions or require income inclusion in specified cases but do not constitute an elective classification regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general VAT registration threshold applies to ordinary taxable persons, including non-established suppliers making taxable supplies in Germany. The small-business scheme (Kleinunternehmerregelung) may exempt qualifying domestic supplies where prior-calendar-year turnover does not exceed EUR 25,000 and current-calendar-year turnover does not exceed EUR 100,000; the rules and consequences depend on the taxpayer's circumstances, and the scheme is not a general registration safe harbor for cross-border supplies. The reduced VAT rate is generally 7%.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods may qualify as VAT-exempt with credit for input tax under the German VAT Act where goods are transported or dispatched from Germany to a destination outside the EU and the supplier retains prescribed proof of export and transaction evidence. Intra-Community supplies may be exempt only if statutory conditions are met, including transport to another EU Member State, a valid customer VAT ID, and timely and accurate recapitulative reporting. AI API and other electronically supplied services are not automatically zero-rated as exports: place-of-supply rules determine whether German VAT applies. B2B services supplied to a business customer established outside Germany are generally taxed where the customer is established; B2C electronic services may be taxable where the consumer is located, with applicable non-EU and EU registration or collection rules. Input VAT recovery remains subject to ordinary statutory requirements.

  • Export customs declaration and export accompanying or exit confirmation evidence, where applicable
  • Commercial invoice, contract or order, and records linking the supply to the exported goods
  • Bill of lading, airway bill, or other transport evidence where available and relevant
  • Proof of the customer's location and, for intra-Community supplies, valid VAT identification and transport evidence
  • Inward remittance certificate: not a generally required German statutory export-VAT document; retain payment and accounting evidence where relevant

Germany does not generally require real-time tax-authority clearance or digital stamping of domestic invoices. From 1 January 2025, domestic businesses must be able to receive compliant electronic invoices for domestic B2B transactions. Issuance is being phased in: during 2025 and 2026, transitional rules permit specified alternative invoice formats; certain suppliers may continue those formats through 2027 subject to statutory conditions, including turnover limits. A structured invoice must comply with the applicable European standard (EN 16931) or an accepted interoperable format. The e-invoicing obligation does not generally apply to B2C invoices or cross-border transactions. VAT returns and recapitulative statements are generally filed electronically through ELSTER.

Germany imposes no general foreign-exchange repatriation deadline for export proceeds. Unsupported or incorrectly claimed VAT exemption can result in assessment of output VAT, interest, correction of VAT returns, and denial or repayment of input VAT credits. Intentional or reckless tax understatements may trigger tax-criminal or administrative proceedings, including fines and, for tax evasion, criminal penalties under the German Fiscal Code. Customs violations may attract separate penalties. Maintain contemporaneous contracts, invoices, transport and customs evidence, customer and VAT-ID checks, and accurate VAT and recapitulative filings.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany, as a Eurozone member, guarantees unrestricted convertibility for both current and capital account transactions under Articles 63-65 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement. Profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is legally unrestricted, but subject to tax compliance verification (e.g., tax clearance certificates) through the financing bank. No exchange control or convertibility limitations exist.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or conversion of export proceeds into EUR is required under the German Foreign Trade and Payments Regulation (AWV). Cross-border payments and receipts exceeding EUR 12,500 must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank within seven days for statistical purposes, retaining underlying transaction documentation.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (central bank and balance-of-payments statistics authority) with supervisory enforcement by Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht (BaFin)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: High for fiat currency transactions through EU/EEA Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) passporting under the Revised Payment Services Directive (PSD2), provided BaFin notification is completed. Legal constraint: EMIs are not authorized to hold or exchange crypto-assets; therefore USDC settlement must be arranged through a licensed Crypto Asset Service Provider (CASP) under the EU Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCAR) or a corresponding German BaFin license.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Cross-border ownership outside the EEA

Root cause: Cross-border ownership outside the EEA

Defense evidence: Notarized proof of identity of all UBOs, audited corporate structure chart, UBO registry declaration under the German Anti-Money Laundering Act (GwG), economic substance documentation (lease, payroll, bank references), certified translations.
Acceptance of cryptocurrency or USDC

Root cause: Acceptance of cryptocurrency or USDC

Defense evidence: Contract with a regulated payment institution or CASP handling conversion/settlement, evidence that the UG does not exercise crypto custody, documented transaction monitoring and AML policies, tax treatment confirmation for digital assets, and customer risk acceptance policy.
High-volume microtransactions originating from overseas

Root cause: High-volume microtransactions originating from overseas

Defense evidence: Detailed business plan and revenue model for LLM API tokens, customer due diligence policy including sanctions screening (EU and OFAC), enterprise customer list where applicable, and a transaction threshold monitoring program with exception reporting.
Low initial capitalisation with thin capital or operational expenses funded by intra-group loans

Root cause: Low initial capitalisation with thin capital or operational expenses funded by intra-group loans

Defense evidence: Shareholders' agreement with committed funds, five-year financial projections, bank reference letter from the parent entity, tax registrations in Germany and residence jurisdiction, and a funds-source declaration supported by recent bank statements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injections from non-residents must be remitted via SWIFT MT103 (or SEPA for EUR-denominated small injections) using Deutsche Bundesbank statistical purpose codes (e.g., E01 for initial equity, E02 for capital increase). The UG must maintain a capital escrow account designated for the investment and file the required balance-of-payments report within seven days for amounts exceeding EUR 12,500. The financing bank requires certified articles of association, commercial register extract, and UBO declaration prior to crediting the funds.Trade settlements for operational purchases, licensing fees, and vendor payments are executed via standard SWIFT MT103 messages with appropriate AWV purpose codes (e.g., C120 for IT services) and underlying invoices. For high-value or contractually secured transactions, documentary collections or bank-issued commercial letters of credit (MT700 series) are permissible.
