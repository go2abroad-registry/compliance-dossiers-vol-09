Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Commercial Register Division of the Local Court (Handelsregister beim Amtsgericht) having local jurisdiction over the registered office; the filing is submitted electronically by a German Notary Public through the Common Register Portal of the German Länder (Gemeinsames Registerportal der Länder).

Governing statute

German Limited Liability Companies Act (Gesetz betreffend die Gesellschaften mit beschränkter Haftung – GmbHG): §§ 1 (legal personality), 2 (notarial certification of Articles), 4a (registered office), 5a (Unternehmergesellschaft (haftungsbeschränkt) – UG), 7 (application and minimum paid-in capital), 8 (content of the application), 10 (registration order), 13 (existence upon registration), read together with § 14 GewO (trade notification) and the Commercial Register Regulation (Handelsregisterverordnung – HRV).

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarially certified application for registration in the Commercial Register (Anmeldung zur Eintragung in das Handelsregister) pursuant to §§ 7–8 GmbHG, accompanied by the notarized Articles of Association (Gesellschaftsvertrag or notarial Musterprotokoll), the shareholder list (Gesellschafterliste), and notarized specimen signatures of the Managing Director.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 23 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

A UG may be formed with a registered share capital below EUR 25,000; the statutory minimum is EUR 1. Every cash contribution must be fully paid in before the Commercial Register filing, and in-kind contributions are excluded (section 5a(2) GmbHG). The UG must retain 25% of annual net profits as a mandatory statutory reserve until the share capital reaches EUR 25,000. The contribution is paid on a German business bank account of the UG (i.G.) or on the notary's segregated escrow account (Notar-Anderkonto), with payment confirmation verified by the notary.

Funding & unlock path: Office workplaces must comply with the Workplace Ordinance (Arbeitsstättenverordnung – ArbStättV), DGUV V3 electrical-installation testing, smoke-alarm and fire-extinguisher requirements under the State Building Codes (Landesbauordnung), and structural-load certification only where a physical esports tournament or LAN venue is operated; no civil-defense clearance is applicable to the virtual head office.

The UG must retain 25% of annual net profits as a mandatory statutory reserve until the share capital reaches EUR 25,000. Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
FIRST BOARD RESOLUTION — The founding shareholders of UG (haftungsbeschränkt) resolve pursuant to §§ 5a, 8 GmbHG: (1) subscription of the registered share capital of EUR 3,000, paid in cash in full on the escrow business account before Commercial Register filing; (2) adoption of the notarized Articles of Association and registered office as set out in the deed; (3) appointment of the Managing Director (Geschäftsführer) with sole representation authority, authorized to execute the statutory filings; (4) adoption of the business purpose: operation of an esports community and tournament platform (ISIC 5820); (5) instruction to the Notary to file the Commercial Register application forthwith. Effective upon signature.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No specialized industrial licensing board is triggered for a software publishing and esports community platform (ISIC 5820 / NAICS 711219 digital tournament operations). The relevant authorities are the municipal Trade Office (Gewerbeamt/Ordnungsamt) for the Gewerbeanmeldung under section 14 GewO, and the state building authorities (Bauaufsicht) only if a physical tournament venue or office premises are later established.

Category: Gewerbeanmeldung (trade notification under section 14 GewO) — a registration duty, not a discretionary permit; no separate statutory operating license is required for the platform under German law, provided that no regulated gambling or real-money betting products are offered, consistent with the Low_Risk_Standard AML classification.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (Federal Central Tax Office) for VAT identification numbers (USt-IdNr) and the ELSTER electronic tax portal (https://www.elster.de); locally competent Finanzamt for income, corporate and trade tax registration.

E-government filing portal

For a newly-established German UG (any non-resident ownership permitted): file the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (registration questionnaire) with the competent local Finanzamt via ELSTER or Form USt 1 A for VAT purposes; obtain a Steuernummer from the Finanzamt; separately apply for a USt-IdNr from the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern using the official application (Form USt 1 A or EÜR-based electronic application) once the UG is commercially active. Required supporting documents include Handelsregister excerpt, articles of association, appointment of managing director, and proof of registered office address.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: The German UG is a legal entity subject to corporate income tax at 15% plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge and municipal trade tax; shareholders are separately taxed on dividends. Unlimited tax liability applies to the UG as a resident corporation. Pass-through treatment is not available.

Available election forms

German law does not provide a check-the-box regime. Electable mechanisms include: corporate income tax group (Organschaft) under Secs. 14-17 KStG; trade tax group under Sec. 2(2) GewStG; VAT group (Organschaft) under Sec. 2(2) UStG; and the small-entrepreneur election (Kleinunternehmer) under Sec. 19 UStG. Hybrid mismatch adjustments must be made automatically pursuant to Sec. 4k EStG / Sec. 12d GewStG and cannot be avoided by election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Small-enterprise exemption threshold: EUR 25,000 prior-year turnover and EUR 100,000 projected current-year turnover; above these thresholds, VAT registration and mandatory charging/collecting of VAT arise. For cross-border distance sales and digital services to German consumers by non-established suppliers, the EU-wide EUR 10,000 annual threshold applies, but the OSS/non-union OSS may be used without separate German registration threshold after exceeding it.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to: (i) physical exports to third countries when the seller transports goods out of Germany to a destination outside the EU; (ii) intra-EU B2B supplies of goods when the buyer provides a valid USt-IdNr and the seller submits an EC Sales List; and (iii) cross-border B2B services supplied to customers whose place of business is outside Germany (place-of-supply rule, reverse charge). For an esports platform, digital service revenue from non-EU business customers falls outside the German VAT scope; intra-EU B2C digital services are taxable at the destination member state VAT rate via the OSS scheme.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Ausfuhrbegleitdokument ABD / electronic customs declaration ZAEDB)
  • Bill of Lading or CMR consignment note (or airway bill / certificate of shipment)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate and corresponding bank/foreign-exchange repatriation evidence

Germany does not require real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital tax stamp certification. Since 1 January 2025, every German taxable person must be able to receive compliant structured electronic invoices (EN 16931 / XRechnung / ZUGFeRD) for domestic B2B supplies. Mandatory issuance of e-invoices applies progressively from 1 January 2027 for taxable persons with prior-year turnover exceeding EUR 800,000 and from 1 January 2028 for all domestic B2B suppliers. Federal public supply contracts already require XRechnung without exception.

The tax authorities scrutinise export substantiation documentation pursuant to Sec. 8 UStG and Secs. 13-15 UStDV. Failure to produce complete evidence within the 180-day statutory deadline triggers retrospective VAT assessment at 19% on the export invoice. Fraudulent zero-rating claims are additionally punishable as tax evasion under Sec. 370 AO with imprisonment and fines, subject to interest of 0.5% per month assessed under Sec. 233a AO, penalty surcharges under Sec. 26a UStG, and extended assessment periods of up to 10 years in cases of intent.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany grants unrestricted euro convertibility on both current and capital account transactions under Articles 63–66 TFEU and the OECD Capital Movements Code. A UG (Unternehmergesellschaft haftungsbeschränkt) may repatriate profits and dividends to non-resident shareholders without exchange-control pre-approval. Dividend remittances are subject to German withholding tax at the statutory rate, generally 25% plus solidarity surcharge, potentially reduced under an applicable double tax treaty. There is no separate capital account conversion approval, but payment-receipt statistics are mandatory and bank counterparty due diligence on the UBO structure must always be satisfied. Sanctions screening under EU/German restrictive measures is the primary non-tax impediment to free remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany imposes no surrender or mandatory conversion of export proceeds. Export receipts may be freely retained or converted in foreign currency. Under the AWV (Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance), residents must report to the Deutsche Bundesbank for balance-of-payments statistics any receipt or payment arising from cross-border transactions with non-residents, including export/import of goods, services, and capital movements, when the amount is at least €12,500. For goods exports specifically, the export transaction must be reported to the Bundesbank within the reporting deadlines if the invoice value exceeds €12,500. Non-compliance with AWV statistical reporting is an administrative offense carrying a fine. A UG engaged in esports platform services must implement systematic AWV reporting workflows to clear banking compliance and avoid late-reporting penalties.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank, in conjunction with the German Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin) for anti-money laundering supervision and the German Federal Foreign Trade Administration (BAFA) and customs authorities for foreign trade control under the Foreign Trade and Payments Act (AWG) and Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (AWV). Germany does not maintain an exchange control administration as a distinct state agency; capital and payment flows are structurally liberalized by the euro-area legal framework.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-constrained for EU/EEA-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) and Low for non-EEA offshore digital banks. An EU EMIs, such as a British or Irish e-money institution after passporting into Germany under the electronic money package, may provide local IBANs and card rails for low-risk subscription collection. However, EMIs generally lack deposit-guarantee coverage, cannot execute certain bespoke L/C or trade settlement operations, and are treated by German clearing counterparties as unregulated payment services rather than full credit institutions. A UG reliant on EMIs for primary treasury operations should maintain a parallel tier-1 bank account; EMIs should be used only for payout dispersion, forex conversion, and peripheral merchant acquiring.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injections from non-resident shareholders into the German UG are implemented by a cross-border SWIFT MT103 credit wire in EUR referencing the corporate resolution or capital increase resolution. The bank must receive the investor bank's source-of-wealth package and a copy of the notarised shareholder resolution before clearing the capital contribution. The purpose code field should accurately identify the transaction as a capital contribution, together with the UG's tax number and a short reference to the German AWV reportable transaction. If the shareholder funding is a subordinated shareholder loan, the mandate should be labelled 'Gesellschafterdarlehen' and evidenced by a loan agreement with arm's-length interest documentation. For AWV statistics, the UG must report the incoming capital transfer to Deutsche Bundesbank where the value is €12,500 or more. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required if cross-border related-party transactions exceed €5 million (goods) or €500,000 (services) per annum; Master File required if group consolidated revenue exceeds €5 million.

Economic substance scope: Germany does not have specific Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), but general anti-abuse rules (Art. 42 AO) and substance requirements for treaty benefits apply. A UG must demonstrate real economic activity (premises, employees, decision-making) to avoid classification as a shell company.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Steuerhinterziehung)

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment up to 5 years (10 years in severe cases) and fines up to €50,000

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for unpaid taxes and penalties; travel bans and asset freezes possible
Shell Company (Briefkastenfirma)

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits, recharacterization of income, fines up to €25,000

Director personal liability: Directors liable for back taxes and interest; potential disqualification from management
AML Violations (Geldwäschegesetz)

Statutory sanction: Fines up to €1 million for companies; imprisonment for individuals

Director personal liability: Directors personally fined and barred from management positions
