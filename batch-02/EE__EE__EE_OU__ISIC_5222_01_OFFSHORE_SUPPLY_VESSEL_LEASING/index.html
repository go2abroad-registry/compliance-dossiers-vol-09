Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet, MTA); portal: e-MTA at https://www.emta.ee/eng

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned OÜ, incorporation with the Estonian Commercial Register (Äriregister) via the e-Business Register portal assigns a registry code (registrikood), which is the corporate tax identification number (TIN). The registry notification is transmitted automatically to the MTA, creating the tax identity. A separate VAT identification number must be applied for through the e-MTA portal; mandatory registration applies once taxable supplies exceed the statutory threshold, or immediately for a non-resident entity making its first taxable supply in Estonia unless the reverse-charge mechanism applies. Foreign shareholders may use mobile ID or e-Residency digital signatures; all subsequent tax filings are made electronically in the e-MTA system.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (deferred taxation at profit distribution; undistributed profits not subject to current tax) — For a foreign-owned OÜ, incorporation with the Estonian Commercial Register (Äriregister) via the e-Business Register portal assigns a registry code (registrikood), which is the corporate tax identification number (TIN). The registry notification is transmitted automatically to the MTA, creating the tax identity. A separate VAT identification number must be applied for through the e-MTA portal; mandatory registration applies once taxable supplies exceed the statutory threshold, or immediately for a non-resident entity making its first taxable supply in Estonia unless the reverse-charge mechanism applies. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (deferred taxation at profit distribution; undistributed profits not subject to current tax)

Available election forms

No check-the-box pass-through election is available; an OÜ is irrevocably a taxable entity. The reduced dividend tax rate of 14/86 (14% of gross distribution) may be elected on regular dividend payments made at least annually provided the company has met certain previous distribution/tax compliance criteria. Hybrid mismatch rules are mandatory under ATAD and are not elective. For VAT purposes, a taxable person established in Estonia may voluntarily register before the threshold is reached; no general hybrid fiscal election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

22%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 40,000 annual taxable turnover for Estonian-established taxable persons; non-established foreign entities have no registration threshold and must register before making any taxable supply in Estonia unless the reverse-charge / customer-accounting mechanism relieves them.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: 0% VAT applies to the sale and export of goods physically removed from Estonia to a destination outside the EU/EEA, provided the exporter has a valid customs export declaration and transport evidence. Services: Offshore vessel chartering is zero-rated (or outside the scope of Estonian VAT) where the charterer is a taxable person established outside Estonia and the general B2B place-of-supply rule applies; additional efficacy is required for services relating directly to the operation of vessels engaged on the high seas or in the Estonian exclusive economic zone where the customer is a non-EU taxable person or the effective use and enjoyment lies outside the EU. Where the charterer is an Estonian taxable person, domestic VAT at 22% applies unless a specific legal exemption is available.

  • Customs export declaration (electronic EXS/ENS accepted via the Estonian Customs Declarations Portal)
  • Bill of lading or sea waybill evidencing final destination outside the EU/EEA
  • Inward remittance certificate or equivalent Swift MT103/202 proof of payment from the non-resident charterer
  • Written statement from the non-resident charterer confirming its taxable-person status and establishment outside Estonia
  • Commercial charter party agreement and voyage instruction letter

Estonia does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance system, digital tax stamping, or continuous transaction controls. All B2G invoices must be submitted as structured e-invoices in Peppol-BIS XML format via the e-MTA portal. B2B invoices have no pre-clearance requirement but must be stored in an accessible, unaltered format for the statutory retention period, and VAT returns are filed electronically through e-MTA.

The MTA may retroactively audit VAT positions for three tax years (extended to six years for deliberate evasion). Wrongly claimed export zero-rating results in retroactive VAT assessment plus statutory interest of 0.06% per day, administrative fines up to EUR 200,000 for false customs declarations, and disqualification from zero-rating for a further period. Fraudulent refund claims constitute tax fraud under the Estonian Penal Code, carrying fines and imprisonment of up to five years for large-scale evasion. The MTA also applies coordinated post-clearance checks with the Estonian Customs and the European Anti-Fraud Office (OLAF) for offshore marine transactions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia is a member of the Eurozone; the euro is fully convertible. There are no balance-of-payments or exchange-control restrictions on current or capital account transactions for EEA transactions, with residual EU/UN sanction screening for non-EEA flows. Profit remittance is freely convertible, subject to Estonian corporate income tax rules (0% on retained earnings; 20% / 14% on dividend distributions) and source-of-tax-residency substance documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export receipts. Proceeds may be held in any freely convertible currency in Estonian or EEA-licensed credit institutions; however, all inflows are subject to AML/CFT transaction monitoring and, for cash and high-value transfers, supplementary source-of-funds verification.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia) and the Estonian Financial Supervision Authority (Finantsinspektsioon); the Ministry of Finance supervises international sanctions implementation.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium — Fully legal under EEA passport (PSD2/EMD2, licensed or passported into Finantsinspektsioon). Suitable for initial volume, crew payments, and operating expenses; but for maritime charter escrow and long-term high-value settlements, a tier‑1 bank is preferred. The EMI must validate KYB, beneficial-ownership and source-of-funds, and must not be a shell or unlicensed platform; Estonian law treats such EMIs as payment institutions with restricted services.
