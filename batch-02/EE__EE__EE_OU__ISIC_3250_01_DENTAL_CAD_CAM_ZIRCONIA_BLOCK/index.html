A serviced or virtual office may be accepted for registry purposes if the company has a valid right to use the address and can receive official correspondence. Manufacture of dental ceramic blocks or milling discs requires a genuine production site, appropriate premises, equipment, quality-management controls, traceability, storage, occupational-safety controls, waste and environmental compliance, and any applicable construction, fire-safety, product, and medical-device registrations or conformity procedures. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Estonia is a fully euroised eurozone member with a single legal tender (EUR). Both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible and unrestricted under EU law (TFEU Articles 63–66), as Estonia applies no exchange control legislation. No approvals are required for invoicing in foreign currency, retaining export receipts, repatriating capital, distributing dividends, or servicing intercompany shareholder loans from foreign investors. The only material remittance hurdle is purely fiscal rather than currency-based: under the Estonian Income Tax Act, resident companies face 20/80 distribution tax on the net amount distributed (i.e., 25% effective on gross dividend), with a reduced 14/86 rate available for profit distributed regularly over the previous three years. There are no statutory payment suspensions apart from EU sanctions screening and Suspicious Transaction Reporting obligations under the Estonian Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Prevention Act.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Estonia imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds, no minimum conversion ratio into EUR, and no statutory repatriation deadline. Export earnings may remain in USD, GBP, or other freely transferable currency onshore or offshore; however, for VAT compliance and tax-audit traceability, export receipts must be substantiated by customs export declarations (Exit Summary Declaration) and underlying sales contracts under the Value Added Tax Act. The OÜ treasury should nonetheless maintain a cross-border receipts ledger aligning incoming MT103 references with custom clearance records to avoid false-positive AML anomalies.

Central bank supervision: Eesti Pank (Bank of Estonia) is the central bank and, together with the Estonian Financial Supervision and Resolution Authority (Finantsinspektsioon), administers the monetary and FX policy framework. Estonia operates under the auspices of the Eurosystem with the European Central Bank (ECB) as the ultimate monetary authority; there is no separate foreign exchange administration or FX licensing body.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium for ancillary treasury operations, limited for primary bank-grade trade flows. PSD2-authorised EMIs (Wise Europe, Revolut Ltd, Monese, Paysera, Zen.com) offer rapid EUR/USD/GBP collection rails and virtual IBANs, and are useful for small operational disbursements and distributor refunds. However, they are not credit institutions: they cannot issue or advise documentary letters of credit, do not extend trade finance lines, and may block client money onward forwarding where the underlying trade document trail is incomplete. Additionally, most EMIs decline to accept equity capital injections into registered capital due to insufficient ability to verify the share-subscription document and the Estonian Commercial Register extract. Recommended configuration: maintain the primary capital escrow and trade clearing account at a Tier-1 bank (LHV or Swedbank), and use EMIs only as a satellite payment rail for low-value purchases not exceeding EUR 5,000 per transaction.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route 1 — Share capital: remit EUR via SWIFT MT103 (faster than or equivalent to TARGET2 for North America, Asia and Middle East corridors) using ISO 20022 purpose code EQUI or Estonian balance-of-payments classification F1 (direct investment equity); instruct the remitting shareholder to insert in field 72 the OÜ registry number and the note SHARE CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION UNDER MEMORANDUM OF SUBSCRIPTION; accompany the transfer with a notarised resolution and updated e-Äriregister extract reflecting the registered capital post-injection. Route 2 — Shareholder loan: use purpose code LOAN (or BPM6-consistent instrument code) and a signed shareholder loan agreement providing the maturity and arm's-length interest-rate benchmark; the bank will treat the loan as neutral for capital adequacy but will request a negative pledge or subordination letter where utilised in a trade-downstream. All inbound funds must be flagged during the pre-transaction call with the relationship manager; amounts above EUR 10,000 will undergo source-of-funds review, and funds will be blocked pending submission of the capital formation dossier and registry filing, settled in central-bank money through TARGET2.For raw-material and equipment suppliers in SEPA: SEPA Credit Transfer with real-time settlement, referencing the purchase-contract number and packing list. For non-EEA and non-EUR payments: SWIFT MT103, purpose code GDS (or TRADE), with the beneficiary bank's BIC and the underlying invoice reference; settlement via TARGET2 or Eurosystem correspondent networks. For high-value rolling orders: commercial letter of credit via SWIFT MT700 under UCP600 rules, or standby credit for performance integrity via MT760; medium-value clean collections via D/P and D/A under URC522 are accepted but are discounted by suppliers only under trade finance relationship.
