A virtual office (coworking address without staffed local management) is not acceptable for the operating UG under the ZAG/payment services licensing regime because BaFin requires a fixed, staffed place of establishment in Germany from which card issuance activities are effectively managed. For pure commercial-register registration purposes a commercial address agent may be used, but the final BaFin pre-licensing decision will require an actual head office with local compliance and management presence. Thus virtual office is impermissible or severely restricted for the licensed operation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Euro is fully convertible. Current-account and capital-account transactions are unrestricted pursuant to Articles 63-65 TFEU and EU Directive 88/361/EEC; no exchange-control approval or import/export permit is required for corporate payments. Profit remittances by a UG to foreign shareholders are obstructed only by German dividend withholding tax of 25% plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge (aggregate 26.375%), which may be reduced under an applicable double-taxation treaty; the distribution must be evidenced by a notarised shareholders' resolution and a tax advisor's withholding certificate. There are no exchange-control hurdles for conversion or repatriation of paid-in capital.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, minimum conversion ratio or repatriation deadline applies in Germany. Exporters and service providers may retain 100% of foreign-currency receipts indefinitely in foreign accounts. The sole compliance duty is monetary reporting to Deutsche Bundesbank: any payment from a non-resident exceeding EUR 12,500 must be reported within five business days under AWV Section 67; non-reporting or mis-reporting is subject to administrative fines.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (central bank and balance-of-payments statistical reporting authority under the Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance, Außenwirtschaftsverordnung 'AWV'); prudential supervision of banking, payment institutions and e-money issuers is exercised by the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht, 'BaFin'). Germany maintains no separate foreign-exchange administration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH as an operating treasury and safeguarding liquidity rail, with material statutory constraints. EU/EEA-licensed electronic-money institutions passported into Germany under Directive 2009/110/EC and PSD2 (ZAG Sections 1a and 8), such as Modulr, MANGOPAY, Airwallex, Currencycloud (Visa) and Checkout.com, may lawfully provide SEPA, SWIFT MT103 and USD treasury accounts to the UG. However, card-scheme settlement (Mastercard Network and Visa Direct) cannot be executed on a standalone EMI account without a sponsoring clearing member bank, and the UG itself must hold a BaFin e-money or payment institution licence (or act as an appointed agent of a licensed programme manager) to conduct ISIC 6619 virtual-card issuance under German law.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound shareholder equity is routed via SWIFT MT103 (non-SEPA) or SEPA Core/Instant credit transfer (IBAN, ISO 20022 pain.001.001.03) with the reference 'Kapitalerhöhung UG (haftungsbeschränkt) - Stammeinlage' and the commercial register file number. No exchange-control permit is required. Verification requires the notarised capital-increase deed, the updated Handelsregister excerpt and proof of unconditional availability of funds. Any inbound payment above EUR 12,500 must be reported to Deutsche Bundesbank within five business days under AWV Section 67. For the UG, 25% of annual net profits must be retained in the statutory reserve until the share capital reaches EUR 25,000; shareholder loans should be documented with a subordination agreement to avoid equity recharacterisation and to protect capital-account verification.EUR treasury disbursements use SEPA Instant/Core credit transfers (ISO 20022 pain.001.001.03) and SEPA Direct Debit (pain.008.001.02). Non-EUR trade invoices are settled via SWIFT MT103 with AWV reporting for outbound payments exceeding EUR 12,500. Documentary trade is supported by commercial letters of credit (SWIFT MT700) and standby LCs (SWIFT MT760) confirmed by a German or EU bank. Card-scheme settlements are cleared through Mastercard Network and Visa clearing systems using MT202 COV cover payments via a sponsoring clearing bank; Visa Direct original credit transactions are permissible for cross-border disbursements to cardholders, subject to Visa programme guidelines and pre-funded settlement-liquidity requirements.Deliverable FX forwards (EUR/USD, EUR/GBP) with a German or EU bank under ISDA 2002 and a Credit Support Annex, subject to EMIR margining and trade-reporting obligations where applicableFX swaps (swap de change) for short-term liquidity rebalancing between EUR and USD clearing accountsCross-currency swaps (EUR/USD, EUR/GBP) for matched funding of long-term shareholder loans and intercompany balancesVanilla over-the-counter FX options (EUR/USD, EUR/GBP) with premium settlement, limited by board-approved cash-management policyNon-deliverable forwards are not a permitted primary hedge for EUR-denominated exposure because the Euro is fully convertible; NDFs may only be used for emerging-market currencies (e.g., CNH, TRY) underlying card spend, with board-approved counterparty limits
